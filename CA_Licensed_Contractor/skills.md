# CA Licensed Contractor — Skills

1. **License Status Verification** — CSLB lookup by name, license number, SSN; determine current status (active, inactive, retired, revoked, expired)
2. **Unauthorized Use Detection** — Identify whether anyone has used the license without the holder's authorization; cross-reference work performed against license records
3. **BPC §7031 Bar Analysis** — Determine whether unlicensed contracting bars recovery; evaluate whether the bar applies to the identity thief's contracts
4. **Pension Administration Audit** — Review UA Local 342 pension statements for unauthorized freezes, benefit diversion, or identity changes; ERISA compliance
5. **Trade Credential Authentication** — Verify apprenticeship completion, journeyman certification, union membership status against official records
6. **Identity Replacement Investigation** — Map discrepancies between the steward's known professional history and the current state of CSLB, pension, and credit records
