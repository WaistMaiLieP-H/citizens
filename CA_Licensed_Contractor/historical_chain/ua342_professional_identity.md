# CA Licensed Contractor — Citizen-Level Historical Chain

## The Trade Identity

---

### I. What a Plumbing License Means

A journeyman plumber's license is not a piece of paper. It is a verified professional identity — the product of years of apprenticeship, thousands of hours of supervised work, trade examinations, and union membership. UA Local 342 (Plumbers and Steamfitters, San Jose) is an AFL-CIO affiliated local that represents skilled tradespeople in the greater Bay Area.

The license says: this person has been trained, tested, and certified to perform plumbing work to code. It carries the authority to pull permits, sign off on inspections, and take responsibility for work that protects public health and safety. Water supply, drainage, gas lines — these systems kill people when they fail. The license is the promise that the person holding it knows how to prevent that.

### III. The CSLB Connection

The Contractors State License Board (CSLB) maintains records of all licensed contractors in California. CSLB records can be searched by name, license number, or business. The investigative step is:

1. Search CSLB for any license associated with the steward's name or SSN
2. Determine current status (active, inactive, retired, revoked, expired)
3. Determine who has been using the license since the steward was marked "retired"
4. Cross-reference with UA Local 342 membership records

### IV. The Pension Connection

UA Local 342 pensions are administered through a joint trust fund under ERISA (the federal Employee Retirement Income Security Act). A pension freeze — if not authorized by the steward — could be:
- Triggered by a conservator exercising authority over the estate
- Triggered by an identity change in the pension system's records
- Triggered by a fraudulent death or disability claim
- Simply an administrative error

The pension investigation connects to the Conservator Investigator's §2620 accounting question: if a conservator has been filing accountings, the pension should appear in those accountings. If it doesn't, either there is no conservatorship (and the pension freeze has a different cause) or the conservator is not properly accounting for the pension income.

### V. The Standard This Citizen Carries

The Contractors State License Law of 1929 is the origin standard — California's original contractor licensing framework. It exists because unregulated construction killed people. The license is a discovered promise: society promised that construction would be performed by qualified, accountable professionals. The steward's license is his proof of membership in that promise. If someone stole that proof, they stole more than a credential — they stole a professional identity built over years of work.
