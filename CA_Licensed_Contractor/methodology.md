# CA Licensed Contractor — Professional Methodology

**Citizen:** CA_Licensed_Contractor
**Filed:** 2026-04-09
**Purpose:** The document is the instruction. When trade credentials, CSLB records, pension documents, or employment records enter, this methodology fires.

---

## Trigger

Documents involving contractor licensing, trade credentials, union membership, pension administration, or professional identity enter a tethered folder.

---

## Phase 1: INTAKE

**The Citizen automatically:**
1. Catalogs document: CSLB record, union card, pension statement, employment record, license application, disciplinary action
2. Verifies license status: CSLB lookup by name, license number, and/or SSN
3. Identifies all actors: license holder, employer, union local, pension administrator, CSLB investigator
4. Cross-references identity: does the name/SSN on this document match the steward's known identity? Flags discrepancies.
5. Flags: status changes (active→retired→inactive), unauthorized use of license number, pension freezes, identity anomalies

**Gate deliverable: INTAKE MEMO**

---

## Phase 2: LEGAL ANALYSIS

**The Citizen automatically:**
1. Applies CSLL framework: is the license valid? Who is authorized to use it? Has it been misused?
2. Checks BPC §7028: has anyone performed licensed work using this license without authorization? (unlicensed contracting prohibition)
3. Checks BPC §7031: if unauthorized person used the license, they cannot recover compensation for work performed
4. Evaluates pension administration: ERISA compliance, unauthorized freezes, benefit diversion
5. Cross-references with Conservator Investigator: could a conservator have directed the pension freeze or authorized someone else to use the license?
6. Cross-references with Records Authentication: are the CSLB records, pension statements, or employment records authentic?

**Gate deliverable: FINDINGS REPORT**

---

## Phase 3: SYNTHESIS & WORK PRODUCT

1. Produces identity verification report: license status, authorized holder, discrepancies found
2. Produces damages assessment: lost pension income, unauthorized use of trade credentials, identity replacement harm
3. Produces draft complaint skeleton if violations found: identity theft, pension fraud, CSLL violations
4. Recommends next steps: CSLB complaint, pension fund inquiry, ERISA claim, identity theft report

**Gate deliverable: CASE ASSESSMENT**
