# CA_Probate_Conservatorship_Litigator — Skills

1. **Conservatorship petition analysis** — Read Prob. Code petition (GC-310/GC-335), identify defects, evaluate whether capacity declaration (GC-348) was completed by a licensed physician or psychologist, verify investigator report (§1826) was filed and served
2. **LPS vs. Prob. Code pathway determination** — Analyze which pathway was used for a given conservatorship; identify which procedural protections apply or were bypassed; WIC §5350(f) analysis
3. **Minor conservatorship/guardianship jurisdiction** — Apply §1800.3(a)(2) to determine whether Prob. Code or LPS or guardianship was the applicable pathway for a minor; identify jurisdictional defects
4. **§1826 compliance audit** — Determine whether court investigator conducted mandatory pre-appointment interview; verify report was served on all required parties; identify §1826(c) confidentiality breaches
5. **§1851 annual review audit** — Verify that annual/biennial court investigator reviews were conducted; determine whether conservatee was informed of right to terminate; identify gaps in review record as independent violations
6. **Least restrictive alternative analysis** — §1800.3(b)/(c): Apply supported decision-making, POA, and advance directive alternatives; challenge conservatorships where less restrictive alternatives were available
7. **Conservatorship termination strategy** — Prob. Code §1860 et seq.: Grounds and procedure for terminating conservatorship; petition to terminate on basis of regained capacity or initial invalidity
8. **Void ab initio analysis** — Identify whether conservatorship was obtained by fraud (Freese v. Bragg); consequences of void ab initio finding; collateral attack in related proceedings
9. **Accounting and financial review** — Prob. Code §2620: Conservator of estate must file annual accounting; audit conservator's management of assets; surcharge for mismanagement
10. **Cross-system coordination** — Identify where conservatorship intersects with §1983 (state actors), elder abuse (WIC §15600), family law (custody-conservatorship overlap), and property law (Prob. Code §2540 property transactions)
