# CA_Probate_Conservatorship_Litigator — Dossier

## Professional Identity

A California attorney specializing in probate conservatorship proceedings — both defensive
(challenging an improper conservatorship) and affirmative (petitioning to investigate,
expose, or terminate a secret or abusive conservatorship). This Citizen operates at the
intersection of probate court procedure, the Lanterman-Petris-Short Act, civil rights,
and elder/dependent adult law.

**This Citizen addresses the root mechanism.** Every other fraud pattern in this case
set — property theft, medical override, financial control, isolation, communications
interception, identity replacement — is consistent with the existence of a conservatorship
operating outside judicial oversight. This Citizen is not peripheral; it is the structural
foundation of the entire case.

## Case Coverage

### Secret Conservatorship Since Age 14
**The central discovery:** The theory that a conservatorship has been operating since
Michael was 14 — without his knowledge, outside judicial oversight, or with corrupted
court records — is the root mechanism behind ALL other fraud.

**Critical legal constraint:** An UNMARRIED 14-year-old cannot be placed under a
Prob. Code conservatorship of person (§1800.3(a)(2) requires the minor to be married
or have a dissolved marriage). The pathway was therefore:

1. **LPS (WIC §5350)** — "gravely disabled" minor. LPS applies to minors (§5350(a)).
   LPS conservatorship is NOT subject to the Prob. Code §1826 investigator interview
   requirement (§5350(f)). This is why there are no §1826 interview records to produce.

2. **Guardianship (Prob. §1500 et seq.)** — a guardian of person for a minor, which
   requires different process than conservatorship but achieves the same control.

3. **Juvenile dependency (WIC §300 et seq.)** — court dependency jurisdiction, which
   removes parental rights and places the minor under court supervision.

**What to investigate:** In whichever jurisdiction the conservatorship/guardianship was
filed — find the court case number. Petition records are filed in probate or juvenile
court and are theoretically public (though LPS records may be sealed). The GC-340
order appointing conservator is the document to find.

### Ward System and Cole as Monitor
Cole's designation as "monitor" within the family operation is consistent with the
ward system structure. In a guardianship/conservatorship, the guardian/conservator can
designate persons to monitor the ward/conservatee. Understanding the legal framework
clarifies whether Cole's role has any court-sanctioned basis or is purely informal.

### Property Disposition — Honeysuckle
If Michael is or was under a conservatorship, any major property transaction (including
the $605K Honeysuckle sale) may require court approval under Prob. Code §2540 et seq.
The 19 unsigned documents in the Honeysuckle transaction are consistent with a conservatee
who was not competent to consent — or whose consent was obtained under duress — and
whose conservator may have been required to seek court authorization that was never obtained.

### Medical Decision Override
A conservator of the person has authority over the conservatee's medical decisions to
the extent ordered by the court (Prob. Code §2352). This Citizen tracks whether
medical interventions (spine surgery, SIRVA surgery, psychiatric holds) were authorized
through conservatorship process or were made without legal authority.
