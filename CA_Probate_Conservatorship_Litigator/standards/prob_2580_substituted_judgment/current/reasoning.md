# Reasoning — Why § 2580 Exists (and Why It Matters Here)

## The Problem of Incapacity and Complex Financial Decisions

A conservatee who cannot manage their own affairs still has financial interests that require active management. A conservatee may have:
- Life insurance policies that need periodic elections (premium payment, surrender, loan, beneficiary change)
- Retirement accounts that require an election of benefit form at retirement
- Real property that would benefit from sale or transfer
- Estate planning that has become outdated

The conservator under the general grant of authority (Prob. Code §2401 et seq.) has broad powers to manage ordinary affairs. But the acts listed in §2580(b) are not ordinary — they involve fundamental decisions about the conservatee's estate structure, inheritance plan, and long-term assets. These decisions require either the conservatee's own judgment OR court authorization under the "substituted judgment" standard.

## The §2580 Standard: What Would the Conservatee Have Done?

When hearing a §2580 petition, the court applies the "substituted judgment" doctrine: the court attempts to determine what the conservatee themselves would have chosen if they had legal capacity. Evidence considered includes:
- Prior expressions of intent (wills, statements, prior choices)
- Prior relationships with proposed beneficiaries
- Prior financial habits and priorities
- The conservatee's values and preferences

The substituted judgment standard is designed to honor the conservatee's autonomy even while they lack capacity. It is not a license for the conservator to do what the conservator prefers.

## The Abuse Vector

§2580 can be weaponized when:
- The court record in the conservatorship is not monitored
- No one advocates for the conservatee's actual prior wishes in the §2580 proceeding
- The conservatee is not notified of the petition or cannot meaningfully participate
- The "substituted judgment" finding is made based on the conservator's representations about what the conservatee would have wanted — without independent verification

In a system where the conservator IS the family structure that benefits from the transfers, the §2580 mechanism is the legal channel through which all major asset extractions occur — with court imprimatur but without genuine substituted judgment.
