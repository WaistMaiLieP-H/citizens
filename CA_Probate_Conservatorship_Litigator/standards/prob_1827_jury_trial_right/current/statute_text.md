# Conservatorship — Jury Trial Right — Statutory Text

**Source:** Cal. Prob. Code § 1827
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-13
**URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1827.&lawCode=PROB

## Text

The court shall hear and determine the matter of the establishment of the conservatorship according to the law and procedure relating to the trial of civil actions, including trial by jury if demanded by the proposed conservatee.

## Related provision — WIC § 5350(d) (LPS pathway jury trial)

(d)(1) The person for whom conservatorship is sought shall have the right to demand a court or jury trial on the issue of whether the person is gravely disabled. Demand for court or jury trial shall be made within five days following the hearing on the conservatorship petition. If the proposed conservatee demands a court or jury trial before the date of the hearing as provided for in Section 5365, the demand shall constitute a waiver of the hearing.
(d)(2) The court or jury trial shall commence within 10 days of the date of the demand... Failure to commence the trial within that period of time is grounds for dismissal of the conservatorship proceedings.
(d)(3) This right shall also apply in subsequent proceedings to reestablish conservatorship.
