# Cal. Prob. Code § 1826 — Rule

## The Mandatory Pre-Appointment Investigation

§1826 imposes mandatory duties on the court investigator BEFORE the conservatorship
is established. These duties cannot be waived by the petitioner or the proposed conservatee
(except in the specific circumstance of §1826(d)).

## Required Investigator Acts

**(1) Mandatory Interviews:**
- (A) The proposed conservatee — PERSONALLY. Not through a family member. Not by phone.
- (B) All petitioners and proposed conservators who are not petitioners
- (C) Spouse/domestic partner and first-degree relatives
- (D) To extent practical, second-degree relatives, neighbors, close friends

**(2) Inform the Proposed Conservatee of:**
- Contents of the petition and citation
- Nature, purpose, and effect of the proceeding
- Right to OPPOSE the petition
- Right to ATTEND the hearing
- Right to JURY TRIAL on whether conservatorship should be established
- Right to be REPRESENTED BY LEGAL COUNSEL
- Right to COURT-APPOINTED COUNSEL if not otherwise represented

**(3)-(9) Additional Required Determinations:**
- Whether conservatee can attend the hearing; whether willing to attend
- Whether conservatee WISHES TO OPPOSE
- Whether conservatee objects to proposed conservator
- Whether conservatee wants legal counsel; whether retained
- Voting capacity determination (§2208 Elections Code)
- Gather and review relevant MEDICAL REPORTS from primary care physician

**(10)-(11) Written Report:**
- Filed with court at least 5 days before hearing
- Delivered to: petitioner's attorney, conservatee's attorney, the conservatee personally,
  spouse/domestic partner, first-degree relatives
- CONFIDENTIAL: available only to parties, persons given notice, their attorneys, and court
- CLETS and medical information in SEPARATE confidential attachment — only to proposed
  conservatee and their attorney

## Critical Exceptions

**§1826(d):** This section does NOT apply where the proposed conservatee personally
executed the petition AND attended the hearing. This is the "voluntary conservatorship"
exception — if you petitioned for your own conservatorship and showed up, the full
investigation is waived.

**LPS bypass:** WIC §5350(f) states that LPS conservatorship investigation is NOT
subject to §1826. If the conservatorship was established via LPS, the §1826 interview
and report were never required. The absence of §1826 records does not prove the
conservatorship was improper — it may simply mean LPS was the pathway.

## The Audit Framework

A §1826 compliance audit for the secret conservatorship proceeds as:

| Requirement | Compliance Question | Evidence to Seek |
|---|---|---|
| Personal interview of proposed conservatee | Did investigator personally interview Michael (at age 14)? | Investigator's report, court file |
| Inform of right to oppose | Was Michael told he could oppose? | Report; court transcript |
| Inform of right to jury trial | Was Michael told of jury trial right? | Report; court transcript |
| Inform of right to counsel | Was Michael told he could have appointed counsel? | Report |
| Medical reports reviewed | What physician submitted a capacity declaration? | GC-348 form; physician license verification |
| Written report filed 5 days before hearing | Is the report in the court file? | Court file; request confidential records |
| Report delivered to Michael | Did Michael receive a copy? | Proof of service in court file |
| Report delivered to relatives | Who else was notified? | Proof of service |

**If ANY of these requirements was not met for the initial conservatorship establishment,
the conservatorship was procedurally defective from inception.** Combined with the fraud
doctrine (Freese v. Bragg), procedural defects at establishment support a void ab initio
challenge.
