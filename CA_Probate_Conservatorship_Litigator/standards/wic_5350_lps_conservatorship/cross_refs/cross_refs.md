# Cross-References: Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 5350

| Standard | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `prob_1801_conservatorship_basis` | §5350 is the alternative to §1801 for gravely disabled persons and minors; the two pathways are mutually applicable but procedurally distinct |
| `prob_1826_investigator_report` | WIC §5350(f) explicitly exempts LPS from §1826 — the bypass that makes LPS the likely pathway for minor conservatorships without §1826 records |
| `prob_1851_annual_review` | WIC §5350(f) also exempts LPS from §1850/§1851 periodic review — LPS has annual renewal proceedings but under different rules |
| `prob_1800_3_minor_conservatee` | §1800.3(a)(2) limits Prob. Code to married minors; §5350(a) allows LPS for any gravely disabled minor — LPS is the statutory pathway for unmarried minors |
