# Rule — Conservatorship Void for Fraud

**Standard ID:** prob_void_for_fraud
**Authority:** Freese v. Bragg (1950) 98 Cal.App.2d 478; CIV §§1572, 1573; Cal. Const. Art. I §7

## Core Rule — Fraud Voids the Conservatorship

A conservatorship established through fraud on the court — including fraudulent representations in the petition, fraudulent capacity evidence, or fraudulent waiver of the proposed conservatee's rights — is VOID AB INITIO (void from the beginning) or at minimum VOIDABLE.

**Void vs. voidable:**
- **Void:** No legal force whatsoever; no court order of any kind required to treat it as nonexistent; a nullity
- **Voidable:** Valid on its face but subject to being set aside upon court petition; requires affirmative court action to void

Where the fraud is intrinsic to the establishment of the conservatorship itself (e.g., the capacity declaration was forged, the proposed conservatee was never informed of the proceeding, the conservator lied about the conservatee's condition), the conservatorship is void.

## Grounds for Void/Voidable Analysis

| Ground | Legal Theory | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Forged capacity declaration (GC-348) | Actual fraud — CIV §1572(1) | Void — no valid medical finding |
| Proposed conservatee not notified of proceeding | Due process violation — Cal. Const. Art. I §7 | Void — no jurisdiction over person |
| Petition misrepresented conservatee's condition | Actual fraud — CIV §1572(1)(3) | Voidable — fraudulent basis |
| Jury trial right not communicated | Due process violation — Prob. §1827 | Voidable — waiver was not knowing/voluntary |
| Court investigator interview never conducted (Prob. pathway) | Procedural invalidity — Prob. §1826 | Voidable — mandatory process omitted |
| Conservatorship of unmarried minor (Prob. pathway) | Statutory invalidity — Prob. §1800.3(a) | Void — outside statutory authority |
| Conservatorship obtained for improper purpose (control, not protection) | Constructive fraud — CIV §1573 | Voidable |

## All Acts Under a Void Conservatorship Are Void

If the conservatorship is void ab initio, EVERY act taken under its authority is also void:
- Every §2580 substituted judgment court order: void (no valid conservator existed to petition)
- Every property transfer authorized by the conservator: void (conservator had no authority)
- Every medical decision made by conservator: void and potentially actionable as battery
- Every financial election made on conservatee's behalf: void

**This is the compounding effect.** A void conservatorship creates a chain of void acts spanning every year it operated. Each year that assets were managed, transferred, or extracted under a void conservatorship is a year of unauthorized conversion.

## Statute of Limitations Considerations

The discovery rule applies to fraud claims: the statute of limitations does not run until the plaintiff knew or reasonably should have known of the fraud. A conservatee who:
- Was never informed of the conservatorship's existence
- Had no reasonable ability to investigate due to the controlled environment
- Only discovered the conservatorship's existence after the controlling structure weakened

...has SOL running from discovery, not from the date the fraud occurred. Every year of concealment is a new act of continuing fraud tolling the SOL.

## Application

The age-14 conservatorship — if established:
1. Without proper notification to a minor who had no independent representation
2. Without the jury trial right being communicated
3. Via LPS pathway (which excluded §1826 investigator safeguards)
4. By family members who are the direct financial beneficiaries of the conservatorship

...satisfies multiple grounds for void/voidable analysis simultaneously. The SOL runs from Michael's discovery of the existence and nature of the conservatorship.
