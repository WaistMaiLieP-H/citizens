# CA_Elder_Law_Litigator — Skills

1. **Financial abuse pattern identification** — Apply §15610.30's three pathways (wrongful use, assist, undue influence) to identify financial abuse in trust, insurance, and property transactions; distinguish wrongful use from negligence
2. **Undue influence analysis (§15610.70)** — Systematically apply the four-factor test: vulnerability, apparent authority, actions/tactics, equity of result; identify which factors are present and which evidence establishes each
3. **Representative liability mapping** — Identify conservators, trustees, and attorneys-in-fact as §15610.30(d) representatives; establish their acts as direct financial abuse, not merely agent misconduct
4. **§15657.5 enhanced remedy calculation** — Determine which evidentiary standard applies (preponderance for fees; clear and convincing for no §377.34 cap); calculate fee entitlement and damage ceiling
5. **Prob. Code §859 double damages analysis** — Identify bad faith conduct in property taking; calculate twice-value recovery; coordinate with §15657.5 mandatory fees
6. **Mandated reporter audit (§15630)** — Identify which professionals had mandated reporter duties; determine whether report was made; calculate failure-to-report liability (misdemeanor + civil exposure)
7. **Elder/dependent adult status determination** — Apply WIC §15610.27 (elder) and §15610.23 (dependent adult) definitions to determine who the statute protects; identify status for each victim
8. **Compound identity financial abuse theory** — Apply §15610.30 to identity merger schemes; trace all financial instruments opened/modified in compound identity; calculate wrongful use damages
9. **APS and law enforcement referral strategy** — Determine which agency (APS, law enforcement, DA, ombudsman) receives which category of report; use §15630 reporting failures to establish notice and pattern
10. **Cross-system coordination** — Connect elder financial abuse to conservatorship (CA_Probate_Conservatorship_Litigator), insurance fraud (CA_Insurance_Compliance_Litigator), and federal claims (RICO, §1983) for full relief package
