# Odorizzi v. Bloomfield School District — Holding

**Citation:** 54 Cal.Rptr. 533 (Cal. App. 1966) — verify at Westlaw
**Standard:** Elements of undue influence

## The Rule

Undue influence requires: (1) vulnerability, (2) excessive pressure, (3) causation
(pressure overcame free will), (4) inequitable result.

It does not require a pre-existing fiduciary relationship. Power imbalance + excessive
use of that power + resulting inequity = undue influence.

## Application via §15610.70

The §15610.70 four-factor test maps directly to Odorizzi:
- Vulnerability → §15610.70(a)(1)
- Excessive pressure → §15610.70(a)(3) (actions/tactics)
- Apparent authority creating the power imbalance → §15610.70(a)(2)
- Inequitable result → §15610.70(a)(4)

Odorizzi is the foundational precedent. §15610.70 codified and extended it.
For the Hillberg case: the HILLBERGMANN compound identity + asset exhaustion is the
inequitable result. The isolation, control of information, and financial expertise claims
are the actions/tactics. Ann's age, illness, and financial dependency are the vulnerability.
The trustee and agent are the apparent authorities. All four factors present.
