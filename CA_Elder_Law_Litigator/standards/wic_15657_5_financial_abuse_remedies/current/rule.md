## Governing Provision

Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 15657.5 (Stats. 1991, Ch. 774; last amended Stats. 2008, Ch. 475)

## The Rule — Two-Tier Remedy Structure

§15657.5 creates two tiers of enhanced remedies for financial elder abuse:

### Tier 1: Mandatory Attorney Fees at Preponderance (§15657.5(a))

**Trigger:** Preponderance of the evidence that defendant is liable for financial abuse (§15610.30)
**Remedy:** Court **SHALL** award reasonable attorney's fees and costs
- Mandatory — not discretionary
- "Costs" includes conservator fees devoted to litigation
- NO RECKLESSNESS REQUIRED — preponderance alone is sufficient for fee shift
- This is the critical distinction from §15657 (physical abuse): financial abuse victims get attorney fees at a LOWER evidentiary bar

### Tier 2: No §377.34 Cap at C&C + Recklessness (§15657.5(b))

**Trigger 1:** Preponderance of evidence of financial abuse (§15610.30) [same as Tier 1]
**Trigger 2:** Clear and convincing evidence that defendant was guilty of recklessness, oppression, fraud, or malice
**Remedy:** §377.34 CCP survival cap does NOT apply — full survival damages available
- Note: Unlike §15657 (physical abuse), there is NO §3333.2 MICRA cap preservation in §15657.5 — the statute is silent on MICRA because financial abuse claims are not medical malpractice claims

### Employer Liability (§15657.5(c))

Before punitive damages against an employer, must satisfy §3294(b) standard:
- Advance knowledge + conscious disregard, OR
- Managing agent, OR
- Authorized or ratified
- **BUT:** This employer limitation does NOT apply to compensatory damages or attorney fees — employer can be liable for those at Tier 1 without satisfying §3294(b)

### Judgment Identification Requirement (§15657.5(e))

Any money judgment under §15657.5 MUST state it is based on financial elder abuse claim.
- If only partial, must specify the amount
- This creates a permanent record — the judgment cannot be ambiguous about its elder abuse basis

## The Proof Matrix

| Proof Level | What's Proven | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Preponderance | Financial abuse (§15610.30) | Mandatory attorney fees + costs |
| Preponderance + C&C recklessness/malice | Financial abuse + aggravated | Attorney fees + no §377.34 cap + compensatory damages uncapped |
| + §3294(b) employer standard | Same + managing agent/ratification | Punitive damages against employer |

## Key Comparison: §15657 vs. §15657.5

| Element | §15657 (Physical Abuse) | §15657.5 (Financial Abuse) |
|---|---|---|
| Attorney fees threshold | C&C + recklessness | **Preponderance alone** |
| §377.34 cap removal | C&C + recklessness (same) | C&C + recklessness (same) |
| MICRA cap | Preserved (§3333.2) | Not applicable |
| Judgment labeling | Not required | **Required** |

Financial abuse victims have a LOWER bar for attorney fees than physical abuse victims. This reflects the Legislature's recognition that financial exploitation is often more sophisticated and harder to prove to C&C standard.
