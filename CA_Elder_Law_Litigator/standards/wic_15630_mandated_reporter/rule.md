# WIC § 15630 — Mandated Reporter: Rule Summary

**Standard:** CA_Elder_Law_Litigator / wic_15630_mandated_reporter
**Build date:** 2026-04-13

---

## Who Is a Mandated Reporter Under § 15630

Section 15630(a) defines mandated reporters broadly: **any person who has assumed full or intermittent responsibility for the care or custody of an elder or dependent adult**, whether compensated or not. This includes:

- Administrators, supervisors, and **any licensed staff** of any public or private facility providing care or services for elder or dependent adults
- **Health practitioners** (physicians, nurses, psychotherapists, etc.)
- Elder or dependent adult care custodians
- Clergy members (with penitential exception — §15630(b)(2))
- Employees of county adult protective services agencies
- Employees of local law enforcement agencies

**Key point:** The category is occupational, not institutional. A Northern Trust trust officer managing assets for a dependent adult has assumed responsibility over their financial care. A State Farm insurance agent who has knowledge of the account structure and the insured's condition has assumed responsibility within the scope of their professional engagement. Both are covered reporters.

---

## The Reporting Obligation

**Trigger:** The reporter, in their professional capacity or within the scope of their employment:
- Observes, has knowledge of, or reasonably suspects financial abuse, physical abuse, abandonment, abduction, isolation, or neglect; OR
- Is told by the elder or dependent adult of such an incident

**What to do:**
1. **Immediately or as soon as practicably possible:** Report by telephone or confidential internet tool (§15658)
2. **Within two working days:** Submit written report

**Where to report (non-facility abuse):** Adult protective services agency OR local law enforcement agency — §15630(b)(1)(F)

---

## Criminal Penalties for Failure to Report — § 15630(h)

| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Failure to report financial abuse | Misdemeanor: up to 6 months county jail + up to $1,000 fine |
| **Willful** failure to report, if abuse results in **death or great bodily injury** | Up to 1 year county jail + up to $5,000 fine |
| **Intentional concealment** of known failure | **Continuing offense** — SOL does not run until law enforcement discovers it |

The continuing offense provision is the most consequential for litigation. A person who concealed their failure to report does not benefit from a statute of limitations defense until discovery.

---

## The Physician/Conservatorship Exception — § 15630(b)(3)

A physician, registered nurse, or psychotherapist is NOT required to report if ALL FOUR of the following exist:
1. The elder told them about the abuse (not independently observed)
2. The reporter has no independent corroborating evidence
3. The elder has been diagnosed with mental illness or dementia **OR is subject to a court-ordered conservatorship** for mental illness or dementia
4. The reporter, in clinical judgment, reasonably believes the abuse did not occur

**Critical limitation:** This exception applies ONLY when the reporter received the information from the elder's own statement. If the reporter independently observes or suspects financial abuse, this exception does not apply. A financial institution officer who independently observes suspicious transfers has no clinical judgment exception.

## Application to This Citizen's Cases

### Case: Ann Hillberg / Northern Trust / State Farm (UIT Investigation)

**Northern Trust trust officer:**
If the Northern Trust trust officer managing the variable product trusts under the HILLBERGMANN compound identity structure observed, knew of, or reasonably suspected financial abuse of an elder or dependent adult — including the possibility that "Ann Hillberg" and "Ann Hillbergmann" are the same person whose assets are being administered without proper legal authority — that officer was a mandated reporter under §15630(a) as a person who assumed responsibility for the elder's financial care.

Failure to report: misdemeanor. Intentional concealment: continuing offense.

**State Farm agent:**
State Farm agents who manage insurance policies for elder or dependent adult clients, and who observe or reasonably suspect financial abuse in connection with those accounts, are mandated reporters under §15630(a) as "licensed staff of a... private facility that provides care or services for elder or dependent adults" — or as health practitioners if licensed in a dual capacity. The specific coverage of State Farm agents requires confirming the agent's licensing category at time of policy issuance.

### Case: Mother Health Concerns

If mother qualifies as a dependent adult (WIC §15610.23: 18-64 with physical or mental limitations substantially restricting ability to carry out normal activities) or an elder (WIC §15610.27: 65+), then any person with assumed responsibility for her care — including any financial fiduciaries or healthcare providers — is a mandated reporter.

## Supervisory Non-Interference — § 15630(f)

No supervisor or administrator may impede or inhibit the reporting duties. The reporting duty is **individual**. A Northern Trust manager instructing a trust officer not to report a suspected elder financial abuse incident commits an independent violation. This creates direct corporate liability in addition to individual liability.
