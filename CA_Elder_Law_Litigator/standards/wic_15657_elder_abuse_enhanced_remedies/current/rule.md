## Governing Provision

Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 15657 (operative; Stats. 1991, Ch. 774; last amended Stats. 2004, Ch. 823)

## The Rule

When a defendant is liable for **physical abuse** (§15610.63), **neglect** (§15610.57), or **abandonment** (§15610.05) of an elder or dependent adult, AND the defendant was guilty of **recklessness, oppression, fraud, or malice** in the commission of the abuse, three enhanced remedies attach — in addition to all other remedies:

### Enhanced Remedy 1: Mandatory Attorney's Fees (§15657(a))
The court **shall** award to the plaintiff reasonable attorney's fees and costs.
- Mandatory — not discretionary
- "Costs" expressly includes reasonable conservator fees devoted to litigation
- Shifts attorney fees to defendant upon proof of recklessness/malice — makes otherwise non-economic cases viable

### Enhanced Remedy 2: No §377.34 Survival Cap (§15657(b))
The §377.34 CCP limitation on survival damages does **not** apply.
- §377.34 ordinarily bars pre-death pain and suffering from survival claims
- §15657(b) removes that bar — full survival damages available
- BUT: damages capped at §3333.2 CIV MICRA limit (§250,000 non-economic) where applicable
- Cross-reference: see CA_Medical_Malpractice_Litigator → §3333.2 standard for MICRA cap analysis

### Enhanced Remedy 3: Employer Liability — §3294(b) Standard (§15657(c))
Employer cannot be liable for enhanced damages unless the §3294(b) standard is met:
- Employer **advance knowledge** of employee's unfitness + conscious disregard, OR
- Employee is **managing agent** of the employer, OR
- Employer **authorized or ratified** the act
- Same standard as punitive damages against employers generally

## Proof Requirements

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Liability for §15610.63 physical abuse, §15610.57 neglect, or §15610.05 abandonment | Clear and Convincing Evidence (or preponderance under §15657.02) |
| Recklessness, oppression, fraud, or malice | Clear and Convincing Evidence (same standard) |

## Key Distinction from § 15657.5

§15657 covers **physical abuse/neglect/abandonment**.
§15657.5 covers **financial abuse** (§15610.30) and has a LOWER bar for attorney fees (preponderance alone — no recklessness required).
The two standards are designed as a complete remedial system for elder abuse.
