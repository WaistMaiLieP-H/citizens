# § 15657.5 Origin: The 1991 Fee Mandate

§15657.5 was added by Stats. 1991 Ch. 774 as part of the Article 8.5 enhanced remedies
package. The specific financial abuse enhanced remedy (§15657.5) was separated from the
physical abuse enhanced remedy (§15657) to recognize that:

1. Financial abuse rarely involves physical harm — it was being excluded from §15657's
   physical abuse/neglect trigger
2. Financial abuse of elders through trust, insurance, and estate instruments often
   produces no visible physical injury but devastating economic loss
3. The proof problems specific to financial abuse (complex financial instruments,
   professional defendants, elderly victims who may be deceased or incompetent by
   trial) required a specific statutory remedy to make cases viable

The preponderance standard for mandatory fees (§15657.5(a)) is LOWER than §15657(a)'s
clear and convincing evidence standard for physical abuse fee awards. The Legislature
chose this asymmetry deliberately — financial abuse is harder to prove because it occurs
through instruments rather than physical acts, so the fee-triggering threshold was set lower.
