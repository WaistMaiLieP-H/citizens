# Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 15657.5 — Rule

## The Two-Track Remedy Structure

### Track 1: Preponderance → Mandatory Attorney's Fees [(a)]
**Standard:** Preponderance of evidence that defendant is liable for financial abuse (§15610.30)
**Remedy:** Court SHALL award reasonable attorney's fees and costs
**"Shall" = mandatory.** This is not the court's discretion. If financial abuse is proven by
preponderance, fees are awarded as a matter of law.

This is the legislative response to §15600(h)/(j): the only way attorneys will take
complex elder financial abuse cases is if fees are guaranteed on a win.

### Track 2: Preponderance + C&C Recklessness/Fraud/Malice → No §377.34 Cap [(b)]
**Standard:** Financial abuse by preponderance PLUS recklessness, oppression, fraud,
or malice by clear and convincing evidence
**Remedy:** In addition to Track 1 fees — the §377.34 CCP limitation on survival damages
does NOT apply.

**What §377.34 limits:** In a survival action (claim brought on behalf of a deceased
plaintiff), damages are limited to losses before death — no pain and suffering after
death, no emotional distress post-death. For an elder who is seriously ill or who has
died, §377.34 would cap recovery significantly.

§15657.5(b) lifts that cap. A financial abuse victim who died before judgment can have
their estate recover full pre-death losses without the §377.34 limitation — IF recklessness,
oppression, fraud, or malice is proven by clear and convincing evidence.

### Employer Liability Standard [(c)]
For punitive damages against an employer (not compensatory or fees):
must satisfy Civ. Code §3294(b) — the managing agent standard. The employer must have
had advance knowledge, authorized, ratified, or consciously disregarded.

### Punitive Damages Not Affected [(d)]
§15657.5 does not replace or limit §3294 punitive damages — they run in parallel.
A plaintiff can seek both enhanced EADACPA remedies AND punitive damages under §3294.

### Judgment Must Identify the Basis [(e)]
Any money judgment must state it is based on a financial abuse claim. This is a
judgment enforcement and insurance coverage protection — making clear the basis of
the award.

## Application to Hillberg/Mother Cases

**Track 1 (Mandatory Fees):** If financial abuse is proven by preponderance → fees
are mandatory. In the Hillberg case involving sophisticated financial institutions
(Northern Trust, State Farm), fees could be substantial — making the case economically
viable for counsel.

**Track 2 (No Cap on Survival Damages):** If mother is seriously ill and dies before
judgment, or if Ann's estate is the plaintiff — the §377.34 cap does not apply if
fraud or malice is proven by clear and convincing evidence. The HILLBERGMANN compound
identity scheme is inherently fraudulent — C&C evidence of fraud should be achievable.

**Combined remedy stack:**
- §15657.5(a) mandatory attorney's fees
- §15657.5(b) no §377.34 cap on survival damages
- §3294 punitive damages
- Prob. Code §859 twice the value of property taken in bad faith
All four run simultaneously — this is the full elder financial abuse remedy package.
