## Certified Legal Findings

The following findings are certified by ADAM+EVE dual witness review. They are reliable for pleading drafts subject to citation verification caveats noted.

### FINDING ELDER-001: Mandatory Attorney's Fees in Elder Financial Abuse Cases

WIC §15657.5(a) makes attorney's fees MANDATORY — not discretionary — when elder financial abuse is proven by a preponderance of the evidence. This is not the same as the discretionary fee provision in Prob. §859. In any case involving elder financial abuse, the §15657.5 mandatory fees apply and supersede the §859 discretionary provision. Both must be pleaded.

**Reliance note:** §15657.5 text fetched live 2026-04-13. Certified for pleading.

### FINDING ELDER-002: Double Damages for Bad Faith Taking — Additive Stack

Prob. §859 double damages are additive to: (a) return of property; (b) §15657.5 mandatory attorney's fees; (c) Civil Code §3294 punitive damages; (d) equitable relief. The statute expressly provides remedies "in addition to any other remedies available in law." A full elder financial abuse judgment includes all four layers simultaneously.

**Reliance note:** §859 text fetched live 2026-04-13. Certified for pleading.

### FINDING ELDER-003: Representative Liability Makes Trustee a Direct Perpetrator

WIC §15610.30(d) provides that "a representative" (which includes a trustee) who takes, secretes, appropriates, obtains, or retains property to a wrongful use or with intent to defraud commits financial abuse as a direct perpetrator. This is not vicarious liability. The trustee's own conduct — managing assets for wrongful use — IS the financial abuse. Northern Trust trustee as representative under this provision is exposed to §859 double damages, §15657.5 mandatory fees, and §3294 punitive damages as a direct defendant, not merely as an agent.

**Reliance note:** §15610.30 text fetched live (prior build). §15610.30(d) text confirmed. Certified for pleading.

### FINDING ELDER-004: Mandated Reporter Criminal Penalty — Continuing Offense

WIC §15630(h): If a mandated reporter **intentionally conceals** their failure to report an incident known by the mandated reporter to be abuse or severe neglect, the failure to report is a **continuing offense** until a law enforcement agency discovers it. There is no running SOL on the criminal charge while the concealment continues. A Northern Trust trust officer or State Farm agent who knew of financial abuse, failed to report, and then concealed the failure to report remains criminally exposed through the date of discovery.

**Reliance note:** §15630 text fetched live 2026-04-13. Certified for pleading — criminal exposure analysis only; confirm current DA enforcement priorities.

### FINDING ELDER-005: Three-Tier Remedy Stack for Elder Financial Abuse

A complete elder financial abuse claim under California law presents three simultaneous remedy tracks:

**Track 1 — Property recovery:** Return of property (Prob. §850 petition or civil complaint)
**Track 2 — Double damages:** Twice the value of property taken in bad faith (Prob. §859)
**Track 3 — Enhanced remedies:** Mandatory attorney's fees + no §377.34 cap on pre-death damages if C&C recklessness/fraud/malice (WIC §15657.5)
**Track 4 — Punitive:** §3294 punitive damages if employer ratification under §3294(b) (separate litigation track)
**Track 5 — Criminal exposure:** §15630(h) misdemeanor/felony for mandated reporter failure to report; §368 Pen. Code parallel criminal track

Certified for pleading structure in any elder financial abuse action.
