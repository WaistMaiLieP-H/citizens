# CA Medical Privacy Officer — Skills

1. **CMIA Disclosure Audit** — Trace every disclosure of medical information, identify the §56.10 exception cited, determine authorization status
2. **Provider Identity Verification** — NPI lookup for every provider named in records; flag non-existent providers
3. **Conservatorship Bridge Detection** — Identify disclosures under §56.10(c)(12) indicating conservatorship investigation activity
4. **Authorization Form Authentication** — Verify that consent/release forms are genuine, properly executed, and within scope
5. **HIPAA/CMIA Interaction Analysis** — Determine which standard governs (stricter applies) when federal and state medical privacy overlap
6. **Damages Calculation** — Calculate §56.35 statutory damages per unauthorized disclosure plus actual damages
