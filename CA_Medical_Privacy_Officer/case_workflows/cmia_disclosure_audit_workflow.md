# CMIA Disclosure Audit Workflow

## Trace Every Disclosure of Medical Information

---

### Phase 1: INVENTORY — Map All Medical Providers and Records

**For each medical event in the steward's timeline:**

| Event | Provider | Date | Records Created |
|---|---|---|---|
| COVID vaccine | Walgreens Brentwood | ~Nov 2021 | Vaccination record, pharmacy records |
| SIRVA onset | Unknown initial provider | Post-Nov 2021 | Diagnostic records, imaging |
| Spine surgery | Dr. Wiseman, Providence St. Joseph | Apr 20, 2022 | Surgical records, pre-op auth, post-op, billing |
| Blue Shield auth | Blue Shield of CA | Pre-surgery | Authorization naming "Dilworth" (non-existent NPI) |
| SSA/DDS contact | Social Security Administration | Various | Disability evaluation records (if they exist) |

**For each provider, request:**
1. Complete medical records (patient right under CMIA §56.20)
2. Disclosure log — every entity the records were disclosed to, with date and authority cited
3. Authorization forms — every consent/release signed by or attributed to the steward

---

### Phase 2: AUDIT — Check Each Disclosure Against §56.10

**For each disclosure in the log:**
1. Was it authorized by the patient (signed release)?
2. If no signed release, which §56.10 exception was invoked?
3. Was the exception properly applied?
4. Was the disclosure limited to information necessary for the stated purpose?

**Red flags:**
- Disclosure to a person or entity NOT covered by any §56.10 exception
- Disclosure citing §56.10(c)(12) (conservatorship investigation) — confirms a conservatorship investigation occurred
- Disclosure to "Dilworth" or any provider not in NPI — indicates fabricated authorization
- Disclosure to family members without patient consent — §56.10 does not generally authorize family disclosure without consent (exceptions for emergencies)
- Disclosure to an employer, insurer, or government agency without proper authorization

---

### Phase 3: MAP VIOLATIONS

**For each unauthorized disclosure:**
1. Who disclosed? (provider identity)
2. To whom? (recipient identity)
3. What information? (scope of disclosure)
4. Under what purported authority? (exception cited, if any)
5. Was the steward's consent obtained? (authorization form — does it exist? Is it authentic?)
6. Damages? (consequential harm from the disclosure)

**Cross-reference with Conservator Investigator:**
- If §56.10(c)(12) was cited → a conservatorship investigation was conducted
- Request the court investigator's report under §1826 or §1851
- The CMIA disclosure log IS evidence of conservatorship activity

---

### Phase 4: WORK PRODUCT

**If unauthorized disclosures found:**
1. CMIA complaint under §56.35 — statutory damages per violation, actual damages, attorney's fees
2. Bane Act claim if disclosure was accomplished through threats/intimidation/coercion (e.g., forged consent, impersonation to obtain records)
3. §1983 claim if state actors (public hospital, county health department) participated in unauthorized disclosure
4. Evidence preservation demand to all providers — do not destroy disclosure logs, authorization forms, or access records

**Deliverables:**
- CMIA disclosure audit report (provider-by-provider, disclosure-by-disclosure)
- Violation summary with damages calculation
- Draft complaint (CMIA + Bane Act + §1983 as applicable)
- Evidence preservation letters
