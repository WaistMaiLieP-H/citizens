CALIFORNIA CIVIL CODE
DIVISION 1. PERSONS [38 - 86]
PART 2.6. CONFIDENTIALITY OF MEDICAL INFORMATION [56 - 56.37]
CHAPTER 2. Disclosure of Medical Information by Providers [56.10 - 56.16]

Section 56.11.

Any person or entity that has received medical information pursuant to an authorization as described in Section 56.10 or pursuant to the provisions of subdivision (c) of Section 56.10 shall not further disclose that medical information except in accordance with a new authorization that meets the requirements of subdivision (a) of Section 56.11 and Section 56.21, or as specifically required or permitted by other provisions of law.

(Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 782, Sec. 2.)
