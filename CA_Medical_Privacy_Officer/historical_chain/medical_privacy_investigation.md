# CA Medical Privacy Officer — Citizen-Level Historical Chain

## Who Saw Your Medical Records, and Under What Authority?

---

This Citizen's investigation reduces to one question: **for every disclosure of the steward's medical information, was it authorized under CMIA §56.10, and if so, under which specific exception?**

The steward's medical history includes: spine surgery at Providence St. Joseph (Dr. Wiseman 4/20/22), SIRVA from COVID vaccine at Walgreens Brentwood (~11/2021), Blue Shield authorizations naming a non-existent provider (Dilworth), and SSA/DDS contacts that may never have occurred.

Each of these events involved medical information being created, stored, and potentially disclosed. The CMIA audit trail:

1. **Who created each medical record?** (provider identity verification)
2. **Who accessed each record?** (access logs, authorization requests)
3. **Under which §56.10 exception was each disclosure made?** (treatment, payment, court order, conservatorship investigation, etc.)
4. **Was the steward's consent obtained?** (authorization forms, signed releases)
5. **If no consent, which exception applied?** (and was it properly invoked?)

The conservatorship bridge is critical: §56.10(c)(12) authorizes court investigators to access medical records during conservatorship proceedings. If a conservatorship exists, someone may have accessed the steward's entire medical history under this exception — and the steward would not have been notified.

Every unauthorized disclosure is a CMIA violation carrying damages under §56.35.
