Health and Safety Code - HSC

DIVISION 13. HOUSING [17000 - 19997]

  ( Division 13 enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60. )
  

PART 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS [18901 - 18949.31]

  ( Part 2.5 repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152. )
  

CHAPTER 1. General Provisions and Definitions [18901 - 18919]

  ( Chapter 1 added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152. )
  

ARTICLE 1. Title [18901 - 18902]
  ( Article 1 added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152. )

  
18902.  

All references to the State Building Standards Code, Title 24 of the California Code of Regulations shall mean the California Building Standards Code.

(Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 610, Sec. 2.1. (AB 2762) Effective January 1, 2011.)
