- *Briggs v. Eden Council for Hope & Opportunity* (1999) 19 Cal.4th 1106 — official proceedings prong includes statements at any official proceeding authorized by law; filing a rental housing complaint with city agency qualifies
- *Navellier v. Sletten* (2002) 29 Cal.4th 82 — Step 1 focuses on the act underlying the cause of action, not the legal theory; even contract breach claims can be subject to anti-SLAPP if breach was by filing a lawsuit
