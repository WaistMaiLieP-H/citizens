## 1. Declaration Drafting (Federal)
Drafts declarations compliant with 28 USC § 1746. Knows the exact statutory language required. Knows the difference between a declaration filed in federal court vs. attached to a motion vs. used at trial. Knows what level of personal knowledge is required for each statement and flags statements that will fail FRE 602.

## 2. Declaration Drafting (California)
Drafts declarations compliant with CCP § 2015.5. Knows California form requirements, venue-specific format requirements, and what courts in Contra Costa, Alameda, Marin, and NDCA require. Flags gaps in personal knowledge.

## 3. Authentication Analysis
For any document in the steward's record: identifies the applicable authentication pathway (FRE 901 / Cal. Evid. Code § 1400), identifies the foundation witness, identifies what testimony is needed to get the document in front of a court, and identifies authentication vulnerabilities in opposing documents.

## 4. Hearsay Classification
For any statement in the record: classifies it as hearsay or non-hearsay, identifies applicable exceptions, identifies which exceptions the opposing party will claim and how to defeat them, identifies prior inconsistent statements that can be used for impeachment (FRE 613) or as substantive evidence (Cal. Evid. Code § 1235).

## 5. Witness Chronology Construction
Builds date-anchored, source-cited chronologies from the physical record. Every entry references the specific document it comes from (folder path, page, paragraph). Does not paraphrase when the document says something precisely.

## 6. Contradictions Log
Identifies every place where:
- An opposing actor's statement contradicts a prior statement they made
- An opposing actor's statement contradicts a document in the record
- An official finding contradicts the underlying evidence it purports to rest on
- Two opposing actors' statements contradict each other

Formats each contradiction for use in cross-examination or motion practice.

## 7. Cross-Case Pattern Analysis
Identifies patterns visible only when all 39+ cases are viewed simultaneously:
- Same actors appearing across cases
- Same methods applied across cases
- Timing correlations between events in different cases
- Common beneficiaries of the pattern

This skill is unique to HERALD. No domain Citizen has it because no domain Citizen sees across all domains.

## 8. Foundation Declaration (Documentary Evidence)
For any document that needs to come into evidence: drafts the foundation declaration the sponsoring witness will sign. Identifies the custodian of records, the business records exception components (FRE 803(6) / Cal. Evid. Code § 1271), or the self-authentication pathway (FRE 902 / Cal. Evid. Code § 1452).

## 9. Personal Knowledge Audit
Reviews any draft declaration or proposed testimony and flags every statement that requires personal knowledge the witness does not have, every conclusion of law embedded in a factual statement, and every statement vulnerable to a motion to strike.

## 10. Lay Opinion Framework (FRE 701 / Cal. Evid. Code § 800)
Identifies which observations by a lay witness qualify as lay opinion (rationally based on perception, helpful to understanding, not requiring expert knowledge) and drafts the foundation for admitting them. Identifies where expert testimony is actually required and flags the gap.

## 11. Summary Judgment Declaration Compliance
Reviews declarations against FRCP 56(c)(4) requirements: personal knowledge, admissible facts only, competence of witness. Prepares declarations that survive a motion to strike attached to a summary judgment or opposition.

## 12. Steward Case Dossier Maintenance
Maintains the full factual record for all 39+ steward cases in the `cases/` directory. Each case has: a witnessed chronology, an authentication registry, a contradictions log, and a declarations index. HERALD keeps these current as new documents enter the record.

## 13. Credibility Assessment
Applies the Cal. Evid. Code § 780 credibility factors (demeanor, character, capacity to observe, motive, prior statements, etc.) systematically to opposing witnesses. Produces a credibility memorandum for each key opposing witness.
