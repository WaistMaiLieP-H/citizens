### PART V — CONTRADICTIONS THE RECORD CONTAINS

HERALD has identified the following contradictions solely from documents in this file:

**Contradiction 1 — Priority Level Discrepancy:**
CAD Form 003.jpg shows the call at **PRIORITY 4.** CAD Form 005.jpg (same event, same night) shows **PRIORITY 1.** These are two CAD printouts for Event #23-033556 with different priority fields. One of them is wrong.

**Contradiction 2 — "No visible injuries" vs. photographs uploaded:**
Officer Neal wrote: *"I did not observe any visible injuries on Cole."* He also wrote: *"I later took photographs of Cole's face and uploaded them into Evidence.com."* You photograph what you can see. The photographs exist but are not in this file. What they show has not been produced.

**Contradiction 3 — Citizen's Arrest Waiver signer vs. incident witness:**
Ann signed the Citizen's Arrest Waiver. The waiver requires the signer to "promise to appear as directed to sign a complaint and testify to the arrest." Ann was not present during the physical altercation — her account is secondhand (she describes what Michael "did"). The citizen's arrest was executed by a witness to a secondhand account, not an eyewitness to the charged act.

**Contradiction 4 — Report date vs. signature date:**
The incident occurred June 16, 2023. Officer Neal and Supervisor Aiello both signed the report on **June 28, 2023** — 12 days later. Penal Code § 13730 requires DV incident reports to be filed on prescribed forms. The 12-day gap between event and signature creates a foundation question about the accuracy of any detail not recorded contemporaneously.

**Contradiction 5 — Caller identity field:**
The person who called 911 was entered as "ANN" with last name "T-MOBILE USA." A person's last name is not a telephone carrier. The actual identity of the caller in the moment of the call was not independently verified — it was attributed from the T-Mobile account associated with the number. How Ann's identity was confirmed before being entered as the reporting party is not documented.

**Contradiction 6 — Ann's relationship to the accused:**
Ann Hillberg is identified in APD's own relational database as Michael's **parent-in-law.** She is not a neutral reporting party. She is a member of the household with a defined relationship to the person she signed a citizen's arrest warrant against. The report does not document any assessment of Ann's relationship to the accused and what weight, if any, was given to that relationship in the arrest decision.
