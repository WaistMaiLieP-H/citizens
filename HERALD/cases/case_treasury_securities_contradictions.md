### PART II — THE FOUR CONTRADICTIONS

#### Contradiction 1 — BONDS EXIST / BONDS DON'T EXIST / RECORDS NOT SEARCHED

Three responses issued within an 8-day window give three mutually incompatible answers to the same question — do bonds exist bearing this name?

**Response A** (page 002, Oct 23, Customer 0006807210P):
> *"The search did identify bonds bearing the name searched, however, based upon the remainder of the information on our records, they belong to another individual bearing the same name."*

**Response B** (page 004, Oct 23, Customer 0003065619P):
> *"A search of our records with the information provided did not disclose any outstanding securities to which you are entitled."*

**Response C** (page 005, Oct 31, Customer 0003065619P):
> *"A search of our records with the information provided did not disclose any outstanding securities to which you are entitled. The search did identify bonds on which the name appears, but the information on the bonds does not match the information you provided on the form."*

**Response D** (page 008, Oct 29, Customer 0003065619P):
> *"Insufficient information was provided for us to be able to establish your entitlement to any bonds located, thus the records were not searched."*

Laid side by side:

| Response | Bonds found? | Entitled? | Reason |
|----------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| A (Oct 23, 0006807210P) | **YES** | No | Belong to another individual with same name |
| B (Oct 23, 0003065619P) | **NO** | No | Not disclosed |
| C (Oct 31, 0003065619P) | **YES** | No | Information on bonds doesn't match |
| D (Oct 29, 0003065619P) | **NOT SEARCHED** | No | Insufficient information |

These four statements cannot all be true simultaneously. Response A found bonds and attributed them to someone else. Response B found nothing at all. Response C found bonds but says the information doesn't match. Response D didn't search. A single federal agency responding to inquiries from one person produced all four answers in eight days.

Two separate customer profiles for the same person may mean Treasury searched two different identity records and produced different results from each. Or it may mean someone else's identity exists in Treasury's system under a partial version of the same name, and that person holds bonds that were purchased using Michael Hartmann's SSN or identity data.

#### Contradiction 3 — "BELONG TO ANOTHER INDIVIDUAL" CONTRADICTS EVERY OTHER RESPONSE

Response A (page 002) is the most significant. It is the only response that affirmatively states bonds were found AND provides a reason why they are not available to the claimant: they "belong to another individual bearing the same name."

This statement has no parallel in any other response. Response C says the information doesn't match — a different explanation. Response D says the records weren't searched. Response B says nothing was found.

The phrase "another individual bearing the same name" is precise. Treasury is not saying the bonds were bought by a different person. Treasury is saying there is a bond registered in the name MICHAEL HARTMANN that belongs to someone who is not the Michael Hartmann making this inquiry. 

There are three ways that can be true:
1. There is genuinely another person named Michael Hartmann who owns those bonds and there is no connection to this claimant.
2. Bonds were purchased in Michael Hartmann's name by someone else using his identity — and Treasury's system shows the registrant's identity data does not match the claimant's because the identity used was synthetic or modified.
3. Michael Hartmann owns those bonds and the system record has been altered to reflect different identifying information, causing the mismatch.

Treasury does not explain which scenario applies. The claimant has no way to know from these four letters.

#### Contradiction 4 — CHRONOLOGICAL IMPOSSIBILITY: RESPONSE PRECEDES INQUIRY

Response on page 005 is dated **October 31, 2025** and references Customer # 0003065619P.

The bar code sheet on page 006 — which is the processing sheet for a new inquiry under Customer # 0003065619P — is dated **October 29, 2025** (Request # RQ-00029658), two days before the October 31 response.

The October 31 response (page 005) does not reference any request number. The October 29 bar code sheet (page 006) was generated before the October 31 response was sent. This means either:

(a) The October 31 response (page 005) was responding to a prior inquiry (before October 29), and the October 29 bar code sheet was generated for a subsequent inquiry that produced yet another response — meaning there is a fifth response not in this file.

(b) The October 31 response was processed before it was dated, and the October 29 bar code sheet reflects an inquiry that came in after the October 31 processing was already underway.

(c) There are more inquiries and responses than the eight pages in this file reflect.

The audit analysis (page 053) identified this as Deficiency 8: "The response dated October 23, 2025 (pages 5-6) was processed before the inquiry acknowledged on October 29, 2025 (page 1)."

### PART III — WHAT THE RESPONSES DO NOT SAY

All four substantive responses lack:

- **The name of any individual** who reviewed the inquiry. All letters close: "Sincerely, Treasury Services." No person's name, title, or employee number appears anywhere.
- **The specific bond information found.** Response A says bonds were found; Response C says bonds were found. Neither identifies the bond serial numbers, denominations, issue dates, or any other detail that would allow the claimant to identify the specific bonds at issue.
- **What information "doesn't match."** Response C says the information on the bonds "does not match the information you provided on the form." What specifically doesn't match — name, SSN, address, date of birth? Not stated.
- **What information was "insufficient."** Response D says insufficient information was provided. What was missing is not identified.
- **Any appeal or administrative review rights.** None of the four responses informs the claimant of any right to challenge the determination, request a supervisor review, or seek judicial review under the Privacy Act or APA.

The audit analysis (pages 054–056) identifies this as Deficiencies 3, 5, and 7 — failure to specify deficient information (due process), no appeal rights stated, and no named signatory.
