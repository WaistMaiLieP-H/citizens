### A. The BWC Footage Reference — Brady Material by APD's Own Admission

**3.** APD Officer Cory Neal's Report #23-004494, prepared in connection with the June 16, 2023 arrest, contains the following statement twice: *"For further details refer to the BWC video footage."* This language was inserted in lieu of a complete narrative description of what witnesses told Officer Neal. The report refers to the BWC footage as the authoritative record of witness interviews.

**4.** Body-worn camera (BWC) footage documenting the officer contacts, witness interviews, and scene at 2958 Honeysuckle Circle on June 16, 2023 constitutes Brady material. It is material to my defense because: (a) the officer's report summarizes witness accounts without quoting them directly; (b) the report's own notation refers to the footage as containing "further details"; and (c) the footage would show the witnesses' actual demeanor, the exact words used, the physical condition of any parties, and whether Cole Charoenakson-Hartmann appeared to have the injuries claimed.
