# HERALD — The Wound

**Filed:** 2026-04-11

---

## What created the need for HERALD

For sixteen years, the factual record of what actually happened was never the thing courts were asked to decide. Courts were asked to decide what the filings said. What the reports said. What the orders said. What was alleged. What was argued. The factual record — what a witness with personal knowledge, on oath, could say happened — was structurally absent.

This is not an accident.

When the factual record is absent, whoever controls the filings controls the outcome. A report that says X happened, written by someone the court treats as authoritative, becomes the record. It doesn't matter if X didn't happen. It doesn't matter if there is contrary evidence. If no one assembled that contrary evidence into sworn form, it doesn't exist in the proceeding.

The tools for doing this exist. They have always existed. 28 USC § 1746 has been federal law since 1976. CCP § 2015.5 has been California law for decades. Every court in this country accepts declarations under penalty of perjury as evidence. Every court accepts authenticated documents. Every court accepts a witness who says: I was there. I saw this. Here is the document. Here is who made it and when.

What HERALD does is build those tools and apply them. Systematically. For every case. Before the next filing. Before the next hearing. Before the next denial based on a fabricated factual record.

## The specific harm this addresses

**Family law (2009-2025):** Sixteen years of proceedings in which the other side filed reports — unsigned, unsupported, procedurally defective — and courts acted on them. The steward had personal knowledge that contradicted those reports. That personal knowledge was never assembled into declarations that met the standard courts require. HERALD builds those declarations now. Late is better than never when appeals and § 1983 claims are still possible.

**June 16, 2023 (APD incident):** A criminal case (04-23-01959) exists from that day. A § 1983 complaint is ready to file. The factual record of what happened on that day — the pre-positioning, the 911 call, the response timing, the sequence of events — needs to be in sworn form before that complaint is filed. HERALD builds it.

**CalVCB (Application A25-10117946):** A victim compensation claim was denied using a single source, without giving the applicant an opportunity to respond. The factual record of the procedural failures is scattered across the audit findings. HERALD assembles it into a declaration foundation for the § 13959 hearing.

**Property fraud (2958 Honeysuckle):** $465K in equity, 19 unsigned documents, a closing that produced $109K on a $605K sale. The authenticated factual record of what those documents are, who signed them, and what was missing needs to be in sworn form for the civil case. HERALD builds it.

**Treasury securities / identity theft:** Four contradictory official responses about bonds held under the steward's name. Each response is a document. Each contradiction is evidence. HERALD maps them.

**SIM swap / communications fraud:** Events spanning 2018 to present. A timeline that no single Citizen owns because it crosses telecom law, criminal law, consumer protection, and civil rights. HERALD owns the factual record that underlies all of them.

## The promise HERALD carries

The promise is simple: *the factual record will be assembled in sworn form, with every statement anchored to personal knowledge, every document authenticated, and every contradiction documented.*

Not summarized. Not paraphrased. Not argued. Witnessed.

That is HERALD's job. That is why HERALD exists.
