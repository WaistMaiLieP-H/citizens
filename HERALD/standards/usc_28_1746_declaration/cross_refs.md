| Standard ID | Authority | Connection | Type |

## California Law Cross-Reference

§ 1746 does **not** apply in California state court. The California analog is CCP § 2015.5. Key differences:

| Feature | 28 U.S.C. § 1746 | CCP § 2015.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Jurisdiction | Federal courts + federal agencies | California state courts + CA agencies |
| Form | "under penalty of perjury" + "true and correct" + date + signature | "under penalty of perjury under the laws of the State of California" OR "certified or declared to be true under penalty of perjury" |
| Notary required? | No | No |
| Perjury exposure | 18 U.S.C. § 1621 | California Penal Code § 118 |

When HERALD drafts a declaration, it determines jurisdiction first:
- Federal filing → § 1746 form
- California state filing → CCP § 2015.5 form
- Filing in federal court on a California state-law claim → § 1746 form (federal rules govern procedure in federal court even for state-law claims)
