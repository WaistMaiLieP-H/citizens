## Direct Legal Cross-References

### Federal Rules — Core Companions

| Standard ID | Authority | Connection |
|---|---|---|

## FRE 613 vs. FRE 801(d)(1)(A) — The Definitive Chart

| Feature | FRE 613 | FRE 801(d)(1)(A) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Impeachment (credibility only) | Substantive evidence (truth of prior statement) |
| Oath required? | No | Yes — must be made under oath at trial, hearing, other proceeding, or deposition |
| Opportunity to explain required? | Yes (for extrinsic evidence) | No independent requirement beyond testimony at hearing |
| Applies to party admissions? | 613(b) carve-out for party admissions | Party admissions are FRE 801(d)(2), not 801(d)(1)(A) |
| Jury instruction | Prior statement for credibility only | Prior statement may be used for truth |

## How to Use FRE 613 and § 1235 Together

When a prior inconsistent statement arises in a case that has both federal and state components (like the steward's § 1983 federal complaint which also implicates state law claims):

1. **Identify jurisdiction of the proceeding** — federal court applies FRE 613; California state court applies § 1235
2. **If in federal court** — use FRE 613 for impeachment; separately analyze whether FRE 801(d)(1)(A) oath requirement is met for substantive use
3. **If in California court** — use § 1235 for both impeachment and substantive evidence; oath not required; § 770 foundation required
4. **For both** — lay the foundation on cross-examination BEFORE introducing extrinsic evidence; document the opportunity to explain
