## Legislative and Rulemaking History

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| 1972 Advisory Committee Proposed Rules | Eliminated Queen's Case rule (common law requirement to show written statement to witness before cross-examining on it); FRE 613(a) codified that reform |
| Pub. L. 93-595 (January 2, 1975) | Enacted the Federal Rules of Evidence; effective July 1, 1975 |
| 2011 Restyling | Stylistic changes only; no substantive amendment to Rule 613 |
| FRE 801(d)(1)(A) companion | Enacted simultaneously in 1975; establishes the oath requirement for substantive use of prior inconsistent statements |

## Key Case Law

### United States v. Dietrich, 612 F.2d 1086 (8th Cir. 1980)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | 612 F.2d 1086 |
| Court | U.S. Court of Appeals, Eighth Circuit |
| Year | 1980 |
| Holding | A witness claiming no memory of a prior statement has been given an "opportunity to explain or deny" under FRE 613(b); extrinsic evidence of the prior statement is properly admitted after the witness denies memory |
| Verification status | CITED — widely cited; text available at Westlaw/Casetext; verify before direct quotation |
| How to apply | Use for the proposition that "I don't remember" satisfies 613(b)'s opportunity requirement; extrinsic evidence proceeds |

### United States v. Russell, 712 F.2d 1256 (8th Cir. 1983)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | 712 F.2d 1256 |
| Court | U.S. Court of Appeals, Eighth Circuit |
| Year | 1983 |
| Holding | A prior statement expressing certainty is inconsistent with current testimony expressing uncertainty; the inconsistency need not be a direct contradiction |
| Verification status | CITED — verify before direct quotation |
| How to apply | Supports broad reading of "inconsistency" — certainty vs. uncertainty, specific vs. vague |

## Flags

- **Dietrich and Russell** — both widely cited for their respective propositions; PROPOSED status for both cases; verify full text before direct quotation in filed documents
- **The oath requirement** — the single most important practical distinction between FRE 613 and FRE 801(d)(1)(A); HERALD always evaluates whether the prior statement was made under oath before characterizing its admissibility as substantive vs. impeachment
- **2011 Restyling** — purely stylistic; substance of Rule 613 unchanged since 1975
