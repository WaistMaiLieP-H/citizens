## Legislative History

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Cal. Law Revision Comm'n Recs. (1964) | Commission deliberately departed from common law and federal approach; rationale: contemporaneous statements often more reliable than in-court testimony; oath not the right reliability proxy |
| Stats. 1965, Ch. 299 | Enacted the full Evidence Code; § 1235 unchanged since enactment |
| FRE contrast | Federal Rules of Evidence (1975) took the opposite approach — FRE 801(d)(1)(A) requires oath; California rejected this requirement 10 years before the FRE were enacted |
