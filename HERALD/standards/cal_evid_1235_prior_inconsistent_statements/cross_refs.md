## Direct Legal Cross-References

### Core California Companions

| Standard ID | Authority | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| *(§ 770)* | Cal. Evid. Code § 770 | Foundation requirement — witness must be given opportunity to explain or deny before extrinsic evidence is admitted under § 1235; always cite § 1235 with § 770 |
| CAL_EVID_780 | Cal. Evid. Code § 780(h) | Credibility — prior inconsistent statement is a credibility factor under § 780(h); § 1235 goes further: it makes the statement substantive evidence, not just a credibility consideration |
| *(§ 1220)* | Cal. Evid. Code § 1220 | Party admission — when the declarant is a party, § 1220 is an additional admission path; § 1220 does not require the § 770 foundation; use both theories when available |
| *(§ 1236)* | Cal. Evid. Code § 1236 | Prior consistent statement — rehabilitation; after § 1235 impeachment with prior inconsistent statement, opponent may rehabilitate with prior consistent statement |
| CAL_EVID_1200 | Cal. Evid. Code § 1200 | Hearsay rule — § 1235 is an exception to § 1200; prior inconsistent statement would otherwise be hearsay; § 1235 makes it admissible |

### Federal Parallel

| Standard ID | Authority | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| FRE_613 | FRE 613 | Federal analog for impeachment use only; compare with § 1235: in federal court, prior inconsistent statement is impeachment only (FRE 613) unless made under oath (FRE 801(d)(1)(A)) |

|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Substantive evidence | Impeachment only | Substantive evidence |

## Jurisdiction Decision Tree

When HERALD encounters a prior inconsistent statement:

```
Is the proceeding in California state court?
  YES → § 1235 applies → statement is substantive if § 770 foundation is laid
  NO (federal court) →
    Was the prior statement made under oath at trial/hearing/proceeding/deposition?
      YES → FRE 801(d)(1)(A) applies → statement is substantive
      NO → FRE 613 only → impeachment use only
```
