## California § 1235 — The Critical Distinction

Cal. Evid. Code § 1235 (prior inconsistent statements as substantive evidence) is broader than its federal counterpart FRE 801(d)(1)(A). Under California law, a prior inconsistent statement is substantive evidence even if not made under oath. This difference is material in the steward's family law and CalVCB cases (California state proceedings). CAL_EVID_1235 is in the priority queue and will be built separately.
