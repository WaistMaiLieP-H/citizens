## The § 1421 / FRE 901(b)(4) Parallel

Both § 1421 and FRE 901(b)(4) use "distinctive characteristics" taken with circumstances as an authentication method. The analysis is the same in both jurisdictions. For documents with: (1) unique identifying numbers (escrow number, account number), (2) letterhead, (3) content that only the purported source would know, (4) circumstantial connection to the claimed transaction — these characteristics combined are sufficient to authenticate under either standard.
