# Layer 3 — Historical Loss
## CAL_EVID_1400 | Authentication Failures in California Proceedings

**Standard ID:** CAL_EVID_1400
**Filed:** 2026-04-12

---

## Core Failure Mode

Same as FRE 901 — documents without authentication foundation are excluded. California family law proceedings, CalVCB appeals, and state court civil claims all require § 1400 authentication for documentary evidence. The failures mirror the federal patterns documented in FRE_901 historical_loss.md, applied to California-specific contexts.

---

## California-Specific Failure Patterns

### 1. Screenshots unauthenticated in family law proceedings

**Pattern:** Parent offers screenshots of co-parent's text messages as evidence in custody proceeding. No authentication — just the screenshots. Opposing counsel objects. Court finds screenshots could be altered or fabricated; no witness to identify the sender's number, the device, or the circumstances of transmission. Excluded.

**Why it matters for the steward's cases:** Any phone communication evidence from the SIM swap / communications fraud track requires § 1421 (distinctive characteristics — phone number, contact name, thread context) plus the steward's personal testimony about the circumstances.

---

### 2. The unsigned escrow document authentication gap — California version

Identical analysis to the federal FRE_901 historical_loss.md pattern #3. California § 1400 applies the same "sufficient to sustain a finding" standard. The unsigned documents in the Honeysuckle closing can be authenticated by the steward as received at closing — but not as to authorship or accuracy of unsigned portions.

---

### 3. CalVCB — documents offered without authentication in administrative appeal

**Pattern:** Claimant submits documents in § 13959 appeal without a declaration identifying them. CalVCB hearing officer notes no authentication. Documents given less weight.

**HERALD's protocol for CalVCB appeal:** Every document offered in the appeal is accompanied by a § 2015.5 declaration from the steward identifying the document, stating when and how it was received, and authenticating it as a true and correct copy.

---

## Authentication Map for California Proceedings

| Document | § 1400 Authentication Method | Foundation Required |
|---|---|---|
| Honeysuckle closing documents | § 1413 (witness with knowledge) + § 1421 (distinctive characteristics) | Steward's § 2015.5 declaration |
| APD Report #23-004494 | § 1414 (admission / official custody) | APD records custodian certification |
| Text message screenshots | § 1421 (phone number, thread, circumstances) | Steward's § 2015.5 declaration identifying sender/recipient/context |
| CalVCB application and letters | § 1413 (witness received them) | Steward's § 2015.5 declaration |
| AT&T/Sprint/T-Mobile records | § 1271 (business records) + custodian declaration | Carrier custodian certification |
