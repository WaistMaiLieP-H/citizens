## Advisory Committee Notes

The 2000 Amendment Committee Note explicitly addresses the expert-workaround concern:

> "The amendment... prevents a party from evading the expert witness disclosure requirements set forth in Fed. R. Civ. P. 26 and the reliability requirements set forth in Rule 702 by simply calling an expert witness in the guise of a layperson."

This confirms that FRE 701(c) is an anti-evasion provision — not a substantive restriction on legitimate lay opinion.
