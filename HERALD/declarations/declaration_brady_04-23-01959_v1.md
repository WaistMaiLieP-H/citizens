**SECTION A — UNSIGNED REPORT**

**3.** APD Case Report #23-004494 was not signed by the reporting officer on the date of the incident. Officer Cory Neal (#3427) and his supervisor both signed the report on June 28, 2023 — twelve days after the incident occurred on June 16, 2023. This fact is documented on the face of the report. An unsigned report prepared twelve days after the incident raises a foundation question about the accuracy of any detail that was not recorded contemporaneously.

**4.** The AUDIT_2023-06-16.json audit of this file identifies Rule DOC-001 as FAIL: the report signature requirement is not satisfied. This is in addition to the twelve-day gap noted above.

---

**SECTION B — BODY-WORN CAMERA FOOTAGE**

**5.** APD Case Report #23-004494 states, in Officer Neal's narrative: "For further details refer to the BWC video footage." Officer Neal referenced the body-worn camera footage as the authoritative record of witness statements at the scene. That footage has not been produced to me in this case.

**6.** I request production of all body-worn camera footage from all officers present at 2958 Honeysuckle Circle on June 16, 2023, including Officer Neal (#3427), Officer Geoffrey Morris, and Officer Kyle Hill. If that footage was deleted, I request the date of deletion, the person who authorized deletion, and the applicable retention policy under which deletion occurred. A reporting officer who references BWC footage as the record source of witness statements creates a Brady obligation to produce or account for that footage.

**SECTION C — PHOTOGRAPHS UPLOADED TO EVIDENCE.COM**

**7.** APD Case Report #23-004494 states, in Officer Neal's narrative: "I later took photographs of Cole's face and uploaded them into Evidence.com." Those photographs have not been produced to me.

**8.** In the same narrative, Officer Neal states: "I did not observe any visible injuries on Cole." Officer Neal then photographed Cole's face and uploaded those photographs to Evidence.com. I request production of all Evidence.com photographs from Event #23-033556 and Case #23-004494.
