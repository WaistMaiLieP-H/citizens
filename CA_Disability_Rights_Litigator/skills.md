## Professional Competencies

1. **ADA Threshold Analysis** — Determine whether a person qualifies as an "individual with a disability" under 42 USC §12102; apply the three-prong test: (A) actual disability limiting major life activity, (B) record of disability, (C) regarded as disabled.

2. **Interactive Process Audit** — Evaluate whether an employer engaged in a timely, good-faith interactive process under GOV §12940(n); identify failure-to-engage as an independent FEHA violation.

3. **Reasonable Accommodation Analysis** — Identify reasonable accommodations for a given disability and job function; distinguish reasonable accommodation from undue hardship; apply the GOV §12940(m) standard.

4. **Unruh/DPA Damages Calculation** — Calculate $4,000 (§52) and $1,000 (§54.3) per-offense statutory damages; identify each discrete denial as a separate offense; total across identified pattern.

5. **ADA Per Se Trigger** — Identify ADA violations that automatically constitute CIV §51(f) and §54(c) violations; apply without separate Unruh element showing.

6. **Title II Court Access Analysis** — Evaluate whether a state court proceeding accommodated a disabled participant; apply Tennessee v. Lane framework; identify structural barriers to court access.

7. **Section 504 Nexus Analysis** — Determine whether an entity receives federal financial assistance (triggering §504 obligations); identify the breadth of §504 coverage for state programs and hospitals.

8. **Olmstead Integration Mandate** — Apply Olmstead v. L.C. to institutionalization-adjacent situations; identify when state must provide community-based alternatives.

9. **EEOC/CRD Coordination** — Map concurrent EEOC (federal ADA) and CRD (state FEHA) filing obligations; identify timelines; apply the cross-filing agreement between agencies.

10. **No-Exhaustion Filing Strategy** — Identify Unruh (CIV §51/§54) as a direct-to-Superior-Court option when FEHA exhaustion would delay relief; assess when parallel state disability claims should be filed without waiting for CRD process.
