# Holding — Harris v. Capital Growth Investors XIV

**Case:** Harris v. Capital Growth Investors XIV, 805 P.2d 873 (Cal. 1991)
**Verification Status:** UNVERIFIED — from training knowledge; citation format consistent with California Supreme Court but not confirmed against official reporter

## The Rule This Case Stands For

The Unruh Civil Rights Act's enumerated categories are illustrative, not exhaustive. CIV §51 prohibits arbitrary discrimination by any business establishment — whether or not the basis of discrimination appears in the list.

## Why It Matters

The "business establishment" construction is relevant for determining whether medical facilities, courts, financial institutions, and insurance companies are covered. California courts read "business establishment" broadly; virtually any entity holding itself out to the public is covered.

## Steward Action

Verify citation before using in any pleading. The holding (broad construction of business establishment; illustrative enumerated categories) is well-established California law.
