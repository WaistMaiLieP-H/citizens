# Holding — Munson v. Del Taco, Inc.

**Case:** Munson v. Del Taco, Inc., 46 Cal.4th 661 (2009)
**Verification Status:** UNVERIFIED — from training knowledge; citation format consistent with California Supreme Court

## The Rule This Case Stands For

A disabled plaintiff seeking ADA/Unruh injunctive relief does not need to prove they attempted access and were denied — knowledge of an ADA-violating barrier that would deter a person with the plaintiff's disability is sufficient for standing.

## Why It Matters

Standing issues arise when disability-based barriers deter or prevent access rather than producing a discrete denial event. Munson protects disability rights claims where the harm is the chilling of access, not only the actual refusal.
