| Year | Document | Significance |
|---|---|---|

| 1973 | Rehabilitation Act § 504 (federal) | First federal disability civil rights law; programs receiving federal funds cannot exclude disabled persons |
| 1977 | HEW sit-in, San Francisco | 25-day occupation; first federal disability rights regulations promulgated |

| 1992 | CIV §51(f) and CIV §54(c) — ADA per se rule | ADA violation = automatic Unruh/Disabled Persons Act violation; $4,000/$1,000 minimum damages |
| Ongoing | CA courts | Broad interpretation of "business establishment"; courts, medical facilities, public entities covered |
