## Statutory Text

### Subdivision (a) — Citation
> This section shall be known, and may be cited, as the Unruh Civil Rights Act.

### Subdivision (b) — Core Right
> All persons within the jurisdiction of this state are free and equal, and no matter what their sex, race, color, religion, ancestry, national origin, **disability**, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, sexual orientation, citizenship, primary language, or immigration status are entitled to the **full and equal accommodations, advantages, facilities, privileges, or services in all business establishments of every kind whatsoever.**

### Subdivision (c) — Scope Limitation
Does not confer rights conditioned or limited by law or applicable alike to all persons.

### Subdivision (d) — No Construction Requirement
Does not require construction/alteration/modification beyond what other law requires.

### Subdivision (e) — Definitions
> (e)(1) "Disability" means any mental or physical disability as defined in Sections 12926 and 12926.1 of the Government Code.
>
> (e)(7) ["Sex, race, color...disability..."] includes any of the following:
> (A) Any combination of those characteristics.
> **(B) A perception that the person has any particular characteristic** or characteristics within the listed categories or any combination of those characteristics.
> **(C) A perception that the person is associated with a person who has**, or is perceived to have, any particular characteristic — broad coverage of association

### Subdivision (f) — ADA Per Se Rule (Critical)
> **A violation of the right of any individual under the federal Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (Public Law 101-336) shall also constitute a violation of this section.**

### Subdivision (a)
> Individuals with disabilities or medical conditions have the **same right as the general public to the full and free use of**: streets, highways, sidewalks, walkways, **public buildings**, **medical facilities, including hospitals, clinics, and physicians' offices**, public facilities, and other public places.

### Subdivision (b)(1)
> "Disability" = any mental or physical disability as defined in GOV §12926

### Subdivision (c)
> **A violation of the right of an individual under the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (Public Law 101-336) also constitutes a violation of this section.**

### Subdivision (a) — Damages
> Any person who **denies or interferes with admittance to or enjoyment of the public facilities** as specified in §§54 and 54.1 or **otherwise interferes with the rights of an individual with a disability** under §§54, 54.1 and 54.2 is liable for each offense for:
> - Actual damages PLUS
> - Up to **three times** the amount of actual damages
> - In no case less than **$1,000** per offense
> - Attorney's fees

### Subdivision (b)
> May also file verified complaint with Civil Rights Department (§12948 GOV). Remedies are **nonexclusive** — in addition to any other remedy provided by law.

### Subdivision (c)
> A person may not be held liable for damages pursuant to BOTH §54.3 AND §52 for the same act. (One recovery max between the two statutes.)
