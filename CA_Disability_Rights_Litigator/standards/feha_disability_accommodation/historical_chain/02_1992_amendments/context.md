# Historical Chain — ADA Alignment and Interactive Process Codification (1992–2001)

**Standard:** feha_disability_accommodation
**Era:** 1992–2001 — ADA alignment; interactive process codified

---

## The ADA Alignment

The Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (effective 1992) created federal minimum standards for disability employment discrimination. California FEHA was amended to align with and exceed the ADA in several key respects:

1. **Employer size:** ADA covers 15+ employees; FEHA covers 5+ — broader CA coverage
2. **Mitigating measures:** FEHA explicitly excludes mitigating measures from the disability determination — a condition controlled by medication still qualifies if it would limit major life activities without the medication
3. **"Regarded as" disability:** Both ADA and FEHA cover persons regarded as having a disability, even if they do not actually have one

## Interactive Process Explicitly Codified

The interactive process requirement was explicitly added to GOV §12940(n) as a separate statutory obligation. Prior to this, California courts had derived the interactive process requirement from the reasonable accommodation duty. Explicit codification made it an independently actionable violation — an employer who fails to engage is liable under §12940(n) regardless of whether a reasonable accommodation ultimately existed.

**Key case confirming the independent obligation:** Jensen v. Wells Fargo Bank (2000) 85 Cal.App.4th 245 — the employer's failure to engage in the interactive process is itself an actionable violation; the fact that no reasonable accommodation may have existed does not excuse the failure to engage.
