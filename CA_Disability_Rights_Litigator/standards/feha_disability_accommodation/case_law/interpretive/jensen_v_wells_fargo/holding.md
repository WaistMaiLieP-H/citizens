# Holding — Jensen v. Wells Fargo Bank

**Case:** Jensen v. Wells Fargo Bank, 85 Cal.App.4th 245 (2000)
**Verification Status:** UNVERIFIED — from training knowledge; citation format consistent with California Court of Appeal

## The Rule This Case Stands For

Failure to engage in the interactive process under GOV §12940(n) is an independent FEHA violation. The duty to engage is not excused by the employer's belief that no accommodation is possible. The process must be timely and conducted in good faith.

## Why It Matters for Steward Cases

If an employer or employer-equivalent entity failed to respond to any accommodation request, or delayed engaging, or refused to consider alternatives without discussion, each failure is an independent §12940(n) violation. The employer cannot escape liability by arguing the accommodation requested was unreasonable — that argument is available only after the interactive process was actually conducted.
