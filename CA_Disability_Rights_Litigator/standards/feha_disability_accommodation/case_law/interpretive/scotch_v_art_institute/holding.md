# Holding — Scotch v. Art Institute of California

**Case:** Scotch v. Art Institute of California, 173 Cal.App.4th 986 (2009)
**Verification Status:** UNVERIFIED — from training knowledge; citation format consistent with California Court of Appeal

## The Rule This Case Stands For

Employer delay in conducting the interactive process constitutes failure to engage. An employer cannot defeat a §12940(n) claim by claiming no accommodation was found, when the employer's delay prevented or foreclosed the accommodation process.

## Why It Matters

In steward cases involving identity replacement and UA342 pension issues, delay in responding to disability-related accommodation needs may itself be actionable. The employer cannot stall and then claim the employee abandoned the accommodation process.
