# Cross-References — 29 U.S.C. § 794 — Rehabilitation Act Section 504

| Standard | Relationship | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `ada_12131_title_ii_public_services` (CA_Disability_Rights_Litigator) | PARALLEL | §504 and Title II cover overlapping territory for state and local government. Always plead both: Title II applies to all public entities; §504 applies to all federal funding recipients. State agencies that are both public entities and federal funding recipients are covered by both. Standards are similar post-ADAAA but mechanisms differ. |

Both may apply to a deficient DDS determination. |

Plead alongside GOV §13950 et seq. and CCP §1094.5 administrative mandamus challenge. |
| `hsc_1340_knox_keene_framework` (CA_Healthcare_Fraud_Litigator) | PARALLEL | Hospitals and health plans receiving Medicare/Medicaid are §504-covered programs. | `ada_12112_title_i_employment` (CA_Disability_Rights_Litigator) | PARALLEL | For employment discrimination: §794(d) incorporates ADA Title I standards into §504. | `feha_disability_accommodation` (CA_Disability_Rights_Litigator) | PARALLEL | FEHA §12940 is the California parallel to §504 employment protection. FEHA has the broadest coverage (5-employee threshold; 3-year SOL). |
