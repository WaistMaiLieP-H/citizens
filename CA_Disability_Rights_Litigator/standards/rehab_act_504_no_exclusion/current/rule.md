## The Rule

No otherwise qualified individual with a disability may be excluded from, denied benefits of, or subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving federal financial assistance — solely by reason of disability. Section 504 covers programs receiving any federal funding, which includes virtually all state agencies, public hospitals, universities, and social services programs. §504 violation is a per se CIV §51 (Unruh) violation under §51(f) (ADA standards incorporated into §504 by §794(d)).

---

## Why Section 504 Matters Alongside ADA

### Coverage: Federal Funding Recipient Programs
Section 504 covers programs receiving **federal financial assistance** — a broader category in some respects than ADA's coverage:
- Any hospital receiving Medicare/Medicaid funding (virtually all hospitals)
- Any school or university receiving federal education funding
- Any state agency receiving federal grants (DDS, SSA-administered state programs, CalVCB receiving federal victims compensation funding)
- Any law enforcement agency receiving federal grants
- Any program receiving any federal money

**What §504 covers that Title II may not:** Some programs operated by private entities that receive federal funding are covered by §504 but not Title II (which covers only public entities). A private hospital that is not a "public entity" but receives Medicare/Medicaid is covered by §504 but not ADA Title II.

### The "Solely" Standard — Narrower Than ADA
§504's "solely by reason of disability" causation standard is technically stricter than ADA's "on the basis of disability." However:
- Courts have found that pretextual exclusions where disability is the real reason but other reasons are articulated do not escape §504
- In employment claims, §794(d) incorporates ADA Title I standards — eliminating the "solely" issue for employment discrimination
- For program access claims, the "solely" issue remains in the text but has been narrowed by courts

### Enforcement Mechanisms — Multiple Tracks
§504 has both private right of action and administrative enforcement:
1. **Administrative complaint:** File with the funding agency (HHS, DOE, DOJ, DOT, etc.) — they investigate and can withhold federal funds
2. **Private right of action:** Direct civil action in federal court without administrative exhaustion (unlike Title VII)
3. **California parallel:** §504 violation → per se Unruh violation (§51(f)) → state court civil action with $4,000 minimum per offense

## Three-Element Framework

### Element 1 — Program or Activity Receiving Federal Financial Assistance
Must identify the specific federal funding received. The funding does not need to be used for the discriminating activity — under the Civil Rights Restoration Act of 1987 (CRRA), if the entity receives federal assistance for any of its operations, all of its operations are covered by §504.

**CRRA correction:** Prior to the CRRA, courts had held that §504 only covered the specific program within an entity that received federal funds. The CRRA reversed this — if the entity receives any federal money, all of its programs are covered. Grove City College v. Bell (1984) was the case that triggered the CRRA response.

### Element 2 — Otherwise Qualified Individual with a Disability
The individual must be "otherwise qualified" — able to participate in the program with or without reasonable accommodation. Same as ADA's "qualified individual" standard.

### Element 3 — Excluded, Denied, or Discriminated Against — Solely by Reason of Disability
The disability was the reason for the exclusion, denial, or discrimination. The "solely" language is technically stricter than ADA but courts have interpreted it consistently with ADA standards in most circuits.

## Application to Active Cases

### DDS/SSA Disability Determination — Section 504
State DDS offices administer SSA disability determinations under contract with the federal government. They receive federal funding. If DDS denied Michael's disability claim based on phantom contacts and fabricated records — and if the disability was the real reason for denial while pretextual reasons were articulated — §504 is violated. This runs parallel to the SSA §405(g) judicial review track.

### Blue Shield/Medical Care — Section 504 via Medicare/Medicaid
If any insurer involved received federal Medicare/Medicaid funds, §504 applies to their denial of medically necessary care related to Michael's disability. This cross-references CA_Healthcare_Fraud_Litigator's framework with the §504 civil rights track.

### CalVCB — Section 504
CalVCB administers the federal-state Crime Victims Assistance Program — which receives federal funding (VOCA, Violence Against Women Act). CalVCB is a §504-covered program. Disability-based discrimination in the CalVCB claims process is a §504 violation on top of the GOV §13950 et seq. statutory violations.
