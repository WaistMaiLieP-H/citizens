## Statutory Text (Training Knowledge — Verify Before Filing)

### § 794(a) — The Core Prohibition
> No otherwise qualified individual with a disability in the United States, as defined in section 705(20) of this title, shall, **solely by reason of her or his disability, be excluded from the participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance** or under any program or activity conducted by any Executive agency or by the United States Postal Service.

### § 794(b) — "Program or Activity" Defined
For purposes of this section, the term "program or activity" means all of the operations of:
- A department, agency, special purpose district, or other instrumentality of a State or of a local government
- A college, university, or other postsecondary institution, or a public system of higher education
- A local educational agency, system of vocational education, or other school system
- A corporation, partnership, or other private organization **if the entity receives federal financial assistance**

### § 794(a) — "Solely by reason of disability"
The original text uses "solely" — meaning that under the unamended §504, the disability must be the SOLE reason for exclusion (not merely a contributing reason). The ADA's broader causation standard (disability need not be sole reason) applies under ADA; §504's "solely" language is more restrictive.

**NOTE:** Courts have interpreted "solely" in context — an employer/entity that articulates pretextual reasons while actually excluding based on disability does not escape §504 by the presence of pretextual reasoning. But the "solely" standard is technically stricter than the ADA's but-for standard.

### § 794(d) — ADA Standards Incorporated
> The standards used to determine whether this section has been violated in a complaint alleging employment discrimination under this section shall be the standards applied under title I of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 12111 et seq.) and the provisions of sections 501 through 504, and 510, of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (42 U.S.C. 12201 through 12204 and 12210), as such sections relate to employment.

*For non-employment claims (program access), the standards are derived from §504's history and implementing regulations, not directly from ADA Title I.*

## Key Implementing Regulations (Training Knowledge)

**45 CFR Part 84 — HHS Section 504 Regulations:**
Applies to recipients of HHS funding (hospitals, state health agencies, Medicaid/Medicare providers). Requires reasonable accommodation; prohibits exclusion from programs; effective communications requirements.

**34 CFR Part 104 — DOE Section 504 Regulations:**
Applies to recipients of DOE funding (schools, educational programs).

**28 CFR Part 42 — DOJ Section 504 Regulations:**
Applies to recipients of DOJ grants; law enforcement agencies receiving federal funding.
