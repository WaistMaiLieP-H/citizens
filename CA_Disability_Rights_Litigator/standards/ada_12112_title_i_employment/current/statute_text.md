## Statutory Text (Training Knowledge — Verify Before Filing)

### § 12112(a) — General Rule
> No covered entity shall discriminate against a qualified individual on the basis of disability in regard to job application procedures, the hiring, advancement, or discharge of employees, employee compensation, job training, and other terms, conditions, and privileges of employment.

### § 12112(b) — Definition of "Discriminate" (Key Provisions)
As used in subsection (a), discrimination includes:
- **(b)(1):** Limiting, segregating, or classifying a job applicant or employee in a way that adversely affects their opportunities or status because of disability
- **(b)(5)(A):** Not making **reasonable accommodations** to the known physical or mental limitations of an otherwise qualified individual with a disability who is an applicant or employee, unless the covered entity can demonstrate that the accommodation would impose an **undue hardship**
- **(b)(5)(B):** Denying employment opportunities to a job applicant or employee who is an otherwise qualified individual with a disability if such denial is based on the need of such covered entity to make reasonable accommodation
- **(b)(6):** Using **qualification standards, employment tests, or other selection criteria** that screen out or tend to screen out an individual with a disability, unless the standard, test, or other selection criteria is shown to be job-related for the position in question and is consistent with business necessity

### § 12111(2) — "Covered Entity"
An employer, employment agency, labor organization, or joint labor-management committee.

### § 12111(5)(A) — "Employer"
A person engaged in an industry affecting commerce who has **15 or more employees** for each working day in each of 20 or more calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year.

### § 12111(8) — "Qualified Individual"
An individual who, with or without reasonable accommodation, can perform the **essential functions** of the employment position that such individual holds or desires.

### § 12111(9) — "Reasonable Accommodation"
Includes: making existing facilities accessible; job restructuring; modified schedules; reassignment to vacant position; modification of equipment/devices; appropriate adjustment/modification of examinations, training materials, or policies; provision of qualified readers or interpreters; other similar accommodations.

### § 12111(10) — "Undue Hardship"
An action requiring significant difficulty or expense; factors include: nature and cost of accommodation; overall financial resources; type of operation; impact on operations.
