The current text reflects all amendments through 2023, including the 2023 human trafficking and military sexual assault additions (§ 13956(b)(4)-(5)) cross-referenced by § 13955(f)(3)(A). The substantive seven-element eligibility structure has been stable since the 2002 recodification with progressive expansion of the (f)(3) enumerated-crime list.

---

Stability note: The core seven-element structure (subdivisions (a) through (g)) has been in place since 2002. The amendments since 2002 have been additive (expanding who qualifies, expanding which crimes count) rather than restrictive. This means case law and board interpretations from 2003 onward remain useful for current applications.
