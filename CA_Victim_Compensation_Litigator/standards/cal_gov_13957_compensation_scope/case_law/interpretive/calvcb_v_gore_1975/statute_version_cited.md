# Statute version cited — § 13957 Compensable Loss Categories (Framework entry)

**Status:** Doctrinal placeholder — specific published citation pending primary-source verification.

**Governing statute version:** California Government Code § 13957 as currently in force (post-2002 recodification from former §§ 13959-13974; post-2008 Marsy's Law amendments). Current caps: $35,000 general; $70,000 for domestic violence victims. Eleven enumerated compensable loss categories.

**Key amendment milestones affecting this entry:**
- 1965 original enactment: narrow victim-only compensation, limited categories
- 1999 amendments: expanded to emotional-injury-only coverage
- 2002 recodification (Stats. 2002, ch. 1141): current structure and current section numbers
- 2008 Marsy's Law (Proposition 9): overlay protections on denial grounds; did not change the compensation categories themselves

**Consistency check:** Any published case interpreting pre-2002 former §§ 13959-13974 compensable loss categories applies to post-2002 § 13957 with the caveat that the 2002 recodification may have changed specific category language. Verify specific category text if citing pre-2002 cases.
