# Provision cited in People v. Pearson (2013) 56 Cal.4th 393

**California Constitution, Article I, § 28 as enacted by Proposition 9 (November 2008)**

The Marsy's Law provisions construed in Pearson are identical to the current 2026 text. The self-executing character of the core rights, the prosecutorial consultation obligation, and the victim standing doctrine established in Pearson apply without modification to current Marsy's Law enforcement.
