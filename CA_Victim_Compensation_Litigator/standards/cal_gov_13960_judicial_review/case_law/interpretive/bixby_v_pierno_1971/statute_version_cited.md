# Statute version cited in Bixby v. Pierno (1971)

*Bixby* construed CCP § 1094.5 — not Gov. Code § 13960 specifically. The *Bixby* framework applies to § 13960 writ petitions because § 13960 expressly incorporates CCP § 1094.5 as the mechanism of judicial review.

CCP § 1094.5 has been amended since 1971 in non-substantive ways; the fundamental independent judgment / substantial evidence distinction established by *Bixby* remains controlling law.

**Consistency check:** *Bixby*'s holding on the standard of review applies directly to § 13960 CalVCB writ petitions. No statutory drift concern.
