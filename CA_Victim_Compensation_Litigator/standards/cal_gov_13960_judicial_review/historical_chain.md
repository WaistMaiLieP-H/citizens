# Historical Chain — Gov. Code § 13960

## Origin

Section 13960 was enacted as part of the original California Victim Compensation program legislation in the early 1970s. When the Legislature established the State Board of Control as the administrator of victim compensation claims, it provided for judicial review via the standard administrative mandamus mechanism — CCP § 1094.5.

**1971:** The judicial review provision was part of the original victim compensation statutory framework (then codified in different sections, later reorganized into the current Gov. Code § 13950 et seq. structure).

## Key developments

**1990s:** The § 13960 text was refined to specify the exact filing deadlines for writ petitions based on the procedural history (no reconsideration, reconsideration rejected, reconsideration granted). These bright-line deadlines prevent ambiguity about when the petition must be filed.

**1998 — CalVCB replaces State Board of Control:** The Board of Control's victim compensation functions were transferred to the newly created California Victim Compensation and Government Claims Board (now California Victim Compensation Board — CalVCB). Section 13960 was amended to reflect the new entity.

**2008 — Marsy's Law (Prop. 9):** While § 13960 was not directly amended by Proposition 9, Marsy's Law elevated victim rights to constitutional status and strengthened the argument that CalVCB denials affecting those rights should receive independent judgment review under *Bixby*.

**Attorney fees:** The $1,000 cap on § 13960 attorney fees has remained unchanged, making CCP § 1021.5 (private attorney general fees) the primary fee-recovery vehicle for represented applicants pursuing CalVCB mandamus.

## Relationship to § 13959

Section 13959 governs the administrative hearing and reconsideration process. Section 13960 governs the post-administrative judicial review stage. Together they create the complete administrative-then-judicial review pathway:
1. Staff determination → § 13959 appeal hearing → § 13959 reconsideration → § 13960 writ petition → COP appeal
