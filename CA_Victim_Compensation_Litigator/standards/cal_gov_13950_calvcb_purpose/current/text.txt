California Government Code § 13950 — CalVCB legislative purpose

(a) The Legislature finds and declares that it is in the public interest to assist residents of the State of California in obtaining compensation for the pecuniary losses they suffer as a direct result of criminal acts.

(b) This chapter shall govern the procedure by which crime victims may obtain compensation from the Restitution Fund.

(c) Any reference in statute or regulations to Article 1 (commencing with Section 13959) of Chapter 5, as it read on December 31, 2002, shall be construed to refer to this chapter.

---

Source: leginfo.legislature.ca.gov via VernenLegal MCP, retrieved 2026-04-10.

Historical note: The "Chapter 5" reference in subdivision (c) refers to a 2002 recodification of the CalVCB statutes. Before 2003, the crime victim compensation program was located in Article 1 of Chapter 5 of Part 4 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code. The 2002 amendments moved the program into its current location and renumbered the sections. Subdivision (c) is a cross-reference rule preserving the validity of older citations to the program.
