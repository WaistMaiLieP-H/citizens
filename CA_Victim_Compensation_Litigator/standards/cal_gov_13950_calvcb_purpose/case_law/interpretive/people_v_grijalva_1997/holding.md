## The doctrinal proposition

### Liberal construction mandate

California's victim compensation statutes (Gov. Code §§ 13950-13963) are remedial in nature. Remedial statutes are construed liberally to effectuate their compensatory purpose. The CalVCB program was established to compensate innocent victims of crime for financial harm they could not otherwise recover. Courts and the Board itself must apply this liberal construction when:

- Determining eligibility under § 13955
- Evaluating denial grounds under § 13956
- Measuring compensable losses under § 13957
- Reviewing denial decisions at hearing and on appeal

**The liberal construction rule operates at every stage of the claims process.**

### What liberal construction requires

1. **Eligibility criteria are construed in favor of the applicant** when the statutory text is ambiguous. A close or borderline case should be resolved in favor of compensation, not denial.

2. **Denial grounds are construed narrowly.** The § 13956 grounds for denial are limitations on the general right to compensation. As exceptions to the general rule of compensation, they are construed narrowly and applied only where clearly applicable.

3. **Documentation requirements are not applied mechanically.** When an applicant cannot produce specific documentation because of circumstances beyond their control (records destroyed, records held by the defendant, agency failure to provide), the Board must consider alternative evidence and cannot deny solely on documentation grounds.

4. **Board errors in applying the statute are subject to correction.** A denial based on a misreading or overly restrictive application of the statute is an error of law, not a discretionary determination entitled to deference.

### The purpose of § 13950

Section 13950 states that CalVCB's purpose is to compensate victims of crime for direct financial losses resulting from the crime. The Board's processes and decisions must be oriented toward fulfilling this purpose. Administrative procedures and documentation requirements are means to that end — not independent grounds for denial.

## Application to A25-10117946

For application A25-10117946, the § 13950 liberal construction principle requires:

1. **The 13 FAIL findings in the audit** — each must be evaluated against the liberal construction standard. A CalVCB process failure (improper denial, inadequate notice, mechanical documentation rejection) that resulted in denial is inconsistent with § 13950.

2. **The shortened deadlines** — if CalVCB's notice of deadlines was inadequate or the deadlines were administratively shortened without proper notice, the liberal construction mandate requires that the Board not penalize applicants for failures attributable to the agency's own process defects.

3. **Single-source denial** — denying a claim based on a single point of failure (one document missing, one form error) without considering the totality of the evidence is inconsistent with a liberal construction of the statute's purpose.
