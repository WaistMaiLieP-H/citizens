# Statute Version Cited

**Standard:** California Government Code § 13956; California Constitution Article I § 28
**Subdivision focus:** § 13956(a) cooperation requirement + § 13956(a)(2) mitigating factors; § 13956(b) contributing conduct; Cal. Const. Art. I § 28(b)(1), (2), (7), (9)
**Version in effect:** § 13956 current version (post-2002 recodification + post-2008 Marsy's Law amendments). Marsy's Law (Cal. Const. Art. I § 28) enacted November 4, 2008 (Proposition 9) — in force for all crimes and proceedings after that date.
**Amendment relevance:** The 2008 Marsy's Law amendments added constitutional protections that overlay and constrain all CalVCB denial grounds, including § 13956. The § 13956 mitigating factor provisions were themselves expanded post-2008 to align with Marsy's Law's victim-protective framework.
**Governing statute for A25-10117946:** Cal. Const. Art. I § 28 applies in full — qualifying crime date 6/16/2023 is well after the November 2008 enactment.
