California Government Code § 13956 — Grounds for denial of CalVCB compensation

[Full text retrieved via VernenLegal MCP on 2026-04-10. Complete statutory text preserved in the retrieval record.]

Abbreviated structure:

(a) Involvement denial. An application MAY be denied if the Board finds the victim/derivative victim was involved in the events leading to the crime.
  (1) Involvement factors: (A) initiated, provoked, or aggravated; (B) reasonably foreseeable consequence of victim conduct; (C) victim committing chargeable felony (with specific exceptions for sexual offense victims).
  (2) Mitigating factors that may overcome involvement: (A) injuries significantly more serious than level of involvement predicted; (B) third-party interference not reasonably foreseeable; (C) Board shall consider age, physical condition, psychological state, and compelling health/safety concerns.

(b) Failure to cooperate denial. An application SHALL be denied if the victim/derivative victim failed to cooperate reasonably with law enforcement.
  (1) Reasonable cooperation factors: age, physical condition, psychological state, cultural/linguistic barriers, compelling health/safety concerns, reasonable fear of retaliation.
  (1) (continued) Special rules: DV victims not found non-cooperative based on conduct at scene; delays in reporting sexual assault/DV/trafficking don't establish non-cooperation.
  (2) Domestic violence applications cannot be denied solely because no police report was made.
  (3) Sexual assault applications cannot be denied solely because no police report was made.
  (4) Human trafficking applications cannot be denied solely because no police report was made.
  (5) Military sexual assault applications cannot be denied solely because not reported to superior officer or law enforcement.

(c) Felony status denial. A person convicted of a PC § 667.5(c) violent felony SHALL NOT be granted compensation until discharged from probation/correctional institution/parole/postrelease community supervision/mandatory supervision for that crime. Compensation shall not be granted while applicant is incarcerated or registered as a sex offender under PC § 290.

(d) Sunset provision (conditional July 2024 / January 2025 repeal if certain General Fund conditions are met; Restitution Fund backfill requirement).
