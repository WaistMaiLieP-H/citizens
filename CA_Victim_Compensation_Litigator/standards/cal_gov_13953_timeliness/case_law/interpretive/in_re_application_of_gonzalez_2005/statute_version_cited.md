# Statute version cited — § 13953 Timeliness Framework (Framework entry)

**Status:** Doctrinal placeholder — specific CalVCB administrative citation pending primary-source verification.

**Governing statute version:** California Government Code § 13953 as currently in force (post-2002 recodification; amended through 2023). Core timeliness structure: seven-year deadline from the latest of (a) the crime date, (b) the minor victim's 21st birthday plus seven years, or (c) the discovery date with ordinary diligence.

**Key structural note:** The seven-year filing window is measured from the latest trigger — not from the earliest. This is materially different from the prior shorter-deadline regime. Any pre-2002 case applying former shorter deadlines (formerly § 13952) must be assessed for drift before citation.

**Application to A25-10117946:**
- Crime date: June 16, 2023
- Seven-year deadline: June 16, 2030
- Current application of § 13953 is straightforward — well within the filing window regardless of trigger

**Consistency check:** Post-2002 § 13953 cases apply the current timeliness framework. Pre-2002 cases under former § 13952 may apply shorter deadlines and should not be cited for the seven-year rule without confirming the former section's text.
