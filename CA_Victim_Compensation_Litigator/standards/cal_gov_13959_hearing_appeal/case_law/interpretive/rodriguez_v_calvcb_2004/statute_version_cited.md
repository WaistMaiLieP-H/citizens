# Statute version cited — § 13959 Hearing and Appeal Framework (Framework entry)

**Status:** Doctrinal placeholder — specific published citation pending primary-source verification.

**Governing statute version:** California Government Code § 13959 as currently in force (post-2002 recodification; post-2008 Marsy's Law amendments). Key procedural structure: six-month application deadline (with tolling); 45-day reconsideration window (administratively stated; statutory window is 60 days from Board denial under § 13959); writ review under CCP § 1094.5 (independent judgment for fundamental vested right findings; substantial evidence for other decisions).

**Key note for A25-10117946:** The CalVCB denial letter stated a 45-day reconsideration window; the statutory window under § 13959 and § 13960 is 60 days from Board action. This discrepancy is RC-9 in the audit findings and an independent ground for equitable tolling if the 45-day deadline passed before the correct 60-day window expired.

**Consistency check:** Post-2002 § 13959 applies the current procedural framework. The writ review standard under CCP § 1094.5 (Bixby v. Pierno fundamental-right doctrine) is not part of § 13959 itself but governs the standard of review on appeal — see `cal_gov_13960_judicial_review/` for that analysis.
