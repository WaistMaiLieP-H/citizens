## I. INTRODUCTION

Applicant Michael Hartmann files this appeal pursuant to Government Code § 13959 to contest the staff determination denying his application for victim compensation, Application No. A25-10117946.

Applicant is a victim of violent crime arising from the events of June 16, 2023, in Antioch, California (Antioch Police Department incident). A pre-filing audit of the CalVCB's handling of Application A25-10117946 identified **thirteen (13) procedural and substantive failures** (FAIL findings) in the Board's determination process. These failures constitute violations of Government Code §§ 13955, 13956, 13959, the Board's own regulations, and the constitutionally guaranteed rights of crime victims under California Constitution Article I, § 28 (Marsy's Law).

Applicant requests that the Board:

1. **Reverse** the staff determination denying compensation;
2. **Find** Applicant eligible for compensation under Government Code § 13955;
3. **Award** compensation for all categories of loss set forth in Government Code § 13957 that are supported by the record; and
4. **Order** the Board to remedy the procedural violations identified in this appeal by adopting corrective practices consistent with § 13959, Marsy's Law, and the Board's regulations.
