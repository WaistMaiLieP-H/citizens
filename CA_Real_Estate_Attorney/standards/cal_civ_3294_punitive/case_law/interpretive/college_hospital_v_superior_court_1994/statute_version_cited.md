# Statute version cited in College Hospital (1994)

The *College Hospital* court applied the 1987-amended version of § 3294, which had become effective January 1, 1988. The court was interpreting the word "despicable" added to subdivisions (c)(1) and (c)(2) by Stats. 1987, ch. 1498.

**Text in force at time of decision (1994):**

Subdivisions (a) and (b) were textually identical to the current 2026 version. Subdivisions (c)(1), (c)(2), (c)(3), and (e) were identical. Subdivision (d) (homicide-based wrongful death) had been added in 1988 and was in force but was not the subject of the *College Hospital* holding.

**Subdivisions construed:**
- (c)(1) malice — specifically the despicable-conduct prong
- (c)(2) oppression — the despicable-conduct requirement

The court's holding on the meaning of "despicable" applies to both subdivisions identically and applies to current § 3294 with full force, because the relevant text has not changed since 1987.

**Consistency check:** The *College Hospital* opinion at 8 Cal.4th 725 quotes the statute verbatim. The quoted text matches the 2026 current text. No drift.
