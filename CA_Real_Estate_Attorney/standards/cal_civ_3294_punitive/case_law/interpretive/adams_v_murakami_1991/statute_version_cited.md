# Statute version cited in Adams v. Murakami (1991)

*Adams* applied the post-1987 version of § 3294 (amendments effective 1988-01-01). This version contained subdivisions (a), (b), (c)(1)-(3), and (e). Subdivision (d) (homicide-based wrongful death) had been added in 1988 but was not relevant to *Adams*.

**Significance:** *Adams* did not construe any of the substantive definitions in (c). Its holding is a procedural overlay on the entire statute: regardless of which subdivision provides the predicate wrong, the plaintiff must introduce financial-condition evidence to sustain any punitive award. This procedural rule applies identically to pre-1988 § 3294 claims, post-1988 claims, and current 2026 claims.

**Consistency check:** The opinion does not depend on statutory text — its holding is grounded in general principles of punitive-damages jurisprudence and the structural requirement that punitives bear a reasonable relationship to defendant's wealth. No statutory drift affects the rule.
