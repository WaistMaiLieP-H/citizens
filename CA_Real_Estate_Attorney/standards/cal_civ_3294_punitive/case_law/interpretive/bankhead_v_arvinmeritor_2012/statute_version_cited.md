# Statute version cited in Bankhead (2012)

*Bankhead* applied the current version of § 3294 (the 1988 version with all subdivisions in place). This is identical to the 2026 text. No amendments since 1988.

**Additional authorities applied:**
- Federal constitutional due process rules from *BMW of North America v. Gore* (1996) 517 U.S. 559
- *State Farm Mutual Auto. Ins. Co. v. Campbell* (2003) 538 U.S. 408
- *Simon v. San Paolo U.S. Holding Co.* (2005) 35 Cal.4th 1159 (California Supreme Court's articulation of how to apply the *Gore/State Farm* guideposts in California courts)

**Significance:** *Bankhead* is a reminder that § 3294 punitive damages are subject to TWO layers of review: (1) the California statutory standard (clear-and-convincing evidence of predicate wrong under (c)(1)-(3), plus financial-condition evidence under *Adams v. Murakami*); and (2) the federal due process ratio analysis from *Gore/State Farm/Simon*. A punitive award must survive both layers to be sustained on appeal.

**Consistency check:** The *Bankhead* court's analysis is fully compatible with current § 3294 text. No statutory drift issues.
