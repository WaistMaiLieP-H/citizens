# Statute version cited in Seeger v. Odell (1941)

*Seeger* applied Civ. Code §§ 1709-1710 as enacted in the 1872 Civil Code. These sections have been textually unchanged for 154 years. The § 1710(3) suppression deceit provision that *Seeger* construed reads identically in 1941 and in 2026.

**Consistency check:** *Seeger*'s holdings on suppression deceit, duty to disclose sources, materiality, and presumption of reliance apply with identical force to current cases. The statutory text has not drifted.
