# Robinson v. Grossman (1997) — Holding Summary

**Citation:** Robinson v. Grossman, 57 Cal.App.4th 634 (Cal. Ct. App. 3d Dist. 1997)
**Standard:** cal_civ_1102_disclosure (Civ. Code § 1102 — Transfer Disclosure Statement)
**Verification status:** PROPOSED — citation based on training knowledge; verify at Casetext / Google Scholar before citing in filed document

## Core holding

The Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) disclosure obligation under Civ. Code § 1102 et seq. extends to **known historical conditions**, not just current active defects:

1. **Prior defects are disclosable even if remediated.** A seller who knows that a material defect existed on the property — even if later repaired — must disclose the prior defect and the remediation. The seller's belief that the condition was "fixed" does not eliminate the disclosure duty.

2. **Materiality is the governing standard.** The test is whether the prior condition would be material to a reasonable buyer's decision. Prior flooding, water intrusion, structural issues, or pest damage are material even if repaired because they affect the buyer's risk assessment and property valuation.

3. **Non-disclosure of remediated defects is actionable.** The § 1102.4 strict-liability framework for TDS omissions applies to historical conditions. Where the omission was intentional, a concurrent § 1572 actual-fraud claim is also viable.

4. **The TDS is a floor, not a ceiling.** Section 1102 requires affirmative disclosure of known material facts. It does not limit common-law fraud and deceit claims for suppressed material facts not explicitly listed on the TDS form.

## Application to Honeysuckle (2958 Honeysuckle Circle)

- Any defects that existed on the property during Michael Hartmann's occupancy — whether repaired or not — were required to be disclosed on the TDS for the October 2023 sale.
- The community property status and disputed authority to sell are material title conditions that the seller knew about and was required to disclose under this principle.
- Potential claims: § 1102.4 strict liability for TDS omissions + § 1572 actual fraud for intentional suppression.
