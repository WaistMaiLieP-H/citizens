# Statute version cited in Calemine v. Samuelson (2009)

*Calemine* applied Civ. Code § 1102 et seq. as it existed in 2009. The core TDS framework has been stable since the 1985 original enactment, with subsequent amendments adding specific disclosure categories but not changing the fundamental affirmative disclosure rule.

**Consistency check:** *Calemine*'s holdings on affirmative disclosure, materiality standard, and rejection of caveat emptor arguments apply with identical force to current § 1102 prosecutions. The 1985 core framework is the same framework in 2026.
