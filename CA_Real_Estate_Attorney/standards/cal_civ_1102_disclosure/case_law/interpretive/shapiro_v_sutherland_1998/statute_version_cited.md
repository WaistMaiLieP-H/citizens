# Statute version cited in Shapiro v. Sutherland (1998) 64 Cal.App.4th 1534

**§§ 1102, 1102.4 as they existed in 1998**

The 1998 version of §§ 1102 and 1102.4 established the TDS framework that the Shapiro court applied. The core provisions — mandatory TDS delivery, seller's affirmative duty of disclosure, prohibition on waiver — have remained stable from 1985 through the current 2026 version. The strict liability character of § 1102.4 and the objective materiality standard established in Shapiro remain governing law.
