# Statute version cited in In re Marriage of Rossin (2009)

*Rossin* applied Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100, and 1101 as they existed in 2009. All three sections have been textually stable since their 1992-1994 recodification from the Civil Code with the fiduciary-duty language from the 1986/1991 amendments incorporated.

The statutory quotations in *Rossin* at 172 Cal.App.4th 734-735 match the 2026 current text verbatim on the operative language.

**No drift issues.**
