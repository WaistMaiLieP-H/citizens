# Statute version cited in Triple A Management Co. v. Frisone (1999)

*Triple A Management* applied Civ. Code § 1213 as it existed in 1999. Section 1213 establishes California's race-notice recording system — the core statutory text setting out the bona fide purchaser protection rule has been stable since the 1872 enactment. No material amendments changed the BFP framework between 1872 and 1999 or between 1999 and 2026.

**Relevant statutory text (1999 = 2026):**
The court applied § 1213's race-notice structure: subsequent purchaser for value who records without notice of prior unrecorded interest takes free of that interest. The elements — value, good faith (no notice), priority of recording — are textually stable across the entire history of the statute.

**Consistency check:** *Triple A Management*'s holdings on constructive notice, inquiry notice, and the three-element BFP test apply to current § 1213 without any drift concerns. The 1999 text construed by the court matches current § 1213. Any case applying California's recording acts from 1872 forward applies the same structural doctrine.
