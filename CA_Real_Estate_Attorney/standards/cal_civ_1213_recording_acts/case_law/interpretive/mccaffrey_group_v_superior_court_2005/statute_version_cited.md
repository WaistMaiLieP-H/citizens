# Statute version cited in McCaffrey Group v. Superior Court (2005) 129 Cal.App.4th 1

**Civil Code § 1213 as it existed in 2005**

Section 1213 establishes California's recording act framework: recorded instruments impart constructive notice to all subsequent claimants. The 2005 version is substantially identical to the current 2026 version. The BFP doctrine, constructive notice chain, and inquiry notice rule articulated in McCaffrey remain governing law and apply to the Honeysuckle transaction without modification.
