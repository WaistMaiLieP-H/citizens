# Statute version cited in Vai v. Bank of America (1961)

*Vai* predates the modern codifications of spousal fiduciary duty. The court applied pre-1975 common-law principles and former Civ. Code § 5125 (the husband's-management version). The doctrinal holdings, however, were grounded in common-law confidential relationship principles that transcended the specific statutory framework of the time.

**Modern application:** The 1986 and 1991 amendments to former Civ. Code § 5125 (codifying the fiduciary duty) and the 1992/1994 Family Code recodification (creating Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100) preserved and built upon *Vai*'s common-law foundation. The statutory language "general rules governing fiduciary relationships that control the actions of persons occupying confidential relations with each other" in § 721(b) is a direct codification of *Vai*'s framework.

**Consistency check:** *Vai*'s doctrinal propositions apply with full force to modern § 721 and § 1100 cases. The statutory scheme has strengthened, not weakened, the doctrinal commitments *Vai* articulated.
