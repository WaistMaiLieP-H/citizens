# Diff from Stage 01 (Pre-1975 Coverture Era) to Stage 02 (1975 Equal Management)

## What was removed
- Husband's exclusive management and control of community personal property (Civ. Code § 155 → abolished by Stats. 1975)
- "Join in executing" as the wife's sole role in real property transactions (replaced with equal participation)

## What was added
- "Highest good faith and fair dealing" duty — the specific phrase that elevates the relationship from generic confidential to fiduciary
- "Neither shall take any unfair advantage of the other" — affirmative prohibition with enforcement teeth
- Equal management rights for each spouse over community property
- A live remedy during marriage (not just at dissolution)

## What remained
- "Either spouse may enter into any transaction with the other, or with any other person, respecting property, which either might if unmarried." — This language from the original § 158 (1872) was preserved and became § 5103(a), now Fam. Code § 721(a).
- The general confidential-relationship framing carried forward.

## Legislative vehicle
Stats. 1973, ch. 987 (SB 569, Senator Beilenson); Stats. 1975, ch. 1272 (SB 364 — definitive reform). Preceded by Stats. 1969, ch. 1608 (initial Family Law Act revisions creating § 5103 as a section). REQUIRES_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFICATION for exact bill text and chapter numbers.
