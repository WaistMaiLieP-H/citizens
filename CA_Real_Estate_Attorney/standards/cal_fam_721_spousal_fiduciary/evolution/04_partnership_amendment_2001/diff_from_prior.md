# Diff from Stage 03 (1994 Family Code) to Stage 04 (Partnership Amendment c. 2001)

## Added to § 721(b)

**New sentence (after "neither shall take any unfair advantage of the other"):**
"This confidential relationship is a fiduciary relationship subject to the same rights and duties of nonmarital business partners, as provided in Sections 16403, 16404, and 16503 of the Corporations Code, including, but not limited to, the following:"

**New subdivisions (1), (2), and (3):**
- (1): Books access obligation
- (2): Information-on-request obligation
- (3): Accounting/trustee obligation

## Unchanged from Stage 03

- Subdivision (a) — general transaction freedom — preserved verbatim.
- "Duty of the highest good faith and fair dealing on each spouse" — preserved verbatim.
- "Neither shall take any unfair advantage of the other" — preserved verbatim.
- Probate Code carve-out in subdivision (b) lead clause — preserved verbatim.
