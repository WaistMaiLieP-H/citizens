# Diff from Stage 02 (1975 Civil Code § 5103) to Stage 03 (1994 Family Code § 721)

## What was added
- Lead clause in subdivision (a): "Subject to subdivision (b)" — makes explicit that the general transaction freedom is limited by the fiduciary duty in (b).
- Lead clause in subdivision (b): "Except as provided in Sections 143, 144, 146, 16040, 16047, and 21385 of the Probate Code" — carves out specific probate/trust transactions from the spousal fiduciary duty framework.
- Subdivision letter designations (a) and (b) — structural formalization.

## What was unchanged
- "Either spouse may enter into any transaction with the other, or with any other person, respecting property, which either might if unmarried." — carried forward verbatim.
- "Duty of the highest good faith and fair dealing on each spouse, and neither shall take any unfair advantage of the other." — carried forward verbatim.
- The core substantive fiduciary duty from the 1975 reform.

## What was NOT yet present (added in stage 04)
- The Corporations Code §§ 16403, 16404, 16503 cross-references
- The three specific obligations: (1) books access, (2) information on request, (3) accounting/trustee obligation
- The "fiduciary relationship subject to the same rights and duties of nonmarital business partners" formulation
