# Statute version cited in Easton v. Strassburger (1984)

*Easton* applied BPC § 10176 as it existed in 1984 and invoked common-law broker fiduciary duty principles. The § 10176 provisions on misrepresentation and fraud (subds. (a), (b), (c), (i)) were substantively similar to the 2026 version.

The case also led to the 1985 enactment of Civ. Code § 2079, which codified the seller's broker's duty of visual inspection and disclosure. The *Easton* rule remains good law through its codification in § 2079 and its continued citation for broader fiduciary duty principles.

**Consistency check:** The *Easton* court's holdings on broker duty to third parties apply with full force to current § 10176 claims and to § 2079 claims. No textual drift concerns.
