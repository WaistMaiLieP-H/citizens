# Statute version cited in Wyatt v. Union Mortgage (1979)

*Wyatt* applied BPC §§ 10176 and 10177 as they existed in 1979. The core fiduciary duty framework and the § 10176(g) secret compensation rule are substantively identical in 1979 and 2026.

**Consistency check:** *Wyatt*'s holdings on broker fiduciary duty, affirmative disclosure, and undisclosed compensation apply directly to current § 10176 claims. The statutory structure has been strengthened (more categories added) but the core provisions are unchanged.
