# Statute version cited in Carleton v. Tortosa (1993) 14 Cal.App.4th 745

**BPC § 10176 as it existed in 1993**

The 1993 version of § 10176 prohibited the same categories of broker misconduct — misrepresentation (a), deceptive inducement (b), undisclosed dual agency (d), secret profit (g), commingling (h) — that exist in the current 2026 version. The Carleton fiduciary breach and disgorgement framework applies without modification to current § 10176 prosecutions and civil actions.
