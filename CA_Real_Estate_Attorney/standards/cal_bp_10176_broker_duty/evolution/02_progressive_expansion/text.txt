CALIFORNIA BUSINESS AND PROFESSIONS CODE § 10176
Progressive Expansion of Disciplinary Grounds (1950s–2000s)

SOURCE: Multiple amendment sessions. REQUIRES_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFICATION for exact bill/chapter numbers.
Current text confirmed via VernenLegal MCP, 2026-04-10.

--- Key additions beyond original core (a)–(e) ---

(f) [Added ~1960s–1970s]: Claiming or taking a fee under an exclusive agreement that does not contain a "definite, specified date of final and complete termination."
→ WOUND ANSWERED: Open-ended exclusive listings that trapped sellers indefinitely.

(g) [Added]: The claiming or taking of any "secret or undisclosed amount of compensation, commission, or profit" or failure to reveal full compensation prior to or coincident with signing.
→ WOUND ANSWERED: Hidden kickbacks from title companies, lenders, or co-brokers — not disclosed to principals.

(h) [Added]: Option-to-purchase abuse — a broker who holds both an option to buy and a listing agreement must disclose the full amount of profit before exercising the option.
→ WOUND ANSWERED: Brokers holding options on listed properties and flipping them for undisclosed profits.

(i) [Core catchall]: "Any other conduct, whether of the same or of a different character than specified in this section, which constitutes fraud or dishonest dealing."
→ PURPOSE: Prevents technical evasion of enumerated categories.

(j) [Added]: Unauthorized solicitation of buyers without the seller's written authorization to list.
→ WOUND ANSWERED: Cold-calling buyers with properties not authorized for listing.

(k) [Added]: Intentional delay in disbursing funds under a committed mortgage loan for the purpose of increasing fees.
→ WOUND ANSWERED: Lender-brokers who held loan proceeds to earn additional interest.

(l) [Added]: Intentionally delaying closing of a mortgage loan solely to increase fees.
→ Related to (k), targeting the disbursement/closing distinction.

(m) [Added]: Violation of any licensing law provision that specifies the violation is also a violation of the licensee's licensing law.
→ Cross-licensing enforcement mechanism.
