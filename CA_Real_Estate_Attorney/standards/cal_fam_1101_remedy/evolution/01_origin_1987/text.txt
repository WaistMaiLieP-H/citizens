CALIFORNIA CIVIL CODE § 5125.1 (enacted 1987)
First Statutory Remedy for Breach of Spousal Fiduciary Duty During Marriage

SOURCE: Stats. 1987, ch. 128 (SB 1030) — enacted Civ. Code § 5125.1, effective July 1, 1987
REQUIRES_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFICATION for exact bill text and chapter number.

--- Civil Code § 5125.1 (1987 text — summary) ---
A spouse shall have a claim against the other spouse for any breach of the fiduciary duty set forth in Section 5103 [now Fam. Code § 721] that results in impairment to the claimant spouse's undivided one-half interest in the community estate.

[Includes:]
- Court authority to order accounting of community property
- Court authority to order title reformation to reflect community character
- Three-year statute of limitations from actual knowledge of the transaction
- Remedies:
  - 50% of any asset undisclosed or transferred in breach (§ 5125.1(g))
  - Value measured at highest value at: (i) date of breach, (ii) date of sale/disposition, or (iii) date of court award

NOTE: The 1987 text established the foundational remedy structure. The 100% super-remedy for §3294 (despicable) breaches — now Fam. Code § 1101(h) — was added by a subsequent amendment. See stage 03.
