# Diff from Stage 02 (1994 Recodification) to Stage 03 (Super-Remedy Amendment)

## Added
- **§ 1101(h)**: 100% remedy for § 3294-level breaches — the most significant substantive addition since the 1987 enactment.

## Unchanged
- § 1101(a)–(g): All original provisions preserved. The 50% remedy of § 1101(g) was NOT replaced — it remains the baseline remedy for ordinary breaches. § 1101(h) creates an escalated remedy for egregious breaches layered on top of § 1101(g), not in place of it.

## Practical effect of the diff
- Before § 1101(h): Maximum community property recovery = 50% of undisclosed/transferred asset.
- After § 1101(h): For § 3294-level breaches, recovery = 100% of undisclosed/transferred asset (plus attorney's fees + costs under § 1101(g), which is incorporated by reference).
