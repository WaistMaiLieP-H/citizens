--- Family Code § 1101 (1994 recodification — substantive content preserved from Civ. Code § 5125.1) ---
[Substantively identical to Civ. Code § 5125.1 (1987); reorganized into labeled subdivisions (a)–(g) of the Family Code structure. Core elements preserved:]
- (a): Claim for breach of fiduciary duty impairing claimant spouse's one-half interest
- (b): Court authority for accounting and property-rights determinations
- (c): Court authority to reform title to reflect community character (with enumerated exceptions for certain business interests)
- (d): Three-year SOL from actual knowledge; no SOL for dissolution/death-concurrent actions; laches available; July 1, 1987 retroactivity limit
- (e): Court may dispense with non-consenting spouse's consent under prescribed conditions
- (f): Action may be brought independently or with dissolution/nullity/legal separation
- (g): 50% remedy + attorney's fees + costs; highest-value measurement rule

NOTE: The 100% remedy — § 1101(h) — was NOT yet present at the 1994 recodification. It was added by a subsequent amendment. See stage 03.
