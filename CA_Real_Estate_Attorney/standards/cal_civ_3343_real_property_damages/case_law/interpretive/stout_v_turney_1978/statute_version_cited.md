# Statute version cited in Stout v. Turney (1978)

*Stout* applied Civ. Code § 3343 as it existed in 1978. The out-of-pocket rule in subd. (a) and the rejection of benefit-of-the-bargain in subd. (b)(1) are substantively identical to the current 2026 text. Some of the enumerated consequential damage categories in subd. (a)(1)-(4) were added after 1978, but the core out-of-pocket measure and the rule against benefit-of-the-bargain are unchanged.

**Consistency check:** *Stout*'s core holding — California applies out-of-pocket damages for real property fraud, and benefit-of-the-bargain damages are prohibited — applies with full force to current § 3343 claims.
