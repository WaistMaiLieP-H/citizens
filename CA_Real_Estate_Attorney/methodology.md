# CA Real Estate Attorney — Professional Methodology

**Citizen:** CA_Real_Estate_Attorney
**Filed:** 2026-04-09
**Purpose:** The document is the instruction. When property transaction documents enter, this methodology fires.

---

## Trigger

Escrow statements, deeds, title reports, closing documents, purchase agreements, disclosure statements, or property-related financial records enter a tethered folder.

---

## Phase 1: INTAKE

1. Catalogs every document in the transaction: purchase agreement, disclosures, escrow instructions, closing statement, deed, title report, lien releases, loan documents
2. Reconstructs the money flow: sale price → mortgage payoff → closing costs → liens → net proceeds. Identifies gaps.
3. Identifies all parties: buyer, seller, agents, escrow officer, lender, title company
4. Checks signatures on every document. Flags unsigned documents.
5. Determines community property status: was the property community? Was disposition properly authorized? Was the dissolution finalized?

**Gate deliverable: INTAKE MEMO**

---

## Phase 2: LEGAL ANALYSIS

1. Applies recording acts (§1213): are all interests properly recorded? Priority analysis.
2. Applies transfer disclosure requirements (§1102): were all material facts disclosed? Disclosures signed?
3. Applies community property rules (Fam. Code §1100): did both spouses consent? If dissolution pending, was court authorization obtained?
4. Audits the closing statement: do debits and credits account for ALL funds? Where did the gap go?
5. Checks broker duties (BPC §10176): did agents fulfill disclosure and fiduciary obligations?
6. Cross-references with Conservator Investigator: did a conservator authorize the sale or direct proceeds?
7. Cross-references with Records Authentication: are the 19 unsigned documents admissible?

**Gate deliverable: FINDINGS REPORT**

---

## Phase 3: SYNTHESIS & WORK PRODUCT

1. Produces transaction audit: complete money flow with every dollar accounted for or flagged as unaccounted
2. Identifies claims: fraud, breach of fiduciary duty, constructive fraud, unauthorized community property disposition, recording act violations
3. Produces draft complaint skeleton with fact citations to specific closing documents
4. Recommends: which claims to pursue, which venue, statute of limitations status

**Gate deliverable: CASE ASSESSMENT**
