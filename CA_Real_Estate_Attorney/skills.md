# CA Real Estate Attorney — Skills

Updated 2026-04-10 with deepened corpus (Civ §1572, Civ §3294, Fam §1100).

## Professional competencies

1. **Transaction Audit** — Reconstruct complete money flow from sale price through closing costs to net proceeds; identify unaccounted funds; map each line item to its governing statute and each omission to its disclosure rule.

2. **Recording Acts Analysis** — Apply Civ. §1213 race-notice priority system; determine whether interests were properly recorded; identify priority gaps.

3. **Community Property Characterization** — Apply Fam. §§760, 2581 presumptions; apply *In re Marriage of Lucas* joint-title presumption; trace separate-property contributions and apply Fam. §2640 reimbursement rule.

4. **Community Property Disposition Review** — Apply Fam. §1100 (personal property) and §1102 (real property); verify both spouses consented in writing where required; check dissolution status and continuous-duty window under §1100(e); identify §1101(g)/(h) remedies.

5. **Spousal Fiduciary Duty Breach Analysis** — Apply Fam. §§721, 1100(e) "highest character" duty; assess affirmative disclosure obligation under *Rossin*; identify fraudulent/oppressive/malicious breaches triggering §1101(h) 100%-of-value remedy under *Walker*.

6. **Transfer Disclosure Compliance** — Audit Civ. §1102 Transfer Disclosure Statements for completeness, accuracy, and proper execution.

7. **Escrow Audit** — Verify escrow instructions, disbursements, and fiduciary compliance under Financial Code §17000 et seq.

8. **Broker Duty Evaluation** — Apply BPC §10176 to assess agent disclosure obligations and fiduciary compliance.

9. **Fraud Pleading Construction (§1572)** — Draft particularity-compliant fraud complaints pleading all applicable subdivisions of Civ. §1572; apply *Committee on Children's Television* particularity rule; apply *Tenzer* circumstantial-evidence framework for promissory fraud.

10. **Suppression Fraud (§1572(3)) Duty Analysis** — Apply *Lovejoy v. AT&T* four-category duty-to-disclose framework (fiduciary, partial disclosure, active concealment, exclusive knowledge); identify which categories apply to the facts.

11. **Punitive Damages Planning (§3294)** — Choose between fraud track (c)(3), malice track (c)(1), and oppression track (c)(2); pre-trial plan for CCP §3295(c) financial-condition discovery; forensic-accountant expert scoping; managing-agent identification for corporate defendants; *Adams v. Murakami* compliance; *Bankhead/State Farm/Simon* ratio analysis.

12. **Deceit Tort Analysis (§§1709-1710)** — Plead tort deceit independent of contract; establish *Robinson Helicopter* economic-loss-rule exception for intentional fraud.

13. **Rescission Analysis** — Apply Civ. §§1567, 1688-1689 for rescission of contracts induced by fraud; coordinate with the remedy of restitution.

14. **SOL and Tolling Analysis** — Apply CCP §338(d) 3-year fraud SOL with discovery rule; apply Fam. §1100(e) continuous-duty tolling for spousal claims; identify fraudulent-concealment tolling for third-party claims.

## Core deliverables

- Intake memo (money flow reconstruction + document cataloging)
- Findings report (legal analysis against all applicable statutes)
- Triple-constraint audit (governing guidelines / standards of creation / standard of care)
- Fraud complaint draft (particularity-compliant, multi-subdivision, with punitive prayer)
- Rescission brief or motion
- CCP §3295(c) motion for financial-condition discovery
- Forensic-accountant expert retention package
