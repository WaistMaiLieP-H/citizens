## INTRODUCTION

1. This action arises from the June 2023 sale of real property at 2958 Honeysuckle [Street/Drive/Road] in [City], California (the "Property"), a community property asset owned by Plaintiff Michael Hartmann and Defendant Christina Marie Cerretani during their marriage.

2. The sale generated gross proceeds of approximately $605,000 on a property with equity of approximately $465,000. Plaintiff received approximately $109,000 of that equity — far less than his community property share — while $72,000 in cryptocurrency and $25,000 in spine surgery costs were diverted or offset against his share without disclosure or consent.

3. The sale was conducted without the lawful dual consent required by California Family Code § 1102 for the disposition of community real property. Nineteen (19) documents in the transaction file bore Plaintiff's purported signature but were not signed by Plaintiff, raising fraud and forgery concerns. Material disclosures required by Civil Code § 1102 (TDS) and § 1710 were not made or were made fraudulently.

4. The real estate broker(s) involved in the transaction owed Plaintiff fiduciary duties under Business & Professions Code § 10176, and violated those duties by failing to disclose conflicts of interest, compensation arrangements, and material facts affecting the transaction.

5. Plaintiff brings this action for: actual fraud (Civ. Code § 1572); deceit (Civ. Code §§ 1709-1710); breach of fiduciary duty (Fam. Code §§ 721, 1100(e)); violation of the community property dual-consent rule (Fam. Code § 1102); statutory remedy for breach of spousal fiduciary duty including 100% of the asset value (Fam. Code § 1101(h)); real property fraud damages (Civ. Code § 3343); broker prohibited conduct (BPC § 10176); and punitive damages (Civ. Code § 3294).

### SECOND CAUSE OF ACTION
**Deceit — Civil Code §§ 1709-1710**
*(Against All Defendants)*

34. Plaintiff incorporates by reference all foregoing paragraphs.

35. Civil Code § 1709 imposes liability for willful deceit causing damage to another. Civil Code § 1710 defines deceit to include: (1) suggesting a false fact; (2) asserting a fact without reasonable grounds; (3) suppressing a fact the defendant is bound to disclose; and (4) a promise made without intent to perform.

36. Defendants made material misrepresentations and suppressions as alleged above. The misrepresentations and suppressions were willful.

### THIRD CAUSE OF ACTION
**Violation of Family Code § 1102 — Community Real Property Dual Consent**
*(Against Defendant Cerretani and Broker Defendants)*

38. Plaintiff incorporates by reference all foregoing paragraphs.

41. A transaction in violation of § 1102 is voidable at the election of the non-consenting spouse.

### FIFTH CAUSE OF ACTION
**Statutory Remedy for Breach of Fiduciary Duty — Family Code § 1101(h)**

Family Code § 1101(h) provides that where a spouse intentionally and in bad faith breaches a fiduciary duty involving community property, or where the breach is in the nature of fraud, the aggrieved spouse is entitled to 100% of any asset subject to the breach, or 100% of the undisclosed or improperly transferred interest.

49. Defendant Cerretani's conduct as alleged above constitutes a breach of fiduciary duty involving fraud. Plaintiff is therefore entitled to 100% of the community property interest in the Property's equity that was improperly managed, concealed, or diverted.
