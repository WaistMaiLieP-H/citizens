# Statute Version Cited — FCC In re AT&T (2015)

## Evolution stage referenced

**Stage:** 02_fcc_rules_2007 (2007 CPNI authentication rules, pre-2024 SIM swap rules)

The 2015 FCC enforcement action against AT&T was decided under the 2007 CPNI rules framework — the authentication requirements codified at 47 C.F.R. Part 64, Subpart U as amended by FCC 07-22. The 2024 SIM swap rules did not yet exist.

## What version governs the steward's cases

For SIM swap events occurring between 2018 and 2024: governed by stage 02_fcc_rules_2007 (general authentication requirements + § 222 general duty) combined with the 2015 FCC AT&T enforcement action as establishing the standard of care for what adequate authentication requires.

For SIM swap events occurring after the 2024 rules' effective date: governed by stage 03_fcc_sim_swap_2024 (explicit SIM swap authentication rules).

## Why the 2015 AT&T enforcement matters

The 2015 FCC action established that AT&T's failure to properly authenticate customer identity before disclosing account information was a § 222 CPNI violation — and that the $25M consent decree AT&T paid established industry knowledge of the authentication requirement. Any subsequent AT&T SIM swap failures occurred after AT&T had explicit notice of its § 222 authentication obligations.
