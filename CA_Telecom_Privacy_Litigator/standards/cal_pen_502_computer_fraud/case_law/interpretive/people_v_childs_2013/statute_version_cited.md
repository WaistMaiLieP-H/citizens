# Statute Version Cited — People v. Childs (2013)

## Evolution stage referenced

**Stage:** 04_current (post-1988, modern § 502 framework)

*People v. Childs* (2013) was decided under the current § 502 framework established by the 1988 comprehensive rewrite. The court applied § 502(c)(7) — the pure unauthorized access provision — which was introduced in the 1988 amendment. The case interpreted the 1988 structural framework, not the narrow 1979 original.
