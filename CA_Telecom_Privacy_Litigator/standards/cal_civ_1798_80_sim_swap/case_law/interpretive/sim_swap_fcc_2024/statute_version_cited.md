# Statute Version Cited — FCC SIM Swap Enforcement Action (2024)

## Evolution stage referenced

**Stage:** 04_current (post-AB 1710, 2014 version)

The 2024 FCC enforcement actions against AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile for SIM swap and location data failures were decided under the current version of 47 U.S.C. § 222 and FCC's implementing CPNI regulations (47 C.F.R. Part 64), as strengthened by the FCC's Second Report and Order in WC Docket No. 21-441.

For the California parallel claim under § 1798.81.5, the applicable version is the post-2014 (AB 1710) version, which added the affirmative reasonable-security mandate. This is the version that requires carriers to implement authentication procedures sufficient to prevent unauthorized SIM swaps.

## Note on regulatory enforcement as case law analogue

The FCC enforcement actions are not court cases but serve as interpretive authority for what "reasonable security procedures" and "adequate authentication" require in the telecom context. They establish the industry standard against which the carriers' SIM swap authentication practices are measured.

## Applicable standards for SIM swap events
- For events 2015+: post-AB 1710 (§ 1798.81.5 reasonable security mandate)
- For breach notification after any unauthorized SIM swap: § 1798.82 (all versions from 2003 forward)
- For events after 2024 FCC rules: carriers have heightened notice of the specific authentication requirements
