# Context — PATRIOT Act Amendments (2001)

The PATRIOT Act expanded CFAA primarily for national security purposes. Its most relevant feature for private civil CFAA litigation is the loss aggregation provision — which makes it easier to meet the $5,000 civil standing threshold by aggregating losses across multiple victims or multiple incidents.

For the steward's cases: the SIM swap attacks affected multiple phone numbers, multiple carrier accounts, and multiple downstream accounts. Aggregating the remediation costs and access-enabled losses across all victims (including any entities whose communications were intercepted through the steward's compromised accounts) makes the $5,000 threshold readily achievable.

## Diff from prior
Primarily government-facing. For private civil litigation, the loss aggregation clarification is the operative change.
