# CA_Telecom_Privacy_Litigator — Skills Registry

## Core Skills

### SK-TELECOM-001: CPNI Violation Analysis
**Statute:** 47 U.S.C. § 222
**Skill:** Identify carrier violations of Customer Proprietary Network Information obligations; apply authentication standard; evaluate carrier liability for SIM swap events.
**Output:** CPNI violation analysis memo; FCC complaint draft; carrier demand letter

### SK-TELECOM-002: SIM Swap Fraud Documentation
**Statute:** Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.81.5; FCC WC Docket No. 21-341
**Skill:** Reconstruct the timeline of unauthorized SIM changes from account records; correlate with harm events; calculate statutory and actual damages.
**Output:** SIM swap timeline; damages calculation; civil complaint causes of action

### SK-TELECOM-003: CFAA Unauthorized Access Analysis
**Statute:** 18 U.S.C. § 1030
**Skill:** Identify CFAA violations in SIM swap and device control scenarios; apply Van Buren scope (outsider attacks are within § 1030 regardless of Van Buren narrowing); analyze civil cause of action requirements including $5,000 loss threshold.
**Output:** CFAA violation analysis; federal civil complaint framework

### SK-TELECOM-004: California Computer Fraud Analysis
**Statute:** Cal. Pen. Code § 502
**Skill:** Identify § 502 violations in SIM swap and device control scenarios; apply the negligence standard for civil claims; identify scope-exceeding insider access (Childs); calculate damages including verification costs.
**Output:** § 502 civil claim analysis; criminal referral memo

### SK-TELECOM-005: Carrier Account Records Investigation
**Sources:** FCC, CPUC, carrier legal departments
**Skill:** Draft account records demand letters; identify what records to request; analyze account activity logs for SIM change events; correlate with harm timeline.
**Output:** Carrier demand letter; account activity analysis

### SK-TELECOM-006: FCC Complaint Drafting
**Forum:** FCC Consumer Complaint Center; FCC Enforcement Bureau
**Skill:** Draft FCC informal and formal complaints for CPNI violations and SIM swap fraud; cite § 222, 47 C.F.R. Part 64, and the 2024 SIM swap order.
**Output:** FCC complaint (informal and formal versions)

### SK-TELECOM-007: Parallel Civil Action Framework
**Statutes:** Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.84; 18 U.S.C. § 1030(g); Cal. Pen. Code § 502(e)
**Skill:** Coordinate parallel federal and state civil claims arising from the same SIM swap event; identify forum selection; calculate cumulative damages from multiple causes of action.
**Output:** Civil complaint framework; cause of action list with damages for each
