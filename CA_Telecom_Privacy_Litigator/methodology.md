# CA_Telecom_Privacy_Litigator — Case Methodology

## Phase 1: Intake and carrier identification

**Step 1.1:** Identify all telephone numbers associated with the steward during the relevant period (2018-present).

**Step 1.2:** Identify all carriers used for each number. A number may have been ported between carriers; obtain records from all carriers that held the number.

**Step 1.3:** File account records demand with each carrier. Address to carrier's Legal Department via certified mail. Cite 47 U.S.C. § 222(c)(2) (right to access own CPNI).

**Output:** Carrier identification list; records demand letters sent.

## Phase 2: Timeline construction

**Step 2.1:** Receive account activity records from carriers.

**Step 2.2:** Extract all SIM change events, number port events, PIN changes, and security changes.

**Step 2.3:** Map each event to the date, time, requestor (if captured), and authentication method.

**Step 2.4:** Overlay the SIM change timeline against:
- Family law court dates
- Medical appointment dates
- Government agency contact dates (SSA, CalVCB, IRS)
- Criminal court dates
- Financial account access events

**Output:** SIM swap timeline; correlation analysis document.

## Phase 3: Harm quantification

**Step 3.1:** For each SIM change event, identify the harm caused during the period the SIM was unauthorized.

**Step 3.2:** Categorize harm:
- Communications intercepted → identify each blocked service
- Accounts accessed via intercepted MFA → identify each account
- Financial loss from intercepted account access
- Medical harm from missed appointments
- Government benefit denial from intercepted communications

**Step 3.3:** Calculate damages:
- Actual damages: documented financial loss
- Statutory damages: $100-$750 × events × carriers (§ 1798.84)
- CFAA: aggregate losses ≥ $5,000 threshold check
- § 502: all compensatory categories including verification costs

**Output:** Damages calculation spreadsheet.

## Phase 4: Complaint and demand preparation

**Step 4.1:** Draft FCC informal complaint for each carrier (free, online).

**Step 4.2:** Draft carrier demand letter (cease, produce records, preserve evidence).

**Step 4.3:** Draft parallel civil complaint causes of action:
- Count 1: Violation of 47 U.S.C. § 222
- Count 2: Negligence (§ 222 as standard of care; § 1798.81.5 as standard of care)
- Count 3: Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.84 (data security violation)
- Count 4: Cal. Pen. Code § 502(e) (unauthorized computer access)
- Count 5: 18 U.S.C. § 1030(g) (CFAA civil)
- Count 6: Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17200 (UCL unfair business practice)

**Step 4.4:** Draft criminal referral memo for FBI (SIM swap fraud), FTC (identity theft), and state AG.

**Output:** FCC complaint; carrier demand letter; civil complaint; criminal referral package.
