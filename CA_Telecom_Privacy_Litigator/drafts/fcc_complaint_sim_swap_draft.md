## COMPLAINT NARRATIVE

**I. Summary of Complaint**

I am filing this formal complaint against [CARRIER(S)] for violations of 47 U.S.C. § 222 (Customer Proprietary Network Information) and FCC regulations at 47 C.F.R. Part 64, related to unauthorized SIM changes and number port-outs that occurred on my account(s) beginning approximately in 2018.

The carrier(s) failed to implement adequate authentication procedures before processing SIM changes on my account, in violation of their § 222 CPNI obligations and, after the effective date, the FCC's 2024 SIM Swap Order (WC Docket No. 21-341).

**II. Factual Background**

Beginning in approximately 2018, I began experiencing unexplained disruptions to my telecommunications services, including:

- Failure to receive incoming calls that I later learned were made to my number
- Disruption of two-factor authentication services that prevented me from accessing accounts
- Interception of communications with medical providers, government agencies (SSA, CalVCB), and courts

Upon investigation, I discovered a pattern consistent with SIM swap fraud — my phone number being ported to another SIM or device without my authorization, causing all calls and texts intended for me to be routed to an unauthorized party.

**III. The Carrier's Failure**

[CARRIER] failed to verify my identity adequately before processing SIM changes on my account. Specifically:

1. The carrier did not require authentication through a secure method adequate to prevent social engineering attacks
2. The carrier did not notify me through a secondary channel before completing SIM changes
3. The carrier did not offer or enforce a SIM lock on my account

These failures violated:
- 47 U.S.C. § 222 (duty to protect CPNI)
- 47 C.F.R. Part 64 (CPNI regulations)
- After February 2024: the FCC's SIM Swap Order (WC Docket No. 21-341)

**IV. Harm**

As a direct result of the carrier's failure to prevent unauthorized SIM changes, I suffered:

1. **Loss of access to medical care:** Calls to and from my medical providers were intercepted, resulting in missed appointments and delayed care
2. **Interference with disability claims:** Calls from SSA and DDS regarding my disability application were intercepted, contributing to the denial of disability benefits
3. **Interference with CalVCB victim compensation application:** Calls and communications regarding my CalVCB application (A25-10117946) were intercepted
4. **Court interference:** Communications related to my pending court matters were intercepted
5. **Financial account access:** Two-factor authentication codes were intercepted, enabling unauthorized access to financial accounts
6. **Emotional distress:** The ongoing surveillance caused significant psychological harm

**V. Relief Requested**

1. Investigation of [CARRIER]'s SIM authentication procedures and compliance with § 222 and 47 C.F.R. Part 64
2. Access to my complete account activity records, including all SIM change events, the requestor identity for each, and the authentication method accepted
3. Enforcement action against [CARRIER] for violations of § 222 and FCC regulations
4. Requirement that [CARRIER] implement the SIM swap authentication safeguards required by WC Docket No. 21-341

**VI. Account Records Demanded**

Pursuant to 47 U.S.C. § 222(c)(2), I request immediate access to all CPNI on my account, including:
- All SIM change events: date, time, requestor, authentication method
- All number port requests
- All account access log entries (customer service contacts, online account access)
- All PIN and security question changes
