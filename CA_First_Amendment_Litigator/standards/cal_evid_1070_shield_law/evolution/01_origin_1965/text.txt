Evidence Code Section 1070 (as added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299)

[NOTE: The exact verbatim text of the 1965 original enactment has not been located in primary source.
What is known from legislative history and secondary sources:]

The 1965 enactment transferred the journalist privilege from Code of Civil Procedure §1881(6) to
Evidence Code §1070 as part of the creation of the new California Evidence Code. The original
shield law had existed since 1935 at CCP §1881(6).

The 1965 version protected:
- Publishers, editors, reporters, and other persons connected with newspapers, magazines,
  periodical publications, press associations, and wire services
- From being held in contempt for refusing to disclose the SOURCE of information procured
  for publication

The 1965 version did NOT protect:
- Radio or television reporters (added 1961 to CCP §1881, carried forward, but confirmation needed)
- "Unpublished information" as a category (that term was added in 1974)
