# Evidence Code §1070 — Origin Context (1965)

## The Pre-History: CCP §1881(6) (1935)

California's journalist privilege did not begin with Evidence Code §1070. It began in **1935** as Code of Civil Procedure §1881(6). California was one of the earliest states to enact a shield law — the privilege was recognized at a time when most states had no statutory protection for journalists at all.

The 1935 provision was narrow: it protected newspaper employees from contempt for refusing to reveal sources. It did not protect broadcast journalists (radio was widespread by 1935 but television was not yet commercially available). It did not protect "unpublished information" as a category — only the identity of sources.

## The 1961 Expansion

In 1961, the Legislature amended CCP §1881 to extend the privilege to radio and television reporters. By 1961, broadcast journalism was a dominant news medium, and the limitation to print journalists was anachronistic.

## The 1965 Transfer

In 1965, California enacted an entirely new Evidence Code (Stats. 1965, Ch. 299), replacing the scattered evidence rules in the Code of Civil Procedure and other codes with a comprehensive, systematically organized evidence code. The journalist privilege was transferred from CCP §1881(6) to **Evidence Code §1070** without substantive change — it was a recodification, not a policy shift.

The 1965 Evidence Code was based on the Uniform Rules of Evidence and the work of the California Law Revision Commission. The shield law was placed in Division 8 (Privileges), Chapter 5 (Immunity of Newsman From Citation for Contempt) — giving it its own chapter, a structural signal of the Legislature's recognition of its importance.

## What the 1965 Version Covered

- **Who:** Publishers, editors, reporters, and persons connected with periodical publications, press associations, wire services, radio stations, television stations
- **What was protected:** Refusal to disclose the SOURCE of information
- **What was NOT protected:** "Unpublished information" as a category (that expansion came in 1974)
- **Protection mechanism:** Immunity from contempt citation by any judicial, legislative, or administrative body

## The Wound Behind the Shield

Journalists were being jailed for refusing to reveal sources. The state understood that if sources could be identified through compelled testimony, sources would stop talking to journalists. If sources stop talking, the press cannot function as a check on government power. The shield exists because the government recognized that its own accountability depends on the press's ability to protect confidential sources.
