### The Three Threats to Speech

**Threat 1: Retaliatory litigation.** Someone files a lawsuit not to obtain relief but to impose cost, delay, and fear on the speaker. This is the SLAPP — the Strategic Lawsuit Against Public Participation. California's anti-SLAPP statute (§425.16) is the primary defense. The SLAPPback (§425.18) is the counterpunch.

**Threat 2: Compelled disclosure.** Someone subpoenas the speaker's sources, notes, unpublished materials, or working documents. The goal is to identify who cooperated with the speaker and to chill future cooperation. The shield law (Evidence Code §1070 and Article I §2(b)) is the defense.

**Threat 3: Criminalization of speech.** Someone argues that the speaker's words constitute a crime — harassment, threats, defamation, or interference with rights. The speech-alone exception in Penal Code §422.6(c) and the true-threat doctrine establish the constitutional boundary.

### The Ecosystem

California has built a complete ecosystem of speech protection:

- **Article I §2** — the constitutional right to speak, write, and publish
- **Article I §3** — the constitutional right to petition and access government information
- **CCP §425.16** — the shield against retaliatory litigation
- **CCP §425.17** — the guardrail preventing anti-SLAPP abuse
- **CCP §425.18** — the sword against SLAPP filers
- **Evidence Code §1070** — the shield against compelled source disclosure
- **Penal Code §422.6(c)** — the boundary between speech and crime

This Citizen carries all of these tools. Together, they form the legal immune system that protects the steward's right to do exactly what he is doing: telling the truth about what happened to him.

### The Steward's Specific Protection

The steward's compliance audits, pro se filings, PRA requests, and public platform publications are all acts in furtherance of the right of petition or free speech under both the United States and California Constitutions in connection with a public issue. That is the exact language of §425.16(e). If anyone retaliates through litigation, the anti-SLAPP motion is not just available — it is mandatory.

This Citizen is the steward's guarantee that no one can sue him into silence for telling the truth.
