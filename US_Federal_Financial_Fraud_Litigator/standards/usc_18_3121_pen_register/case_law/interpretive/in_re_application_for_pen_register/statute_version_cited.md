# Statute Version Cited — In re Application / Forrester URL Debate

**Cases decided:** 2005 (SDTX); 2008 (9th Cir.)
**Statute versions applicable:** 18 U.S.C. §§3121-3127 (post-PATRIOT Act 2001 version) compared with §§2511, 2701

## The Statutory Distinction at Issue

**Pen Register Act §3127(3):** Pen register captures "dialing, routing, addressing, or
signaling information" but "does not include the contents of any communication."

**Wiretap Act §2510(8):** "Contents" means information concerning the substance, purport,
or meaning of any wire, oral, or electronic communication.

The URL debate: is "https://www.ssa.gov/disability/claim/status" —
(A) "routing information" (URL = address, like a phone number) → Pen Register Act; or
(B) "contents" (URL reveals what the person is doing, seeking, communicating about) → Wiretap Act?

## Forrester's Holding

The 9th Circuit held IP addresses and email headers are routing information. The
court acknowledged that URLs are more content-like but declined to decide the question.

## Current State of Law (as of Aug. 2025 knowledge cutoff)

The URL/content question remains unsettled in the 9th Circuit. For the most conservative
litigation strategy: treat URL access logs as potential Wiretap Act content — if they
are held to be content, $10,000/violation floor applies. If they are held to be
routing information, they are still evidence even without §2511 civil damages for
those specific records.
