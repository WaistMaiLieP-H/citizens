# Logical Diff: Stage 01 (1977) → Stage 02 (2010 + 2021)

## What the 1977 statute left unaddressed

1. **No rulemaking authority:** FTC could enforce but not issue binding rules. Inconsistent
   interpretation across circuits left gaps.
2. **No electronic communication rules:** Email, text, social media did not exist in 1977.
   Courts split on coverage.
3. **No explicit call frequency cap:** "Causing a phone to ring repeatedly with intent to
   annoy" was in the statute but hard to enforce without a bright line.

## What Dodd-Frank (2010) changed

- Enforcement → CFPB with rulemaking authority
- Direct supervision of large collectors
- Core prohibitions: UNCHANGED

## What Regulation F (2021) changed

- Added electronic communication framework (opt-in email/text with opt-out mechanism)
- Added 7-in-7 bright-line call frequency cap
- Added electronic validation notice option
- Core prohibitions: UNCHANGED

## What has NOT changed since 1977

The six core prohibitions (§§1692c-g), the damages structure (§1692k: $1,000 statutory +
actual + mandatory fees), the one-year SOL (§1692k(d)), the "debt collector" definition
(§1692a(6)), and the consumer debt requirement (§1692a(5)).

FDCPA is structurally stable. The 2010/2021 changes are procedural and channel-specific
additions to a framework whose core has not changed in 47 years.
