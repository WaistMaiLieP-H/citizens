# Statute Version at Time of Rosenthal v. Great Western (1996)

**Primary statute:** California Civil Code §§1788–1788.33 (Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection
Practices Act) — California statute, enacted 1977 (same year as federal FDCPA), amended
multiple times including significant 1999 amendment extending to medical debt.

**Federal analog:** 15 USC §§1692–1692o (federal FDCPA) — as of 1996, pre-Dodd-Frank.
The Court analyzed both statutes to establish how they interrelate.

**Key distinction:** California Civil Code §1788.2(c) defines "debt collector" to include
"any person who, in the ordinary course of business, regularly, on behalf of himself or
herself or others, engages in debt collection." This expressly includes original creditors,
unlike the federal FDCPA's §1692a(6) which limits coverage to persons who collect debts
"owed or due another."

**Current operative law:** Rosenthal v. Great Western remains the leading case on the
Rosenthal Act's scope. The 1999 Rosenthal Act amendments extended the Act to include
medical debt collectors specifically. The compound federal (FDCPA) + state (Rosenthal)
claim structure is standard practice in California consumer debt litigation.
