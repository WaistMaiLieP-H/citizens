# Statute Version at Time of Heintz v. Jenkins (1995)

**FDCPA version:** 15 USC §1692a(6) as amended by Pub. L. 99-361 (1986), which removed the
prior exemption for attorneys who regularly collected debts through legal proceedings.

The key statutory change the Court relied on: the 1977 original FDCPA contained a clause
exempting "any attorney-at-law collecting a debt as an attorney on behalf of and in the name
of a client." The 1986 amendment deleted this exception entirely. The Court held that this
deletion made clear that attorneys are now fully covered "debt collectors" under the statute.

**Current operative law:** Heintz remains controlling. All subsequent CFPB guidance and
Regulation F confirm that attorneys engaged in consumer debt collection are debt collectors
subject to the full FDCPA framework.
