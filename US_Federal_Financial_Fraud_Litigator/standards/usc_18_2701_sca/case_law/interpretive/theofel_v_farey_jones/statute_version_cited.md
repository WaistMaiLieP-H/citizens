# Statute Version Cited — Theofel v. Farey-Jones

**Case decided:** 2004
**Statute version applicable:** 18 U.S.C. §§2701, 2707, 2510(17) (post-PATRIOT Act version; civil framework unchanged from 1986)

## §2510(17) — Electronic Storage (The Definition at Issue)

The court interpreted §2510(17), which defines "electronic storage":

> (17) "electronic storage" means —
> (A) any temporary, intermediate storage of a wire or electronic communication
> incidental to the electronic transmission thereof; and
> (B) any storage of such communication by an electronic communication service for
> purposes of backup protection of such communication;

**The court's reading of (B):** "Backup protection" is not limited to technical backup
copies. Any email remaining on the ISP's server after delivery is in "storage ... for
purposes of backup protection" because it could be accessed again by the subscriber.
This gives the SCA ongoing protection to emails that have been read but not deleted.

## Current Status

The §2510(17) definition was not changed by subsequent amendments. Theofel's
interpretation of the backup protection prong (subsection (B)) has been influential
but not universally adopted in all circuits. It is binding in the Ninth Circuit.

## Ninth Circuit Significance

As 9th Circuit precedent, Theofel directly controls SCA claims in the Northern
District of California (where SIM swap claims would be filed against AT&T and
California-based co-conspirators).
