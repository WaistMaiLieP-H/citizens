# Statute Version at Time of Van Buren v. United States (2021)

**§1030(a)(2) version:** As amended through the Identity Theft Enforcement and
Restitution Act of 2008 (Pub. L. 110-326). The operative language — "exceeds authorized
access" — was in its current form when Van Buren was decided.

**§1030(e)(6) — "exceeds authorized access" definition at time of Van Buren:**
"[T]he term 'exceeds authorized access' means to access a computer with authorization
and to use such access to obtain or alter information in the computer that the accesser
is not entitled so to obtain or alter."

**The Court's interpretation:** Barrett, J. held that "not entitled so to obtain" means
not entitled based on the scope of access granted — i.e., the files/systems the user
was permitted to access. It does not refer to the purpose for which the user was
permitted to access the system.

**Current operative text:** §1030(a)(2) and §1030(e)(6) are unchanged since Van Buren.
The gates-up/gates-down interpretation is now controlling precedent.
