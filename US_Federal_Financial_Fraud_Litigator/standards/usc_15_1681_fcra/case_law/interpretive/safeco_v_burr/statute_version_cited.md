# Statute Version at Time of Safeco v. Burr (2007)

**FCRA version:** 15 USC §1681n as amended through Pub. L. 108-159 (FACTA 2003)

The court interpreted §1681n's willfulness standard as it existed in the post-FACTA consolidated
statute. The damages provisions interpreted:

- §1681n(a)(1)(A): actual damages or statutory damages $100–$1,000 per violation (willful)
- §1681n(a)(2): punitive damages (willful)
- §1681o(a): actual damages + attorney fees (negligent)

**Key statutory text interpreted:**
§1681n(a): "Any person who willfully fails to comply with any requirement imposed under this
subchapter with respect to any consumer is liable to that consumer..."

§1681o(a): "Any person who is negligent in failing to comply with any requirement imposed under
this subchapter with respect to any consumer is liable to that consumer..."

**The statute did not define "willfully."** Safeco supplied the definition: objectively
unreasonable interpretation = reckless = willful.

This version (post-FACTA 2003) is the current operative version as of 2026. No subsequent
amendment has altered §1681n's damages structure.
