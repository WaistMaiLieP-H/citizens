# Statute Version at Time of Dubin (2023)

**§1028A version:** As enacted 2004 (Pub. L. 108-275). No amendments to text before or after Dubin.

**§1028A(a)(1) operative text:**
"Whoever, during and in relation to any felony violation enumerated in subsection (c), knowingly
transfers, possesses, or uses, without lawful authority, a means of identification of another
person shall, in addition to the punishment provided for such felony, be sentenced to a term of
imprisonment of 2 years."

**The Court's interpretive problem:** The word "uses" is unmodified. The Court resolved the
ambiguity by reference to the statute's title ("Aggravated Identity Theft"), the "in relation to"
relational requirement, and the absurd-results canon (if any use triggered §1028A, it would attach
to virtually all Medicare/Medicaid fraud — a result inconsistent with the statute's purpose).

**Current operative text:** Unchanged. Dubin is a judicial gloss on the original 2004 text.
No legislative response to Dubin as of 2026-04-11.
