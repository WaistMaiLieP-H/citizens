# Wire Fraud Historical Chain — 18 USC §1343

## The wound (pre-1952)

Mail fraud (1872) federalized fraud-by-mail. By the 1940s, radio and telephone had become the
dominant medium for long-distance fraud: boiler room operators telephoned victims across state
lines, radio advertisements promoted fraudulent investments. The mail fraud statute did not
reach these schemes because no mail was used. Federal prosecution of interstate telephone and
radio fraud required shoehorning the facts into other statutes or leaving prosecution to
underfunded state authorities.

---

## 1952: The mirror statute

Congress created §1343 by copying §1341 (mail fraud) verbatim and substituting the medium:
"wire, radio, or television communication in interstate or foreign commerce" replaced "the
mails." Every element, every defense, every penalty was identical. Courts immediately applied
mail fraud case law to wire fraud without distinction.

---

## 35 years of expansion: honest services (1952–1987)

Courts expanded "scheme to defraud" to cover honest services — the theory that depriving an
employer, government, or the public of an employee's/official's honest, loyal services was a
form of fraud. Wire fraud became a powerful anti-corruption tool. Federal prosecutors used
it to reach bribery, kickbacks, and self-dealing that caused no direct financial loss.

---

## McNally and the 1988 fix

McNally v. United States (1987) held that "scheme to defraud" protects only property, not
honest services. Thirty-five years of honest-services wire fraud prosecution evaporated.

Congress responded within a year with 18 USC §1346 (1988): "scheme to defraud" includes
"the intangible right of honest services." Honest services fraud was back — but undefined.

---

## Skilling and Kelly: the judicial narrowing era (2010, 2020)

**Skilling (2010):** §1346 is constitutional only if limited to bribery and kickback schemes.
Undisclosed self-dealing (without quid pro quo) is not covered. The Court avoided striking
down §1346 by narrowing it to avoid vagueness.

**Kelly (2020):** Clarified the property requirement in the core §1343 statute — the object
of the scheme must be money or property, not merely regulatory authority. Unanimous.

---

## Current framework (2026)

The core §1343 — scheme to defraud as to property — is stable and powerful. The honest
services branch (§1346) is limited to bribery/kickbacks. The property requirement (Kelly)
is satisfied by any financial account, instrument, or identity credential. The 3-element
structure (scheme + materiality (Neder) + wire in furtherance) has been stable since 1999.

**The arc:** 1872 mail fraud → 1952 mirror statute → 35-year expansion → McNally contraction
→ §1346 restoration → Skilling/Kelly narrowing → current stable property-fraud core.
