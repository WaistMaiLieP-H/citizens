# Wire Fraud — Evolution Stage 01: Origin 1952

**Enacted:** June 25, 1952, ch. 879, §18(a), 66 Stat. 722
**Codified:** 18 USC §1343

---

## The wound this enactment answered

Mail fraud (18 USC §1341) had existed since 1872 as Congress's tool to federalize fraud
schemes that used the postal system. By the 1940s and early 1950s, radio and (increasingly)
television had created a second mass communication channel that fraudsters were actively
exploiting — boiler room operations, fraudulent investment promotions, con artists soliciting
victims over the radio. Mail fraud did not cover these schemes because no letter was mailed.

**The jurisdictional gap:** Federal courts could reach mail fraud (interstate mail use) but
had no statute to cover the identical scheme executed over a telephone call from New York to
California, a radio advertisement broadcast across state lines, or a telegram.

**Congress's answer:** Mirror the mail fraud statute exactly, replacing "mails" with "wire,
radio, or television communication in interstate or foreign commerce." Every element, every
defense, every penalty parallels §1341. The logical structure is deliberately identical so
that the same body of mail fraud case law applies to wire fraud.

---

## The 1952 logical structure

**Text (1952):** "Whoever, having devised or intending to devise any scheme or artifice to
defraud, or for obtaining money or property by means of false or fraudulent pretenses,
representations, or promises, transmits or causes to be transmitted by means of wire, radio,
or television communication in interstate or foreign commerce, any writings, signs, signals,
pictures, or sounds for the purpose of executing such scheme or artifice..."

**Three logical moves:**
1. **Scheme to defraud** (identical to §1341 mail fraud): the fraudulent plan must pre-exist
   or be in the process of formation; the wire communication need not itself be the fraud
2. **Wire, radio, or television in interstate commerce**: the jurisdictional hook; by routing
   through interstate channels the scheme became a federal crime
3. **In furtherance**: the communication must advance the scheme; casual communications
   unrelated to the fraud are not covered

**What 1952 did NOT include:**
- Television was added explicitly but was a minor element in 1952; the target was telephone
  and radio
- No "affecting a financial institution" enhancement (added 1988)
- No §1346 honest services definition (added 1988 after McNally)
- Penalty was identical to mail fraud: $10,000 fine and/or 5 years

---

## Why the mirror structure matters

By deliberately mirroring mail fraud, Congress imported the entire 80-year body of mail fraud
case law into wire fraud. Courts uniformly apply mail fraud precedents to wire fraud cases.
This means:
- Every Supreme Court decision on what "scheme to defraud" means in the mail fraud context
  applies directly to wire fraud
- Every circuit court ruling on materiality, intent, "in furtherance," and jurisdictional nexus
  under mail fraud applies to wire fraud
- The two statutes are essentially interchangeable — prosecutors routinely charge both in the
  same information when a scheme uses both mail and wire
