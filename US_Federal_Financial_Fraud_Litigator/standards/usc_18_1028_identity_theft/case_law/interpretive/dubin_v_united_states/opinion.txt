CORE HOLDING FOR § 1028 CONTEXT:
Dubin's "at the crux" standard clarifies when identity information use rises to the level of criminal identity theft rather than incidental use of identifying information in an unrelated fraud.

For § 1028 predicate charges supporting § 1028A aggravated identity theft, the "at the crux" test determines whether the conduct crosses the line from fraud-with-incidental-ID-use to identity theft.

For the steward's cases: SIM swap fraud is unambiguously "at the crux" under Dubin. The fraud consists entirely of appropriating the victim's telecom identity — assuming control of the victim's phone number, receiving the victim's communications, and impersonating the victim to all systems that use phone-based authentication. The victim's telecom identity is not incidental to the fraud; it IS the fraud.
