# Statute Version Cited — FCC CPNI Enforcement History

**Pattern period:** 2007-2023
**Rules cited across the enforcement actions:** 47 U.S.C. §222 + 47 C.F.R. §§64.2010, 64.2011

## Progression of the Standard of Care

**2007 (FCC 07-22):** Password/PIN authentication required; biographical alone insufficient
**2015 (AT&T settlement):** Carrier duty to supervise agents who have CPNI access confirmed; $25M penalty establishes the seriousness of the obligation
**2023 (FCC 23-67):** SIM swap specifically identified as CPNI violation; pre-swap customer notification required; enhanced verification required

## Current Standard (as of Aug. 2025 knowledge cutoff)

The current operative authentication standard is 47 C.F.R. §64.2010 as implemented
pursuant to FCC 07-22 (2007), further strengthened by FCC 23-67 (2023) for SIM swap
specifically. The 2023 rules apply to incidents occurring after the rules took effect.

For SIM swap attacks occurring approximately 2018 (within the FCC 07-22 post-2007
framework but before FCC 23-67), the applicable standard is FCC 07-22's authentication
rules — carriers must use passwords/PINs and cannot rely on biographical information alone.
