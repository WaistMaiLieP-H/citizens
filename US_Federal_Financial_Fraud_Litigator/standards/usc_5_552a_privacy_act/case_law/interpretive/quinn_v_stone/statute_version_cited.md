# Statute Version at Time of Quinn v. Stone (1991) / Albright v. United States (1980)

**§552a(g)(1)(D) version:** As originally enacted 1974. The "intentional or willful"
language has not been amended. Albright v. United States (1980) and subsequent cases
applying the reckless-disregard formulation interpret the original 1974 text.

**Operative text of §552a(g)(1)(D):**
"Whenever any agency ... fails to comply with any other provision of this section ...
in such a way as to have an adverse effect on an individual, the individual may bring
a civil action against the agency, and the district courts of the United States shall
have jurisdiction in the matters under the provisions of this subsection."

Combined with §552a(g)(4): intentional or willful violations under (g)(1)(D) = $1,000
minimum or actual damages, whichever is greater + attorneys' fees + costs.

**Current operative text:** §552a(g)(1)(D) is unchanged from 1974 enactment. The
Albright reckless-disregard standard remains the controlling formulation in the D.C.
Circuit and has been adopted by most other circuits.
