# Statute Version at Time of H.J. Inc. (1989)

**§1961(5) version:** As enacted 1970. Text:
"'pattern of racketeering activity' requires at least two acts of racketeering activity, one of
which occurred after the effective date of this chapter and the last of which occurred within ten
years (excluding any period of imprisonment) after the commission of a prior act of racketeering
activity."

**The Court's interpretive problem:** The statutory text only says "at least two acts" — it does
not say "related" or "continuous." The Court derived the relatedness + continuity requirements
from the legislative history (S. Rep. No. 91-617's reference to "pattern" as something more than
isolated criminal acts) and the ordinary meaning of "pattern."

**Current operative text:** §1961(5) text is unchanged from 1970. H.J. Inc.'s relatedness +
continuity standard is the governing judicial gloss. No legislative response to H.J. Inc.

**The 10-year lookback period:** Still operative. Predicate acts must occur within 10 years of
each other. For Michael's cases: 2018–2022 is well within this window.
