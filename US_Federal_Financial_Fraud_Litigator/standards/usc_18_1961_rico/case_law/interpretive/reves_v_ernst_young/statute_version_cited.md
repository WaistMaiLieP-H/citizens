# Statute Version at Time of Reves (1993)

**§1962(c) version:** As enacted 1970. Text:
"It shall be unlawful for any person employed by or associated with any enterprise engaged in,
or the activities of which affect, interstate or foreign commerce, to conduct or participate,
directly or indirectly, in the conduct of such enterprise's affairs through a pattern of
racketeering activity or collection of unlawful debt."

**The Court's interpretive focus:** "to conduct or participate, directly or indirectly, in the
conduct of such enterprise's affairs." The phrase "conduct...in the conduct of" requires
direction — not mere association or service.

**Current operative text:** §1962(c) is unchanged from 1970. Reves' operation-or-management
test is the governing standard. Congress has not responded to Reves by broadening §1962(c)
to cover outside facilitators.

**Note on §1962(d) (conspiracy):** Reves applies to §1962(c). Under §1962(d), a person who
conspires to violate §1962(c) is liable even without personally conducting the enterprise's
affairs. For defendants who agreed to the scheme but did not have directorial roles, §1962(d)
conspiracy is the charging vehicle.
