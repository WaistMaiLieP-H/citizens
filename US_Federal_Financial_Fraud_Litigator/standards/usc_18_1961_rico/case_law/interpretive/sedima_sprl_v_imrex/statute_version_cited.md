# Statute Version at Time of Sedima (1985)

**§1964(c) version:** As enacted 1970. Text:
"Any person injured in his business or property by reason of a violation of section 1962 of this
chapter may sue therefor in any appropriate United States district court and shall recover
threefold the damages he sustains and the cost of the suit, including a reasonable attorney's fee."

**No change from enacted to Sedima:** The 1985 text was identical to 1970. Sedima interpreted
the original enacted language.

**Current operative text:** The 1996 amendment added: "except that no person may rely upon any
conduct that would have been actionable as fraud in the purchase or sale of securities to establish
a violation of section 1962" — the securities fraud exclusion. This exclusion does NOT apply to
Michael's cases (which involve identity theft, wire fraud, and financial fraud, not securities
fraud in the Exchange Act sense).

**Sedima remains controlling on the no-prior-conviction and no-racketeering-injury holdings.**
