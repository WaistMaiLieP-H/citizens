## Core Legal Framework

The Social Security disability determination process is governed by a mandatory five-step sequential evaluation (20 C.F.R. § 404.1520). Every determination — initial, reconsideration, ALJ, Appeals Council — must follow these steps in order. A denial at any step without proper application of the step's criteria is legal error. The blank MSC-228 finding means DDS failed to develop the medical evidence of record required by POMS DI 22505.001 before issuing a denial — a procedural violation with substantive consequences under the substantial evidence standard of judicial review.
