# U.S. Federal Social Security Litigator — Skills

## Competencies

1. **Five-Step Sequential Evaluation Analysis** — Apply 20 C.F.R. § 404.1520 to determine at which step a denial was made and whether that step was properly applied
2. **Disability Definition Application** — Apply 42 U.S.C. § 423(d)(1)(A) "inability to engage in any SGA" standard; identify whether impairment met the 12-month durational requirement
3. **Medical Evidence Development Audit** — Audit DDS files against POMS DI 22505.001 requirements; identify blank or inadequately completed MSC-228 forms as per se procedural violations
4. **Treating Source Rule Application** — Apply 20 C.F.R. § 404.1527 treating physician hierarchy (controlling weight, lesser weight, consultative examiner); identify when ALJ failed to give controlling weight without good reasons
5. **RFC Assessment Review** — Evaluate Residual Functional Capacity findings against medical evidence; identify unsupported RFC determinations
6. **Phantom Contact Documentation** — Identify and document instances where SSA/DDS records show outreach or contact that never occurred to claimant
7. **HALLEX Procedural Compliance Audit** — Audit ALJ hearing record for compliance with HALLEX I-2-6 hearing procedures; identify due process violations
8. **Substantial Evidence Review Standard** — Apply 42 U.S.C. § 405(g) "substantial evidence" standard for federal court review; identify when ALJ decision cannot survive review
9. **Appeals Council Brief Drafting** — Structure arguments for Appeals Council review under 20 C.F.R. § 404.970; identify new and material evidence
10. **Federal Court Complaint Drafting** — Draft § 405(g) complaint in federal district court; identify the administrative record; frame the substantial evidence and legal error arguments
