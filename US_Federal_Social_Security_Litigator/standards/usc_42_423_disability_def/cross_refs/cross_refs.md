## Critical Durational Requirement

The 12-month durational requirement of § 423(d)(1)(A) intersects with SIRVA (Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration — Case #37):
- SIRVA from COVID vaccine (~November 2021) followed by failed shoulder surgery (April 2022)
- Post-surgical impairment continuing beyond 12 months satisfies the durational requirement for any SSDI claim arising from those events
- The durational requirement runs from onset of the impairment, not from the date of the SSA application

## Objective Evidence Requirement

§ 423(d)(2)(B): impairment must result from "anatomical, physiological, or psychological abnormalities which are demonstrable by medically acceptable clinical and laboratory diagnostic techniques."

This requires that medical records exist — imaging, operative reports, clinical notes — demonstrating the impairment objectively. Denial based on absence of such records where records were requested through phantom contacts and not obtained is a record development failure, not an evidentiary deficiency by the claimant.
