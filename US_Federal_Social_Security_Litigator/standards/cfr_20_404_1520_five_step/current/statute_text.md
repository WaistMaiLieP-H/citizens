## Key Operative Provisions

**(a) General:**
We will use the following five-step sequential evaluation process to determine whether you are disabled:
(1) Is claimant doing substantial gainful activity?
(2) Does claimant have a severe medically determinable physical or mental impairment?
(3) Does the impairment meet or equal one in the Listings?
(4) Does claimant have the residual functional capacity to perform past relevant work?
(5) Considering claimant's RFC, age, education, and work experience, does claimant have the ability to do any other work?

**(b) Step 1 — Substantial gainful activity:**
If you are doing substantial gainful activity, we will find that you are not disabled. (We explain what we mean by substantial gainful activity in §§ 404.1571–404.1576.)

**(c) Step 2 — Severe impairment:**
If you do not have a severe medically determinable physical or mental impairment that meets the duration requirement in § 404.1509, or a combination of impairments that is severe and meets the duration requirement, we will find that you are not disabled. (We explain what we mean by a severe impairment in § 404.1522. We explain what we mean by the duration requirement in § 404.1509.)

**(d) Step 3 — Listing of Impairments:**
If you have an impairment(s) that meets or equals one of our listings in appendix 1 of this subpart and meets the duration requirement, we will find that you are disabled.

**(e) Step 4 — Past relevant work:**
(1) Assessment of RFC: We will assess your residual functional capacity based on all the relevant evidence in your case record to determine your residual functional capacity.
(2) Past relevant work: If you have the residual functional capacity to perform your past relevant work, we will find that you are not disabled.

**(f) Step 5 — Other work:**
(1) If you cannot do your past relevant work, we will consider your residual functional capacity and your vocational factors (your age, education, and work experience) to determine if you can do other work.
(2) If you cannot do other work, we will find that you are disabled.
(3) If you can do other work, we will find that you are not disabled.

**(g) Evidence:**
Before we make a determination that you are not disabled at steps 3, 4, or 5 of this process, we are responsible for developing your complete medical history, including arranging for a consultative examination(s) if necessary, and making every reasonable effort to help you get medical reports from your own medical sources.
