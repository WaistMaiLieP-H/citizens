## Judicial Review Connection

42 U.S.C. § 405(g) — federal district court reviews final SSA decisions under the substantial evidence standard. Failure to properly apply any step of § 404.1520 is reviewable error. Failure to apply the five-step framework at all, or to reach a conclusion at a step without evidentiary support, is grounds for remand.
