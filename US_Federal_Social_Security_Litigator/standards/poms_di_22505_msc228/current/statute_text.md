## Key Operative Requirements

**DI 22505.001 — Development of Medical Evidence of Record:**

DDS is responsible for developing adequate medical evidence of record (MER) to make disability determinations. This includes:

1. **Identifying all medical sources:** Obtaining from the claimant a complete list of all treating physicians, hospitals, and other health care providers who have examined or treated the claimant in connection with the alleged impairment.

2. **Requesting records:** Contacting each identified medical source and requesting all relevant records — office notes, diagnostic tests, imaging, operative reports, and any functional assessment forms. DDS must make at least two attempts to obtain records before proceeding without them.

3. **Assessing adequacy:** After obtaining records, DDS must assess whether the records are adequate to adjudicate the claim. Adequate means: sufficient to evaluate severity at step 2, evaluate listing criteria at step 3, and assess RFC for steps 4-5.

4. **Ordering consultative examinations:** If existing medical evidence is insufficient or inconsistent, DDS must order a consultative examination (CE) with a licensed medical professional. DDS may not simply deny on an inadequate record when a CE would resolve the inadequacy.

5. **Medical consultant review:** A licensed physician or psychologist employed by or under contract to DDS must review the medical evidence and provide a medical opinion on the claimant's impairments and functional limitations. This review is documented on the Medical Summary of Claim (MSC-228 or electronic equivalent).

**DI 24515.064 — MSC-228 Medical Summary of Claim:**

The MSC-228 (or its electronic equivalent) is the form that documents the DDS medical consultant's clinical assessment. It must include:
- Identification of all medically determinable impairments
- Assessment of whether any impairment meets or equals a listing
- RFC assessment (physical and/or mental)
- Rationale for the RFC findings referencing the medical evidence
- Medical consultant's signature and credentials

A blank MSC-228 with no completed fields and no medical consultant signature indicates that no medical consultant review was performed prior to the disability determination.
