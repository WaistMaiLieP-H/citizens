## The Blank MSC-228 Chain of Proof

```
Step 1: Request SSA administrative file under Privacy Act / § 405(c)(1)
Step 2: Identify MSC-228 or electronic equivalent in DDS portion of file
Step 3: Document: blank fields, no RFC, no consultant signature
Step 4: Cross-reference with DDS contact log — purported contacts with treating providers
Step 5: Obtain declaration from treating providers that no SSA contact occurred
Step 6: Per se violation established: POMS DI 24515.064 + 20 C.F.R. § 404.1512
Step 7: Legal consequence: denial without required medical foundation = reversible error
```

## Record Request Mechanism

The administrative record is obtained by:
1. Submitting a Privacy Act request to SSA (5 U.S.C. § 552a)
2. Submitting an administrative appeal requesting the complete claim file
3. After federal suit filed under § 405(g), SSA must lodge the certified administrative record with the court

The blank MSC-228 will appear in the DDS portion of the record. It cannot be concealed or amended after the fact without creating an additional fraud on the record.

## SIRVA Connection (Case #37)
