## The Rule — Preemption Scope and Limits

### What VICP Preempts

Under 42 USC §300aa-22(b)(1) as construed by *Bruesewitz v. Wyeth LLC* (2011) 562 U.S. 223:

**VICP preempts all design defect claims against vaccine MANUFACTURERS** for vaccine-related injuries where the vaccine was properly prepared and accompanied by proper warnings. This is an express federal preemption of Greenman/Barker design defect tort liability.

Before filing any civil action against a vaccine manufacturer for damages exceeding $1,000, the injured person must first file a VICP petition under §300aa-11(a)(2)(A).

### What VICP Does NOT Preempt

| Category | Preempted? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Design defect against vaccine manufacturer | **YES** — Bruesewitz | §300aa-22(b)(1) express preemption |
| Failure to warn against manufacturer | **PARTIAL** — §300aa-22(c) | Manufacturer not liable for failing to warn patient IF manufacturer warned administering provider |
| Manufacturing defect against vaccine manufacturer | **NO** — §300aa-22(b)(1) exception | "properly prepared" requirement: a defectively manufactured unit is NOT covered |
| Negligent administration by healthcare provider | **NO** — not manufacturer liability | §300aa-22 covers only "vaccine manufacturers" |
| Device defect (syringe/needle) | **NO** | Manufacturer is the syringe maker, not the vaccine manufacturer |
| Medical malpractice by administering party | **NO** | Ordinary tort claim; VICP does not reach providers |

## The Walgreens Rule — Case-Dispositive Analysis

**Walgreens is NOT a vaccine manufacturer.** Walgreens is a pharmacy/healthcare provider that administered the COVID vaccine on behalf of the federal COVID vaccination program.

The COVID-19 vaccines were manufactured by:
- Moderna, Inc.
- Pfizer-BioNTech
- Johnson & Johnson / Janssen

Walgreens administered vaccines manufactured by these entities. Walgreens does not make vaccines. Walgreens is not in the chain of vaccine manufacturing.

**Therefore:** §300aa-22 manufacturer immunity does NOT apply to Walgreens.

**The negligent administration claim against Walgreens:**
- Standard of care: An administering pharmacist or provider must inject vaccines into the appropriate anatomical site (deltoid muscle, middle third, 1-inch depth) using the appropriate technique
- SIRVA mechanism: Injecting above the deltoid into the subacromial space/bursa causes SIRVA (Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration)
- Evidence: Pain onset within 48 hours of injection; range-of-motion loss; MRI showing subacromial bursitis or rotator cuff injury
- This is a **healthcare provider negligence claim** governed by California medical malpractice and ordinary negligence law
- **Owned by:** CA_Medical_Malpractice_Litigator (SIRVA doctrine standard)

## VICP Filing Strategy — Dual Track

**Track 1 — VICP Petition:**
- File in U.S. Court of Federal Claims within 3 years of first symptom onset
- SIRVA is a Table Injury for COVID vaccines → presumption of causation → no-fault compensation
- VICP compensates for medical costs, lost wages, pain and suffering (up to $250,000), death benefits
- **No legal fees if claim compensated** — attorneys' fees paid separately under §300aa-15(e)

**Track 2 — Civil Action Against Walgreens:**
- Negligent administration (wrong injection site causing SIRVA)
- Does NOT require prior VICP filing — VICP filing requirement applies to suits against MANUFACTURERS, not providers
- Governed by California negligence/malpractice standards
- CACI No. 500 (Negligence) / CACI No. 505 (Standard of Care for Professionals)
- SOL: CCP §335.1 (2-year personal injury) OR CCP §340.5 (3-year/1-year medical malpractice — see CA_Medical_Malpractice_Litigator)

**The tracks are NOT mutually exclusive.** A plaintiff can file a VICP petition AND pursue civil action against Walgreens simultaneously or sequentially.

## Timing Issue — STEWARD ALERT

**VICP Filing Deadline:** 3 years from first symptom of the vaccine-related injury.

**SIRVA symptoms:** Typically onset within 48 hours of vaccination.

**Vaccination date:** Approximately November 2021.

**VICP deadline:** Approximately November 2024.

**As of 2026-04-13:** The VICP filing window has likely CLOSED (approximately 18 months past deadline).

**Consequence:** If the VICP petition was not filed by approximately November 2024, the no-fault VICP compensation pathway is likely foreclosed. The civil action against Walgreens for negligent administration (which does NOT require VICP filing) remains AVAILABLE.

**Steward must verify:** (1) whether a VICP petition was ever filed; (2) exact vaccination date; (3) exact first symptom date. If VICP deadline is passed, shift focus entirely to civil negligent administration claim against Walgreens.

## Cross-Case Application

This standard establishes the framework that explains WHY the primary SIRVA theory is negligent administration against Walgreens rather than vaccine design defect against the manufacturer. The preemption analysis is the gateway determination for any vaccine injury claim.
