## 42 U.S.C. § 300aa-11 — Petition for Compensation (Operative Provisions)

**(a)(1)** Any person who has suffered a vaccine-related injury or death, or the legal representative of such a person, may petition the court [United States Court of Federal Claims, acting as the "Vaccine Court"] for compensation under the Program.

**(a)(2)(A)** No person who has sustained a vaccine-related injury or death may bring a civil action against a vaccine manufacturer for damages in an amount greater than $1,000 in any state or federal court for such injury or death unless—
  - (i) such person has filed a petition under subsection (b) of this section, AND
  - (ii) either (I) the United States Court of Federal Claims has entered judgment under section 300aa-12 [on the petition], or (II) such person has withdrawn the petition.

[Note: §300aa-11(b) sets out petition requirements; §300aa-11(c) addresses petitions for vaccine-related deaths; filing deadline is 3 years after first symptom or manifestation of onset of the vaccine-related injury.]

## 42 U.S.C. § 300aa-22 — Manufacturer Liability (Operative Provisions)

**(a)** Except as provided in subsections (b), (c), and (e), the standards of responsibility applicable to a manufacturer of a vaccine under any applicable state law shall apply.

**(b)(1)** No vaccine manufacturer shall be liable in a civil action for damages arising from a vaccine-related injury or death associated with the administration of a vaccine after October 1, 1988, if the injury or death resulted from side effects that were **unavoidable** even though the vaccine was **properly prepared** and was accompanied by **proper directions and warnings**.

**(b)(2)** For purposes of paragraph (1), a vaccine shall be presumed to be accompanied by proper directions and warnings if the vaccine manufacturer shows that it complied in all material respects with all requirements under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act [and applicable regulations] applicable to the vaccine and related to vaccine-related injury or death for which the civil action was brought.

**(c)** No vaccine manufacturer shall be liable in a civil action for damages arising from a vaccine-related injury or death associated with the administration of a vaccine after October 1, 1988, solely due to the manufacturer's failure to provide direct warnings to the injured party (or the injured party's legal representative) if the manufacturer did provide such warning to the health care provider who administered the vaccine.

## Supreme Court — Bruesewitz v. Wyeth LLC (2011)

**Citation:** *Bruesewitz v. Wyeth LLC*, 562 U.S. 223 (2011)
**Holding:** Section 300aa-22(b)(1) expressly preempts ALL design defect claims against vaccine manufacturers in state tort law. A plaintiff cannot use Greenman/Barker design defect to sue a vaccine manufacturer, even if the plaintiff can show a safer vaccine design existed and the manufacturer could feasibly have adopted it.

**Rationale:** Congress intended the VICP to be the primary compensation mechanism for vaccine injuries, with manufacturer design defect claims as a trade-off for no-fault compensation through the VICP. Allowing design defect tort suits would undermine the VICP's purpose and potentially reduce vaccine availability.

## VICP Vaccine Injury Table — SIRVA

The National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program Vaccine Injury Table (42 C.F.R. § 100.3) includes **Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration (SIRVA)** as a Table Injury for COVID-19 vaccines (added 2022 for COVID-19 mRNA vaccines).

**What "Table Injury" means for SIRVA:**
A petitioner whose SIRVA onset is within 48 hours of vaccination, who had no prior shoulder condition, and who meets the other Table criteria is entitled to a **PRESUMPTION OF CAUSATION** — no need to prove the vaccine caused the injury. The VICP pays compensation on that presumption.

**This is a PLAINTIFF RIGHT, not a DEFENDANT SHIELD.** The Table Injury listing helps the injured person get compensation quickly through the VICP. It does NOT shield Walgreens from liability for the negligent administration that caused the SIRVA.
