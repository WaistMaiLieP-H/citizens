| Skill ID | Skill Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| PL-001 | Strict Liability Analysis | Apply Greenman framework: was the product used in a reasonably foreseeable way; did it fail to perform as safely as an ordinary consumer would expect? |
| PL-002 | Design Defect Dual Test | Apply Barker test: (1) consumer expectations prong — did the product fail to perform as safely as an ordinary consumer would expect; AND/OR (2) risk-utility prong — did the design's risks outweigh its benefits? Burden shifts to defendant under risk-utility. |
| PL-003 | Manufacturing Defect Analysis | Distinguish manufacturing defect (product deviated from intended design) from design defect. Apply res ipsa loquitur where appropriate. |
| PL-004 | Failure to Warn | Identify known or reasonably foreseeable risks not disclosed in product labeling or instructions. Apply *Finn v. G.D. Searle* (1984). |
| PL-005 | VICP Preemption Boundary | Determine whether the defendant is a MANUFACTURER (VICP-covered) or ADMINISTERING PARTY (ordinary tort). Walgreens = administering party; VICP immunity does NOT apply. |
| PL-006 | Implied Warranty Analysis | Evaluate COM §2314 merchantability breach: was the product fit for its ordinary purposes? Was seller a merchant? Was warranty properly disclaimed under §2316? |
| PL-007 | SOL Calendar (Product Claims) | Apply CCP §338(c) (3-year personal property damage) and §338(d) (3-year fraud with discovery rule). Map injury date → discovery date → filing deadline. |
| PL-008 | Strict Liability Chain of Distribution | Identify all entities in the distribution chain (manufacturer, distributor, retailer, lessor) subject to strict liability under Greenman and Price v. Shell Oil (1970). |
| PL-009 | Comparative Fault in Product Cases | Apply Li v. Yellow Cab (1975) pure comparative fault rules in product liability context. Distinguish from assumption of risk and misuse. |
| PL-010 | SIRVA Administration Negligence | Analyze vaccine administration error (wrong muscle, wrong depth, wrong technique) as ordinary negligence claim independent of VICP. Document injection site records, pain onset within 48 hours, range-of-motion loss. |
