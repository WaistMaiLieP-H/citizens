## VICP Preemption — Critical Boundary (Case-Dispositive)

**Rule:** 42 USC §300aa-11 and the VICP framework immunize vaccine MANUFACTURERS from design defect claims when the vaccine is properly prepared and accompanied by proper warnings (*Bruesewitz v. Wyeth LLC*, 562 U.S. 223 (2011)).

**Exception:** The VICP does NOT immunize ADMINISTERING PARTIES (pharmacies, pharmacists, nurses, clinics). Walgreens administered the COVID vaccine. Walgreens did not manufacture it. Walgreens's liability for SIRVA (wrong injection site — bursa/subacromial space instead of deltoid muscle belly) is an ordinary negligence/healthcare provider claim, not a vaccine manufacturer claim.

**Consequence:** VICP filing is NOT required before suing Walgreens for negligent administration. The VICP Vaccine Injury Table includes SIRVA (added 2017), but that Table governs petitions TO the Vaccine Court against the vaccine program — it does not shield Walgreens.

## Product Liability Framework — Three Defect Types

| Defect Type | Standard | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing defect | Greenman strict liability | Product deviated from intended design |
| Design defect | Barker dual test | Consumer expectations OR risk-utility (burden shifts to defendant) |
| Failure to warn | Finn v. G.D. Searle (1984) | Warning inadequate for known or reasonably foreseeable risks |
