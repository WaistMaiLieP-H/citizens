**Training-knowledge summary (not for citation until verified):**
34 U.S.C. § 12491 prohibits covered housing providers (those participating in federal housing programs) from denying admission, denying assistance, evicting, or penalizing victims of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking on the basis of that status or incident of violence. Requires emergency transfer plans. Requires confidentiality of victim information. Permits self-certification as documentation. Permits bifurcation of leases to remove perpetrator while retaining victim.
