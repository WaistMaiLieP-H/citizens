# GOV § 12955 — Operative Rule

**Standard:** gov_12955_feha_housing_discrimination
**Layer:** current/rule
**Status:** VERIFIED (live MCP fetch 2026-04-13)

---

## The Rule

California's FEHA housing anti-discrimination statute prohibits a comprehensive list of housing discrimination acts. The statute operates in 16 subdivisions, each targeting a different discriminatory mechanism. The protected characteristics are broader than the federal Fair Housing Act — California adds source of income, gender identity/expression, genetic information, and veteran/military status.

| Subdivision | Who | What is prohibited | Protected basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | Owner of housing accommodation | Discriminate or harass in sale, rental, any housing | All 16+ characteristics |

| (c) | Any person | Advertise preference, limitation, or discrimination | All protected characteristics |
| (e) | **Bank, mortgage company, financial institution** | Discriminate in terms/conditions/privileges of financial assistance for purchase/refinance/construction | All protected characteristics |
| (f) | Owner | Retaliate (harass, evict, discriminate) against person who opposed unlawful practices or participated in proceedings | N/A — anti-retaliation |
| (g) | Any person | Aid, abet, incite, compel, or coerce unlawful acts | N/A |
| (h) | Any person, for profit | Blockbusting — inducing sales by representations about protected-class persons entering neighborhood | All |
| (i)(1) | Real estate-related transaction businesses | Discriminate in making transaction available or in terms/conditions | All |
| (i)(2) | Appraisers | Discriminate in appraisal services | Most protected characteristics |
| (j) | Any person | Deny access to MLS, real estate broker organization, or other service | All |
| (k) | Any person | Otherwise make unavailable or deny a dwelling | All |
| (l) | Any person | Discriminatory land use — restrictive covenants, zoning, use permit denials | All |

## Critical Rules for UA342 / Honeysuckle Scenario

### §12955(e) — Financial Institution Discrimination
Banks, mortgage companies, and other financial institutions providing financial assistance for **purchase, refinance, organization, or construction** of housing cannot discriminate in the **terms, conditions, or privileges** of that assistance based on any protected characteristic.

**Application:** If any financial institution involved in the Honeysuckle sale or refinancing discriminated against Michael based on disability, source of income (WC benefits, SSA benefits), or perceived characteristics — §12955(e) is the cause of action. The equity gap ($465K equity → $109K received) requires investigation of the terms each financial institution imposed.

### §12955(i)(1) — Real Estate Transaction Participants
Real estate businesses — brokers, agents, title companies, escrow companies — cannot discriminate in making a transaction available or in the terms and conditions of the transaction.

**Application:** If any participant in the Honeysuckle closing (broker, escrow, title company) discriminated against Michael in the transaction terms — this is the hook. Appraisers who undervalued the property based on protected-class associations also fall under §12955(i)(2).

### §12955(l) — Restrictive Covenants / Land Use
The existence of a restrictive covenant in any document is itself discrimination under §12955(l), regardless of whether the covenant has been declared void. 

**Application — HILLBERGMANN deed:** If the HILLBERGMANN compound identity documents recorded in connection with real property contained any discriminatory covenant or restriction, §12955(l) applies independent of whether anyone attempted to enforce the covenant.

### §12955(m) — Perception Rule
Protection extends to persons **perceived** to have a protected characteristic, and persons **associated** with a protected person.

**Application:** If discriminatory treatment was based on perception of disability, perception of financial status, or association with other protected persons — §12955(m) extends protection to those situations.

### §12955(f) — Anti-Retaliation
Retaliation against a person for opposing discriminatory practices or for participating in proceedings is itself unlawful under §12955(f). The unlawful detainer carve-out is narrow — it only prevents using the anti-retaliation provision to delay an otherwise-valid eviction.

## Civil Enforcement Path

- **GOV §12989.1:** 2-year SOL from occurrence or termination of discriminatory practice (whichever is last); tolled during CRD administrative proceeding; no exhaustion required to file civil suit
- **GOV §12989.2:** Actual damages + punitive damages + injunction/TRO + attorney's fees (discretionary, prevailing party standard) + state-agency offset provision
- **GOV §12955(d):** §12955 violations by persons subject to CIV §51 (Unruh Act) = Unruh Act claim with mandatory $4,000/violation statutory damages

## Source of Income Definition (§12955(p))

"Source of income" = lawful, verifiable income paid directly to tenant, to a representative, or on behalf of tenant — **including federal/state housing subsidies, Section 8 vouchers, and HUD-VASH vouchers**. A financial inquiry about the level or source of income is not itself discrimination — but refusing to rent based on that source is prohibited.
