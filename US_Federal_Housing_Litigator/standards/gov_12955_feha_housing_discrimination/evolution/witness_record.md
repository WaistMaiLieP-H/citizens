## CHRONICLE FLAG

The 1963–1967 period contains CHRONICLE-eligible content. Proposition 14's text explicitly protected the right to discriminate in housing. The California Supreme Court described the mechanism in *Reitman v. Mulkey*: the state "enlist[ed] the power of the State to make discriminatory housing practices lawful."
