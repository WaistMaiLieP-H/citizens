# GOV § 12955 — Evolution Stage 02: FEHA Recodification (1980)

**Stage:** 02_feha_1980
**Authority:** Stats. 1980, Ch. 992 (Fair Employment and Housing Act); former Lab. Code §1413; recodified as Gov. Code §12955
**Status:** PROPOSED

---

## The 1980 FEHA Recodification

The Fair Employment and Housing Act (1980) consolidated California's employment and housing anti-discrimination law under a single administrative framework. The Rumford Act provisions were recodified as Gov. Code §12955. The DFEH (Department of Fair Employment and Housing — now the Civil Rights Department, CRD) was created as the administrative enforcement mechanism.

### What the 1980 FEHA Added

The FEHA created:
- Administrative complaint procedure with DFEH as investigator and enforcer
- Right-to-sue mechanism paralleling the employment discrimination administrative process
- Parallel civil action pathway (§12989.1)
- Civil damages and attorney's fees provisions (§12989.2)

The 1980 FEHA kept the same protected class base as the Rumford Act (race, color, religion, national origin, ancestry, sex, marital status) and added:
- **Familial status** — families with children under 18 cannot be excluded from most housing
- **Physical disability** — expanded California's disability protection in housing beyond what federal law then required

### Text Stability Through Recodification

The core prohibitions of §12955(a)-(d) remained structurally stable through the 1980 recodification. The major post-1980 changes were expansions of the protected class list, not restructuring of the underlying prohibition framework.
