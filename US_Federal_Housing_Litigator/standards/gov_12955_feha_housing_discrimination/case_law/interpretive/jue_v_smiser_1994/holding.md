# Jue v. Smiser (1994)

**Citation:** 23 Cal.App.4th 312 (1994)
**Court:** California Court of Appeal
**Standard:** gov_12955_feha_housing_discrimination (via CIV §1102 TDS — cross-standard)
**Status:** PROPOSED — verify at courts.ca.gov or Google Scholar before reliance

---

## Holding

*Jue v. Smiser* established that the Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) requirements of CIV §1102 et seq. set a floor for disclosure, not a ceiling.

**Key doctrine:**
1. **TDS compliance does not bar a fraudulent concealment claim** — a seller who fully completes the TDS but still conceals material facts through other means (omissions, misrepresentations outside the TDS form) can still be liable for fraudulent concealment
2. **Material fact disclosure obligation runs beyond the TDS form** — if a seller knows of a material defect or material condition affecting the property that is not captured by the TDS form questions, common-law disclosure duties may still require disclosure
3. **The TDS is not a shield** — a seller cannot hide behind TDS compliance to extinguish other disclosure-based claims

## Relevance to Corpus

*Jue v. Smiser* is the foundational TDS case establishing that CIV §1102 compliance is necessary but not sufficient to insulate from disclosure liability. In the Honeysuckle context:

1. **19 unsigned documents** — if the TDS was among the unsigned documents, there was no TDS compliance. *Jue* confirms that even if some other form of disclosure occurred, the §1102 violation remains.

2. **Equity gap documentation** — if the proceeds disbursement concealed material information about where the $356K equity gap went, the *Jue* principle (TDS is the floor, not the ceiling) supports a fraudulent concealment claim alongside the §1102 statutory violation.

3. **Cross-standard relevance** — this case is most directly applicable to the `civ_1102_tds_scope` standard but is cross-referenced here because TDS non-compliance in a housing transaction can also constitute a §12955(i)(1) violation if the real estate transaction was manipulated on a discriminatory basis.
