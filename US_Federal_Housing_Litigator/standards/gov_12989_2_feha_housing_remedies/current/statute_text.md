## GOV § 12989.1 — Civil Action — Full Text

(a) An aggrieved person may commence a civil action in an appropriate court not later than two years after the occurrence or the termination of an alleged discriminatory housing practice, or the breach of a conciliation agreement entered into, whichever occurs last, to obtain appropriate relief with respect to the discriminatory housing practice or breach. The computation of the two-year period shall not include any time during which an administrative proceeding under this part was pending with respect to a complaint under this part based upon the discriminatory housing practice or breach.

(b) An aggrieved person may commence a civil action whether or not a complaint has been filed under this part and without regard to the status of any complaint. Any aggrieved person who is aggrieved with respect to the issues to be determined in a civil action filed under this part, may intervene in that civil action. However, if the department has obtained a conciliation agreement with the consent of an aggrieved person, no action may be filed under this part by the aggrieved person with respect to the alleged discriminatory housing practice that forms the basis for the complaint, except for the purpose of enforcing the terms of the agreement.

---

## GOV § 12989.2 — Remedies — Full Text

In a civil action brought under Section 12981 or 12989.1, if the court finds that a discriminatory housing practice has occurred or is about to occur, the court may award the plaintiff actual and punitive damages and may grant other relief, including the issuance of a temporary or permanent injunction, or temporary restraining order, or other order, as it deems appropriate to prevent any defendant from engaging in or continuing to engage in an unlawful practice. In a civil action brought under this section, the court may, at its discretion, award the prevailing party, including the department, reasonable attorney's fees and costs, including expert witness fees, against any party other than the state. If the court finds that the defendant has engaged in an unlawful practice under this part and is liable for actual or punitive damages any amount due to the defendant by a state agency may be offset to satisfy the court's final order or decision.

## Key Structural Notes

- **§12989.1(a):** 2-year SOL — runs from OCCURRENCE or TERMINATION of practice (whichever is last); tolled during pending CRD administrative proceeding
- **§12989.1(b):** NO CRD exhaustion required — aggrieved person may file civil action WHETHER OR NOT a complaint was filed with CRD
- **§12989.2 damages:** Actual damages; punitive damages; temporary or permanent injunction; TRO; any other appropriate order
- **§12989.2 attorney fees:** Discretionary, prevailing party (other than state) — defendant pays if plaintiff prevails; plaintiff pays if defendant prevails and action was objectively groundless (Civil Rights Attorney's Fees Awards Act standard applies)
- **§12989.2 state offset:** If defendant also owes money to state agency — that debt may be offset to satisfy the court's judgment; unusual provision; relevant if defendants have state contracts or outstanding state obligations
- **Expert witness fees:** Explicitly recoverable under §12989.2 — important where expert appraisers or document examiners are required
- **"About to occur" standard:** Court may act even before discrimination is consummated; injunctive relief available on prospective threat
