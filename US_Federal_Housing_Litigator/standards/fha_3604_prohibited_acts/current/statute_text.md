> **Training-knowledge summary (not for citation until verified):**
> § 3604 prohibits discrimination in housing sale, rental, advertising, and terms on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, familial status, national origin (original 1968 classes plus sex added 1974), and handicap/disability + familial status (added by FHAA 1988, Pub. L. 100-430). The disability provisions at § 3604(f)(1)-(3) require reasonable modifications at the expense of the person with a disability and reasonable accommodations in rules, policies, and services.
