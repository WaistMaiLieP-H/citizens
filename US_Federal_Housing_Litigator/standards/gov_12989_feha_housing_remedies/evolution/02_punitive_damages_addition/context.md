## The Punitive Damages Addition

The original FEHA housing remedies provision did not include explicit punitive damages. The addition of punitive damages to the §12989.2 remedy package reflected the legislature's recognition that actual damages alone were insufficient to deter systematic housing discrimination.

The key departure from CIV §3294 (punitive damages): §12989.2 does not require a showing of "malice, fraud, or oppression" as a separate element. A finding that a discriminatory housing practice occurred is sufficient to authorize punitive damages. This is the structural difference between housing discrimination punitives (§12989.2) and tort punitives (§3294).
