## The 1980 FEHA Remedies

The Fair Employment and Housing Act (1980) created the civil action pathway for housing discrimination claims. Before the FEHA, the primary enforcement mechanism was administrative — complaint to a state agency, investigation, administrative hearing. The civil action track was added to give aggrieved persons a direct courthouse option.

The 1980 FEHA established:
- DFEH (Department of Fair Employment and Housing) administrative complaint track
- Civil action pathway as alternative to administrative track
- Actual damages as the baseline remedy
- Attorney's fees for successful plaintiffs

**The no-exhaustion principle** was built in from the start: California chose not to require exhaustion of the administrative remedy before civil action could be filed. This distinguished the California housing discrimination track from federal employment discrimination (which requires EEOC exhaustion before filing Title VII suit).
