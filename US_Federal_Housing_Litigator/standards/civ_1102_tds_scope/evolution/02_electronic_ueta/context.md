# CIV §§ 1102 / 1102.3 — Evolution Stage 02: Electronic Delivery (UETA)

**Stage:** 02_electronic_ueta
**Authority:** Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (Stats. 1999, Ch. 428); CIV §1102.3 amended to include electronic delivery
**Status:** PROPOSED

---

## UETA Electronic Delivery Addition

The Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA), adopted in California in 1999, authorized electronic documents and signatures for most transactions. CIV §1102.3 was amended to add electronic delivery as a valid delivery method for the TDS.

The amendment added the third delivery option: "5 days after delivery of an electronic record in transactions where the parties have agreed to conduct the transaction by electronic means, pursuant to provisions of the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act."

**Key constraint:** Electronic delivery only triggers the 5-day cancellation window if the **parties agreed** to conduct the transaction by electronic means. If only the seller or only the agent uses electronic delivery without the buyer's agreement under UETA, the electronic delivery may not be legally effective — potentially meaning the TDS was not delivered at all.

**Application — Honeysuckle:** If any of the 19 unsigned documents were delivered electronically, the threshold question is whether the parties validly agreed to electronic transaction conduct under UETA. An unsigned document delivered electronically without a valid UETA agreement is both unsigned and improperly delivered.
