# CIV §§ 1102 / 1102.3 — Operative Rule

**Standard:** civ_1102_tds_scope
**Layer:** current/rule
**Status:** VERIFIED (live MCP fetch 2026-04-13)

---

## The Rule

The Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) is the mandatory written disclosure that sellers of single-family residential property must provide to buyers. It is not optional. It cannot be waived. Failure to deliver it creates both statutory liability and cancellation rights.

---

## TDS Scope (§1102)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Property type | Single-family residential property — any transfer by sale, exchange, real property sales contract, lease with option, ground lease + improvements |
| Exception | §1102.2 (probate sales, trust sales, certain government sales, new construction) |
| Waiver | **VOID AS AGAINST PUBLIC POLICY** — §1102(c); any contract clause waiving TDS = unenforceable |
| BPS definitions | Business & Professions Code Chapter 1 §10000 et seq. definitions apply |

---

## TDS Delivery Timeline (§1102.3)

| Timing | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **As soon as practicable before transfer of title (sale)** | Timely delivery — no cancellation right triggered |
| **As soon as practicable before execution of contract (RPSC/lease-option)** | "Execution" = making or acceptance of offer |
| **After execution of offer to purchase** | Buyer has 3 days (in-person delivery), 5 days (mail), 5 days (electronic) to cancel by written notice |
| TDS not delivered at all | §1102 violation; buyer's cancellation right may survive; seller liability for damages |

**Cancellation clock starts:** When Sections I and II (and Section III if seller's agent represented) of the §1102.6 form are completed and delivered to buyer or buyer's agent.

---

## Key Attack Points — Honeysuckle 19 Unsigned Documents

### Attack 1 — Non-Delivery
If the TDS (§1102.6 form) was among the 19 unsigned documents, the TDS was never properly delivered. A TDS must be completed by the seller AND signed. An unsigned TDS is not a completed TDS. Non-delivery = §1102/§1102.3 violation.

### Attack 2 — Cancellation Right Never Triggered
If the TDS was delivered after the offer was accepted but the party was under conservatorship control, the 3/5-day cancellation clock ran without a meaningful opportunity to cancel. A person under conservatorship who cannot exercise the cancellation right independently was denied the statutory protection §1102.3 was designed to provide.

### Attack 3 — Waiver Is Void
If any of the 19 documents contained a clause purporting to waive TDS compliance or the buyer's inspection rights — that clause is void as against public policy under §1102(c). It cannot be used to defeat a §1102 claim.

### Attack 4 — Agent Liability
Under §1102.3, the seller's agent must complete Section III of the TDS form. If the agent knew the TDS was unsigned or not properly delivered and proceeded with the closing, the agent faces:
- BPC §10176 broker misconduct liability (owned by CA_Real_Estate_Attorney)
- Possible GOV §12955(i)(1) liability if the transaction terms were manipulated

### Attack 5 — §1102(c) Waiver-Void Interplay With Equity Extraction
The $356K equity gap in the Honeysuckle sale must be documented against the closing statement. Each disbursement — payoff of liens, closing costs, broker commissions, escrow charges — must be independently verified. Any disbursement direction in the unsigned documents is suspect: an unsigned document is not a valid authorization for a disbursement.

## Civil Remedies for §1102 Violation

- **§1102.13** — Buyer who did not receive a required TDS may rescind the sale if within 3 years of transfer of title (or later if concealment tolls), or may recover actual damages including all costs incurred after the violation
- **Fraud/concealment** — Per *Jue v. Smiser* (1994), §1102 sets the floor; fraudulent concealment claims survive TDS compliance and certainly survive TDS non-compliance
- **Agent liability** — Agents who fail to deliver TDS or complete their section can be independently liable; no respondeat superior insulation needed if the agent personally participated
