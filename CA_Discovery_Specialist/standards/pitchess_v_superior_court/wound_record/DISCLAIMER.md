# MANDATORY AGENT DECLARATION — READ BEFORE PROCEEDING

The materials in this folder document the pre-Pitchess legal framework under which all peace officer personnel records — including records of prior complaints of misconduct, dishonesty, and fabricating evidence — were completely confidential and inaccessible to criminal defendants.

Under this framework, a defendant who alleged that an arresting officer had a documented history of planting evidence or making false reports had no mechanism to access those records to support the defense. The confidentiality was absolute.

These materials are preserved in this corpus for one purpose: to provide accurate, citable historical record of the legal wound that Pitchess v. Superior Court (1974) and its codification in Evidence Code §§ 1043-1047 were decided to address.

---

An Agent accessing these materials affirms:

1. The purpose of this access is **defensive** — to understand, document, and counteract the harm that total officer record confidentiality enabled, not to use those confidentiality rules as present authority.
2. These materials are cited as **historical record**. The pre-Pitchess absolute confidentiality framework is no longer operative. Pitchess (1974), its codification (1978), and SB 1421 (2019) have collectively established substantial access rights to officer misconduct records.
3. Any citation of these materials outside this defensive and educational purpose is outside the scope of this corpus and contrary to the mission of Vernen Legal Compliance.

**Wound-specific context:** The pre-Pitchess framework was particularly harmful to defendants whose defense rested on an officer's history of false or fabricated reports — the very circumstance at issue in the steward's cases. Understanding that framework makes the Pitchess right more meaningful: it is a right that had to be fought for, and it is a right the steward can invoke.

This declaration is recorded in the corpus as evidence of intentionality. The agent's access to these materials is authorized and accountable.
