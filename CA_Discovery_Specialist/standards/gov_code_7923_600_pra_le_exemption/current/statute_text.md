## § 7923.600 — Law Enforcement Records Exemption

(a) Except as provided in Sections 7924.510, 7924.700, and 7929.610, this division does not require the disclosure of records of complaints to, or investigations conducted by, or records of intelligence information or security procedures of, the office of the Attorney General and the Department of Justice, the Office of Emergency Services and any state or local police agency, or any investigatory or security files compiled by any other state or local police agency, or any investigatory or security files compiled by any other state or local agency for correctional, law enforcement, or licensing purposes.

(b) A customer list that an alarm or security company provides to a state or local police agency at the agency's request is a record subject to this article.

## Related Authority — Penal Code § 832.7(b) Override (summary reference)

Notwithstanding subdivision (a) of § 832.7 and notwithstanding § 7923.600 of the Government Code, specified categories of peace officer personnel records shall be made available for public inspection pursuant to the California Public Records Act. These mandatory disclosure categories include: use-of-force incidents resulting in death or great bodily injury; sustained findings of excessive force; sustained findings of sexual assault on a member of the public; sustained findings of dishonesty in criminal investigation or prosecution; sustained findings of prejudice/discrimination; sustained findings of unlawful arrest or unlawful search.

## Legislative History Note

Former Gov. Code § 6254(f) was the predecessor provision containing this exemption. It was reorganized as § 7923.600 by AB 473 (Stats. 2021, Ch. 516), effective January 1, 2023. The text is substantively the same; the reorganization was structural.
