# California Public Records Act — Law Enforcement Exemption — Cross-References

| Standard | Relationship | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| PEN_832_7_PEACE_OFFICER_RECORDS | SUPERSEDED_BY (partial) | PC § 832.7(b) explicitly overrides § 7923.600 for five categories of sustained officer misconduct — disclosure is mandatory notwithstanding this exemption; § 7923.600 retains scope for records outside the § 832.7(b) categories |
| PEN_1054_CRIMINAL_DISCOVERY | CROSS_DOMAIN | § 7923.600 governs public access to LE records; Chapter 10 governs criminal case discovery — a CPRA-exempt record may still be obtainable via § 1054 informal request if the prosecution possesses it and it is material |
| PEN_832_18_BODY_CAMERAS | CROSS_REFERENCE | § 832.18(d) explicitly preserves CPRA public access rights to BWC data; § 7923.600 does not shield BWC footage of § 832.7(b)(1)(A) incidents from CPRA disclosure |
| PITCHESS_V_SUPERIOR_COURT | SUPPLEMENTS | Pitchess (Evid. Code § 1043) is the court-ordered pathway for LE records in pending cases; § 7923.600 is the CPRA exemption that covers LE records outside pending cases; the two operate in different contexts |
| CCP_2023_SPOLIATION_SANCTIONS | CROSS_DOMAIN | Wrongful withholding of CPRA-required records is not sanctionable under § 2023 (which applies to civil discovery); CPRA enforcement is via Gov. Code § 7923.000 mandate petition — different enforcement track |
