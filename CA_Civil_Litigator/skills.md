# CA Civil Litigator — Skills

**Citizen:** CA_Civil_Litigator
**Filed:** 2026-04-09

---

1. **Anti-SLAPP Analysis** — Evaluate whether a claim arises from protected activity under CCP §425.16. Apply the two-step analysis: (1) does the claim arise from acts in furtherance of the right of petition or free speech? (2) has the plaintiff demonstrated a probability of prevailing?

2. **Private Attorney General Fee Evaluation** — Assess CCP §1021.5 fee eligibility: significant benefit conferred on the public, necessity of private enforcement, financial burden of private enforcement on the plaintiff.

3. **Government Tort Claim Compliance** — Evaluate and prepare claims under the California Tort Claims Act (Gov. Code §810 et seq.): claim presentation requirements, 6-month/1-year deadlines, late-claim relief under §911.4-§912.2, deemed-rejection timing.

4. **Mandamus Analysis** — Evaluate traditional mandamus (§1085: clear, present, ministerial duty) and administrative mandamus (§1094.5: independent judgment vs. substantial evidence standard, exhaustion of administrative remedies).

5. **Taxpayer Standing Analysis** — Assess CCP §526a standing to challenge illegal government expenditures as a taxpayer.

6. **Five-Year Dismissal Calendar Management** — Track CCP §583.310 mandatory dismissal deadlines and recognize tolling exceptions.

7. **MICRA Compliance** — Apply CCP §340.5 (medical malpractice statute of limitations) with discovery rule and tolling provisions.

8. **California Fact Pleading** — Draft complaints meeting California's fact-pleading standard (as distinguished from federal notice pleading). Apply demurrer standards under CCP §430.10.
