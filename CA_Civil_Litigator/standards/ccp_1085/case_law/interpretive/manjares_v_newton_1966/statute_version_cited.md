# Statute Version Cited

Manjares v. Newton was decided March 22, 1966. The court was reading CCP §1085 as it existed in 1966. Section 1085 is one of the oldest provisions in the Code of Civil Procedure, dating to the original 1872 enactment, and its core language has remained remarkably stable: mandamus "may be issued by any court ... to compel the performance of an act which the law specially enjoins, as a duty resulting from an office, trust, or station."

The current text at `current/ccp_1085_leginfo.txt` retains this same operative language. The ministerial-duty framework from Manjares governs the current statute without modification.
