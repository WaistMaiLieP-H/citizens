# Statute Version Cited

Common Cause v. Board of Supervisors was decided August 7, 1989. The court was reading CCP §1085 as it existed in 1989. The statute's core mandate language — authorizing mandamus "to compel the performance of an act which the law specially enjoins, as a duty resulting from an office, trust, or station" — has been essentially unchanged since the 1872 original enactment.

The court was also reading the Brown Act (Gov. Code §54950 et seq.) as the source of the underlying ministerial duty. The current text at `current/ccp_1085_leginfo.txt` retains the same operative language. The holding — that "shall" in a statute creates a ministerial duty enforceable by §1085 mandamus — applies equally under the current statute.
