# Statute Version Cited

AIDS Healthcare Foundation v. LA County DPH was decided August 4, 2011. The court was reading CCP §1085 as it existed in 2011 — the same core language as the 1872 original: mandamus to compel performance of "an act which the law specially enjoins, as a duty resulting from an office, trust, or station."

The court's key interpretive move was reading "the law" broadly to include not just statutes but also the agency's own regulations. The current text at `current/ccp_1085_leginfo.txt` retains this same operative language. The holding — that agency-adopted regulations with mandatory language create ministerial duties enforceable by §1085 — governs current practice.
