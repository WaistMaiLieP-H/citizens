# Evolution Stage 02: Current

The current text of CCP 1085 is at `../../current/ccp_1085_leginfo.txt`.

The statute has changed very little since 1872. The current text reflects all amendments through 2026.

Key changes from origin:
- Subdivision (b) added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, extending mandamus jurisdiction to the appellate division of the superior court over limited civil cases and misdemeanor/infraction cases

The core provision in subdivision (a) -- a court may issue a writ to compel performance of a ministerial duty or to compel admission to a right or office -- is substantively identical to the original 1872 text. The language traces directly back to the common-law writ of mandamus.

The distinction between traditional mandamus (1085, ministerial duties) and administrative mandamus (1094.5, discretionary decisions with a hearing record) remains the fundamental dividing line in California writ practice.
