# Statute Version Cited

Baral v. Schnitt was decided August 18, 2016. The court was reading CCP §425.16 as amended through 2016. The statute had been significantly amended multiple times since its original enactment in 1992, including the 1997 amendments that broadened the scope of protected activity and the addition of §425.16(e)(4) (catch-all public interest category).

The current text at `current/ccp_425_16_leginfo.txt` reflects subsequent amendments, but the two-step framework (protected activity analysis at step one, probability of prevailing at step two) interpreted in Baral remains operative under the current text.
