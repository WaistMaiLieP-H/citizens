# Statute Version Cited

FilmOn.com v. DoubleVerify was decided May 23, 2019. The court was reading CCP §425.16 as amended through 2019, with particular focus on §425.16(e)(4) — the catch-all provision covering "any other conduct in furtherance of the exercise of the constitutional right of petition or the constitutional right of free speech in connection with a public issue or an issue of public interest."

The current text at `current/ccp_425_16_leginfo.txt` retains this same §425.16(e)(4) language. The FilmOn framework for analyzing commercial speech under the public-interest prong governs current practice.
