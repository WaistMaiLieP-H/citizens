# Statute Version Cited

Navellier v. Sletten was decided November 21, 2002. The court was reading CCP §425.16 as amended through 2002, including the significant 1997 amendments (Stats. 1997, Ch. 271) that broadened the statute's scope by redefining protected activity in §425.16(e) to include statements made in connection with issues of public interest.

The current text at `current/ccp_425_16_leginfo.txt` retains the same two-step framework and the same §425.16(e) categories that the court interpreted in Navellier. The step-one/step-two separation from this case governs current practice.
