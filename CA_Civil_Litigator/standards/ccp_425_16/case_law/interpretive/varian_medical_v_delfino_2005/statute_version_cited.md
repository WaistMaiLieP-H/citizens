# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Varian Medical Systems v. Delfino, 35 Cal.4th 180 (2005)
**Statute at issue:** CCP § 425.16 (anti-SLAPP)
**Version the court was reading:** Post-1997 amendment version (SB 1296, which broadened "public issue" and added § 425.16(e)(3)-(4) catchall provisions)
**Evolution stage:** `../../ccp_425_16/evolution/02_current/` (closest match)

The Court applied the broadened definition of protected activity under subdivisions (e)(3) and (e)(4) — conduct in connection with a public issue or an issue of public interest. The 1997 amendment was specifically enacted to overrule narrow judicial interpretations that had limited the statute's scope. Varian confirmed that the amendment achieved its purpose: internet speech about public company governance falls within the statute's protection.
