# CCP 425.16 -- Diff from Prior

## Prior State

No prior state. Before 1992, California had no procedural mechanism to dispose of meritless lawsuits filed to punish someone for exercising free speech or petitioning the government. A defendant sued for speaking at a city council meeting had to litigate the full case -- discovery, motions, trial -- even if the claim was transparently retaliatory. The cost of that defense was the plaintiff's real weapon.

## What SB 1264 Created

An entirely new section of the Code of Civil Procedure. From nothing to:

- A special motion to strike any cause of action "arising from any act of that person in furtherance of the person's right of petition or free speech under the United States or California Constitution in connection with a public issue"
- A two-step burden-shifting test: defendant shows the claim targets protected activity, then the plaintiff must demonstrate a probability of prevailing
- A 30-day hearing requirement after filing
- An automatic stay of discovery while the motion is pending
- Mandatory attorney's fees and costs to the prevailing defendant
- A reciprocal fee provision if the motion was frivolous or solely intended to cause delay

## What Did Not Exist Before

Everything. This was new law. There was no federal analogue. California was one of the first states to enact anti-SLAPP legislation. The statute created a procedural tool that had no precedent in California civil practice.
