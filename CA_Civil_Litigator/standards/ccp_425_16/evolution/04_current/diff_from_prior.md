# CCP 425.16 -- Diff from 03_amendment_2003 to Current

## Prior State (Post-2003)

Core anti-SLAPP statute fully operational with four categories of protected activity, broad construction mandate, and companion statutes addressing exemptions (425.17) and SLAPPback (425.18).

## Subsequent Refinements

Between 2003 and the current text, several amendments refined procedural mechanics without altering the statute's substantive framework:

**Filing window.** The time to file an anti-SLAPP motion was set at 60 days after service of the complaint (extendable for good cause). This replaced earlier provisions that were less specific.

**Immediate appealability.** Subdivision (i) codified that an order granting or denying an anti-SLAPP motion is directly appealable. This had been established by case law but was written into the statute to remove any ambiguity.

**Discovery stay refinements.** The automatic discovery stay during pendency of the motion was refined to allow limited discovery on noticed motion showing good cause, with the court able to permit specified discovery.

**Government enforcement clarification.** Additional provisions clarified that anti-SLAPP motions cannot be used against enforcement actions brought by public prosecutors and certain government officers.

**Judicial Council reporting.** Subdivision (j) required the Judicial Council to report on the frequency and disposition of anti-SLAPP motions.

## What Did Not Change

- The two-step test (arising from protected activity + probability of prevailing) is unchanged since 1992
- The four categories of protected activity under (e)(1)-(4) are unchanged since 1997
- The fee-shifting provisions are unchanged in structure
- The "shall be construed broadly" directive remains in subdivision (a)
- The companion statutes (425.17, 425.18) remain in their 2003 form

## Net Assessment

The anti-SLAPP statute reached its substantive maturity by 2003. Everything since has been procedural tuning. The statute today operates on the same framework that was fully assembled through the 1992-1997-2003 sequence.
