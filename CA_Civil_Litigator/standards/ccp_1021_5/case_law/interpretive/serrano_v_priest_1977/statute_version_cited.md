# Statute Version Cited

Serrano v. Priest was decided on September 22, 1977. CCP §1021.5 was enacted the same year by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1197 (S.B. 947), effective January 1, 1978. The Serrano decision did not apply §1021.5 — it applied the common-law private attorney general doctrine, which the Legislature then codified in §1021.5 in direct response to this case.

The court was exercising inherent equitable fee-shifting power. The statutory version at `current/ccp_1021_5_leginfo.txt` is the codification of the doctrine Serrano announced.
