# Statute Version Cited

Graham v. DaimlerChrysler was decided December 16, 2004. The court was reading CCP §1021.5 as it existed in 2004, which had been amended several times since original enactment in 1978 but retained the same core structure: fee-shifting for "a successful party" in litigation enforcing "an important right affecting the public interest."

The current text at `current/ccp_1021_5_leginfo.txt` reflects subsequent amendments but the "successful party" language the court interpreted in Graham remains substantively the same.
