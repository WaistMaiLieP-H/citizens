# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Los Angeles Police Protective League v. City of Los Angeles, 102 Cal.App.4th 85 (2002)
**Statute at issue:** CCP § 1021.5 (private attorney general fee provision)
**Version the court was reading:** Current version (originally enacted 1977, codifying Serrano v. Priest (1977) private attorney general doctrine)
**Evolution stage:** `../../ccp_1021_5/evolution/02_current/` (closest match)

The court applied the three-part test from Woodland Hills Residents Ass'n v. City Council (1979), which interpreted the 1977 codification: (1) significant benefit to the general public or a large class; (2) necessity and financial burden of private enforcement; (3) fees disproportionate to the litigant's individual stake.
