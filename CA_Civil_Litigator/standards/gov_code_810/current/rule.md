## Gateway Section — § 810

**(a)** Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions contained in this part govern the construction of this division.

**(b)** This division may be referred to as the Government Claims Act.

## Framework — Core Provisions

**§ 815 — Public entity liability only by statute:**
> A public entity is not liable for an injury, whether such injury arises out of an act or omission of the public entity or a public employee or any other person, except as otherwise provided by statute.

**§ 815.2 — Vicarious liability:**
> A public entity is liable for injury proximately caused by an act or omission of an employee of the public entity within the scope of his employment if the act or omission would have given rise to a cause of action against that employee.

**§ 900–915.4 — Claim presentation requirement (mandatory pre-lawsuit step):**
- A party with a claim for money or damages against a public entity must file a written claim with the entity before filing suit.
- **Personal injury / property damage:** claim must be filed within **6 months** of the accrual of the cause of action (§ 911.2(a)).
- **Other claims:** within **1 year** of accrual.
- Failure to present a timely claim bars the lawsuit — the court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction.

**§ 945.4 — No suit without claim:**
> No suit for money or damages may be brought against a public entity until a written claim therefor has been presented to the public entity and has been acted upon by the board, or has been deemed to have been rejected by the board.

**§ 912.4 — Entity must act within 45 days** (or claim is deemed rejected).

## Claim Presentation — Critical Deadlines

| Claim Type | Filing Deadline | Authority |
|------------|----------------|-----------|
| Personal injury / property damage | 6 months from accrual | Gov. Code § 911.2(a) |
| Other money claims | 1 year from accrual | Gov. Code § 911.2(b) |
| Application to file late claim | Within 1 year of accrual | Gov. Code § 911.4 |
| Suit after rejection | Within 6 months of rejection notice | Gov. Code § 945.6 |

---

## Plain English

Before you can sue the government for money, you must file a written claim with the government agency first — within 6 months for personal injury claims. The agency has 45 days to accept or reject it. If rejected, you have 6 months from the rejection notice to file suit. Skip the claim presentation step and your lawsuit is dead on arrival — no exceptions for missing the 6-month deadline unless the court grants a late-claim application.
