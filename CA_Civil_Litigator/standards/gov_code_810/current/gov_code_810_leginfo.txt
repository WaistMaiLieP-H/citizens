California Government Code § 810 — Government Claims Act (Gateway Section)

(a) Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions contained in this part govern the construction of this division.

(b) This division may be referred to as the Government Claims Act.

[Source: leginfo.legislature.ca.gov via VernenLegal MCP tool, fetched 2026-04-08]

---

NOTE ON SCOPE: §810 is the gateway section to the Government Claims Act (Gov. Code §§810-996.6). The full framework includes:

- §§810-814.2: Definitions
- §815: Public entity liability only where statute provides
- §815.2: Vicarious liability through employee acts
- §§818-825.6: Specific immunities
- §§900-915.4: Claim presentation requirement (government tort claim must be filed BEFORE lawsuit)
- §§940-949: Actions against public entities
- §§950-951: Actions against public employees
- §§995-996.6: Defense and indemnification of public employees

The statute that governs is the DIVISION (§§810-996.6). §810 is the entry point that names it and establishes that its definitions govern the entire framework.
