## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: the Government Claims Act is the threshold issue for every civil claim against a California government entity. The Act does not apply to federal constitutional claims under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 — those go directly to court without a government claim. But any companion state tort claims must comply. In mixed federal/state cases against government entities, the § 1983 claim survives regardless of the government claim deadline; the state law claims do not. Practitioners must track both independently.
