# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** State of California v. Superior Court (Bodde), 32 Cal.4th 1234 (2004)
**Statute at issue:** Government Code §§ 810, 835, 910 et seq.
**Version the court was reading:** Current version of the Government Claims Act (originally enacted 1963, replacing sovereign immunity with statutory liability framework per Muskopf)
**Evolution stage:** `../../gov_code_810/evolution/02_current/` (closest match)

The Court applied the current claim presentation requirements of §§ 910-912.4. The substantial compliance standard had evolved through decades of case law interpreting the 1963 statutory framework. Bodde clarified that "substantial compliance" has limits — the claim must at minimum identify the what, where, when, and how of the incident, even if it need not be legally precise.
