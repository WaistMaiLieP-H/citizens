## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: the five-year rule is a ticking clock from day one of filing. In cases involving government entity defendants, the Government Claims Act prerequisites consume time before filing, and discovery disputes can consume additional years — making the five-year window genuinely tight in complex cases. Any case involving a government entity defendant should be calendared with both the Government Claims Act deadline and the five-year trial deadline tracked separately.
