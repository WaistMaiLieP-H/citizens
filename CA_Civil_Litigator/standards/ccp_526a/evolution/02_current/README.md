# Evolution Stage 02: Current

The current text of CCP 526a is at `../../current/ccp_526a_leginfo.txt`.

The statute has been substantially modernized since its 1909 enactment, most recently by Stats. 2021, Ch. 224 (A.B. 1578), effective January 1, 2022. The current text reflects all amendments through 2026.

Key changes from origin:
- The 2021 rewrite reorganized the statute into subdivisions (a) through (d)
- "Resident" was defined broadly: lives, works, owns property, or attends school in the jurisdiction
- The qualifying taxes were enumerated: income, sales/use, property (including tenant-paid), business license
- "Local agency" was defined to include cities, counties, districts, public authorities, and any other political subdivision
- Special calendar precedence was added for actions to enjoin public improvement projects

The core promise -- taxpayers can sue to stop illegal expenditure, waste, or injury to public property -- has remained unchanged since 1909.
