# Statute Version Cited

Blair v. Pitchess was decided July 1, 1971. The court was reading CCP §526a as it existed in 1971, which authorized taxpayer suits to restrain "any illegal expenditure of, waste of, or injury to, the estate, funds, or other property" of local government entities.

The key phrase the court interpreted — "injury to ... other property" — appears in the current text at `current/ccp_526a_leginfo.txt`. The court's expansion of "property" to include intangible public interests remains the governing interpretation of that phrase.
