# Statute Version Cited

Coshow v. City of Escondido was decided in 2005. The court was reading CCP §526a as it existed at that time, which enumerated specific local government entity types (county, town, city, city and county, and other political subdivisions) against which taxpayer suits could be brought.

The current text at `current/ccp_526a_leginfo.txt` retains this same enumeration of local government entities. The Coshow holding — that §526a standing does not extend to state government — remains operative because the statute's text still does not include the state.
