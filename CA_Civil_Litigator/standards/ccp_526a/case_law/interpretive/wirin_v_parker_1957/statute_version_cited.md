# Statute Version Cited

Wirin v. Parker was decided December 20, 1957. The court was reading CCP §526a as it existed in the mid-1950s. Section 526a was originally enacted in 1909 (Stats. 1909, Ch. 348) and had been amended periodically. The 1957 version authorized taxpayer suits to restrain "any illegal expenditure of, waste of, or injury to, the estate, funds, or other property of a county, town, city or city and county."

The current text at `current/ccp_526a_leginfo.txt` has been amended since 1957, including updates to cover additional categories of local government entities, but the core principle from Wirin — that illegal conduct is itself waste — remains operative under the current statute.
