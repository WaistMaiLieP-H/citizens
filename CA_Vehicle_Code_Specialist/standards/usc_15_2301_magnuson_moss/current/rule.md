# Rule — 15 U.S.C. § 2301 (Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act)

**Statute:** 15 U.S.C. §§ 2301–2312 (Magnuson-Moss Warranty — Federal Trade Commission Improvement Act of 1975). Implemented at 16 C.F.R. Parts 700–703.

**Core rule:**
1. **§ 2302 — Full disclosure of warranty terms.** A supplier who offers a written warranty on a consumer product costing more than $15 must fully and conspicuously disclose the terms in simple and readily understood language, and must designate the warranty as either **"full"** or **"limited"** on the face of the document.
2. **§ 2308 — Implied warranty may not be disclaimed.** If a supplier offers ANY written warranty (full or limited) on a consumer product, the supplier **may not** disclaim or modify any implied warranty (merchantability, fitness). A limited warrantor may limit the **duration** of implied warranties to the duration of the written warranty, if reasonable.
3. **§ 2304 — Federal minimum standards for "full" warranties.** Remedy within reasonable time at no charge; if not fixed after reasonable attempts, buyer's choice of refund or replacement; no limitation on consequential damages unless conspicuously noted.
4. **§ 2310(d) — Private right of action.** A consumer damaged by breach of warranty or supplier obligation may sue in state court (any amount) or federal district court (if amount in controversy > $50,000, or class action with 100+ plaintiffs and aggregate > $50,000).
5. **§ 2310(d)(2) — Attorney's fees.** Prevailing consumer may recover reasonable costs and attorney's fees based on actual time expended.
6. **§ 2310(a)(3) — Informal dispute resolution.** Supplier may require participation in an IDSM (Informal Dispute Settlement Mechanism) compliant with 16 CFR Part 703 **before** suit, but IDSM outcomes are NOT binding on the consumer.

**Application to motor vehicles:** 15 USC §2301 applies to any consumer product, defined at § 2301(1) as "any tangible personal property which is distributed in commerce and which is normally used for personal, family, or household purposes." Motor vehicles sold to consumers qualify. Magnuson-Moss is the federal parallel to Song-Beverly (Civ §1793.2) and coexists with state lemon law.

**Relation to state law:** § 2311 — nothing in Magnuson-Moss supersedes state warranty law; consumer may plead federal and state warranty claims in the same action. Song-Beverly's 2x civil penalty is **additive to** Magnuson-Moss remedies.
