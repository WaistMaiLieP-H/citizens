# Rule — VEH § 11700 Dealer Licensing Requirement

**Standard ID:** veh_11700_dealer_licensing
**Authority:** Cal. Veh. Code § 11700

## Core Rule

No person may act as a vehicle dealer, remanufacturer, manufacturer, or transporter without a valid DMV-issued dealer license (VEH §11701) or temporary permit.

**Acting without a license = unlicensed dealer activity** — a criminal violation subject to fines and DMV administrative action.

## Post-License-Termination Obligations

When a dealer's license is canceled, suspended, revoked, or expired:

| Vehicle Type | Required Action |
|---|---|
| Dealer-owned inventory | May ONLY be sold WHOLESALE to a licensed dealer; must provide written statement that seller is not licensed |
| Consigned vehicles | Must be RETURNED to consignor |
| Vehicles in possession but not owned or consigned | Must be RETURNED to owner |

## Application — RedJag Transaction

If the RedJag dealer or any associated entity:
- Was operating without a valid DMV dealer license at the time of the Jaguar XE transaction
- Had its license under suspension or investigation at the time of the sale
- Used a licensed dealer's name/number to facilitate an unlicensed transaction

...then the sale was conducted in violation of §11700, the contract may be voidable, and DMV enforcement and criminal referral are available.

**DMV dealer license verification:** DMV maintains a public dealer license lookup at dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/occupational-licensing/. License status, any suspension history, and enforcement actions are public record.

## Relationship to §11713

§11700 establishes the licensing requirement (the threshold). §11713 governs the prohibited acts of licensed dealers. A dealer operating under a license can violate §11713. A dealer operating without a license violates §11700 per se regardless of how the transaction was conducted.
