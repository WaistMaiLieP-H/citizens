# Rule — Cal. Civ. Code § 2982 (Rees-Levering Motor Vehicle Sales & Finance Act)

**Statute:** Cal. Civ. Code §§ 2981–2984.6 (Rees-Levering Automobile Sales Finance Act). Core disclosure and content rule at § 2982.

**Core rule — § 2982(a):** Every conditional sale contract for a motor vehicle sold to a retail buyer on deferred payment terms **shall** contain, in a single document signed by the buyer, itemized disclosures including:
- cash price of the vehicle;
- amount of any trade-in and its evaluation;
- documentary preparation fee (capped by statute);
- smog abatement fee, tire fee, DMV fees;
- cash down payment;
- unpaid balance of cash price;
- amounts for insurance, GAP waiver, service contracts (each itemized and **optional** — not required as a condition of sale);
- total finance charge, APR, total amount financed, total of payments, payment schedule.

**§ 2982.5 — right to rescind on financing failure ("yo-yo" rule):** If the conditional sale contract is conditioned on subsequent third-party assignment and the assignment is not consummated, the dealer **must** return the buyer's trade-in and down payment within a statutory window and rescind the contract. The dealer may **not** demand that the buyer return on new terms, keep the trade-in, keep down payment, or treat the buyer as being in default.

**§ 2983 — remedy for violation:** The buyer may **rescind** the contract, recover all payments made, recover the value of the trade-in, and is discharged from all further obligations under the contract. § 2983.4 awards attorney's fees to the prevailing party (two-way fee-shifting).

**§ 2983.1 — election of remedies:** Buyer may elect rescission and restitution, OR actual damages, OR both where consistent.

**§ 2984.4 — civil penalty:** A willful violation may result in additional civil penalties.

**Scope:** Applies to conditional sale contracts for motor vehicles sold to retail buyers in California for personal/family/household use. Covers new and used vehicles. Does NOT cover commercial/fleet sales, leases (those fall under separate Vehicle Leasing Act, Civ §§ 2985.7-2985.9), or cash sales.
