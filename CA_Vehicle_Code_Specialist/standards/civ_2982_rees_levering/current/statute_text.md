# Statute Text — Rees-Levering Key Provisions

**Source:** Cal. Civ. Code §§ 2981–2984.6 — California Legislative Information. Pull current leginfo HTML for verification before filing.

## § 2981 — Definitions

Defines "conditional sale contract," "motor vehicle," "buyer," "seller," "holder." A conditional sale contract includes any contract in which the buyer agrees to pay the price at some future time and title or right of repossession is retained by the seller or assigned to a financing party.

## § 2982(a) — Itemized disclosure requirement

> "Every conditional sale contract … shall contain … the following items: (1) The cash price of the motor vehicle … (2) The amount of the buyer's downpayment … (3) The difference between (1) and (2) … (4) The cost to the buyer of any insurance … (5) The amount charged for a … service contract … (6) The total cash price … (7) The amount of the finance charge … (8) The annual percentage rate …" [abridged — pull verbatim before filing].

Each line item must appear separately. Add-ons must be marked as optional and separately signed.

## § 2982.5 — Contracts subject to assignment approval ("yo-yo")

> "If the seller's performance under a conditional sale contract is conditioned upon a subsequent assignment of the contract, and the seller is unable to obtain the assignment on the terms agreed upon, … the seller shall, within a reasonable time, return to the buyer the downpayment, trade-in vehicle, and any other consideration received from the buyer, and shall rescind the contract and cancel any security interest …"

Dealer **may not**: (a) charge the buyer for use during the spot-delivery period, (b) retain any portion of down payment or trade-in, (c) demand new terms as condition of returning trade-in, (d) report the transaction to credit bureaus as a defaulted obligation.

## § 2983 — Violation = buyer's right to rescind

> "If the seller … violates any provision of Section 2982 … the contract shall not be enforceable … and the buyer shall have the right to rescind the contract, or to assert [the violation] as a defense …"

Recovery: all payments made, value of trade-in, costs of rescission, damages.

## § 2983.4 — Attorney's fees

> "Reasonable attorney's fees and costs shall be awarded to the prevailing party in any action on a contract or purchase order subject to the provisions of this chapter …"

Two-way fee-shifting (contrast Song-Beverly one-way).

## § 2984.4 — Civil penalty for willful violation

Permits civil penalty on top of rescission and damages when violation is willful.
