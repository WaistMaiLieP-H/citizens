# Rule — VEH § 11713 Dealer Prohibited Acts

**Standard ID:** veh_11713_dealer_prohibited_acts
**Authority:** Cal. Veh. Code § 11713

## Core Rule

A licensed dealer (VEH §11700) is prohibited from committing any of the enumerated acts in §11713. Each violation is:
- A ground for DMV license suspension/revocation under VEH §11705
- Potentially an unfair business practice under UCL §17200 (unlawful prong)
- Potentially a CLRA violation if vehicle is "goods" in a consumer transaction

## High-Priority Provisions for RedJag Case

| Provision | Prohibited Act | RedJag Application |

| §11713(a) | Untrue or misleading statements in any communication | Wrong CARFAX provided = misrepresentation of vehicle history |
| §11713(d) | Representing used vehicle as new | Stolen/stripped Jaguar — if represented as different vehicle than delivered |
| §11713(i) | Delivering vehicle not meeting equipment requirements | Stripped vehicle may not meet Division 12 equipment requirements |
| §11713(l)(1) | Participating in sale without making full tax payment to DMV | Tax payment fraud concurrent with title fraud |
| §11713(m) | Permitting another person to use dealer's license | If the transaction was run through a nominal licensee by an unlicensed operator |
| §11713(u) | **Advertising prior use/ownership history inaccurately** | **CARFAX misrepresentation = §11713(u) direct violation** |

## CARFAX / Vehicle History Fraud (§11713(a) and §11713(u))

Providing a CARFAX report for the wrong vehicle, or a CARFAX that does not reflect the actual history of the vehicle delivered, is:
- **§11713(a):** A misleading statement in connection with the sale that the dealer knew or should have known was misleading
- **§11713(u):** Advertising the prior use or ownership history inaccurately — a specific enumerated violation

A "wrong CARFAX" is not a paperwork error. It is a representation about a material fact — the vehicle's history of accidents, prior owners, theft reports, and service records — that determines the vehicle's fair market value. Providing an inaccurate CARFAX with intent to obtain a better price from the buyer is fraudulent misrepresentation.

## Stolen / Stripped Vehicle (§11713(i))

Delivering a vehicle that does not meet Division 12 (Equipment Requirements) standards is a §11713(i) violation. A vehicle that has been stripped of components would fail equipment inspections. Delivery of such a vehicle:
- Violates §11713(i) per se
- Creates product liability and warranty claims
- May violate VEH §10751 if component VINs were altered (separate standard)
