# Rule — Cal. Civ. Code § 1793.2 (Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act / "Lemon Law")

**Statute:** Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1790–1795.8 (Song-Beverly Consumer Warranty Act); operative "lemon law" presumption at § 1793.2(d) and § 1793.22.

**Core rule:** If a manufacturer or its representative in this state fails to service or repair a new motor vehicle sold with an express warranty to conform to that warranty after a reasonable number of attempts, the manufacturer shall either (1) promptly replace the vehicle or (2) promptly make restitution to the buyer — at the buyer's election — in an amount equal to the actual price paid (including collateral charges and incidental damages), less a reasonable mileage offset attributable to use prior to the first repair attempt (§ 1793.2(d)(2)).

**Presumption (§ 1793.22(b)):** A "reasonable number of attempts" is presumed if, within 18 months from delivery or 18,000 miles (whichever first occurs):
- The same nonconformity results in a condition likely to cause death or serious bodily injury and has been subject to repair 2+ times, OR
- The same nonconformity has been subject to repair 4+ times, OR
- The vehicle is out of service for 30+ cumulative calendar days.

**Damages (§ 1794):** Buyer may recover actual damages, consequential damages, and — where the failure to comply was **willful** — a **civil penalty of up to two (2) times the amount of actual damages**. Prevailing buyer recovers attorney's fees and costs (§ 1794(d)).

**Scope:** Applies to consumer goods sold at retail in California, including new and (per § 1795.5) used motor vehicles sold with a manufacturer's or dealer's express warranty. Dealers who sell used vehicles with written warranties become the equivalent of manufacturers under § 1795.5 for purposes of Song-Beverly obligations.

**Key definitional anchors:**
- "Consumer" — buyer/lessee for personal/family/household use (§ 1791(a))
- "Manufacturer" — entity that manufactures, assembles, or produces consumer goods (§ 1791(j))
- "Nonconformity" — failure to conform to express warranty that **substantially impairs** use, value, or safety (§ 1793.22(e)(1))
