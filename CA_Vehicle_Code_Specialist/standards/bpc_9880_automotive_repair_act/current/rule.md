# Rule — BPC §§ 9884.7 / 9884.9 Automotive Repair Act

**Standard ID:** bpc_9880_automotive_repair_act
**Authority:** Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 9884.7, 9884.9 (Automotive Repair Act, BPC §9880 et seq.)
**Regulator:** Bureau of Automotive Repair (BAR), California Department of Consumer Affairs

## Core Rule

Any automotive repair dealer operating in California must be registered with BAR (BPC §9884) and is subject to discipline under §9884.7 for enumerated prohibited acts. Additionally, every repair job requires a written estimate before any work begins (§9884.9).

## §9884.7 — Grounds for Discipline (Key Violations)

| Ground | Description |
|---|---|
| §9884.7(a)(1) | Untrue or misleading statements — objective knowledge standard |
| §9884.7(a)(2) | Work order does not state requested repairs OR omits odometer reading |
| §9884.7(a)(3) | Failure to give customer copy of signed document immediately |
| §9884.7(a)(4) | Any conduct constituting fraud (broadly defined in §9884.7(e)) |
| §9884.7(a)(7) | Willful departure from accepted trade standards — prejudicial to customer |
| §9884.7(a)(8) | False promises to induce repair authorization |
| §9884.7(a)(9) | Unauthorized third-party repair |

## Expanded Fraud Definition (§9884.7(e))

"Fraud" under the Automotive Repair Act is broader than common law fraud. It includes:
- Constructive fraud (CIV §1573) — no intent required; breach of duty that deceives without guilty knowledge
- Gross negligence misrepresentation of material fact
- Any promise not made honestly and in good faith
- Intentional failure to disclose a material fact
- Any act in violation of Pen. §484 (theft)

This broad definition means a repair dealer who negligently (not just intentionally) misrepresents the condition of a vehicle or the repairs needed commits "fraud" under the Automotive Repair Act.

## §9884.9 — Written Estimate Requirement

**Before any work begins:**
- Written estimate of labor and parts must be given to customer
- Customer must authorize before work commences
- No charges may exceed estimate without affirmative customer consent (oral or written) after informing customer of insufficiency

**For body/collision repairs (§9884.9(c)):**
- Itemized written estimate required
- Each part labeled: new / used / rebuilt / reconditioned
- Each crash part labeled: OEM or aftermarket

**Odometer at intake:** Work orders must record odometer reading at time vehicle is received for repair (§9884.7(a)(2)).

## Application — RedJag

If the RedJag dealer or any affiliated service entity performed work on the Jaguar XE (preparation, reconditioning, VIN-related work) without:
- Providing written estimates for any such work charged to the buyer
- Disclosing that the vehicle had been stripped and components replaced
- Identifying replaced parts as used/rebuilt/reconditioned rather than OEM

...then each failure is a separate §9884.7 violation, reportable to BAR for license discipline. The failure to identify non-OEM/stripped parts in a written estimate is also actionable as fraud under §9884.7(a)(4)/(e)(4) (intentional failure to disclose material fact).
