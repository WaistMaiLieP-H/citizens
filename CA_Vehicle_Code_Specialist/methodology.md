# CA Vehicle Code Specialist — Professional Methodology

**Citizen:** CA_Vehicle_Code_Specialist
**Filed:** 2026-04-09
**Purpose:** The document is the instruction. When vehicle purchase documents, financing agreements, or registration records enter, this methodology fires.

---

## Trigger

Purchase agreements, financing contracts, CARFAX reports, registration documents, title records, debt collection notices, or vehicle-related correspondence enter a tethered folder.

---

## Phase 1: INTAKE

1. Catalogs every document: purchase agreement, financing application, CARFAX, registration, title, insurance, debt collection notice
2. Reconstructs the transaction timeline: test drive → purchase → financing → delivery → callback (if yo-yo) → resolution
3. Identifies all parties: buyer, dealer, finance company, insurer, debt collector
4. Verifies CARFAX against actual vehicle: VIN match, history match, odometer match
5. Flags: yo-yo callback, CARFAX discrepancy, financing term changes, missing title, unauthorized charges

**Gate deliverable: INTAKE MEMO**

---

## Phase 2: LEGAL ANALYSIS

1. Applies Automobile Sales Finance Act (Civ. Code §2982): was the contract properly formed? Were all required disclosures made? Was the financing final or conditional?
2. If yo-yo: did the dealer offer full unwind (return down payment + trade-in)? Or did the dealer pressure for worse terms? (Dagher v. Ford Motor)
3. Checks CARFAX integrity: does the report match the VIN on the purchase agreement? Does it match the actual vehicle history?
4. Evaluates debt collection validity: is the underlying obligation enforceable? Was the transaction fraudulent? FDCPA/Rosenthal Act compliance?
5. Cross-references with Records Authentication: are the purchase documents authentic and complete?
6. Cross-references with Conservator Investigator: did a conservator authorize the purchase or the debt?

**Gate deliverable: FINDINGS REPORT**

---

## Phase 3: SYNTHESIS & WORK PRODUCT

1. Produces transaction audit: complete timeline with every dollar and document accounted for
2. Identifies claims: ASFA violation, fraud, CLRA, UCL (BPC §17200), FDCPA/Rosenthal Act
3. Produces draft complaint skeleton
4. Evaluates debt defense: if collection is based on fraudulent transaction, produces debt dispute letter and affirmative defense framework

**Gate deliverable: CASE ASSESSMENT**
