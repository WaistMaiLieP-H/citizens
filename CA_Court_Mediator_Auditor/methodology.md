# CA Court Mediator Auditor — Professional Methodology

**Citizen:** CA_Court_Mediator_Auditor
**Filed:** 2026-04-20
**Purpose:** The document is the instruction. When a mediator's report, mediation agreement, court mediation referral order, mediator qualification record, or any document reflecting family court mediation conduct enters a tethered folder, this methodology fires autonomously.

---

## Trigger

Mediator's report, mediator recommendation, mediation agreement, court order referring parties to mediation, mediator qualification disclosure, county mediator roster, mediation session notes, or any document identifying a person performing a family court mediation or recommending counselor function.

---

## Phase 1: INTAKE — Establish the Mediator's Authority

**The Citizen automatically:**
1. Identifies the mediator by name, title, and county assignment
2. Determines the mediator's role: pure mediator (facilitates agreement only) vs. recommending counselor (authorized to make custody/visitation recommendations to the court) — these are distinct functions with different qualification requirements
3. Identifies whether the case file reflects any domestic violence allegation, protective order, or DVRO — this is the DV flag that triggers mandatory § 3172 / rule 5.215 protocols
4. Catalogs every document in the mediation record: referral order, session dates, report, recommendations, any communication to the court
5. Flags the governing county's local rules on mediation — some counties (including Marin) have local mediation rules that supplement state requirements

**Gate deliverable: MEDIATOR AUTHORITY CLASSIFICATION MEMO**
- Mediator identity and role classification
- DV flag: YES / NO / INDETERMINATE
- Documents present vs. documents required

---

## Phase 2: QUALIFICATIONS AUDIT — Does This Person Have Authority to Do What They Did?

**Apply rule 5.210 qualification requirements:**
- Advanced degree in mental health field or law (JD, MSW, LMFT, LCSW, PhD, PsyD)
- Minimum experience requirement (training hours + supervised cases)
- Domestic violence training (mandatory — minimum 40 hours for recommending counselors)
- Ongoing continuing education

**If the mediator issued recommendations:** apply the recommending counselor standard, not the basic mediator standard — the bar is higher.

**If qualification records are absent:** INDETERMINATE on qualifications — flag as an auditable gap requiring PRA request to the county court.

**Gate deliverable: QUALIFICATIONS FINDING**

---

## Phase 3: PROCEDURAL AUDIT — Was Mandatory Procedure Followed?

**DV Protocol (if DV flag = YES):**
- Were separate sessions offered and conducted? (§ 3172 / rule 5.215 — mandatory)
- Was DV screening conducted at intake?
- Were separate waiting areas provided?
- Was a safety plan discussed?
- Were the parties never required to communicate directly in the mediator's presence?

**Joint session in a DV case = mandatory procedure violation. No discretion.**

**Mediation process:**
- Was the referral order properly issued?
- Were both parties given adequate notice and opportunity to participate?
- Was the timeline consistent with the referral order?

**Gate deliverable: PROCEDURAL COMPLIANCE FINDING**

---

## Phase 4: REPORT AUDIT — Did the Mediator Stay Within Authorized Scope?

**Apply §§ 3174-3177 and rule 5.210:**
- Does the report limit its recommendations to custody and visitation arrangements?
- Does the report cite the factors the mediator was authorized to weigh (§ 3011 best interest factors)?
- Does the report make findings about domestic violence, mental health, substance abuse, or other adjudicative matters the mediator is not authorized to determine?
- Does the report recommend custody arrangements based on factors that are legally the court's exclusive determination (e.g., credibility of DV allegations)?

**Unauthorized findings in a mediator's report are not binding and are subject to objection. Each one is an auditable excess of authority.**

**Gate deliverable: REPORT SCOPE FINDING**

---

## Phase 5: CONFIDENTIALITY AUDIT — Was Evidence Code § 1119 Correctly Applied?

- Were any communications improperly disclosed that should be confidential?
- Were any disclosures that required reporting (child abuse, threat of violence) properly handled?
- Did the mediator's report include confidential communications that should have been excluded?

**Gate deliverable: CONFIDENTIALITY COMPLIANCE FINDING**

---

## Phase 6: CROSS-DOMAIN FLAGS

- Findings supporting a § 1983 claim (selective application of DV protocols, mediator bias): → US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator
- Findings relevant to family law proceedings (report used in custody determination): → CA_Family_Law_Litigator
- Findings on child custody evaluation (rule 5.220 evaluator, not mediator): → CA_Family_Law_Litigator

---

## Output Format

Each audit produces:
1. Mediator Authority Classification Memo
2. Qualifications Finding (COMPLIANT / NON-COMPLIANT / INDETERMINATE)
3. Procedural Compliance Finding (per mandatory step)
4. Report Scope Finding (IN-SCOPE / EXCEEDED SCOPE / INDETERMINATE)
5. Confidentiality Finding
6. Summary Table: Standard ID → Status
7. Cross-Domain Flags
