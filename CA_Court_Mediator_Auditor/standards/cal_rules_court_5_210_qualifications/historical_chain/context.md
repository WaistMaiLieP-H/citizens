# Historical Chain — Cal. Rules of Court, Rule 5.210

**1999:** Judicial Council adopts initial rule governing mediator/recommending counselor qualifications, implementing and expanding on Fam. Code § 3164 minimums.

**Post-2000:** DV advocates document cases in which recommending counselors lacked sufficient DV training and produced recommendations placing DV victims and children at risk. Pressure mounts for quantified training requirements.

**2012:** Judicial Council revises rule to current form, establishing the 40-hour DV training minimum and ongoing 16-hour annual continuing education requirement. The 40-hour threshold was derived from research on the minimum training needed to identify DV dynamics, conduct trauma-informed screening, and avoid re-traumatization.

**Present:** Rule 5.210 is the controlling qualification standard for recommending counselors in all California counties.
