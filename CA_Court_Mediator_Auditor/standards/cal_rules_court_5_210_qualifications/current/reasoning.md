# Reasoning — Cal. Rules of Court, Rule 5.210 (Recommending Counselor Qualifications)

**Standard ID:** CAL_RULES_COURT_5_210_QUALIFICATIONS
**Layer:** 2 — Reasoning
**Status:** ACTIVE
**Filed:** 2026-04-20

---

## Why the 40-Hour DV Training Requirement Is the Key Audit Metric

Rule 5.210(d) sets a specific, quantifiable threshold: 40 hours of DV training. This number is not aspirational — it was chosen by the Judicial Council as the minimum required to ensure a recommending counselor can identify DV dynamics, conduct proper screening, apply § 3172 separate session protocols, and avoid recommendations that place DV victims and children at risk.

A counselor who has completed fewer than 40 hours of DV training, or whose training records cannot be verified, does not meet rule 5.210(d). In a case where DV is alleged — which is the majority of high-conflict custody cases — an under-trained recommending counselor is not a technical violation. It is a structural failure that compromises the integrity of every finding in their report.

---

## Why Qualifications Must Be Verified Before Appointment

Rule 5.210 imposes the verification obligation on the court, not on the parties. The county court is responsible for confirming that every recommending counselor on its roster meets the minimum standards. Parties are not in a position to independently audit their mediator's credentials before the session — they receive whoever the court assigns.

This means the qualification audit happens after the fact, through PRA. The court's qualification records for the assigned mediator are public records. If those records show the mediator did not meet rule 5.210 at the time of appointment, the appointment was improper and the report was issued without proper authority.

---

## Why Non-Compliant Appointments Produce Ultra Vires Reports

A recommending counselor who does not meet rule 5.210 qualifications is not a recommending counselor within the meaning of the rule. Their report is not a rule 5.210 report — it is a document produced by someone without the credentials to produce it. The court is not obligated to credit such a report. A party who discovers the qualifications deficiency after the report is issued can move to strike the report and request a qualified replacement.

---

## Application in Practice

PRA request to the county court: all qualification documentation for the recommending counselor assigned to the case. Verify: degree, field of study, years of experience, DV training hours and dates, continuing education records. Any gap is an auditable finding. A gap in DV training in a DV-affected case is the most significant finding.
