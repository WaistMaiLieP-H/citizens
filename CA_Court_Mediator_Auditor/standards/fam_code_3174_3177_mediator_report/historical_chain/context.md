# Historical Chain — Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3174–3177

**1993:** Original codification. California counties divided into two models: non-recommending (mediator facilitates only, no report to court) and recommending (mediator submits custody recommendation when parties don't agree).

**Recommending model controversy:** Critics argue the recommending model gives mediators quasi-judicial power without the procedural protections of a trial. Supporters argue it provides courts with expert input in contested cases. Legislature retains both models but imposes scope limits on recommending mediators via §§ 3174-3175.

**2000s:** Litigation establishes that mediator reports exceeding § 3175 scope are challengeable. Courts develop procedure for parties to object to specific findings in mediator reports before a hearing.

**2010 (Stats. Ch. 352):** Amendment clarifies report content requirements and strengthens the § 3177 limitation on mediator testimony.

**Present:** Recommending counties (including Marin County) must comply with §§ 3174-3177 for every contested custody case where parties do not reach full agreement.
