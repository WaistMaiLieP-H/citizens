# Mediator Report, Recommendations, and Witness Limitations — Rule

**Authority:** Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3174–3177
**Jurisdiction:** California
**Effective:** Stats. 1993, Ch. 219; amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 352

## The Rule

**§ 3174 — Counties with Recommending Mediators:** In counties that use recommending mediators (as opposed to pure facilitative mediators), if the parties do not reach full agreement, the mediator SHALL prepare a written report setting forth a recommended parenting plan and submit it to the court and both parties.

**§ 3175 — Contents of Mediator's Report:**
The report SHALL include:
(a) A description of the mediation process, including who was interviewed and the number of sessions
(b) A summary of the issues in dispute
(c) A description of the proposed parenting plan
(d) A statement of the reasons for the recommendations
(e) Any other information the mediator deems relevant to the best interest of the child

The report SHALL NOT include:
- Confidential mediation communications (Evidence Code § 1119)
- Adjudicative findings on disputed facts (e.g., whether abuse occurred, credibility determinations)
- Opinions on matters outside the mediator's professional competence
- Recommendations on legal issues that are the court's exclusive determination

**§ 3176 — Distribution of Report:** The mediator's report shall be provided to both parties and their counsel at least 10 days before any hearing at which it will be considered, unless the court orders otherwise for good cause.

**§ 3177 — Mediator as Witness:**
(a) A mediator may not be called as a witness in any civil proceeding to testify about any communication made during the mediation proceeding that is a confidential communication under Evidence Code § 1119.
(b) In a recommending county, the mediator may be called to testify about the contents of their written report.
(c) The mediator may not be compelled to offer opinions or conclusions beyond what is contained in the written report.

## Key Elements

1. **Report is mandatory when parties don't reach full agreement** (in recommending counties) — failure to produce a report is a § 3174 violation.
2. **Report content is bounded** — adjudicative findings, credibility determinations, and legal conclusions are outside scope; their inclusion exceeds the mediator's authority.
3. **10-day notice rule** — parties must receive the report at least 10 days before the hearing where it will be used; a report sprung at hearing violates § 3176.
4. **Mediator testimony is limited to the report** — the mediator cannot testify to confidential communications or offer opinions beyond the written report.
5. **Exceeded scope = exceeded authority** — a recommendation the mediator was not authorized to make is not a recommendation the court must credit.
