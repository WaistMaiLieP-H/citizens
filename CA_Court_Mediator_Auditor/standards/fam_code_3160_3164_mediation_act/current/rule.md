# California Family Court Mediation — Foundation and Qualifications — Rule

**Authority:** Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3160–3164
**Jurisdiction:** California
**Effective:** Stats. 1993, Ch. 219 (original codification); amendments through 2006

## The Rule

**§ 3160 — Legislative Purpose:** The Legislature finds and declares that it is the public policy of California to prioritize the health, safety, welfare, and best interest of children in custody and visitation disputes. Mediation proceedings are intended to reduce acrimony, develop parenting plans, and reach agreements protecting children's best interests. Mediation is not a mechanism for assigning fault or making adjudicative findings.

**§ 3161 — Scope:** Mediation proceedings under this chapter apply to all contested custody or visitation proceedings.

**§ 3162 — Mediation Orientation:** The court may provide orientation sessions to parties before mediation. Orientation must include information about the mediation process, domestic violence resources, and parties' rights.

**§ 3164 — Mediator Qualifications (mandatory):** A mediator appointed by the court under this chapter shall have the following minimum qualifications:

1. A master's degree or doctoral degree in psychology, social work, marriage and family therapy, or a related behavioral science field; OR a juris doctor degree; AND
2. At least two years of experience in counseling or psychotherapy; AND
3. Knowledge of the court system and family law; AND
4. Knowledge of child development issues, child abuse, domestic violence, and substance abuse.

**§ 3164(b):** Persons who do not meet the minimum qualifications may not be appointed as mediators by the court. A mediator who does not meet these qualifications has no authority to conduct court-ordered mediation or issue any report to the court.

**§ 3164(c):** The Judicial Council shall establish standards for the training and continuing education of mediators.

## Key Elements

1. **Purpose is agreement, not adjudication** — mediation exists to help parties reach parenting plans, not to make findings of fact about their conduct or character.
2. **Qualifications are mandatory prerequisites** — not aspirational guidelines; an unqualified mediator's report is ultra vires.
3. **Best interest of the child is the governing standard** — every mediator action is measured against this benchmark.
4. **DV knowledge is a required qualification** — a mediator who lacks domestic violence training does not meet § 3164 minimum requirements.
