# Historical Chain — Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3160–3164

**1980s:** DV advocates and family law researchers document that custody litigation systematically re-traumatizes DV survivors and children. Traditional adversarial hearings produce winners and losers but rarely workable parenting plans.

**1981:** California begins experimenting with mandatory custody mediation in pilot counties. Evidence shows reduced litigation costs and greater compliance with mediated agreements vs. court-ordered arrangements.

**1993 (Stats. Ch. 219):** Legislature codifies the Mediation of Custody and Visitation Disputes Act, §§ 3160-3186. Section 3164 establishes minimum mediator qualifications for the first time statewide. The requirement for DV knowledge as a mandatory qualification reflects sustained advocacy by DV organizations who documented mediator failures in high-risk cases.

**2000s:** Judicial Council implements Standards of Judicial Administration § 30 providing more detailed qualification verification procedures.

**2012:** Rules of Court rule 5.210 (recommending counselor qualifications) supersedes and expands on § 3164 minimums for the recommending counselor function.
