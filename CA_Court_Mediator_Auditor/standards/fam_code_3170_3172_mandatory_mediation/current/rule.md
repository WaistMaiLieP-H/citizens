# Mandatory Family Court Mediation and Domestic Violence Protocols — Rule

**Authority:** Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3170–3172
**Jurisdiction:** California
**Effective:** §§ 3170-3171: Stats. 1993; § 3172 DV protocols: Stats. 1996, Ch. 761

## The Rule

**§ 3170 — Mandatory Mediation:** In any proceeding where custody or visitation is contested, the court shall set the matter for mediation before or concurrent with any contested hearing. Mediation is not optional — it is a prerequisite to a contested custody hearing.

**§ 3171 — Mediation Procedure:**
(a) The mediator shall meet with the parties and, where appropriate, with the minor children.
(b) If the parties reach agreement, the mediator shall prepare a written agreement and submit it to the court for approval.
(c) If the parties do not reach agreement, the mediator shall so report to the court (in recommending counties — see § 3174).

**§ 3172 — Domestic Violence — Separate Sessions (MANDATORY):**

(a) In any case in which there has been a history of domestic violence between the parties, or in which a protective order is in effect, the mediator SHALL make reasonable efforts to meet with the parties separately and at different times. **This is not discretionary.**

(b) The mediator shall inform the parties before the commencement of mediation of the manner in which the mediation process works, that the mediator is a mandatory reporter of child abuse, and that communications in mediation are confidential except as provided in Evidence Code § 1119.

(c) If domestic violence is alleged or evident, the mediator must:
- Conduct screening for DV at intake
- Offer and conduct separate sessions
- Provide information about DV services and resources
- Not require the parties to communicate directly with each other in the mediator's presence

**Joint sessions in a domestic violence case = mandatory procedure violation.** The word "shall" in § 3172(a) removes all mediator discretion.

## Key Elements

1. **Mediation is mandatory before contested custody hearings** — but the manner must comply with §§ 3171-3172.
2. **§ 3172 separate sessions are mandatory in DV cases** — "shall make reasonable efforts" means the mediator must offer and conduct separate sessions; conducting joint sessions after DV is disclosed or alleged is a per se violation.
3. **Mediator is a mandatory child abuse reporter** — disclosure obligations exist regardless of mediation confidentiality.
4. **No direct communication requirement in DV cases** — the mediator cannot require parties to face each other or communicate directly when DV is a factor.
