# Historical Chain — Cal. Fam. Code §§ 3170–3172

**Pre-1981:** Custody mediators in California had discretion about session format, including whether to conduct joint or separate sessions. DV advocates documented cases where joint sessions produced agreements that placed victims at risk — agreements reached under duress in the mediator's presence.

**1981:** Initial mandatory mediation statute. Section 3172 not yet enacted; DV protocols left to mediator discretion.

**1990s:** A series of incidents in which DV victims were harmed after reaching mediated agreements at joint sessions with their abusers produces legislative response.

**1996 (Stats. Ch. 761):** Legislature amends § 3172 to mandate separate sessions in DV cases. The "shall make reasonable efforts" language is chosen deliberately — "shall" imposes the obligation, "reasonable efforts" acknowledges that logistical challenges may arise but does not excuse failure.

**2012:** Rules of Court rule 5.215 operationalizes § 3172 with specific screening, session, waiting area, and documentation requirements.
