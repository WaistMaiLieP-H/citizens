# Historical Chain — Cal. Rules of Court, Rule 5.215

**Pre-2012:** DV protocols in mediation governed primarily by § 3172 (separate sessions) without detailed procedural implementation. Counties developed inconsistent local practices; some counties did not screen for DV before sessions.

**Research findings (2000s):** Studies document that DV victims frequently do not disclose abuse when screened in non-private settings, when screened after entering a joint session space, or when screened by the same mediator who will conduct the joint session. Pre-session, private, separate screening is identified as the only reliable disclosure method.

**2012:** Judicial Council adopts rule 5.215 operationalizing § 3172 with specific mandatory steps: pre-session screening, separate waiting areas, separate entry/exit, no direct communication requirements, safety planning, and documentation. Each step was chosen based on documented evidence of harm when that step was omitted.

**Present:** Rule 5.215 applies in all California counties to all mediation and recommending counseling where DV is a factor.
