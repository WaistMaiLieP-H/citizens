## EVID § 1270 — "Business": Definition

*(Reproduced from source prep pre-fetch 2026-04-12; live-fetch confirms text)*

"A business" includes every kind of business, governmental activity, profession, occupation, calling, or operation of institutions, whether carried on for profit or not.

## EVID § 1271 — Business Records Exception

Evidence of a writing made as a record of an act, condition, or event is not made inadmissible by the hearsay rule when offered to prove the act, condition, or event if:

**(a)** The writing was made in the regular course of a business;

**(b)** The writing was made at or near the time of the act, condition, or event;

**(c)** The custodian or other qualified witness testifies to its identity and the mode of its preparation; and

**(d)** The sources of information and method and time of preparation were such as to indicate its trustworthiness.

---

## Operative Provisions Parsed

**What qualifies:** ANY writing made as a record of an act, condition, or event — offered to PROVE that act, condition, or event.

**Threshold requirement:** The writing is otherwise hearsay — it is an out-of-court statement offered for the truth of the matter asserted. §1271 provides the exception.

**The four conjunctive requirements (all required):**
1. Regular course of business — the record type is one routinely kept as part of how the business operates
2. At or near the time — contemporaneous or near-contemporaneous with the event recorded
3. Custodian testimony — someone with knowledge of the record system can testify to identity and preparation
4. Trustworthy preparation — sources of information, method, and time of preparation indicate reliability

**Broad "business" definition:** §1270 encompasses governmental agencies, non-profits, professions, and any organized activity. Police departments, courts, hospitals, auto dealers, insurance companies, federal agencies — ALL qualify as "businesses" under §1271.
