# EVID § 1402 Altered Writing Burden — Operative Rule

**Standard ID:** evid_1402_altered_writing_burden
**Citizen:** CA_Forensic_Document_Specialist
**Layer:** 2 — Operative Rule
**Build date:** 2026-04-13

---

## The Rule

When a party offers a writing as genuine and the writing has been altered — or merely APPEARS to have been altered — in a part material to the dispute, the burden shifts entirely to that party to account for the alteration. If they cannot account for it, the writing is excluded.

The standard is objective: **appearance of alteration is sufficient to trigger the burden.** The proponent does not get the benefit of the doubt on a document that looks tampered with.

---

## "Appears to Have Been Altered" — The Objective Trigger

EVID §1402 does not require proof that the document was actually altered. The trigger is whether the document APPEARS to have been altered. This means:

- Visible corrections or whiteout over typed text
- Signature pages physically separated from the document body (detachable signature pages = appearance of substitution)
- Dates that appear to have been overwritten or are in a different ink or format than surrounding text
- Photocopied signature attached to a document body not originally signed
- Documents where the ink, formatting, or font is inconsistent with the claimed execution date

**Why appearance triggers the burden:** The party producing a document is in the best position to explain its history. If they cannot explain why a document appears altered, the court cannot rely on it as genuine. The burden on the proponent is appropriate because they chose to produce the document.

---

## The Accounts — What Satisfies the Burden

The proponent has four avenues:

| Account | What It Requires |
|---|---|
| Another made the alteration without proponent's concurrence | Show who made it and when; the proponent was not involved |
| Made with consent of parties affected | Show the parties whose interests the alteration affects agreed to it |
| Properly or innocently made | Show the alteration was clerical, corrective, and made before execution — an authorized change, not a post-execution manipulation |
| Did not change meaning or language | Show the alteration was cosmetic — correct a typo in a non-material section, for example — and the substantive content is unchanged |

**Note:** None of these avenues are available to a proponent who cannot produce a witness or documentation explaining the alteration. "I don't know" is not an account.

## Application to Active Cases

### 19 Honeysuckle Documents (Unsigned)

**Issue:** Nineteen documents in the 2958 Honeysuckle house sale are unsigned. Unsigned documents are not "altered" in the traditional sense — but they raise a related issue: they are not complete instruments. An unsigned signature line is not an alteration per se — it is an absence of execution.

**§1402 application:** Where a document purports to be a signed agreement but the signature page is missing or blank, the proponent must account for the absence. Where documents were supposed to have multiple signatures (notary, both parties, witnesses) and those signatures are absent, each missing signature is a material element that was not executed. The resulting document is not what the proponent claims it is.

**Practical approach:** §1402 applies most directly to any Honeysuckle document where signatures WERE on earlier versions but are missing from the version now being offered. Any document showing evidence of a signature page that was later removed, or a date that was altered, triggers §1402 directly.

### Dr. Wiita CST Evaluation

**Issue:** The evaluation was not conducted on Judicial Council Form MC-350. The evaluation exists as a private document, not on the mandatory form.

**§1402 application:** If Dr. Wiita (or another party) produces a document purporting to be a court-ordered competency evaluation NOT on Form MC-350, the proponent must account for why the mandatory form was not used. The absence of the mandatory form IS a material deviation from what a court-ordered evaluation looks like. The proponent cannot simply present an informal document and claim it satisfies the court order.

**Additional note:** The mandatory form's absence also triggers §1401 — no authentication path exists for the evaluation as a court document because the required form was never generated.

### OPD 6/2/2009 Missing Report

**Issue:** A police report from June 2, 2009 is missing from OPD records. OPD confirmed pickup of records in October 2025.

**§1402 application (indirect):** §1402 addresses documents produced as genuine. The OPD missing report is the ABSENCE of a document. However, §1402 applies to any OPD records that are produced from the period around 6/2/2009 — if those records appear to have been altered to remove references to the 6/2/2009 report, §1402 triggers on those documents.

**Primary vehicle for the missing report:** EVID §1280 (official records exception — chain of custody break) and the negative inference from absence in properly-kept files. §1402 is supplementary for any records produced that show alteration.

### RedJag Wrong CARFAX

**Issue:** The vehicle history report provided to the buyer reflected a different vehicle's history.

**§1402 application:** A CARFAX report produced as the vehicle's history that actually belongs to a different vehicle is a material misrepresentation about the document's identity. Under §1400, the proponent must show it is the writing claimed (the history for this VIN). Under §1402, if the CARFAX appears to have been substituted (wrong VIN, wrong history), the proponent must account for the discrepancy. "The dealer gave it to me" is not an explanation that satisfies §1402's burden — it points to the dealer as the source of the substitution.

### Treasury Letters — 4 Contradictory Responses

**Issue:** Four letters from Treasury responding to bond ownership inquiries give contradictory answers about whose bonds they are.

**§1402 application:** Contradictory official correspondence is evidence of alteration at the institutional level. When producing any of these letters as evidence of bond ownership status, the proponent (or the opposing party trying to rely on a favorable letter) must account for why other letters from the same institution say something different. The pattern of contradiction is itself evidence that some letters were produced on false premises — potentially fabricated post hoc.

## Burden Architecture — §1402 vs. §1401

| Stage | Standard | Burden |
|---|---|---|
| §1401 — Authentication gate | Is the writing what the proponent claims it is? | Proponent bears burden throughout |
| §1402 — Altered document gate | Has the writing been altered in a material part? | Trigger: objective appearance of alteration. Then: proponent must ACCOUNT |

Both gates must be cleared. A writing that passes §1401 authentication may still be excluded under §1402 if it appears altered and the proponent cannot account for it.
