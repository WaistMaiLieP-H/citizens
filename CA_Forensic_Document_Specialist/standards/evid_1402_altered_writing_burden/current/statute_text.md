# Altered Documents — Burden on Proponent — Statutory Text

**Source:** Cal. Evidence Code § 1402
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-13 via VernenLegal MCP (california_get_section)
**URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1402.&lawCode=EVID
**Verified:** true

## Text

The party producing a writing as genuine which has been altered, or appears to have been altered, after its execution, in a part material to the question in dispute, must account for the alteration or appearance thereof. He may show that the alteration was made by another, without his concurrence, or was made with the consent of the parties affected by it, or otherwise properly or innocently made, or that the alteration did not change the meaning or language of the instrument. If he does that, he may give the writing in evidence, but not otherwise.

---

## Operative Provisions Parsed

**Trigger:** A writing that:
1. Has been altered after its execution, OR
2. APPEARS to have been altered after its execution

in a part **material to the question in dispute**.

**Effect:** The party PRODUCING the writing as genuine (the proponent) **MUST ACCOUNT** for the alteration or appearance.

**Permitted Accounts (any one sufficient):**
- Alteration was made by **another** without the proponent's concurrence
- Alteration was made with the **consent of the parties affected**
- Alteration was **properly or innocently made**
- Alteration **did not change the meaning or language** of the instrument

**Consequence of failure:** If the proponent cannot account for the alteration — **the writing may NOT be received in evidence.**

---

## Companion Statute — EVID § 1401 (also fetched)

**(a)** Authentication of a writing is required before it may be received in evidence.
**(b)** Authentication of a writing is required before secondary evidence of its content may be received in evidence.

**Relationship:** §1402 is an additional burden layered on top of §1401's general authentication requirement. Even if a writing would otherwise be authenticated, §1402 imposes a further accounting burden when the writing appears altered. Both must be satisfied.
