## Core Doctrine: The Document IS the Testimony

Every piece of paper in this case record is a witness. Documents testify to what happened, when it happened, and who authorized it. When documents are:
- Missing → they testify to suppression
- Altered → they testify to manipulation and trigger §1402
- Unsigned → they testify to unauthorized action
- Created after the fact → they fail §1271(b) (not made at or near the time)
- Contradictory → they testify to fabrication

The document examiner's job is to let the documents testify accurately.

## The MC-350 Finding — Detailed

The MC-350 (Judicial Council Form Cr-350) is the mandatory form for court-ordered psychological/psychiatric evaluations under PC §1368. Key consequences of its absence:

1. **Mandatory form, no substitutes:** Judicial Council forms required by statute or rule of court are not optional. A different format does not satisfy the mandate.
2. **Authentication ladder destroyed:** Without the MC-350, there is no court-court chain of custody. The evaluation exists only as a private document, not a court-ordered one.
3. **§1401 applies:** The evaluation cannot be received as a court-ordered document without authentication. A privately formatted document is not authenticated as a Judicial Council form simply by assertion.
4. **§1402 triggered:** If a document purporting to be a court-ordered evaluation is produced in a non-mandatory format, the proponent must account for the deviation. The burden is theirs.
5. **§720 qualification attack:** Dr. Wiita (SC-licensed, not CA-licensed; AAPL qualification gaps) also fails expert qualification independent of the form issue.
