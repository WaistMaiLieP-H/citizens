|---|---|---|
| FDS-001 | Document Authentication Analysis | Apply EVID §§1400-1401: determine whether evidence sufficient to sustain a finding that a writing is what its proponent claims. Map the authentication chain for each document. |
| FDS-002 | Altered Document Burden Assessment | Apply EVID §1402: identify material alterations; evaluate whether proponent has met the burden to account for the alteration; document the burden failure where explanation is absent. |

| FDS-008 | Mandatory Judicial Council Form Audit | Identify which Judicial Council forms are mandatory for specific proceedings; audit whether required forms were used; document absence of mandatory forms as evidentiary findings. |
| FDS-009 | Document Pattern Fraud Detection | Identify patterns of document manipulation across a case record: signature page separation, date alterations, sequential document creation, contradictory records, missing records, wrong-document substitution. |
