# Umbrella 12 — Property / Title / Ownership

**Definition.** Real estate, intellectual property, water rights, mineral rights, recording acts, eminent domain, adverse possession, possessory and non-possessory interests in things and ideas. The rules that establish who owns what, how ownership is proven, how it transfers, and how disputes about it are resolved.

**Scope.** Real property law (deeds, mortgages, easements, covenants, leaseholds); recording statutes; title insurance; quiet title actions; eminent domain and inverse condemnation; landlord-tenant; common-interest developments and HOAs; intellectual property (patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret); water rights (riparian, appropriative, groundwater); mineral rights; oil and gas leases; secured transactions (UCC Article 9).

**Examples.**
- California Civil Code, Division 2, Part 2 (Real or Immovable Property) — particularly §§ 654, 658, 660 et seq.
- California Civil Code §§ 1091-1218 (Conveyance of Real Property)
- California Government Code § 27201 et seq. (County Recorder duties)
- Recording acts generally (Cal. Civ. Code § 1213 — race-notice rule)
- 35 U.S.C. (Patents)
- 17 U.S.C. (Copyright)
- 15 U.S.C. § 1051 et seq. (Lanham Act / Trademarks)
- Uniform Commercial Code Article 2 (Sales) and Article 9 (Secured Transactions), as adopted in California Commercial Code
- California Water Code (riparian and appropriative rights)
- Eminent Domain Law (Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1230.010 et seq.)
- Davis-Stirling Common Interest Development Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 4000 et seq.)

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Civil Code § 1213 — Recording Acts (race-notice rule).** California's statute that establishes the priority of competing claims to real property: an unrecorded instrument is valid as between the parties to it but is void as against any subsequent purchaser for value who records first without notice of the unrecorded interest. The foundation of California's recording system and the basis on which the title insurance industry exists.
