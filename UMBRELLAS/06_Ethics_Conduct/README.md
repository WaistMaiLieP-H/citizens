# Umbrella 06 — Ethics / Conduct

**Definition.** How professionals must behave. Fiduciary duty, conflict-of-interest, professional codes of conduct, the obligations a license holder takes on when entering a regulated profession.

**Scope.** Bar association rules of professional conduct, medical ethics, accounting professional standards, engineering codes of ethics, fiduciary duty doctrine in trustee/executor/guardian/attorney roles, judicial ethics.

**Examples.**
- ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct
- California Rules of Professional Conduct (State Bar of California)
- AMA Code of Medical Ethics
- NSPE Code of Ethics for Engineers
- AICPA Code of Professional Conduct (accountants)
- California Code of Judicial Ethics
- Common-law fiduciary duty doctrine (Meinhard v. Salmon, 1928 — Cardozo's "punctilio of an honor the most sensitive")
- IRS Circular 230 (practice before the IRS)

**Canonical Citizen owners.**
- State Bar Compliance Specialist
- Medical Ethics Officer
- CPA / Accounting Ethics Specialist
- Professional Engineer (PE) Ethics Specialist
- Judicial Ethics Officer
- Fiduciary (trustee, executor, guardian, conservator) Compliance Specialist

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 1.1 (Competence).** The first numbered rule in the California State Bar's Rules of Professional Conduct. States the foundational duty of a lawyer to perform legal services competently, including the duty to apply diligence and to act with reasonable promptness. Cross-references continuing legal education obligations and the duty to associate or consult with another lawyer when the matter exceeds the lawyer's competence.

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://www.calbar.ca.gov/Portals/0/documents/rules/Rule_1.1-Exec_Summary-Redline.pdf
- https://www.calbar.ca.gov/Attorneys/Conduct-Discipline/Rules/Rules-of-Professional-Conduct (canonical landing page)

**Why this seed:** Lawyer competence is the foundational ethical duty. It's also directly relevant to the steward's case — many of the failures the steward documents in his family-law audit are competence failures (or worse) by appointed counsel and court-connected professionals.

**Backup candidate.** California Probate Code § 2101 (Conservator's standard of care) — the fiduciary standard for conservators, directly relevant to the steward's conservatorship discovery (per `project_conservatorship_breakthrough.md`).

**Status.** No standards built yet under this umbrella.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Ethics is the umbrella where professional self-regulation lives. When self-regulation fails (Bolitho test from earlier in this build session), Ethics standards become unenforceable from within and require external enforcement via Authority (Umbrella 01) or Procedure (Umbrella 02).
- The Probate Code conservator standard is also a Family/Personal Status (Umbrella 11) standard. Real standards usually live in two or three umbrellas simultaneously.
