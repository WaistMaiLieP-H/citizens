# Umbrella 17 — Energy

**Definition.** Energy policy, utility regulation, oil and gas, electricity, renewables, nuclear, grid reliability, fuel standards. The rules that govern how energy is produced, transmitted, distributed, sold, and consumed.

**Scope.** Electric utility regulation (rates, service obligations, grid interconnection, transmission planning); natural gas regulation; oil and gas leasing and production; refined petroleum product standards; renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, biomass); nuclear power regulation; energy efficiency standards; energy storage; demand response; wholesale electricity markets; retail electricity markets; carbon markets and cap-and-trade; pipeline safety (cross-cuts Travel/Transportation Umbrella 16).

**Examples.**
- Federal Power Act (16 USC § 791a et seq.)
- Natural Gas Act (15 USC § 717 et seq.)
- Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 (PURPA)
- Energy Policy Act of 2005
- Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007
- 18 CFR (FERC regulations)
- 10 CFR (Department of Energy regulations; also Nuclear Regulatory Commission)
- California Public Utilities Code (the entire code)
- California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) decisions and General Orders
- California Energy Commission (CEC) regulations
- California Renewables Portfolio Standard (RPS, Cal. Pub. Util. Code § 399.11 et seq.)
- California cap-and-trade program (AB 32 / Cal. H&S Code § 38500 et seq.)
- NERC Reliability Standards (mandatory under Section 215 of the Federal Power Act)

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Public Utilities Code § 399.11 — Renewables Portfolio Standard (RPS).** California's statutory requirement that load-serving entities (electric utilities, ESPs, CCAs) procure a specified percentage of their retail sales from eligible renewable energy resources. Originally adopted in 2002, repeatedly accelerated; current law requires 60% renewables by 2030 and 100% zero-carbon by 2045 (the latter via SB 100, 2018).

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=399.11&lawCode=PUC
- Full RPS article: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?lawCode=PUC&division=1.&title=&part=1.&chapter=2.3.&article=16.

**Why this seed:** The RPS is one of the most consequential California energy statutes, drives billions of dollars of investment, and is the model for similar laws in many other states. Building it as a standard establishes the umbrella's foundational policy direction.

**Secondary seed candidate.** California Public Utilities Commission General Order 95 (Rules for Overhead Electric Line Construction) — the technical standard for overhead power lines in California, repeatedly cited after wildfire investigations (PG&E Camp Fire, etc.).

**Tertiary seed candidate.** AB 32 (Cal. H&S Code § 38500 et seq.) — California Global Warming Solutions Act of 2006, the foundation of California's cap-and-trade program.

**Status.** No standards built yet under this umbrella.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Energy overlaps Environmental (08) heavily — most modern energy regulation is also climate/environmental regulation.
- Energy overlaps Safety (05) — pipeline safety, electrical grid safety, nuclear safety all sit in both umbrellas.
- Energy overlaps Authority (01) — every Energy standard is itself a statute or regulation.
