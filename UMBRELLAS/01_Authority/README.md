# Umbrella 01 — Authority / Governing Law

**Definition.** The binding-source layer. Statutes, regulations, court rules, constitutional provisions, treaties, and executive orders that create the obligations every other umbrella's standards are interpreted against.

**Scope.** Anything that *creates* a legal duty, right, or power. Constitutions sit at the top; statutes and treaties below them; regulations and court rules below those; executive orders, agency guidance, and binding case law fill in the rest.

**Examples.**
- U.S. Constitution
- California Constitution (1879, with major revisions)
- United States Code (USC)
- California Codes (29 codes including Civil, Penal, Family, Probate, Business and Professions, etc.)
- Code of Federal Regulations (CFR)
- California Code of Regulations (CCR)
- Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP), Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE)
- California Rules of Court (CRC)

**Canonical Citizen owners.**
- Constitutional Law Specialist (federal and state)
- Statutory Interpretation Specialist
- Regulatory Drafter / Office of Administrative Law professional
- REGULIS (existing catalog persona for general regulatory work)

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Constitution, Article I (Declaration of Rights).** This is the most foundational California-specific source. Article I Section 1 — the unalienable rights clause — predates the U.S. Bill of Rights' application to states via incorporation and is independently enforceable in California courts.

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?tocCode=CONS&division=&title=&part=&chapter=&article=I.

**Why this seed:** Every California standard ultimately rests on the California Constitution. Building Article I as a witnessed standard establishes the root of the chain for every California-jurisdiction standard the corpus will ever contain. The federal constitutional analog (U.S. Const. Bill of Rights) becomes the second seed.

**Status.** No standards built yet under this umbrella.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Authority is presupposed by every other umbrella. A Safety standard (Field Act) is only a standard because some Authority (1933 Statutes Ch. 59) created it.
- The line between Authority and Procedure (Umbrella 02) is the line between *what the rule is* and *how the rule is applied in a forum*. FRCP is Procedure; the Rules Enabling Act that authorizes FRCP is Authority.
