# Umbrella 05 — Safety

**Definition.** How things must protect human life, health, and physical wellbeing. The rules whose violation creates direct foreseeable risk of injury or death.

**Scope.** Workplace safety, building safety, transportation safety, drug and device safety, food safety, fire and life safety, electrical safety, explosive and hazardous materials safety.

**Examples.**
- OSHA / Cal-OSHA (workplace)
- California Plumbing Code (water/sewer/gas safety)
- Field Act (school construction earthquake safety) — **already built as a witnessed standard**
- Riley Act (general building seismic safety) — **already built as a proposed standard**
- NFPA 70 (NEC, electrical safety)
- NFPA 101 (Life Safety Code)
- FMVSS (Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards)
- FDA drug and device safety regulations
- DOT hazardous materials regulations

**Canonical Citizen owners.**
- Cal-OSHA Compliance Officer
- Certified Building Official (B3-001 in catalog) — **owns Riley Act**
- Licensed Structural Engineer — **owns Field Act**
- Fire Marshal
- DOT Hazmat Specialist
- Drug/Device Safety Officer

**Candidate next standard.**

**Cal-OSHA General Industry Safety Orders, Title 8 CCR § 3203 (Injury and Illness Prevention Program / IIPP).** Every California employer with employees must have a written IIPP under § 3203. It is the most universally-applicable Cal-OSHA standard and the most-cited violation in California workplace inspections.

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/3203.html (Cal-OSHA)
- https://govt.westlaw.com/calregs/Document/I9D14A610B61711DEA8298AAFF4D9E737 (CCR official)

**Why this next:** It is the doctrinal "do it right the first time" applied to workplace safety — a written program, posted, with documented training, evaluated periodically. Mirrors the steward's UA Local 342 ethos.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Safety overlaps Substance (Umbrella 03) when a safety standard prescribes physical material requirements.
- Safety overlaps Procedure (Umbrella 02) when a safety standard requires specific reporting, inspection, or appeals procedures.
- The 1933 Long Beach earthquake produced THREE 1933 statutes (Field Act Ch. 59, Riley Act Ch. 601, and the still-undocumented Chapter 602 reconstruction funding act). Documenting Chapter 602 as a third Safety-umbrella standard is outstanding work.
