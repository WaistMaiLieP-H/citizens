# Umbrella 18 — Agriculture / Food

**Definition.** Farming standards, food safety, organic certification, pesticide regulation, animal welfare in food production, agricultural labor rules where they are agriculture-specific. The rules that govern how food is grown, processed, labeled, and brought to market.

**Scope.** Crop production standards; livestock standards; dairy standards; organic certification; pesticide registration and use; food safety throughout the supply chain (farm, processing, transport, retail, restaurant); food identity and labeling; animal welfare in food production; agricultural water use; seed certification; commercial fishing; aquaculture.

**Examples.**
- Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA, 21 USC § 301 et seq.)
- Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA, 2011) and its implementing regulations (21 CFR Parts 117, 112, 121, etc.)
- Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (FIFRA, 7 USC § 136 et seq.)
- USDA regulations on meat, poultry, and egg inspection (FMIA, PPIA, EPIA)
- National Organic Program (NOP, 7 CFR Part 205)
- Country-of-Origin Labeling (COOL)
- California Food and Agricultural Code (the entire code)
- California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA) regulations
- California Department of Pesticide Regulation (DPR) regulations
- California Retail Food Code (CalCode, Cal. H&S Code § 113700 et seq.)
- 3 CCR § 6000 et seq. (California pesticide regulations)
- Proposition 12 (Cal. H&S Code § 25990 et seq., 2018) — farm animal confinement standards

**Canonical Citizen owners.**

- USDA Inspector (FSIS)
- Certified Organic Inspector

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Health and Safety Code § 113700 et seq. — California Retail Food Code (CalCode).** California's comprehensive retail food safety statute, governing every restaurant, grocery store, food truck, school cafeteria, and food vendor in California. It is the law that drives the county environmental health letter-grade placards on restaurant windows. **Directly relevant to the school food allergy law example the steward raised earlier in this build session** — modern school food safety and allergy accommodations are governed in California through the intersection of CalCode, the Education Code, and Health and Safety Code provisions on food allergens.

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?lawCode=HSC&division=104.&title=&part=7.&chapter=&article=

**Why this seed:** Directly relevant to the steward's "no peanuts in school cafeterias" methodological example. Building CalCode establishes the umbrella's connection to the laws-have-a-beginning insight he articulated. Also high-impact: every food establishment in California is regulated by it.

**Secondary seed candidate.** California Food and Agricultural Code provisions on pesticide use (FAC §§ 11401 et seq., 14001 et seq.) — California has the most comprehensive pesticide regulatory regime in the United States, administered by the Department of Pesticide Regulation.

**Tertiary seed candidate.** Proposition 12 (Cal. H&S Code § 25990 et seq.) — farm animal confinement standards adopted by initiative in 2018; the subject of the 2023 U.S. Supreme Court decision in National Pork Producers Council v. Ross.

**Status.** No standards built yet under this umbrella.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Agriculture/Food overlaps Safety (05) — food safety is a Safety concern; school cafeteria allergy rules are at the intersection of Food umbrella, Safety umbrella, and Education (a candidate umbrella not yet promoted to first-class).
- Agriculture/Food overlaps Environmental (08) — pesticide regulation is both an agricultural standard and an environmental standard.
- Agriculture/Food overlaps Substance/Specification (03) — food identity standards (what may be called "milk," "olive oil," "organic," etc.) are spec standards.
