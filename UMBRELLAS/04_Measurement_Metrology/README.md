# Umbrella 04 — Measurement / Metrology

**Definition.** How things are measured, what units are used, what counts as a calibrated instrument. The rules that establish a common reference frame for quantitative claims.

**Scope.** Units of measure, calibration, traceability to national standards, measurement uncertainty, laboratory accreditation, instrument certification.

**Examples.**
- NIST SI (International System of Units) realizations
- NIST traceable calibration chains
- ISO/IEC 17025 (laboratory accreditation)
- California Division of Measurement Standards (DMS) regulations
- 4 CCR § 4000 et seq. (California weights and measures)
- USP general chapters on analytical method validation
- ASTM testing methods (which are also Substance/Specification entries depending on use)

**Canonical Citizen owners.**
- Metrologist (NIST-traceable calibration)
- Weights and Measures Inspector (county sealer of weights and measures)
- Laboratory Director (ISO 17025-accredited)
- Quality Assurance Specialist

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Business and Professions Code §§ 12001 et seq. — Weights and Measures Law.** California's foundational weights-and-measures statute. Establishes the State Sealer of Weights and Measures, the county sealers, the duties of testing scales/pumps/meters used in commerce, the penalties for short measure. Every gas pump, every grocery scale, every taxi meter in California is regulated by this law.

**Primary-source URL:**
- https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&division=5.&title=&part=&chapter=&article=

**Why this seed:** This is the modern California descendant of the lineage we discussed earlier — the cooper's grain measure that was "slightly wrong by a cooper who genuinely believed it was correct." The codified protection against exactly that good-faith failure mode.

**Backup candidate.** NIST Handbook 44 (Specifications, Tolerances, and Other Technical Requirements for Weighing and Measuring Devices) — adopted by reference into California's weights and measures law.

**Status.** No standards built yet under this umbrella.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Metrology underpins every quantitative compliance regime. An emissions limit (Environmental, Umbrella 08) is meaningless without a calibrated measurement.
- Metrology overlaps Integrity (Umbrella 09) — a calibration certificate is a chain-of-custody document for the trustworthiness of an instrument.
