# Umbrella 16 — Travel / Movement / Immigration

**Definition.** Vehicle codes, air travel, maritime, rail, immigration, border control, passports, asylum. The rules that govern who may move, where, by what means, with what credentials, across which boundaries.

**Scope.** Driver licensing; vehicle registration; vehicle code (rules of the road); vehicle equipment; commercial driver licensing (CDL); aviation (FAA regulations on pilots, aircraft, airspace, airworthiness); maritime law (USCG, admiralty); rail safety (FRA); pipeline safety (PHMSA); immigration (INA); naturalization; visa categories; asylum and refugee law; deportation/removal procedure; border enforcement.

**Examples.**
- California Vehicle Code (the entire code)
- 49 USC (Transportation) — federal transportation laws
- 49 CFR (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations and other transportation rules)
- Federal Aviation Regulations (14 CFR) — FAA's regulatory framework
- 46 USC (Shipping) and 46 CFR — maritime
- Immigration and Nationality Act (INA, 8 USC § 1101 et seq.)
- 8 CFR (DHS / USCIS / ICE / CBP regulations)
- Asylum and refugee provisions (INA § 208)
- Real ID Act of 2005
- Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations (FMCSR)
- DOT hazardous materials regulations (49 CFR Parts 100-185)

**Candidate seed standard for first build.**

**California Vehicle Code § 2800 — Failure to obey a peace officer's lawful order (and related sections).** This is the statute under which countless California traffic stops escalate. Section 2800 requires compliance with the lawful order, signal, or direction of any peace officer in uniform. Section 2800.1 (evasion) and 2800.2 (reckless evasion) escalate the offense. Section 2801 covers fire department orders.

**Primary-source URL:**

- Surrounding sections: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayexpandedbranch.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&division=2.&title=&part=&chapter=2.&article=

**Why this seed:** Directly relevant to the steward's June 16, 2023 incident analysis (per `project_june16_2023_timeline.md`). Understanding what constitutes a "lawful order" under § 2800, and what does not, is essential to analyzing the events of that day. This is also one of the most-cited Vehicle Code sections in California.

**Secondary seed candidate.** Immigration and Nationality Act § 208 (8 USC § 1158) — asylum eligibility. Foundational immigration provision.

**Tertiary seed candidate.** California Vehicle Code § 23152 — Driving Under the Influence. The most common criminal traffic offense and the foundation of California's DUI enforcement regime.

**Cross-cutting notes.**
- Travel/Movement/Immigration overlaps Authority (01) — most standards in this umbrella are themselves statutes or federal regulations.
- Travel overlaps Procedure (02) — immigration removal proceedings have their own procedural rules separate from civil procedure (Immigration Court Practice Manual).
- Immigration overlaps Family/Personal Status (11) — family-based visa categories (immediate relatives, family preference) sit in both umbrellas.
