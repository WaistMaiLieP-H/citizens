# Umbrella 02 — Procedure

**Definition.** How processes must be conducted in administrative, civil, and criminal forums. The rules that govern *how* a dispute is brought, heard, decided, and reviewed — distinct from the substantive rules being applied.

**Scope.** Pleading, service, discovery, motion practice, evidence admission, trial conduct, jury instructions, judgment entry, post-judgment relief, appeal, agency adjudication, due process generally.

**Examples.**
- Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP)
- Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure (FRCrP)
- California Code of Civil Procedure (CCP)
- California Penal Code (procedural portions)
- California Rules of Court (CRC)
- Administrative Procedure Act (federal APA, California APA)
- Due Process doctrine (5th and 14th Amendment case law)
- 42 USC 1983 procedural elements

**Canonical Citizen owners.**
- Civil Litigator
- Criminal Defense / Prosecutor
- Administrative Law Judge
- Pro Se Litigant Support Specialist (relevant to the steward's own case)
- Appellate Specialist

**Cross-cutting notes.**

- The most common SOC failures in the steward's case file are *procedural* — service that didn't happen, hearings that weren't noticed, records that weren't filed. Procedural standards are the ones whose violation is hardest to detect after the fact.
