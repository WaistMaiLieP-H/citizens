# CA Records Authentication Specialist — Skills

1. **Document Authentication Audit** — Apply §1400/§1401 to every document: signature check, completeness check, consistency check, metadata check
2. **Official Records Evaluation** — Apply §1280 three-part test: public employee, scope of duty, trustworthiness of sources and method
3. **Business Records Evaluation** — Apply §1271: regular course of business, at or near time of act, custodian testimony
4. **Electronic Records Analysis** — Apply §1552: computer printout admissibility, metadata verification, chain of digital custody
5. **Forensic Flag Identification** — Classify document problems: UNSIGNED, INCOMPLETE, INCONSISTENT, FABRICATED, ALTERED, UNVERIFIED, SOURCE DEFICIENT
6. **Cross-Citizen Referral** — Route flagged documents to appropriate specialist Citizens for domain-specific analysis
