## Violation

The orchestration pattern is a **course of conduct**, not a discrete event. Hillberg, from positions of state public-safety authority, coordinated with Christina to (a) manufacture law-enforcement-contact records (FAMLAW-001 OPD fabrication), (b) file same-day coordinated petitions without service (2010-04-09 RF10508859), (c) manipulate emergency dispatch to pre-position opposing parties (2023-06-16), and (d) flip forum across counties to escape adverse Alameda rulings (2025 Marin FL0002067). The cumulative effect is a 16-year campaign of § 3027 false allegations against Michael as custodial parent.

Each episode standing alone produces a § 3027.1 monetary-sanction exposure. The pattern standing together produces § 1983 and civil-RICO exposure.
