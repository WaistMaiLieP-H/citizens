# FAMLAW-004 — § 3044 Rebuttable DV Presumption Never Properly Adjudicated

**Finding ID:** FAMLAW-004
**Citizen:** CA_Family_Law_Litigator
**Status:** PUBLISHED — ADAM-certified 2026-04-14
**Severity:** HIGH
**Subject cases:** RF09456481 (2010-09-02 Ajaniku/Delucchi reversal), FL0002067 (2025-10-17 DV-130)

## Facts

1. Cal. Fam. Code § 3044 creates a **rebuttable presumption** that an award of sole or joint custody to a person who has perpetrated domestic violence within the previous five years is detrimental to the best interest of the child.
2. The 2009-06-29 Alameda court order (Judge Thompson) gave Michael sole legal and physical custody of Cole. This ruling embedded a de facto § 3044 finding against Christina as the DV perpetrator — the DVRO was against her and custody followed.
3. On 2010-09-02, Mediator Sala Ajaniku recommended modifying the DVRO and granting Christina unsupervised visitation. Judge Paul A. Delucchi adopted the recommendation. **The record does not contain a § 3044(b) rebuttal-factor analysis** — no written findings that (1) Christina completed a batterer intervention program, (2) she complied with the protective order, (3) there is no further DV, and (4) the enumerated § 3044(b)(1)-(7) factors were considered.
4. Ajaniku lacked verifiable credentials to conduct the § 3164/§ 1815/§ 1816 mediation that produced this recommendation (see CREDENTIAL-AUDIT-AJANIKU-PAREDES-DITSWORTH-2026-03-23.md).
5. In every subsequent custody-adjacent proceeding through 2025 — Christina's ex parte dissolution RF10508853, Hillberg's RF10508859 grandparent petition, the 2025 DVRO sequence (25FL122591, 25FL125059), and FL0002067 — the § 3044 presumption that attached to Christina in 2009 has never been formally rebutted on the record by written findings.
6. The § 3044 presumption operates prospectively: subsequent DV findings (e.g., Marin 2025-10-17 DV-130 against Michael) cannot satisfy § 3044 against Michael without written findings, and cannot dissolve the 2009 presumption against Christina without a § 3044(b) proceeding.

## Violation

The 2010-09-02 order modifying the DVRO and granting unsupervised visitation to Christina — the party against whom the § 3044 presumption had attached in 2009 — was entered without the § 3044(b) rebuttal-factor findings § 3011(a)(5)(A) requires in writing or on the record. The order is facially defective and every subsequent order that compounds on it inherits the defect. The § 3044 presumption against Christina has **never been properly rebutted** and remains, as a matter of operative law, in force.

Conversely, the 2025-10-17 Marin DV-130 against Michael cannot, without § 3044(b)(1)-(7) factor findings in writing, support a § 3044 presumption reversal that reassigns custody to Christina — and no such findings are in the Marin record.

## Remedy

1. **Motion to vacate** the 2010-09-02 Ajaniku/Delucchi order under CCP § 473(d) as facially defective for want of § 3011(a)(5)(A) written findings.
2. **Declaratory relief** in any active custody proceeding: the § 3044 presumption against Christina, attached in 2009, remains in force absent § 3044(b) rebuttal.
3. **§ 3011(a)(5)(A) compliance demand** in any order that would override the § 3044 presumption — written findings on the record, factor-by-factor, or the order fails on its face.
4. **Federal § 1983 predicate** — repeated denial of § 3011(a)(5)(A) written-reason protections in a custody sequence that has deprived Michael of his parent-child liberty interest is a Fourteenth-Amendment procedural-due-process claim. Incorporated into `case_workflows/federal_section_1983_complaint/`.
