# Junction: Family Law DV abuse definition ↔ Gov Code § 13955 CalVCB eligibility

## Doctrinal relationship

Family Code § 6203 defines "abuse" for DVPA purposes. Government Code § 13955 defines victim eligibility for CalVCB compensation. The two definitions overlap but are not identical:
- § 6203 is broader — includes non-criminal harassing conduct.
- § 13955 requires a qualifying crime with a factual nexus and cooperation with LE.

**The cross-frame rule.** A DV finding under § 6203 does not automatically establish § 13955 eligibility, and vice versa. Each framework requires its own evidentiary record.

## Key doctrinal points

1. § 13955(a) requires direct victim, derivative victim, or specified family member of a qualifying crime.
2. § 13955(f) requires "cooperation" with LE/prosecution — commonly weaponized to deny pro se applicants who have been obstructed by LE themselves.
3. § 6203 DVPA abuse includes conduct that would not support § 13955 eligibility (e.g., disturbing peace, harassment without physical crime).
4. Family-court finding of DV is admissible in the CalVCB proceeding but not preclusive.
