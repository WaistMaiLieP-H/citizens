# Junction: Family Law § 3118 / Evid. § 730 ↔ Mental Health LPS safeguards

## Doctrinal relationship — procedural bridge

Family Code § 3118 governs the court-ordered child-custody evaluation where DV is alleged; Evidence Code § 730 authorizes court-appointed experts generally. Both are civil-evaluation frameworks operating *outside* the LPS Act's mental-health-commitment safeguards (Welf. & Inst. Code §§ 5150, 5250, 5350 et seq.) and outside Penal Code § 1368 competency-to-stand-trial procedures.

The **procedural-bridge** relationship runs both ways:
1. An evaluator appointed under § 3118 / § 730 cannot lawfully conduct an *LPS-equivalent competency or commitment assessment* without the corresponding LPS procedural safeguards (right to counsel, right to jury trial under § 5350, least-restrictive-alternative, time-limited holds).
2. An evaluator's report that functionally operates as a § 1368 competency assessment or an LPS pre-commitment screen must comply with those frameworks — a § 730 or § 3118 appointment does not substitute.

## Key doctrinal points

1. **§ 3118 elements:** (a) court-ordered appointment in DV-allegation custody case; (b) qualifications under § 3110.5 + Cal. Rule of Court 5.220; (c) written report filed; (d) cross-examination rights preserved.
2. **Evid. § 730 elements:** (a) court appointment; (b) reasonable need; (c) expert qualification; (d) opportunity to depose/cross-examine.
3. **LPS non-substitution:** neither § 3118 nor § 730 can lawfully produce a *functional* involuntary hold, forced medication, or conservatorship predicate — those require LPS procedure (WIC §§ 5150, 5250, 5350).
4. **§ 1368 non-substitution:** criminal competency is separately governed; a § 730 civil evaluation cannot substitute for § 1368 protections (Dusky standard; Pate v. Robinson; CA_Mental_Health_Litigator PEN_1368).
5. **Evidence Code § 1016 privilege:** psychotherapist-patient privilege applies — Evaluator's access to historical records requires patient-litigant exception analysis.

## How Family Law Citizen uses this

- Audit any § 3118 / § 730 evaluation in the case record for the "functionally performing an LPS or § 1368 task without the corresponding safeguards" defect.
- Where the evaluator's report asserts or implies lack of competency or need for involuntary intervention, that is the line — LPS safeguards must attach or the report is methodologically disqualified.
- Pair with CA_Forensic_Document_Specialist (EVID §§ 720, 1400, 1402) for expert-qualification and authentication attacks.
