# Junction: Family Law § 3027 / § 3027.1 ↔ Penal Code § 148.5 false report

## Doctrinal relationship — predicate

Penal Code § 148.5 makes it a misdemeanor to report to a peace officer or prosecutor a criminal offense known by the reporter to be false. Family Code § 3027 authorizes sanctions in a custody proceeding for knowingly false allegations of abuse; § 3027.1 authorizes reasonable monetary sanctions for a party who makes such allegations in bad faith.

§ 148.5 is the **criminal predicate** for the § 3027.1 civil-sanction theory: where the custody-proceeding allegation is a report to APD/OPD/DA (as in the 6/11/2009 OPD narrative), both bodies of law attach. § 3027.1 does not require a § 148.5 conviction — but a § 148.5 conviction (or provable violation) is dispositive evidence of the "knowing falsity" element of § 3027.1.

## Key doctrinal points

1. **§ 148.5 elements:** (a) report of criminal offense; (b) to a peace officer, DA investigator, or police-department employee; (c) knowledge by reporter that report is false.
2. **§ 3027 vs. § 3027.1:** § 3027 directs the court to consider credibility and sanction; § 3027.1 provides the monetary-sanction authority against a party or attorney who knowingly makes false allegations.
3. **Independent proof paths:** Family court can find "knowingly false" under § 3027.1 without a Penal § 148.5 conviction (different burdens, different forums).
4. **Cross-agency effect:** A § 148.5-qualifying false report that triggers CLETS entry or DA charging decisions is actionable simultaneously under § 3027.1 (civil, family) and § 148.5 (criminal).

## How Family Law Citizen uses this

- On any allegation made in a custody proceeding that was *also* reported to law enforcement, pull the underlying LE report (via CPRA under Gov. Code § 7923 / § 13701(c) hook).
- Audit the report under § 148.5 elements; if knowingly false, move under § 3027.1 for sanctions.
- Refer the criminal predicate to CA_Criminal_Law_Specialist for § 148.5 analysis and to the DA via a citizen's referral letter.
- Pair with HERALD declaration drafting to authenticate the contradictions (report vs. contemporaneous record).
