# Escalation Pipeline

**Citizen:** CA_Family_Law_Litigator
**Created:** 2026-04-15
**Purpose:** When a request is ignored, denied, or obstructed, this pipeline defines the legal escalation, required forms, and filing requirements.

---

## Section 1: CPRA Ignored or Denied → Writ of Mandate (CCP § 1085)

**Trigger:** Public Records Act request receives no response within 10 calendar days (Gov. Code § 7922.535), or receives a denial that is legally insufficient.

**Applicable Investigations:** INV-02 (Ajaniku PRA), INV-06 (mediator switch PRA), INV-08 (fee waiver records), INV-09 (Benicia PD call recordings)

**Escalation Steps:**

1. **Day 11 (no response) or Day 1 (after denial):** Send a formal demand letter. State: (a) original request date, (b) statutory deadline, (c) that failure to produce constitutes a deemed denial under Gov. Code § 7922.535(b), (d) that a petition for writ of mandate will be filed if records are not produced within 10 additional calendar days. Send via certified mail and email.

2. **Day 21 (still no production):** File Petition for Writ of Mandate.

**Filing Requirements:**
- **Court:** Superior Court of the county where the agency is located
  - Alameda County Superior Court (for INV-02, INV-06, INV-08)
  - Solano County Superior Court (for INV-09 Benicia PD)
- **Petition form:** No Judicial Council form — verified petition (CCP § 1086)
- **Required contents:**
  - Verification under penalty of perjury (CCP § 446)
  - Copy of original PRA request with proof of delivery
  - Copy of any denial received (or declaration of non-response)
  - Copy of formal demand letter with proof of delivery
  - Memorandum of points and authorities citing Gov. Code §§ 7920.000-7930.215 and CCP § 1085
- **Filing fee:** Standard civil filing fee (~$435) OR fee waiver (FW-001, FW-003)
- **Service:** Personal service on agency's designated agent or counsel (CCP § 1088)
- **Attorney fees:** Gov. Code § 7923.115 — prevailing petitioner is entitled to costs and reasonable attorney fees (or reasonable costs for pro se litigant)

**Fee Waiver Forms:**
- FW-001 (Request for Fee Waiver)
- FW-003 (Order on Court Fee Waiver)

**Timeline:**
- Hearing: Court sets within 20 days of filing (Gov. Code § 7923.105)
- Opposition due: 5 court days before hearing
- Reply: 2 court days before hearing

## Section 2: Subpoena Quashed or Ignored → Motion to Compel (CCP § 1987.1)

**Trigger:** A subpoena duces tecum is met with a motion to quash, an objection, or non-compliance by the return date.

**Applicable Investigations:** INV-05 (CMIA disclosure log subpoenas), INV-11 (carrier subpoenas)

**Escalation Steps:**

1. **If motion to quash filed:** Oppose the motion. File opposition within the timeline set by the court (typically 9 court days before hearing under CCP § 1005(b)).

2. **If no objection but no production by return date:** File Motion to Compel Compliance with Subpoena (CCP § 1987.1).

3. **If records are produced but incomplete:** Meet and confer within 10 calendar days, then file Motion to Compel if deficiency persists.

**Filing Requirements:**
- **Court:** The court where the underlying action is pending
  - Alameda County Superior Court (family law cases RF09456481 et seq.)
  - Marin County Superior Court (FL0002067)
  - U.S. District Court, N.D. Cal. (§ 1983 action, if filed)
- **Motion form:** No Judicial Council form — noticed motion
- **Required contents:**
  - Declaration of facts showing proper service of subpoena
  - Copy of subpoena as served
  - Copy of objection or motion to quash (if any), or declaration of non-compliance
  - Declaration of meet-and-confer efforts (CCP § 2016.040 for discovery motions)
  - Memorandum of points and authorities citing CCP §§ 1987.1, 1987.2
  - Proposed order
- **Notice of motion:** CCP § 1005(b) — 16 court days before hearing + 5 calendar days for mail service
- **Sanctions:** CCP § 1987.2 — reasonable expenses including attorney fees against the non-compliant party unless substantial justification shown

**Key Forms (Judicial Council):**
- SUBP-001 (Civil Subpoena for Personal Appearance)
- SUBP-010 (Civil Subpoena — Duces Tecum for Personal Appearance and Production of Documents)
- SC-107 (Subpoena for Small Claims — if applicable)

**For Federal Court (§ 1983 action):**
- FRCP Rule 45 subpoena
- Motion to compel under FRCP Rule 37 / Rule 45(d)(2)(B)
- Sanctions under FRCP Rule 37(b)(2)(A)

## Section 3: Court Clerk Refuses Correction → Ex Parte Application

**Trigger:** A court clerk refuses to correct a clerical error (wrong case number, misfiled document, wrong party designation) or refuses to file a document without legal basis.

**Applicable Investigations:** INV-08 (fee waiver filing error), INV-10 (dual petitioner/respondent status)

**Escalation Steps:**

1. **Immediate:** Document the refusal in writing. If the clerk refuses orally, send a confirming letter to the clerk's office within 24 hours: "This confirms that on [date], [clerk name if available] refused to [action] because [stated reason]."

2. **Within 5 business days:** File an Ex Parte Application for Order Correcting Clerical Error.

**Filing Requirements:**
- **Court:** The court where the error exists
- **Form:** No Judicial Council form — ex parte application
- **Required contents:**
  - Declaration describing the error and the clerk's refusal
  - Copy of the confirming letter to the clerk
  - Copy of the document the clerk refused to file or correct
  - Evidence of what the correct record should show
  - Memorandum of points and authorities citing CCP § 473(d) (correction of clerical error) and CRC 3.1200-3.1207 (ex parte procedures)
- **Notice:** CRC 3.1203 — notice to all parties by 10:00 AM the court day before the ex parte hearing, or as soon as practicable
- **Hearing:** Ex parte hearings are typically held at 8:30 AM on the next available court day

**Alternative Path — CCP § 473(d):**
- The court may correct clerical mistakes in its judgments, orders, or records at any time on its own motion or on motion of any party.
- If the clerk's error resulted in an incorrect judgment or order (e.g., wrong party designation in INV-10), a noticed motion under § 473(d) may be more appropriate than ex parte.

**Forms for § 473(d) noticed motion:**
- MC-031 (Attached Declaration)
- No specific Judicial Council motion form — use general noticed motion format
- CCP § 1005(b) notice requirements apply (16 court days + 5 for mail)

## Section 4: Evidence Preservation Demand Ignored → Emergency Motion

**Trigger:** A litigation hold / preservation demand is ignored and there is reason to believe evidence is being or will be destroyed.

**Applicable Investigations:** INV-09 (Benicia PD call recordings — 6-12 month retention), INV-11 (carrier records — § 2703(f) 90-day preservation)

**Escalation Steps:**

1. **Immediately upon learning preservation demand was ignored:** File an emergency ex parte application for a preservation order.

**Filing Requirements:**
- **Court:** The court with jurisdiction over the matter
- **Required contents:**
  - Declaration of the original preservation demand (date, method, recipient)
  - Declaration of non-compliance or evidence of ongoing destruction
  - Declaration of irreparable harm if records are lost
  - Proposed temporary preservation order
  - Evidence of the retention schedule showing imminent destruction
- **Standard:** Good cause + irreparable harm (same as preliminary injunction standard, but courts are liberal with preservation orders because the alternative is permanent evidence loss)

**For Federal Court:**
- FRCP Rule 65(b) — TRO without notice if facts clearly show immediate and irreparable injury
- 18 U.S.C. § 2703(f) — carrier must preserve for 90 days upon request of governmental entity; for private litigants, subpoena or court order required

## Deadline Tracker

All escalation deadlines are calculated from the triggering event (non-response date, denial date, quash filing date). The Citizen must track:

1. **CPRA 10-day response deadline** — calendar days from agency receipt
2. **Demand letter 10-day cure period** — calendar days from demand letter
3. **Motion to compel filing deadline** — 60 days from objection/quash (CCP § 2025.480 by analogy)
4. **Ex parte notice deadline** — 10:00 AM the court day before hearing (CRC 3.1203)
5. **§ 2703(f) 90-day preservation window** — carrier must preserve for 90 days; renewable for an additional 90 days with second request
