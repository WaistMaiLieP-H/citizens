**Vehicle:** Judicial Council SUBP-010 (state) or Fed. R. Civ. P. 45 subpoena (federal, once § 1983 complaint is filed) — whichever is available first.

## Recipient format (per subpoena)

- **To:** "[Provider] — Custodian of Records / Privacy Officer"
- **Service address:** provider's registered service address (for corporate entities, the CA Secretary of State registered agent; otherwise the listed records-custodian address). Verify at time of issuance; do not rely on stale addresses.
- **Cc:** provider's HIPAA Privacy Officer (by name if listed on the Notice of Privacy Practices).

## Delivery methods

- **Service:** personal service by registered process server OR certified mail with return receipt (check SUBP-010 service rules + CCP § 2020.220 / Fed. R. Civ. P. 45(b)).
- **Proof of service:** file POS-010 (state) or affidavit of service (federal) for each subpoena.
- **Payment of witness/reproduction fees:** include the statutory witness/production fee at service (CCP § 1986.5 / § 1987; Fed. R. Civ. P. 45(b)(1)).

## Notice to consumer / patient

Because the patient is the steward himself, a CCP § 1985.3 **Notice to Consumer** to a third-party consumer is not required. However, an internal CMIA-authorization packet under Civ. § 56.11 should accompany each subpoena to eliminate any "no authorization" objection.
