# Subpoena Duces Tecum — Master Template
## CMIA § 56.10(c)(12) Disclosure Logs

**Form:** Judicial Council form **SUBP-010** (Civil Subpoena (Duces Tecum)) for records-only production, to be issued in the pending / filed action that gives subpoena power (primary vehicle: the in-progress § 1983 federal complaint; state vehicle: any open FL or civil matter). For a pre-litigation posture the parallel vehicle is the CCP § 2020.410 deposition subpoena for production of business records.

**One subpoena per medical provider** on the roster below. Each subpoena reuses this **Attachment — Records Demanded**.

## Attachment — Records Demanded

To: **[Privacy Officer / Custodian of Records]**, **[Provider Name]**, **[Address]**

**Patient:** Michael Hartmann (DOB: [redacted for filed version])
**Time window:** January 1, 2009 through the date of service of this subpoena.

You are commanded to produce the following records in your custody, possession, or control:

1. The complete **CMIA disclosure log / accounting of disclosures** maintained under Civ. Code § 56.10 and Civ. Code § 56.101 for the above-named patient, for the time window above.
2. Every **written request for disclosure** received under Civ. Code **§ 56.10(c)(12)** — i.e., any request from a probate court investigator in a conservatorship proceeding under Prob. Code § 1400 et seq., OR from a probate court investigator, probation officer, or domestic relations investigator engaged in determining initial or continuing guardianship under Prob. Code § 1500 et seq. — regardless of whether the request was granted.
3. Every **logged disclosure** made pursuant to § 56.10(c)(12): the disclosure date, identity and agency of the requesting investigator, the proceeding / case caption cited, the scope of information disclosed, and the disclosing employee's name.
4. Every **court order, subpoena, search warrant, administrative summons, or other compulsory process** received for the patient's medical information during the time window (Civ. § 56.10(b)(1)–(3)), and your response to each.
5. Every **§ 56.11 written authorization** signed by or on behalf of the patient that purported to authorize disclosure during the time window, including the identity of any attorney-in-fact, conservator, guardian, or agent who signed on the patient's behalf, and the facial-validity documentation (§ 56.11 form attestation).
6. Every record of **disclosure to an attorney-in-fact, conservator, or guardian** purportedly acting on the patient's behalf under Civ. § 56.10(b) or § 56.11 (Cerretani, Christina Marie; Hillberg, Ann; and any other identified representative) — production should include the authorizing document.
7. Any **internal compliance review, audit report, or breach-notification record** under Civ. § 56.36 / 45 CFR § 164.400–414 (HIPAA Breach Notification Rule) concerning disclosures of the patient's information during the time window.
8. Every **request-log audit or periodic review** your privacy office performs under your § 56.101 reasonable-safeguards program that references the patient during the time window.

**Scope clarifications:**

- "Disclosure" includes transmission, oral report, fax, email, portal share, printed copy, or remote access.
- "Investigator" includes employees, contract investigators, and third-party vendors acting under color of Prob. § 1826 / § 1851 / Fam. § 3118 / § 3164.
- If responsive records are destroyed under a retention schedule, produce the retention schedule and the destruction certification.

**Form of production:** Certified copies by records-custodian declaration (Evid. Code §§ 1560–1562). Electronic production (searchable PDF + native metadata) preferred; paper acceptable.

**Objections:** State with statutory specificity under § 56.10 / § 56.104 / Evid. § 1560. Blanket objections waived.

**Preservation demand:** Upon receipt, preserve all responsive records and any automated purge jobs pending compliance or court order.

## Legal anchors

- **Civ. Code § 56.10** — confidentiality + authorized disclosures.
- **Civ. Code § 56.10(c)(12)** — probate / conservatorship / guardianship investigator disclosure channel.
- **Civ. Code § 56.101** — reasonable-safeguards standard.
- **Civ. Code § 56.11** — written authorization requirements.
- **Civ. Code § 56.36** — remedies (actual damages, $1,000 nominal, punitive, attorney fees).
- **Civ. Code § 56.104** — psychotherapy disclosure limits.
- **Evid. Code §§ 1560–1562** — business-records custodian declaration.
- **Prob. Code § 1400 et seq. / § 1500 et seq.** — conservatorship / guardianship procedures.
- **45 CFR § 164.400–414** — HIPAA Breach Notification Rule (parallel federal).
- **CCP § 2020.410** — pre-trial deposition subpoena for business records (alternative vehicle).
- Standards: CA_CIV_56_10_CMIA, CA_PROB_1801_CONSERVATORSHIP_APPOINTMENT.
