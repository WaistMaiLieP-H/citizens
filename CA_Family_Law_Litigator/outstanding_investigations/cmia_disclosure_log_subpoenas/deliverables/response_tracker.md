# Response Tracker — CMIA § 56.10(c)(12) Subpoena Batch

## "Answered" looks like

Per provider, ANY ONE of:

1. **POSITIVE HIT** — provider produces a § 56.10(c)(12) disclosure log AND responsive records showing at least one disclosure to a probate / probation / domestic-relations investigator. This is the direct documentary proof of the covert conservatorship disclosure pipeline. File immediately into FAMLAW-005 (orchestration) + project_conservatorship_discovery.md evidence chain.
2. **NEGATIVE ATTESTATION** — custodian declaration that no § 56.10(c)(12) disclosures were made during the time window. Preserve — supports the "no investigator ever spoke to the providers" branch, which itself evidences a separate channel (fraud on records / spoof authorizations).
3. **GAP / DESTRUCTION** — responsive records were destroyed under a retention schedule. Obtain retention schedule + destruction certification. Preservation-spoliation brief candidate.
4. **OBJECTION** — provider objects under § 56.104 (psychotherapy) or § 56.10 confidentiality. Meet and confer; if unresolved, motion to compel (CCP § 1987.1 / Fed. R. Civ. P. 45(d)).

## Expected response time

- **State SUBP-010:** production date stated on the subpoena face; typical minimum 20 days from service (CCP § 2020.410(c)).
- **Fed. R. Civ. P. 45:** typical 14-day objection window; compliance date per subpoena (reasonable time).
- **Objection window:** 14 days (federal) / 5 days before production date (state meet-and-confer under CCP § 1985.3(g) by analogy).

## Follow-up protocol

- **Day 0:** service + POS filed.
- **Day 7:** confirm delivery.
- **Production date:** if no response, meet-and-confer letter within 3 business days.
- **Production date + 10:** file motion to compel (CCP § 1987.1 / Rule 45(d)(2)(B)(i)).
- **Any objection asserting § 56.104 psychotherapy:** narrow subpoena to the log-only (which is administrative metadata, not psychotherapy content) and re-serve.
