## Part 1 — Narrative: what went wrong with the 2025 fee waiver

The steward asserts the following on the record of the Clerk of the Court, and requests written correction:

1. In **2025**, Michael Hartmann, as a self-represented indigent litigant, filed or attempted to file an application to proceed in forma pauperis (**FW-001 Request to Waive Court Fees**) in Alameda County Superior Court in case **25FL122591** (and/or the parent case **RF10508853**). A **FW-003 Order on Court Fee Waiver** was either (a) never entered, (b) entered as a denial without the required hearing or written reasons under CRC Rule 3.56, or (c) entered but misrouted so as to block the intended **transfer to Solano County**.
2. Upon information and belief, the **Alameda clerk represented** that the case had been **transferred to Solano**, while **Solano confirmed no transfer had been received**. The fee waiver application, or the associated filing fee status, was the hinge on which transfer turned — because Alameda will not transmit a file where fees are unresolved and the receiving court will not accept an incomplete transmittal (CRC Rule 3.300).
3. That fee waiver failure was **structurally load-bearing**: it stranded case 25FL122591 between two counties, creating the jurisdictional vacuum that Christina Cerretani then exploited by taking the minor child Cole to Marin County, where a new filing (FL0002067) was initiated on or before 8/5/2025.
4. The steward is unable to determine — without the records requested here — whether the error was (a) an applicant error the clerk had a duty to flag under CRC Rule 3.54, (b) a clerk-generated rejection, (c) a court-generated FW-003 deficient under Rule 3.56, or (d) a combination of the above. Any one of these alternatives creates a right to correction.

## Part 3 — Clerk correction request (ministerial)

Under **CRC Rule 3.54** (clerk's duty to help applicants complete fee waiver forms) and the general ministerial-correction authority of the Clerk of the Court (CCP § 473(d); Gov. Code § 69844), the steward requests the following:

1. If an **FW-001** was filed but never ruled on within **5 court days** as required by CRC Rule 3.52(a), **enter a deemed-granted order** under Gov. Code § 68634(e) nunc pro tunc to the filing date and transmit that order to Solano.
2. If a **FW-003 denial** was entered without the written reasons required by CRC Rule 3.56(c) or without offering the applicant the **FW-006 hearing** required by Rule 3.56(d), **vacate the denial** and reset for hearing.
3. If a **clerk rejection** was issued under Gov. Code § 68634(b) for a form defect that the clerk had a duty to help the applicant cure under CRC Rule 3.54, **rescind the rejection** and deem the application filed as of the original tender date.
