**Note on Judicial Branch CPRA posture.** California superior courts are subject to **Rule 10.500** of the California Rules of Court for judicial administrative records rather than the full CPRA, but the substantive disclosure rights are materially equivalent for personnel / credential records of non-judicial employees (mediators, FCS staff). The request cites Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq. in the alternative; the Court should apply Rule 10.500 if it deems that framework controlling. The deadline language in the letter aligns with Rule 10.500(e)(4) as well.

**If the Court invokes Rule 10.500 exemption categories**, require the specific citation in writing and preserve for reply.
