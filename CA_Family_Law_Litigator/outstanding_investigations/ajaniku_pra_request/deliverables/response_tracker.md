## "Answered" looks like

Any ONE of:

1. **PRODUCTIVE:** Alameda Superior Court produces responsive records — credentials, certificates, personnel file. Record each document in evidence index. Cross-reference against Family Code §§ 1815 / 1816 / 3164(b). Update credential-audit finding.
2. **NEGATIVE CONFIRMATION:** Court responds in writing that **no responsive records exist**. This is the strongest possible outcome for FAMLAW-004 and the § 3164(b) challenge — puts facial defect on the record with a Court admission. Preserve the response in permanent evidence.
3. **PARTIAL + EXEMPTION:** Court produces some records and asserts exemption for the remainder. Capture exemption citations verbatim; prepare § 7922.000 reverse-CPRA / writ analysis.
