## Theory

The DV-130 issued 2025-10-17 is subject to vacatur under Fam. Code §§ 6320/6203 and procedural due process where the supporting petition / declarations fail strict compliance with Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 2015.5 (Kulshrestha v. First Union Commercial Corp. (2004) 33 Cal.4th 601) or where petition factual allegations do not meet the § 6203(a) statutory definition of abuse.

---

## Operative standards (PUBLISHED ADAM+EVE 2026-04-14)

- **CAL_CCP_2015_5** — declaration under penalty of perjury. Three elements (subscription; date of execution; place of execution if in-state OR "under the laws of the State of California" language if out-of-state). Missing any element = facial inadmissibility under Kulshrestha.
- **CA_FAM_6203** — § 6203(a) defines "abuse": (1) intentional or reckless infliction of bodily injury; (2) sexual assault; (3) reasonable apprehension of imminent serious bodily injury; (4) § 6320-enjoinable behavior. Allegations that do not map to one of these four are not "abuse" for DVPA purposes.
- **CA_FAM_6320** — § 6320(c) (SB 50, effective 2026-01-01) captures coercive-control-by-third-party-orchestration. Available as counter-theory to characterize Christina's conduct (Christina-Hillberg-McClaran orchestration pattern).
- **CA_FAM_6321** — § 6321 exclusion-from-residence orders: scope and findings required.
- **CA_FAM_6323** — § 6323 custody/visitation consequences of DVRO.
