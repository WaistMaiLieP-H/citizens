Marin contact, if any, is derivative of Homeward Bound Marin shelter placement — which was itself the product of the fabricated DVRO (see VAWA inversion under US_Federal_Housing_Litigator).
3. **Forum shopping:** Filing in Marin after two Alameda DVRO denials (25FL122591 petitioner denied; 25FL125059 petitioner denied) is textbook forum shopping — the doctrinal remedy is transfer back to Alameda or dismissal.
