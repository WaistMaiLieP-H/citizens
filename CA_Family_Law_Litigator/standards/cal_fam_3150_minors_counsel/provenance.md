## Chapter enactment

Chapter 10 (Appointment of Counsel to Represent Child) was enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10 as part of the 1993 Family Code recodification and became operative January 1, 1994.

## Evolution layer notes (partial)

The 2010 AB 939 amendments (effective January 1, 2011) refined §§ 3150 and 3151 — principally the cross-references to the Rules of Court framework for appointment, compensation, and qualifications. Earlier subject-matter provisions for appointment of counsel for children appeared in former Civil Code sections prior to the 1993 recodification; the recodification gathered and structured the authority in the current Chapter 10 form.
