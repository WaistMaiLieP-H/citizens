# Case Law — §§ 3150-3153 Minor's Counsel

## Scope

This directory holds case law that construes §§ 3150-3153 (Chapter 10). Minor's-counsel appellate law is comparatively thin because (a) minor's counsel's role itself limits the party who can appeal and (b) the witness bar and records-access procedures generate fewer evidentiary rulings than the custody merits.

## Priority cases to populate

1. **In re Marriage of Metzger (2014) 224 Cal.App.4th 1441** — Addresses scope of minor's-counsel participation and standing in a custody modification context; often cited for the proposition that minor's counsel is a party-equivalent within the meaning of § 3151(b).
2. **In re Marriage of Obergfell** — Appointment discretion under § 3150 and the best-interest threshold.
3. **Cases applying Cal. Rules of Court rule 5.242** — Qualifications and continuing-education standards for appointed counsel.
4. **Cases addressing the § 3151(b) witness bar** — Where trial courts permitted examination of minor's counsel and appellate courts reversed or remanded for the structural bar violation.
5. **CPS-records access cases under § 3152** — In-camera-review procedure and the non-waiver of confidentiality doctrine.
