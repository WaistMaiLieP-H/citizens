# Cross-References — Family Code § 1816

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator

| Standard | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `cal_fam_1815_counselor_qualifications` | § 1815 establishes the credential floor; § 1816 is the mandatory DV training layer on top of that floor. An evaluator must satisfy BOTH. "Evaluator" in § 1816(a)(2) expressly cross-references § 1815. |
| `cal_fam_3164_mediator_qualifications` | § 3164 mirrors § 1815 for mediators; § 1816(a)(2) expressly includes "a mediator described in Section 3164." Same relationship — § 3164 is credential floor, § 1816 is DV training overlay. |
| `cal_crc_5_215_dv_protocol` | CRC Rule 5.215 governs mediator conduct in DV cases including separate sessions; § 1816 training is the predicate competency for applying CRC 5.215 correctly. A mediator cannot properly implement CRC 5.215 without § 1816 training in DV dynamics and safety structuring. |
| `cal_crc_5_210_custody_mediation` | CRC 5.210 governs the custody mediation program; § 1816 sets the training requirement for every mediator in that program. |
| `family_code_3011_best_interest` | Best interest of the child is the ultimate standard; § 1816 training is how the Legislature ensures the evaluators making best-interest recommendations are competent to assess DV dynamics that bear directly on child safety. |
| `cal_fam_6320_dvpa_enjoinable_behaviors` | DV cases triggering DVRO also require competent custody evaluation; § 1816 is the training floor for evaluators in those cases. |
| `cal_pen_13701_le_dv_response` | Police incident reports are collateral sources expressly named in § 1816(d)(5)(I); a § 1816-trained evaluator must seek these. |

## Statutory Cross-References

- **Family Code § 1815** — minimum qualifications for supervising/associate counselors in family conciliation courts
- **Family Code § 3110** — court-appointed investigators
- **Family Code § 3110.5** — child custody evaluator qualifications (private and court-connected)
- **Family Code § 3113** — separate sessions requirement; referenced in § 1816(d)(1)(D) as part of mandatory advanced training curriculum
- **Family Code § 3164** — mediator qualifications; "evaluator" in § 1816(a)(2) expressly includes § 3164 mediators
- **Evidence Code § 730** — court-appointed experts; covered by § 1816 training requirement
- **Penal Code § 1203.097(c)** — certified batterer intervention programs; referenced in § 1816(d)(4)(A) as training content for evaluators

## Administrative References

- **Administrative Office of the Courts (now Judicial Council)** — § 1816 vests training approval authority in the AOC/Judicial Council; authorized providers must be AOC-approved
- **Judicial Council Standard 5.230** — implements § 1816 training standards for child custody evaluators
- **CRC Rule 5.220** — child custody evaluation standards; incorporates § 1816 training requirements
