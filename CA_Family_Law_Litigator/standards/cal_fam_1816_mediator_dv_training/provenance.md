## Legislative Intent

§ 1816 was enacted in response to legislative findings that:
1. Untrained mediators and custody evaluators were systematically misreading DV dynamics in custody cases
2. "High conflict" mislabeling was exposing children and victim parents to continued danger
3. Evaluators needed specific training in collateral source review, psychological trauma, power/control dynamics, and community resources — topics not covered by general mental health licensure
4. DV training must be ongoing (not one-time) because law, social science, and community resources evolve
