# Family Code § 3027 — False Allegations of Child Abuse or Neglect

## Layer 1 — Rule

### Statutory text (current, as of 2026-04-15)

**§ 3027(a).** "Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit the court from considering, as a factor in determining the best interest of the child, an allegation of child abuse or neglect."

**§ 3027(b).** "If allegations of child abuse, including child sexual abuse, are made during a child custody proceeding and the court has concerns regarding the child's safety, the court may take any reasonable, temporary steps as the court, in its discretion, deems appropriate under the circumstances to protect the child's safety until an investigation of the allegations can be completed."

**§ 3027.1(a).** "If a court determines, based on the investigation described in Section 3027 or other evidence presented to it, that an accusation of child abuse or neglect made during a child custody proceeding is false and the person making the accusation knew it to be false at the time the accusation was made, the court may impose reasonable money sanctions, not to exceed all costs incurred by the party accused as a direct result of defending the accusation, and reasonable attorney's fees incurred in recovering the sanctions, against the person making the accusation."

**§ 3027.1(b).** Sanctions authorized under subdivision (a) shall be imposed only after notice to the person against whom the sanctions are proposed to be imposed and an opportunity for that person to be heard.

### Plain English

If a parent accuses the other parent of child abuse or neglect during a custody case, the court can take temporary protective steps while it investigates. But if the court later finds the accusation was false **and** the accuser knew it was false when they made it, the court can order the accuser to pay the other parent's legal fees and defense costs — up to the full amount spent fighting the accusation. The accuser must be given notice and a hearing before sanctions are imposed.

Two pieces matter: the accusation must be **false**, and the accuser must have **known it was false at the time**. Honest-but-mistaken accusations do not trigger § 3027.1 sanctions.
