# Family Code § 3027 / § 3027.1 — Layer 4: Cross-References (read-only)

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator (internal)

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — § 3027(a) expressly ties abuse allegations to § 3011 best-interest analysis.
- **FAMCODE-§3044** — rebuttable presumption against custody to DV perpetrator (pending manifest build).
- **FAMCODE-§3118** — court-ordered evaluation where sexual abuse alleged (pending manifest build).
- **cal_fam_6320_dvpa_enjoinable_behaviors** — DVROs are a frequent vehicle for false abuse claims; § 6320 defines the conduct, § 3027.1 polices knowing falsity.
- **cal_fam_3020_custody_policy** — § 3020 public-policy declaration (frequent and continuing contact) is the backdrop § 3027.1 protects.
- **cal_fam_3046_absence_dv** — parallel anti-weaponization concern: § 3046 protects a parent from absence-based penalty where DV drove the absence.
- **cal_fam_217_live_testimony_right** — § 3027.1 "knew it to be false" element requires credibility findings that demand live testimony under § 217.
- **cal_ccp_1005_motion_notice** — § 3027.1(b) notice requirement interacts with CCP § 1005 motion timing.
- **cal_ccp_2015_5_declaration_perjury** — knowingly false declarations invoking § 3027 allegations trigger CCP § 2015.5 perjury exposure in addition to § 3027.1 sanctions.

## Cross-Citizen (read-only)

## Federal cross-Citizen
