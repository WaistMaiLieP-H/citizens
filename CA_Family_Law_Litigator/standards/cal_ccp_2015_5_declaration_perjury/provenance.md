## Legislative History

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1965 | CCP § 2015.5 originally enacted — established unsworn declarations under penalty of perjury as equivalent to sworn affidavits for California proceedings |
| 1991 | Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 900, Sec. 1 — added Form (b) (execution at any place, within or without the state); this is the version currently in force |

**Significance of 1991 amendment:** Before 1991, the statute required place of execution in all cases. The 1991 amendment added Form (b) — allowing declarations executed anywhere to satisfy the statute using only a date and the "under the laws of the State of California" phrase. This is why Form (b) is universally preferred: it is the more recent, more flexible form, and it eliminated the place-defect trap.
