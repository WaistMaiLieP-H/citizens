## ADAM Review (Triple Constraint)

**Governing Guidelines:** PASS — § 2015.5 verbatim correctly extracted. Two statutory forms ((a) executed-within-California: "under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct" + date + place; (b) executed-anywhere: adds "under the laws of the State of California" + date only) accurately preserved. 1991 amendment (Stats. 1991, Ch. 900, § 1) noted.

**Standards of Creation:** PASS — The three load-bearing elements (subscription, execution date, California-laws language if executed outside state) are the defect-audit checklist.

**SOC:** PASS — § 2015.5 has been interpreted strictly: missing date, missing place (for in-state execution), or missing "under the laws of the State of California" language (for out-of-state execution) each renders the declaration inadmissible.
