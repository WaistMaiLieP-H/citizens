## Statutory Cross-References

- **Penal Code § 118** — perjury; a willfully false § 2015.5 declaration constitutes perjury
- **28 U.S.C. § 1746** — federal analog; applicable in NDCA and other federal proceedings; NOT applicable in California state courts or state agencies
- **Welfare & Institutions Code § 13950 et seq.** (CalVCB) — CalVCB proceedings are California state proceedings governed by § 2015.5
- **CRC Rule 2.257** — Electronic signatures in California courts; governs electronically signed declarations; does not relax § 2015.5 language requirement
- **CRC Rule 3.1306** — Declarations and affidavits as evidence at hearings; declarations must comply with § 2015.5 to be considered
