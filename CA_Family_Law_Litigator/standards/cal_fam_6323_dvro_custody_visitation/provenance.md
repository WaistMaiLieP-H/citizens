# Provenance — Cal. Fam. Code § 6323

## Statute Origin

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 6323
**Title:** DVPA — Temporary Custody and Visitation Orders

**Legislative Purpose:** § 6323 was enacted to ensure that DVPA proceedings do not result in custody and visitation orders that expose DV victims and children to continued risk. The Legislature recognized that the exchange moment — the moment of physical child transfer — is a high-risk moment for violence and harassment. § 6323(c)'s specificity requirement was designed to eliminate ambiguity that perpetrators exploit to manufacture conflicts.

**Original Enactment:** Part of the DVPA codification in Family Code, effective January 1, 1994. The "shall" requirements in subsections (c)-(f) have been added and strengthened through subsequent amendments reflecting legislative intent to make DV-protective custody provisions mandatory rather than discretionary.

## Amendment History

Notable subsequent amendments added or strengthened the "shall" language in subsections (c)-(f). The five-year DV lookback in subsection (d) and the firearm provision in subsection (f) (referencing § 6389) reflect later additions. Full amendment history available at the LegInfo URL.
