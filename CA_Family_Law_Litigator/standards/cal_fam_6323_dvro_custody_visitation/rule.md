# California Family Code § 6323 — DVRO Custody and Visitation Orders

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 6323
**Title:** DVPA — Custody and Visitation Orders in DVRO Proceedings

---

## Verbatim Statute Text

(a) In a proceeding described in Section 6221, the court may issue an order determining the temporary custody and visitation of a minor child.

(b) An order under this section does not determine or affect the right to custody or visitation of a minor child except as provided in subdivision (a). An order under this section does not constitute a finding of paternity.

(c) The court, when making an order for visitation in cases in which domestic violence is alleged and where the court determines that visitation is in the best interest of the minor child, shall specify the time, day, place, and manner of transfer of the child for visitation to limit the child's exposure to potential domestic conflict or violence and to ensure the safety of all parties.

(d) The court, when ordering custody or visitation in a proceeding under this section, shall consider whether any party seeking custody of, or visitation with, the child has committed domestic violence within the preceding five years.

(e) The court shall consider whether the best interest of the child requires that any visitation be denied or any custody arrangement be limited to require a neutral third person be present during the visit or during the exchange, or whether the safety of any party requires that the visit or exchange take place in a protected setting.

(f) The court shall consider whether a party is required to possess, or has been found to own or possess, a firearm in violation of Section 6389.

---

**SHALL Language:** Subsections (c), (d), (e), and (f) all use "SHALL" — mandatory consideration, not discretionary.
**Transfer Protocol:** § 6323(c) mandates specific time/day/place/manner of transfer — this is not optional.
**Five-Year Lookback:** § 6323(d) requires consideration of DV within preceding five years.
**Firearm Finding:** § 6323(f) requires consideration of § 6389 firearm prohibition.
