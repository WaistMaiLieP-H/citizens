# California Penal Code § 11165.6 — Definition of Child Abuse and Neglect

**Code:** California Penal Code
**Section:** 11165.6
**Title:** Child Abuse and Neglect Reporting Act (CANRA) — Definition of Child Abuse

---

## Verbatim Statute Text

As used in this article, "child abuse or neglect" includes physical injury or death inflicted by other than accidental means upon a child by another person, sexual abuse as defined in Section 11165.1, neglect as defined in Section 11165.2, the willful harming or injuring of a child or the endangering of the person or health of a child, as defined in Section 11165.3, and unlawful corporal punishment or injury as defined in Section 11165.4. "Child abuse or neglect" does not include a mutual combat situation to which both parties consent.

"Child abuse or neglect" does not include the use of reasonable and necessary force by a peace officer acting within the course and scope of employment as a peace officer, or a school authority exercising the authority granted to them by law.

---

## Component Definitions (by cross-reference)

- **§ 11165.1** — Sexual abuse: sexual assault, sexual exploitation
- **§ 11165.2** — Neglect: general neglect and severe neglect
- **§ 11165.3** — Willful cruelty or unjustifiable punishment; endangering health or person
- **§ 11165.4** — Unlawful corporal punishment or injury

## Exclusions
- Mutual combat (both parties consenting adults)
- Reasonable and necessary force by peace officers acting in scope of employment
- School authorities exercising lawful authority

## Cross-Reference to Family Code
Cal. Fam. Code § 3011(a)(2)(B) uses the § 11165.6 definition when evaluating whether a party has perpetrated child abuse in the best-interest analysis. Any act that qualifies as "child abuse or neglect" under § 11165.6 is a mandatory factor in the § 3011 custody analysis.
