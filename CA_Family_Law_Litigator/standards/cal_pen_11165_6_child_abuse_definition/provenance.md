## Context

§ 11165.6 is the umbrella definition section in CANRA. It defines "child abuse or neglect" to encompass all of the specific conduct categories defined in §§ 11165–11165.5. It functions as an aggregating definition: when a CANRA provision refers to "child abuse or neglect," it means the conduct defined across the preceding sections, all of which § 11165.6 incorporates by reference.

---

## Legislative History

CANRA was enacted in the 1980s to create a mandatory reporting framework for child abuse. § 11165.6 has been amended multiple times to reflect expanding understanding of what constitutes reportable child abuse, including:
- Physical abuse
- Sexual abuse and exploitation
- Willful cruelty or unjustifiable punishment
- Unlawful corporal punishment
- Neglect (general and severe)
- Abuse in out-of-home care
