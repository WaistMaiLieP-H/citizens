# Cross-References — Cal. Pen. Code § 11165.6

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — § 3011(a)(2)(B) expressly incorporates the § 11165.6 definition; any conduct meeting § 11165.6 is a mandatory consideration in the best-interest analysis; this is the primary operational connection
- **cal_fam_3020_custody_policy** — § 3020's mandate to ensure child safety and welfare operates through the § 11165.6 definition; what the Legislature defines as child abuse is what § 3020 commands courts to protect against
- **cal_fam_6203_dvpa_abuse_definition** — § 6203 (DVPA abuse) and § 11165.6 (CANRA abuse) have overlapping but distinct definitions; conduct can qualify as DVPA abuse without being § 11165.6 child abuse, and vice versa; both must be analyzed
- **cal_fam_1816_mediator_dv_training** — § 1816(c)(2) requires evaluators to receive basic training in child abuse (cross-referencing § 11165.6's definitional scheme); an evaluator who cannot identify § 11165.6 conduct is not qualified under § 1816
- **cal_pen_13701_le_dv_response** — Where § 11165.6 child abuse is involved, § 13701's DV response policies apply; law enforcement response to child abuse calls follows the same § 13701 framework

## Within Other Citizens

- **Cal. Pen. Code §§ 11165.1-11165.4** — Component definitions incorporated by reference in § 11165.6; physical abuse (§ 11165.3), sexual abuse (§ 11165.1), neglect (§ 11165.2), unlawful corporal punishment (§ 11165.4)
- **Cal. Pen. Code § 11166** (mandatory reporting obligation) — Where conduct meets § 11165.6's definition, § 11166 mandatory reporters must report; failure to report is itself a misdemeanor; this creates an evidentiary layer for custody proceedings
- **Welf. & Inst. Code § 300** (dependency jurisdiction) — § 300 dependency petitions are filed where § 11165.6 child abuse is established; the definitional link between § 11165.6 and § 300 means a CANRA finding can trigger dependency court jurisdiction
