# Cross-References — CCP § 1005

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator

| Standard | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `family_code_3011_best_interest` | Best interest hearings (RFOs for custody modification) are subject to § 1005 notice; a court cannot grant a custody modification on improperly noticed motion |
| `cal_fam_6320_dvpa_enjoinable_behaviors` | DVRO applications: temporary DV orders are ex parte (not subject to § 1005); contested hearings on permanent DVRO are subject to § 1005 notice |
| `cal_fam_6321_dvro_exclusion` | Same — contested exclusion order hearings require § 1005 notice |
| `cal_ccp_2015_5_declaration_perjury` | Moving and opposing declarations must comply with § 2015.5; § 1005 governs when they must be filed/served |

## Statutory Cross-References

- **CCP § 1010.6** — electronic service; § 1005(b) provides that electronic service adds 2 court days to the notice period
- **CCP § 1013** — mail service extensions for response periods; does NOT apply to § 1005 advance notice calculations — common confusion point
- **CCP § 1010** — service methods generally
- **CCP § 1013a** — proof of service requirements
- **Family Code § 215** — service requirements in family law proceedings; § 215 incorporates § 1005 for RFOs
- **CRC Rule 5.92** — Request for Order procedures in family court; incorporates § 1005 notice requirements
- **CRC Rule 5.165** — Ex parte applications in family court; ex parte relief bypasses § 1005 with court permission

## Practice Note

In family court, the local rules of the specific superior court often provide additional notice and filing deadlines that supplement (and sometimes shorten) § 1005 defaults. Always check local rules of the specific county — Alameda, Contra Costa, San Francisco, etc. — in addition to § 1005.
