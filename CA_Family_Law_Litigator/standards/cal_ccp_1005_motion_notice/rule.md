# California Code of Civil Procedure § 1005 — Notice of Motion; Time Requirements

**Status: PROPOSED**
**Steward: Michael Hartmann**
**Citizen: CA_Family_Law_Litigator**

---

## Verbatim Statute Text

*Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1005(b):*

Unless otherwise ordered or specifically provided by law, all moving and supporting papers shall be served and filed at least 16 court days before the hearing. The moving and supporting papers served shall be a copy of the papers filed or to be filed with the court. However, if the notice is served by mail, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by 5 calendar days if the place of mailing and the place of address are both within the State of California, 10 calendar days if either the place of mailing or the place of address is outside the State of California but within the United States, and 20 calendar days if either the place of mailing or the place of address is outside the United States. If the notice is served by facsimile transmission or express mail or another method of delivery providing for overnight delivery, the required 16-day period of notice before the hearing shall be increased by two calendar days.

*Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1005(b) — Opposition Papers:*

Any opposition to the motion shall be served and filed at least nine court days, and any reply to the opposition shall be served and filed at least five court days, before the hearing. The court, or a judge thereof, may prescribe a shorter time.

*Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1005(c):*

This section does not apply to proceedings pursuant to Part 4 (commencing with Section 1285) [arbitration proceedings] or to any proceeding for which a statute specifically provides for a different notice period.

## Service Extension Summary Table

| Service Method | Extension |
|---|---|
| Personal delivery | 0 days added |
| Mail (CA to CA) | +5 calendar days |
| Mail (one party out-of-state) | +10 calendar days |
| Mail (international) | +20 calendar days |
| Fax or overnight delivery | +2 calendar days |
| Electronic service (Code Civ. Proc. § 1010.6) | +2 court days |

| Paper | Minimum Notice Before Hearing |
|---|---|
| Moving papers (personal service) | 16 court days |
| Moving papers (CA mail) | 16 court days + 5 calendar days |
| Opposition | 9 court days |
| Reply | 5 court days |

## Critical Rule: § 1013 Does NOT Apply to Motion Papers

California Code of Civil Procedure § 1013, which extends time for response periods when service is by mail, does NOT apply to the timing of motions and hearings governed by § 1005. The § 1013 extension adds time after receipt of a document to respond — it does not extend the advance notice required before a hearing. These are separate rules that serve different purposes. Confusing them is a common error.
