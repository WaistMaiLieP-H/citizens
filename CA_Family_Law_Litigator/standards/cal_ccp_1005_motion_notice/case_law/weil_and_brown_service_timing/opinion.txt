Weil & Brown, California Practice Guide: Civil Procedure Before Trial
Chapter 9 — Motions

NOTE: This folder contains a Weil & Brown practice guide reference, not a reported case. It is included as a reference for the standard practitioner understanding of § 1005 but cannot be cited as binding authority in any filed document.

REFERENCE SUMMARY:
Weil & Brown confirm the standard service timing rules under § 1005: 16 court days minimum for personal service, plus applicable additions for mail/fax/overnight/electronic. The guide specifically addresses the § 1013 confusion point: "Do not confuse the rules for extending the time to respond to papers served on you (§ 1013) with the rules for computing notice periods before a hearing (§ 1005). These are separate rules with separate purposes." The guide recommends always building in extra time beyond the minimum to avoid disputes over counting.

USE: For explanation and internal understanding of § 1005 mechanics. Do not cite in filed documents. Use Carlton v. Quint (2000) or other reported cases for citation purposes.
