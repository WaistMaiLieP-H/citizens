## Origin

The best-interest-of-the-child standard in California predates the Family Code. The predecessor was Civil Code § 4600, which used similar language. When the Family Code was enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, the Legislature consolidated family law statutes and codified the best-interest factors in their current location. The 1993 reorganization (Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 115.5) repealed and re-added Chapter 2 in its current form.
