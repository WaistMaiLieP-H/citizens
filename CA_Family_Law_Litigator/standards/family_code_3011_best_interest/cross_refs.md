## Steward Case Connections

**Alameda County RF09456481 / Marin FL0002067**
- Both proceedings required § 3011 best-interest analysis at every custody modification.
- The September 2010 custody change via Sala Ajaniku mediation fed recommendations into the § 3011 analysis without a qualified mediator — the foundational input was defective.
- Three unsigned police reports placed before the court as § 3011(a)(2)(B) corroboration do not meet the statute's corroboration standard.
- The court's failure to issue § 3011(a)(5) written findings when abuse allegations were before it is preserved as appellate error in each proceeding where the omission occurred.

**Cole Hartmann (DOB 10/24/2008)**
- Every custody modification from 2009 to present required application of § 3011 to Cole's circumstances. Each order made without adequate written findings when abuse allegations were present is defective under § 3011(a)(5).
