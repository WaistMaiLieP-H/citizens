## Legislative History (Compressed)

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1999 | **AB 840 (Stats. 1999, Ch. 980)** — original enactment of § 3044. Rebuttable presumption introduced in response to Judicial Council studies documenting that DV findings were being treated as one § 3011 factor among many, with the § 3020 frequent-contact preference functionally controlling. |
| 2003 | Refinement of rebuttal factors; introduction of batterer's treatment, substance counseling, parenting-class elements in what became § 3044(b)(2). |
| 2012 | Restructuring of § 3044(b) to clarify the paragraph (1) best-interest requirement as a condition precedent to the paragraph (2) factor balancing. |
| 2018 | **Jaime G. v. H.L.** (2018) 25 Cal.App.5th 794 — Court of Appeal holds § 3044 requires specific findings on each § 3044(b) factor. Conclusory rebuttal orders reversed. |
| 2024 | **SB 899 (Stats. 2024, Ch. 544, Sec. 7)** — repeal and re-add of § 3044 effective 1/1/2025, operative 1/1/2026. Codifies *Jaime G.* specificity in new § 3044(f). Cleans up § 3044(d) predicate mechanisms. Preserves the core inversion and stripped-contact-preference structure. |
