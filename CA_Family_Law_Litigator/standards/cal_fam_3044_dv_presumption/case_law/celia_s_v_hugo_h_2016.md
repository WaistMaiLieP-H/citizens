# Celia S. v. Hugo H.

**Citation:** (2016) 3 Cal.App.5th 655
**Court:** California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District, Division Three (2016)

## FACTS

The trial court applied § 3044 and determined the presumption was rebutted, basing its rebuttal determination substantially on the Family Court Services (FCS) recommendation and a child custody evaluator's report. The trial court did not make independent factfindings on the § 3044(b) factors; it adopted the FCS and evaluator conclusions. The mother appealed.

## HOLDING — Family Code § 3044(e)

The Court of Appeal reversed. § 3044(e) expressly prohibits a court from basing its findings on § 3044 "solely on conclusions reached by a child custody evaluator or on the recommendation of the Family Court Services staff." The court must consider any relevant, admissible evidence submitted by the parties and must make independent factfindings. Adoption of the FCS recommendation without independent analysis violates § 3044(e) and is reversible error.

## SIGNIFICANCE

*Celia S.* is the anti-capture case. It enforces § 3044(e)'s structural safeguard against trial-court delegation to mediators and evaluators. Where a DV-perpetration finding or a § 3044 rebuttal rests on FCS or evaluator conclusions alone, the order is defective.
