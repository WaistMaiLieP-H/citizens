## Statute Origin

**Code:** California Civil Code
**Section:** 52.1
**Popular Name:** Tom Bane Civil Rights Act (named for Assemblymember Tom Bane)

## Current Form

**Leginfo URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=52.1.&lawCode=CIV

## Amendment History

- **1987:** Enacted as AB 63 (Bane). Original purpose: protect individuals from interference with rights through violence or threats of violence. Initially focused on hate crimes and racial intimidation.
- **2000s:** Judicial expansion beyond original hate crime context; courts apply to law enforcement misconduct
- **2015:** Clarification of the "speech alone" exception (§ 52.1(k)) following constitutional challenge concerns
- **2019:** Amendments specifying peace officer immunity exclusion more explicitly (§ 52.1(n))
- **2020:** B.B. v. County of Los Angeles resolved circuit split on the coercion element standard for law enforcement defendants
- **Current:** As of January 1, 2024

## Related Statute — Damages

Civil Code § 52(b) provides the triple damages and $25,000 civil penalty framework referenced in § 52.1. It must be read together with § 52.1 to understand the full damages exposure.
