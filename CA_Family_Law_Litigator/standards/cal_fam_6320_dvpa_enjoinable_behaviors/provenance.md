## Origin

Division 10 (Prevention of Domestic Violence) was added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Section 6320 was part of the original codification of the Domestic Violence Prevention Act (DVPA) into the Family Code. The DVPA's predecessor was the former Civil Code § 4516 and related provisions. The 1993 reorganization consolidated all domestic violence protective order authority into Division 10.

The 2021 SB 1141 amendment was a major substantive change: it moved California from a case-by-case "disturbing the peace" analysis (under Nadkarni and similar cases) to a statutory definition that explicitly includes coercive control. California became one of the first states to codify coercive control as domestic violence.

The 2025 SB 50 amendment refined the 2021 language and added the third-party orchestration provisions — addressing conduct committed "through the use of a third party."
