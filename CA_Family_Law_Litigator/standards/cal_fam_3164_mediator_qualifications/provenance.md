## Origin

Section 3164 was enacted as part of the massive 1993 reorganization of the Family Code (Stats. 1993, Ch. 219). Chapter 11 of Division 8 — governing mediation of custody and visitation — was repealed and re-added in its current form. Section 3164 has not been amended since its 1994 enactment. The statute reflects the Legislature's deliberate decision to apply the § 1815 counselor qualification floor to all custody mediators, regardless of whether they are employed by a conciliation court or designated from outside the court system.

The SHALL language in § 3164(b) was original to the 1993 enactment — it was not added by amendment. The Legislature chose mandatory language from the beginning.
