# Family Code § 1815 — Cross-References

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator

| Standard | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `cal_fam_3164_mediator_qualifications` | § 3164(b) incorporates § 1815 by reference; § 1815 is the substantive qualification standard; § 3164 is the procedural trigger — these two statutes must always be read together |
| `family_code_3011_best_interest` | An unqualified mediator's recommendation is not a valid input into the § 3011 best-interest analysis; a court that relies on a defective recommendation has not actually conducted the required § 3011 analysis |
| `cal_fam_3020_custody_policy` | A custody determination that flows from an unqualified mediator's recommendation violates § 3020's mandate that health/safety/welfare be the primary concern — the court cannot outsource that determination to an unqualified person |
| `cal_crc_5_210_custody_mediation` | CRC 5.210 governs the mediation process; mediators operating under CRC 5.210 must satisfy § 1815 through the § 3164(b) chain |
| `cal_fam_1816_mediator_dv_training` | § 1815(a)(7) requires DV training per § 1816; the two statutes form a pair; § 1816 defines the DV training content requirement |

## Steward Case Connections

**Sala Ajaniku — September 2010, Alameda County RF09456481**
- Ajaniku served as the mediator whose recommendation led to the September 2010 custody change giving custody to Christina Cerretani.
- No verifiable credentials exist for Ajaniku in the California BBS or Board of Psychology public databases.
- If Ajaniku: (a) did not hold a master's degree in a qualifying field (§ 1815(a)(1)); or (b) did not have two years of qualifying counseling experience (§ 1815(a)(2)); or (c) did not have DV training per § 1816 (§ 1815(a)(7)) — then Ajaniku did not satisfy § 1815 and the mediation was conducted by an unqualified person.
- Via § 3164(b), this means the mediation violated § 3164(b)'s SHALL provision, making the recommendation procedurally defective.
- Under Montenegro v. Diaz, a custody order entered in reliance on a procedurally defective mediation is not entitled to changed-circumstances protection and is subject to full § 3011 re-examination.
