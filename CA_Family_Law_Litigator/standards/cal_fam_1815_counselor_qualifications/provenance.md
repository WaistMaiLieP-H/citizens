## Origin

The qualification requirements for conciliation court counselors predate the 1992 Family Code. They were carried forward from predecessor provisions in the Code of Civil Procedure governing family conciliation courts. The 1992 codification into the Family Code restructured and restated these requirements. The 2006 amendment reflected the Legislature's recognition that DV training was essential given the overlap between family law proceedings and domestic violence situations.
