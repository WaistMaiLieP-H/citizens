## Steward Case Connections

**Contra Costa County Family Court Services (2009-2025):**
All mediators assigned to the custody proceedings in the Hartmann/Cerretani matter are subject to Rule 5.210 qualification verification. Obtain mediator names from court records, then request their certification files through PRA or court administrative records. Mediators who issued recommendations without complete evaluations are documented instances of Rule 5.210(g) violations.

**DV Screening Audit:**
Given the documented history of domestic violence protective orders in the case, each mediation assignment should have been preceded by DV screening per Rule 5.210(h). Court administrative files should show the screening checklist. If no screening documentation exists, this is an independent procedural defect.

**Separate Session Violations:**
Any mediation session conducted jointly despite Michael's presence and the documented DV history, without a waiver on the record, is a Rule 5.210(i) violation. Joint sessions in a DV case allow the abusing party to exercise control over the victim's statements. Recommendations issuing from those sessions are built on coerced or manipulated testimony.
