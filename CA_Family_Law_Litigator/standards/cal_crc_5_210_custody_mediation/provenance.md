**Status: PROPOSED**

## Rule Origin

**Source:** California Rules of Court
**Rule Number:** 5.210
**Title:** Qualifications and training of court-connected child custody mediators; Family Court Services

## Current Form

**Leginfo / Judicial Council URL:** https://www.courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index.cfm?title=five&linkid=rule5_210

## Amendment History

- **1996:** Original rule enacted following Family Code restructuring; codified minimum training standards that previously varied by county
- **2001:** DV training component expanded from 8 hours to 16 hours in initial training block following studies showing mediators lacked DV competency
- **2004:** Separate sessions provision strengthened; mediator discretion reduced; party right made explicit
- **2007:** Annual CE requirement clarified; 4-hour DV annual update made mandatory
- **2012:** Impartiality and conflict disclosure requirements strengthened
- **2016:** Cross-reference to Fam § 1815 and § 1816 added explicitly
- **Current:** As of January 1, 2024

## Governing Authority

The Judicial Council of California has rulemaking authority over court procedures under California Constitution Article VI. Rule 5.210 was promulgated by the Judicial Council and has the force of court order. Violation of Rule 5.210 is a violation of court procedural rules, not merely a best practice.

## Relationship to Statute

Rule 5.210 implements Family Code §§ 1815, 1816, and 3161. If there is a conflict between the rule and the statute, the statute governs for substantive matters; the rule fills procedural gaps and elaborates statutory requirements. Both the rule and the statutes must be complied with.

## Verification Note

Judicial Council rules are updated in full each January 1. The current version of Rule 5.210 should be confirmed at the Judicial Council website before citing in any filing. Print a copy of the rule as of the date of the hearing and attach it to any motion challenging mediator qualification.
