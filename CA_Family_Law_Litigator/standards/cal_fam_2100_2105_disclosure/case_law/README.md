# Case Law — §§ 2100-2105 Disclosure

## Scope

This directory holds case law construing Chapter 9 disclosure obligations and the § 2122 set-aside machinery. Because Chapter 9 is well-established, the appellate case law is substantial and growing.

## Priority cases to populate

1. **In re Marriage of Rossi (2001) 90 Cal.App.4th 34** — Wife concealed $1.3M lottery winnings from dissolution disclosure. Court applied § 1101(h) and awarded 100% of the asset to the deceived spouse for willful nondisclosure. Anchor case for § 1101(h) + Chapter 9 integration.
2. **In re Marriage of Feldman (2007) 153 Cal.App.4th 1470** — Established that disclosure noncompliance is independently sanctionable; fiduciary-duty breach does not require specific asset loss to justify sanctions.
3. **In re Marriage of Kieturakis (2006) 138 Cal.App.4th 56** — Mediated settlements and § 2105(d) waiver adequacy; addresses the line between valid mutual waiver and inadequate waiver.
4. **In re Marriage of Brewer & Federici (2001) 93 Cal.App.4th 1334** — Evaluation of disclosure adequacy; addresses particularity requirement under § 2104(c).
5. **In re Marriage of Fell (1997) 55 Cal.App.4th 1058** — Early case applying set-aside for inadequate disclosure.
6. **Marriage of Heggie (2002) 99 Cal.App.4th 28** — Discretion in set-aside analysis; balancing finality interest against Chapter 9 integrity.
