## Chapter enactment

Chapter 9 (Disclosure of Assets and Liabilities, §§ 2100-2113) was added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 107 as part of the 1993 Family Code recodification and became operative January 1, 1994.

## Evolution layer notes (partial)

Chapter 9 has been amended at multiple points since its 1993/1994 introduction. The pattern of amendments has generally been tightening: shortened waiver paths, broader fiduciary scope, expanded partial-set-aside relief. AB 1817 (2019) was the most recent consolidated tightening, refining § 2102's fiduciary scope and § 2104's tax-return attachment rule.
