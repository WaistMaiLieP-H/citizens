# Family Code § 2030 — Layer 4: Cross-References (read-only)

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator (internal)

- **cal_fam_217_live_testimony_right** — fee hearings are § 217 hearings; live testimony right applies to contested income/asset disputes.
- **cal_fam_3170_mandatory_custody_mediation** — mediation does not extinguish § 2030 access; fees can fund preparation for mediation.
- **cal_fam_3027_false_child_abuse_sanctions** — § 3027.1 sanctions are a separate cost-recovery mechanism (wrongdoing-based); § 2030 is need-based; both can stack.
- **cal_ccp_1005_motion_notice** — § 2031 noticed-motion procedure runs on CCP § 1005 timing.
- **cal_ccp_2015_5_declaration_perjury** — Income and Expense Declarations (FL-150) submitted in § 2030 motions are under penalty of perjury.

## Cross-Citizen (read-only)

- **HERALD** — authentication of income/asset declarations; contradictions logs where opposing party's FL-150 contradicts tax returns, loan applications, or lifestyle evidence.
- **CA_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — Elkins access-to-justice lineage; due process right to meaningful participation.
- **CA_Financial_Fraud_Litigator** — concealment of income or assets in § 2030 proceedings overlaps with financial-fraud indicia; funneling through third parties, undisclosed accounts, crypto.
- **US_Federal_ERISA_Litigator** — ERISA-plan income concealment in § 2030 proceedings implicates § 502(a) beneficiary disclosure duties for spouse/dependents.
