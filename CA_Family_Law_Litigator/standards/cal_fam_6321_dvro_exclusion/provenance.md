# Provenance — Cal. Fam. Code § 6321

## Statute Origin

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 6321
**Title:** DVPA — Exclusion from Dwelling (Ex Parte)

**Legislative Purpose:** Part of the Domestic Violence Prevention Act (DVPA), enacted to give courts immediate authority to remove a dangerous party from a shared dwelling without waiting for a full hearing. The ex parte mechanism reflects the Legislature's recognition that waiting for notice and hearing can result in further violence.

**Enacted:** Family Code § 6321 is part of the DVPA codification; the DVPA was originally enacted as Code of Civil Procedure § 540 et seq. and was re-enacted and re-codified as Family Code §§ 6200-6409 effective January 1, 1994.

## Amendment History

The DVPA has been extensively amended since 1994. Specific amendment history for § 6321 is available at the LegInfo URL above. Notable changes have expanded the definition of covered relationships and the types of harm supporting exclusion.
