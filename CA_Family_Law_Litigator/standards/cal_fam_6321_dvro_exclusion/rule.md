# California Family Code § 6321 — DVRO Exclusion Order

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 6321
**Title:** Domestic Violence Restraining Order — Exclusion from Dwelling

---

## Verbatim Statute Text

(a) The court may issue an ex parte order excluding a party from the family dwelling, the dwelling of the other party, the common dwelling of both parties, or the dwelling of the person who has care, custody, and control of a child to be protected from domestic violence if the court finds both of the following:

(1) The facts show that physical or emotional harm would otherwise result to the other party, to a person under the care, custody, and control of the other party, or to a minor child of the parties or of the other party.

(2) In the case of an order excluding a party from the family dwelling only, one of the following:

(A) The party to be excluded has assaulted or threatens to assault the other party or any other person under the care, custody, and control of the other party, or any minor child of the parties or of the other party.

(B) The party to be excluded has made a credible threat of physical injury to the other party, or to any person under the care, custody, and control of the other party, or to any minor child of the parties or of the other party.

(b) An order issued under this section shall not determine or affect the right to possession of property or the permanent possessory rights of the parties.

---

**Three-Part Test (for family dwelling exclusion):**
1. Physical or emotional harm would otherwise result; AND
2. The party to be excluded has assaulted, threatened assault, or made credible threats of physical injury; AND
3. The party has color of law right to possession of the premises (implied — they must have some basis to be present).

**Limitation:** Exclusion order does not determine property rights (§ 6321(b)).
