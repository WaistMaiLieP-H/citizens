# Family Code § 3041 — Cross-References

## Direct statutory cross-references on the face of § 3041

- **Subdivision (e) Indian Child Welfare Act references:**
  - 25 U.S.C. § 1912(d), (e), (f) — ICWA evidentiary standards.
  - 25 U.S.C. § 1922 — ICWA placement preferences.
  - W&I § 224.6 — California implementation of ICWA.
  - W&I § 361.7 — ICWA evidentiary standards in state dependency cases.
  - W&I § 361.31 — ICWA placement preferences in state dependency cases.

## Implied statutory cross-references (Chapter 2 architecture)

- **§ 3040** — Order of preference: parents > persons in whose home child has been living in a stable and wholesome environment > any other person or persons deemed suitable. § 3041's detriment gate is how § 3040's subordinate-tier placements are reached over parental objection.
- **§ 3041.5** — Drug testing for substance abuse in custody proceedings.
- **§ 3042** — Child's preference.
- **§ 3044** — Domestic violence rebuttable presumption.
- **§ 3046** — Absence or relocation due to domestic violence.
- **§ 3048** — Custody order abduction-prevention findings.

## Doctrinal bridges within Family Code

- **§ 3011** — Best-interest factor inventory. § 3041(a) "required to serve the best interest" finding is informed by § 3011 factors.
- **§ 3020** — Best-interest policy floor; frequent-and-continuing-contact preference.
- **§ 3100** — Visitation orders (DV considerations).
- **§ 3150** — Appointment of counsel for the child. In a § 3041 contest, minor's counsel under § 3150 is structurally relevant — the child's interest is analytically distinct from both the parent and the proposed nonparent custodian.
- **§ 7611** — Presumed-parent status (the related question of who counts as a parent for § 3041 purposes).
- **§ 7822** — Abandonment / termination of parental rights (distinct from § 3041 custody question; but factual overlap in some cases).

## Constitutional and federal bridges

- **14th Amendment substantive due process** — parental liberty interest in care, custody, and control of children. *Troxel v. Granville* (2000) 530 U.S. 57.
- **42 U.S.C. § 1983** — federal civil-rights claim where state actors manipulate state custody process to deprive a parent of the constitutional interest.
- **Indian Child Welfare Act, 25 U.S.C. § 1901 et seq.** — full federal framework incorporated by § 3041(e).

## Evidence Code bridges

- **Evid. § 720** — Foundation for expert witness. Any nonparent custody case that relies on expert testimony about parental detriment requires independent § 720 qualification.
- **Evid. § 780** — Credibility factors. In state-actor-nonparent cases, credibility of the nonparent witness is a live issue; the witness's institutional role and any financial or positional interest are admissible credibility factors.
- **Evid. § 1150** — Juror mental processes (not relevant here directly; noted for completeness because it appears in the cross-file of related custody-trial authorities).

## Criminal-code bridges (relevant in state-actor contexts)

- **Pen. § 148.5** — False report to a peace officer. Relevant where nonparent party has provided reports feeding into the custody record.
- **Pen. § 273.6** — Violation of a protective order; relevant to CLETS entries that preceded the § 3041 motion.
- **Pen. § 278.5** — Deprivation of custody; relevant in cases with cross-allegations of child-take situations.

## Welfare & Institutions Code bridges

- **W&I § 300 et seq.** — Dependency jurisdiction. § 3041 operates in family court; § 300 operates in juvenile court. The two systems sometimes coordinate on the same child.
- **W&I § 15610.07** — Elder abuse definition (not directly applicable, but noted because the companion adult-dependency framework parallels the child-custody structure).

## Probate Code bridges

- **Prob. § 1510 et seq.** — Guardianship of minors (distinct from § 3041 custody, but the probate-court guardianship route is an alternative path some third parties attempt).

## Citizen cross-tethers

- **CA_Family_Law_Litigator** — primary owner.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — cross-tether for 42 U.S.C. § 1983 parent-liberty-interest claims arising out of state-actor-nonparent custody proceedings.
- **CA_Elder_Law_Litigator** — cross-tether (analytical parallel on protected-liberty-interest detriment findings; no direct statutory overlap).
- **CA_Criminal_Law_Specialist** — cross-tether for Pen. § 148.5 / § 273.6 / § 278.5 collateral criminal exposure.

## Findings bridges

- **FAMLAW-005** — Mother-as-dispatcher orchestration finding. § 3041 is the analytical frame for any custody award reached in the shadow of state-actor coordination. Where a state-employee third party has used institutional access to manipulate the custody process, § 3041's detriment standard must be applied with full foundation scrutiny under Evid. § 720 and with the clear-and-convincing burden preserved under (b).
- **FAMLAW-006 F-006-01** — Wiita NPPES finding. Feeds the § 720 foundation attack on any evaluator whose report contributes to the detriment record in a § 3041 proceeding.

## Case law (foundational)

- ***Troxel v. Granville*** (2000) 530 U.S. 57 — federal constitutional floor.
- ***In re B.G.*** (1974) 11 Cal.3d 679 — California detriment doctrine antecedent.
- ***Guardianship of Phillip B.*** (1983) 139 Cal.App.3d 407 — further development of detriment standard.
- ***In re Marriage of Gayden*** (1991) 229 Cal.App.3d 1510 — clear-and-convincing application in custody context.
- ***Guardianship of Vaughan*** (2012) 207 Cal.App.4th 1055 — de-facto-parent analysis under § 3041(c).
