# Family Code § 3170 — Mandatory Mediation in Contested Custody / Visitation

## Layer 1 — Rule

### Statutory text (current, as of 2026-04-15)

**§ 3170(a)(1).** "If it appears on the face of a petition, application, or other pleading to obtain or modify a temporary or permanent custody or visitation order that custody, visitation, or both are contested, the court shall set the contested issues for mediation."

**§ 3170(a)(2).** "Domestic violence cases shall be handled in accordance with Sections 3113, 3181, and 3192."

**§ 3170(b).** "Actions filed under the Domestic Violence Prevention Act, where issues of child custody or visitation are contested, shall be set for mediation of those contested issues only if mediation is deemed appropriate by the court, considering the factors listed in Section 3181."

Related sections governing how the mediation is conducted:
- **§ 3175** — separate sessions available on request in DV cases
- **§ 3177** — confidentiality of mediation
- **§ 3181** — separate-session rules where DV history exists
- **§ 3183** — mediator recommendation authority (where county has adopted "recommending" model under § 3183(a))
- **§ 3188** — mediator qualifications; cross to § 1815, § 1816

### Plain English

If custody or visitation is contested in any filing — temporary or permanent, initial or modification — the court **must** set the disputed issues for mediation before ruling on them. The court does not have discretion to skip this step.

Two carve-outs exist for domestic-violence cases:

1. The victim may request separate sessions so the parties are not in the same room (§ 3181).
2. DVPA cases go to mediation only if the court decides it is appropriate considering DV history (§ 3170(b), § 3181).

Mediation is confidential (§ 3177). In "recommending counties" (Alameda, Contra Costa, and others under § 3183), the mediator may make a custody recommendation to the court; in "non-recommending counties," the mediator may not. Rule of Court 5.210 governs the conduct of mediation; Rule 5.215 governs DV protocol.
