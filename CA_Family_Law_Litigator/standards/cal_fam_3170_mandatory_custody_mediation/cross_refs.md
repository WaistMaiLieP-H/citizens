# Family Code § 3170 — Layer 4: Cross-References (read-only)

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator (internal)

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — mediator must evaluate best-interest factors under § 3011 when making recommendations.
- **FAMCODE-§3044** — DV presumption against custody: mediator in recommending county must apply § 3044 where triggered. (pending manifest build)
- **FAMCODE-§3118** — court-ordered custody evaluation (distinct from § 3170 mediation; triggered where sexual abuse alleged). (pending manifest build)
- **cal_fam_3027_false_child_abuse_sanctions** — false-allegation sanctions apply to statements made during § 3170 mediation only to the extent they escape § 3177 confidentiality; see *Rinaker v. Superior Court* (1998) 62 Cal.App.4th 155 line of authority.
- **cal_fam_1815_counselor_qualifications** — § 1815 minimum qualifications for § 3170 mediators.
- **cal_fam_1816_mediator_dv_training** — § 1816 mandatory DV training (1999 reform).
- **cal_fam_3164_mediator_qualifications** — § 3164 additional qualifications including DV and child abuse training.
- **cal_crc_5_210_custody_mediation** — Rule 5.210 conduct of mediation.
- **cal_crc_5_215_dv_protocol** — Rule 5.215 DV protocol in mediation.
- **cal_fam_217_live_testimony_right** — post-mediation contested hearings are § 217 hearings.

## Cross-Citizen (read-only)

- **CA_Mental_Health_Litigator** — § 3170 mediation intersects § 1368 competency concerns; mediator obligation where capacity is questionable.
- **HERALD** — authentication of mediator reports (§ 3183 recommending counties), declaration drafting regarding § 3177 confidentiality waivers.
- **CA_Probate_Conservatorship_Litigator** — when a party to § 3170 mediation is a conservatee, separate capacity and representation concerns attach.
