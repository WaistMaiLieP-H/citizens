# Cross-References — Cal. Fam. Code § 3046

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — § 3046(a) modifies how the best-interest analysis (§ 3011) is conducted; DV-nexus departure is shielded from the § 3011(a)(6) stability factor
- **cal_fam_3020_custody_policy** — § 3020 requires courts to ensure safety and welfare; § 3046 gives courts the tool to honor § 3020's safety mandate by protecting the departing victim-parent
- **cal_fam_6320_dvpa_enjoinable_behaviors** — The behaviors enjoined under § 6320 are the same behaviors that create the DV-nexus required by § 3046(a); the DVPA complaint is the foundation for § 3046 protection
- **cal_fam_6321_dvro_exclusion** — § 3046(c) exception applies if the departing parent is subject to an exclusion order under § 6321; interplay between § 6321 and § 3046(c) must be analyzed for every case
- **cal_fam_6323_dvro_custody_visitation** — Where departure leads to emergency custody arrangements under § 6323, § 3046 ensures the departure itself is not used to undermine those arrangements
- **cal_pen_13701_le_dv_response** — Police reports documenting the DV that caused departure are the primary evidence for establishing the § 3046(a) nexus; inadequate reports (unsigned, incomplete) weaken the nexus showing
