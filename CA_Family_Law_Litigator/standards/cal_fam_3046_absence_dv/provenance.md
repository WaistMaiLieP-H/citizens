# Provenance — Cal. Fam. Code § 3046

## Statute Origin

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 3046
**Title:** Absence or Relocation Due to Domestic Violence — Custody Proceedings

**Legislative Purpose:** Enacted to prevent the perverse outcome where a DV victim's decision to flee is used as evidence of parental unfitness. Before § 3046, opposing parties routinely argued that a parent who left the family home had disrupted the child's stability and demonstrated inability to provide continuous care. § 3046 closes that loop: the departure is shielded; the interference with contact is weaponized against the abuser instead.

**Enacted:** As part of California's DV reform package; Family Code codification of DV protections in custody proceedings.
