# California Family Code § 3046 — Absence of Parent Under Domestic Violence Circumstances

**Code:** California Family Code
**Section:** 3046
**Current Form:** Cal. Fam. Code § 3046

---

## Verbatim Statute Text

(a) If a party is absent or relocates from the family residence and the absence or relocation is a result of an act of domestic violence by the other party, the absence or relocation shall not be used as a factor in determining whether a parent is able to provide care for a child or as a factor in determining the best interest of a child under this division.

(b) The court may consider attempts to interfere with the other party's regular contact with the child as a factor in determining the best interest of the child under this division.

(c) This section does not apply if the party who is absent or has relocated is subject to a protective order that prohibits the party from returning to the family residence or if the party has abandoned the child under the circumstances described in Section 7822.

---

**Core Protection:** A DV victim's decision to leave with the child cannot be used against them in custody proceedings.
**Counter-Abuse Tool:** Subsection (b) expressly arms the court to penalize interference with contact.
**Exceptions:** Protective order excluding from home; abandonment under § 7822.
