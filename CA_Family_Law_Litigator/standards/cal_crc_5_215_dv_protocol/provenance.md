# cal_crc_5_215_dv_protocol — Provenance

**Status: PROPOSED**

---

## Rule Origin

**Source:** California Rules of Court
**Rule Number:** 5.215
**Title:** Domestic Violence Protocol for Family Court Services

## Current Form

**Judicial Council URL:** https://www.courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index.cfm?title=five&linkid=rule5_215

## Amendment History

- **1999:** Rule enacted following California Judicial Council's Domestic Violence Task Force report identifying systematic failures in family court DV case handling
- **2003:** Separate sessions provision strengthened from "should offer" to mandatory "shall offer" language
- **2004:** Training requirements for all FCS staff added; previously training was mediator-specific only
- **2007:** 16/4 training hour requirements set at current levels
- **2010:** Prohibition on mediating restraining order terms made explicit
- **2012:** Mandatory referral requirement (Rule 5.215(g)) added
- **2016:** § 3183(c) cross-reference added to make restraining order recommendation authority explicit
- **Current:** As of January 1, 2024

## Governing Authority

Judicial Council of California. Same constitutional authority as Rule 5.210. The DV protocol has the force of court order — it is not a guideline or recommendation.

## Implementing Statute

Family Code § 3160 et seq. (court-connected custody mediation), specifically § 3161 (purpose and DV considerations) and § 3183(c) (mediator authority to recommend restraining orders).

## Verification Note

The specific subdivision letters within Rule 5.215 have been renumbered across amendment cycles. When citing any subdivision, confirm the current letter designation at the Judicial Council URL above before filing. The substantive requirements described in this standard reflect current rule content but subdivision citations should be verified.
