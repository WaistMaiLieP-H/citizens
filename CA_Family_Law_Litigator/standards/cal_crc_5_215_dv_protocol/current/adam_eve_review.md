## ADAM Review (Triple Constraint)

**Governing Guidelines:** PASS — Rule 5.215 correctly extracted: (a) purpose tied to Fam. Code §§ 3160–3186; (b) application to all FCS staff; (c) DV definition via Fam. Code § 6211; (d) six mandatory protocols (DV-case coding, victim safety, DV-service referral, prohibition on negotiating the violence or protective orders, § 3183(c) TRO recommendation, *proactive* separate-session offer upon identification); (e) five prohibited staff actions; (f) 16-hr advanced DV training within first 12 months of assignment.

**Standards of Creation:** PASS — Mandatory ("SHALL") vs prohibited ("shall NOT") sub-rules correctly separated. The "offer separate sessions immediately upon identification of DV — not upon request only" language is the affirmative-duty anchor.

**SOC:** PASS — Current Rule 5.215; pairs with CRC 5.210 (qualifications) and Fam. Code §§ 1816, 3164. An FCS staff member violating Rule 5.215 simultaneously implicates Rule 5.210 qualification compliance.

**Case anchors:** Every FCS encounter in the steward's record audits against: (1) Was the case coded DV per § 3181/§ 6211? (2) Was a separate session offered proactively? (3) Did FCS staff attempt to mediate terms of any protective order? (4) Was the victim required to justify the violence? (5) Did the responding FCS staff have the 16-hr advanced DV training current at time of service? Each "no" is a Rule 5.215 violation pleadable under § 52.1 (Bane Act) when tied to a resulting rights deprivation.
