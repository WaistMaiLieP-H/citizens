## Steward Case Connections

**Contra Costa County FCS — PRA Targets:**
The Rule 5.215 audit requires the following PRA requests to CCFCS or the Superior Court Clerk:
1. Case coding records for all mediation assignments in Hartmann/Cerretani matter
2. Training certification records for all FCS staff who handled any session (16-hour initial + annual 4-hour updates)
3. Session notes or summaries showing whether separate sessions were conducted
4. Restraining order recommendation records (or absence thereof) for each session
5. DV resource referral documentation

**Interaction with the Competency Evaluation:**
The fraudulent competency evaluation (referenced in family law audit) was conducted by a person in an evaluation role within or adjacent to FCS processes. Rule 5.215's training requirements apply to evaluators as well as mediators. If the evaluator was not DV-trained, the evaluation itself was conducted without the required protocol competency.

**June 16, 2023 Setup:**
On the date of the incident, the DV coding and session protocols that should have protected Michael throughout the custody proceedings either were not in place or were not functioning. The failure of FCS protocols over the 14-year history created the conditions for the 2023 event. This is the "accumulated violation" theory: each Rule 5.215 failure in the preceding years contributed to the outcome on that date.
