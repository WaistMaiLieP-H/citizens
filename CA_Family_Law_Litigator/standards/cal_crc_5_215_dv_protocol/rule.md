# California Rules of Court, Rule 5.215 — Domestic Violence Protocol for Family Court Services

**Status: PROPOSED**
**Steward: Michael Hartmann**
**Citizen: CA_Family_Law_Litigator**

---

## Rule Text (Key Provisions)

*Cal. Rules of Court, Rule 5.215:*

**(a) Purpose:**
This rule establishes the domestic violence protocol for Family Court Services consistent with Family Code sections 3160 through 3186. It is intended to protect the safety of domestic violence victims and their children during custody mediation and evaluation.

**(b) Application:**
This rule applies to all Family Court Services staff — mediators, evaluators, investigators, and support staff — in all court-connected custody mediation and evaluation proceedings.

**(c) Definitions:**
"Domestic violence" has the meaning set forth in Family Code § 6211. "Victim" means a person who is the subject of domestic violence as defined therein.

**(d) Required Protocols — All DV Cases:**
When a domestic violence case is identified, Family Court Services staff SHALL:
1. Identify and code the case as a DV case in the court's case management system
2. Ensure victim safety during all court-connected proceedings
3. Make appropriate referrals to DV services and resources
4. Prohibit any negotiation about the violence itself or about restraining orders (these are not subject to mediation)
5. Recommend restraining orders when appropriate pursuant to Family Code § 3183(c)
6. Offer separate sessions immediately upon identification of DV — not upon request only

**(e) Prohibited Conduct:**
Staff shall NOT:
- Require parties to meet jointly over a party's safety objection
- Mediate or negotiate the terms of protective orders
- Require a victim to justify or explain the violence to the mediator
- Place the burden on the victim to demonstrate why separate sessions are needed

**(f) Training Requirements — All Staff:**
All Family Court Services staff must complete:
- 16 hours of advanced DV training within the first 12 months of assignment
- 4 hours of DV update training annually thereafter
- Training must comply with Rule 5.230 standards

**(g) Referrals to Community Resources:**
Staff must maintain current lists of DV resources and make referrals as appropriate. Failure to refer a victim to DV resources when indicated is a protocol violation.

**(h) Interaction with Restraining Orders:**
When an existing restraining order is in place, mediation must be structured so the parties do not violate the order's terms. Contact prohibited by the order cannot be required as a condition of mediation participation.
