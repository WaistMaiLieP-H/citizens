## Legislative History

§ 3020 was enacted as part of the Family Code codification in 1992. The text encodes two principles the Legislature found essential:

1. **Health, safety, and welfare as primary concern** — an affirmative legislative finding that child safety is not one factor among many, but the primary concern. This forecloses judicial minimization of safety concerns in favor of relationship preservation.

2. **Detrimental impact of DV in the household** — a legislative finding of fact that DV in the child's home is detrimental. Courts do not need expert testimony to establish this baseline; the Legislature has already established it.

3. **Frequent and continuing contact** — a secondary policy goal that must yield to the primary health/safety/welfare concern when they conflict.
