## Statutory Cross-References

- **Family Code § 3011** — factors for determining best interest; § 3020's policy is implemented through § 3011
- **Family Code § 3040** — order of preference in custody awards; the § 3020 health/safety/welfare standard governs the entire priority scheme
- **Family Code § 3044** — rebuttable presumption against granting custody to DV perpetrator; directly implements § 3020(a)'s finding that DV is detrimental
- **Family Code § 3046** — restriction of visitation when there is risk of abduction or DV
- **Family Code § 3100** — visitation rights; subject to § 3020 health/safety/welfare override
- **Family Code § 6203** — definition of abuse for DVPA purposes
- **Family Code § 6320** — enjoinable DV behaviors

## Policy Note

§ 3020 serves as the statutory statement of legislative intent for all custody proceedings. Courts cite it as the overriding framework when tension exists between the § 3020(b) "frequent contact" preference and the § 3020(a) "health, safety, and welfare" priority. The statute resolves that tension explicitly: health, safety, and welfare are PRIMARY. Frequent contact is secondary and shall not be ordered at the expense of the primary concern.
