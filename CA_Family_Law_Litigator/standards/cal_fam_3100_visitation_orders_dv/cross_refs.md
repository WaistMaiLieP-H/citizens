# Family Code § 3100 — Layer 4: Cross-References (read-only)

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator (internal)

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — § 3100(a) "best interest of the child" is defined by § 3011 factors.
- **cal_fam_3020_custody_policy** — § 3100(a) frequent-contact presumption is § 3020(a); safety override is § 3020(c).
- **FAMCODE-§3044** — DV presumption against custody informs § 3100(b) restraining-order analysis. (pending manifest build)
- **cal_fam_3048_custody_jurisdiction_abduction** — abduction-risk findings may warrant supervised visitation under § 3100(b) even absent DV.
- **cal_fam_3027_false_child_abuse_sanctions** — false-abuse-allegation analysis matters where a § 3100(b) supervision order was based on the disputed allegation.
- **cal_fam_217_live_testimony_right** — § 3100 visitation hearings are § 217 hearings; live testimony right applies.
- **cal_fam_6203_dvpa_abuse_definition** — abuse definition anchors § 3100(b)/(e) DV analysis.
- **cal_fam_6320_dvpa_enjoinable_behaviors** — conduct that supports a protective order is the conduct § 3100(b) tells the court to weigh.
- **cal_fam_6323_dvro_custody_visitation** — § 6323 visitation-within-DVRO interacts with § 3100.
- **§ 6218** — defines "protective order" for § 3100(b) purposes.

## Cross-Citizen (read-only)

- **CA_Criminal_Law_Litigator** — Penal Code § 136.2 (criminal protective orders referenced in § 3100(d)); § 273.6 (violation of protective orders); § 278 / § 278.5 (abduction).
- **HERALD** — authentication of CPO certified copies, supervised-visitation reports, exchange logs; contradictions logs where provider observations conflict with declarations.
- **CA_Mental_Health_Litigator** — § 1368 competency and capacity issues may bear on supervised-visitation provider adequacy.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — where state court orders visitation in facial violation of a federally enforceable protective order or VAWA § 2265 full-faith-and-credit order, § 1983 exposure may attach.
