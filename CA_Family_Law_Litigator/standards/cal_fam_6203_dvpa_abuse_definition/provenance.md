## Legislative History

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1993 | Domestic Violence Prevention Act enacted; § 6203 definitions established |
| 1994 | Amendments to expand covered conduct |
| 2014 | AB 2089 — amended to broaden "disturbing the peace" to include coercive control conduct |
| 2020 | AB 3087 — added reproductive coercion as a form of abuse under § 6203(a)(5) |
| 2023 | AB 1388 (Stats. 2023) — updated definitions to clarify coercive control |

**Note:** The 2020 and 2023 amendments significantly expanded § 6203's coverage. Any assessment of abuse in a case spanning pre-2020 conduct should note which version of § 6203 applied at the time of the conduct.
