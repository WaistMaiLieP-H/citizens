# Family Code § 3048 — Layer 4: Cross-References (read-only)

## Within CA_Family_Law_Litigator (internal)

- **family_code_3011_best_interest** — best-interest analysis must incorporate abduction-risk findings.
- **FAMCODE-§3044** — DV presumption is a § 3048(b)(2) abduction-risk factor (history of DV). (pending manifest build)
- **cal_fam_3027_false_child_abuse_sanctions** — false-abuse-allegation patterns can co-occur with abduction-risk patterns; sanctions analysis and § 3048(b) analysis run in parallel.
- **cal_fam_217_live_testimony_right** — abduction-risk hearings are § 217 hearings; live testimony required absent good-cause findings.
- **Fam. Code §§ 3400–3465 (UCCJEA)** — jurisdictional framework referenced in § 3048(a)(1).
- **Fam. Code §§ 3134, 3134.5** — law-enforcement recovery orders (separate but related tool).

## Cross-Citizen (read-only)

- **CA_Criminal_Law_Litigator** — Penal Code § 277 (definitions), § 278 (abduction by non-custodian), § 278.5 (deprivation of custody by custodian) — § 3048(a)(4) warning maps directly to these penal statutes.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — ICARA (22 U.S.C. §§ 9001–9011) Hague petitions are federal; § 3048(a)(5) habitual-residence finding is the factual anchor.
- **HERALD** — authentication of passports, travel records, foreign-court filings; contradictions logs for parties denying ties to another country while holding dual citizenship.
