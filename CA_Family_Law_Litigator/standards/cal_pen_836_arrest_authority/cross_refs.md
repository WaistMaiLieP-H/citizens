**Federal § 1983 Complaint:**
Balistreri v. Pacifica Police Dept. is the direct precedent for a § 1983 DV enforcement failure claim. The theory: consistent non-enforcement of mandatory DV arrest statute constitutes deliberate indifference to constitutional rights.
