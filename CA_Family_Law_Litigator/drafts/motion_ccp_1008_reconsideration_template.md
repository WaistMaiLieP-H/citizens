## How to use this template

1. Fill every bracketed blank. If a blank is not applicable, delete the bracket and its label — do not leave the bracket in the filed document.
2. Section 2 (Notice Period) is strict: the § 1008(a) motion must be filed within **10 days after service of written notice of entry of the order sought to be reconsidered**. Calendar this date the moment the adverse order is served. Calendar the service-plus-10 deadline by UTC-safe date math (CCP § 12a if the 10th day falls on a weekend or holiday). A day late = statutory ineligibility; the court loses jurisdiction to entertain a party-initiated § 1008 motion and can only act sua sponte under *Le Francois*.
3. The § 1008(a) motion MUST be based on **new or different facts, circumstances, or law**. An argument the movant could have made the first time does not qualify. *Gilberd*, 32 Cal. App. 4th at 1500; CCP § 1008(a), (b).
4. The motion MUST be accompanied by **an affidavit stating (i) what application was made before, (ii) when and to what judge, (iii) what order or decisions were made, and (iv) what new or different facts, circumstances, or law are claimed to be shown**. CCP § 1008(a). This affidavit is load-bearing; a § 1008 motion without it is defective on its face.
5. If filing in family court, also comply with CRC rule 5.92 (request-for-order procedure) and any local-rule mandatory form use (FL-300 Request for Order, FL-310 Temporary Orders, etc.). In strict § 1008 practice the Judicial Council has no dedicated § 1008 form — attach this motion as an MC-031 declaration supplement to FL-300 if the court requires RFO format.
6. Sign under penalty of perjury at the end. Serve per CCP § 1005 if the court requires full notice; if seeking ex parte consideration, comply with CRC rules 3.1200-3.1207.

## Practice notes — HERALD

- *Le Francois v. Goel*, 35 Cal. 4th 1094 (2005): a trial court has **inherent power** to reconsider its own interim orders sua sponte even when a § 1008 motion is untimely. If the 10-day window is missed, shift the strategy to an informal communication inviting the court to reconsider sua sponte — do NOT file an untimely § 1008 motion purporting to invoke the statute.
- *Global Protein Products v. Le* (2020) 42 Cal. App. 5th 352 — § 1008(b) renewed-motion path is a separate authority track; use it only when the prior motion was denied and the renewed facts satisfy § 1008(b)'s "new or different facts, circumstances, or law" *and* affidavit requirements.
- § 1008 applies to virtually any order the court has entered, but a final judgment's "order" character is complicated by appeal rules; if the order has merged into a judgment, consider CCP § 663 (motion to vacate) or CCP § 473 paths instead.
- Family-court-specific: CRC rule 5.92 mandates FL-300 Request for Order format for most family-court motions. Many family-law judges require the § 1008 motion to be captioned as a FL-300 RFO. Check local rules.
