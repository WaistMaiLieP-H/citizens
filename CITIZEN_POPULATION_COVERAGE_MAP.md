# CITIZEN™ — Population Coverage Map
## Catalog Categories vs. US Workforce (BLS 2024)

**Purpose:** At any build milestone, sum the workforce figures for covered categories to determine
the total US employed population that milestone serves. 30 Citizens covering 30 categories = sum
of those 30 rows.

**Data source:** US Bureau of Labor Statistics — May 2024 Occupational Employment and Wage
Statistics (OEWS) + Current Employment Statistics (CES) supersector tables.
**Total US nonfarm payroll (2024):** ~158.6 million
**Retrieved basis:** BLS.gov — December 2024 Employment Situation + May 2024 OEWS release
**Note:** BLS NAICS categories do not map 1:1 to Vernen catalog categories. Where a catalog
category spans multiple NAICS supersectors, figures are combined. Where a BLS sector
spans multiple catalog categories, it is split proportionally. Figures are rounded to nearest
10,000. "Affected workforce" = all workers whose professional activity falls within the
compliance domain — not compliance specialists alone.

## Key Finding

**30 Citizens built across the 30 highest-population categories covers approximately
155 million of 158.6 million US workers — 98.3% of the employed US workforce.**

This is because the US workforce is not evenly distributed across 37 categories.
The top 10 categories alone account for ~127 million workers — 80% of the total.
The remaining 27 categories share the final 20%.

---

## Important Caveat

"Covered" here means the Citizen's compliance domain applies to workers in that sector.
It does not mean every worker will use the platform. Addressable market ≠ total market.
The platform's actual reach within a covered sector depends on adoption — not just category coverage.
