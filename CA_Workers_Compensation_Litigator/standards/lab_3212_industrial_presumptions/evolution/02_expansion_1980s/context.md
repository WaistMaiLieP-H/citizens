# LAB §3212 — Stage 02: 1980s Expansion to Additional Categories

**Stage:** 02_expansion_1980s
**Period:** Multiple amendments through 1970s-1990s

---

## Incremental Expansion

Through the 1970s and 1980s, the legislature incrementally expanded §3212's coverage to additional categories of public safety workers:

- **CHP members** added (coverage of highway patrol officers)
- **DA inspectors and investigators** added
- **Warden service** (Wildlife Protection Branch, Fish & Game) added
- **Active firefighting members of CDF** (Department of Forestry and Fire Protection) added
- **County forestry/firefighting** departments added

## The §3212.X Companion Statutes

§3212 is the root provision. Through the 1980s-2000s, the legislature also enacted a series of companion statutes (§§3212.1 through 3213.2) extending presumptions to specific diseases:
- §3212.1: Cancer for firefighters and peace officers
- §3212.5: HIV for peace officers exposed to blood
- §3212.85: PTSD for peace officers and firefighters
- §3212.9: Various heart conditions for corrections officers

These companion statutes follow the same structure as §3212: presumption during service + post-termination extension + no-attribution rule + disputable but burden-shifting.
