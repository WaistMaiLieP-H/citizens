# LAB §3212 — Stage 03: SB 1159 (2020) — COVID-19 Presumption

**Stage:** 03_sb2094_2020
**Enactment:** SB 1159 (Stats. 2020, Ch. 84), effective September 17, 2020

---

## COVID-19 Presumption (Temporary and Ongoing)

SB 1159 added a COVID-19 presumption for specified workers during the pandemic period. While technically creating a new §3212.88 rather than amending §3212 directly, SB 1159 represents the most recent expansion of the §3212 presumption framework and illustrates how the legislature responds to new occupational health crises.

The COVID-19 presumption covered:
- Peace officers, firefighters, and other §3212-covered workers
- Healthcare workers at specified facilities
- Specific date windows for positive test results

## The 2026 Federal Installation Amendment

SB/AB (Stats. 2025) added subd. (b) to §3212 effective January 1, 2026, extending the original §3212(a) presumptions to:
- DoD installation firefighters (certified to DoD standards)
- NASA installation firefighters (adhering to HSC §13155 training)
- FAA Part 139 airport firefighters (trained/certified to Fire Control 5 standards)

This is the most recent expansion confirmed in the 2026-04-13 MCP fetch.
