# LAB §3212 — Industrial Presumptions — Operative Rule

**Standard ID:** lab_3212_industrial_presumptions
**Citizen:** CA_Workers_Compensation_Litigator
**Layer:** Current — Operative Rule
**Build date:** 2026-04-13

---

## The Rule

For specified categories of public safety workers (peace officers, firefighters, CHP, DA investigators, wildlife wardens), hernia, heart trouble, and pneumonia that develop during service are **presumed** to arise out of and in the course of employment. The presumption is disputable but shifts the burden to the employer/insurer to prove the condition is not industrial.

---

## Covered Workers

| Worker Category | Covered Conditions |
|---|---|
| Peace officers (sheriff, CHP, police) | Hernia, heart trouble, pneumonia |
| Firefighters (city, county, CDF) | Hernia, heart trouble, pneumonia |
| DA staff (inspectors/investigators) | Hernia, heart trouble, pneumonia |
| Wildlife wardens (active LEO duties) | Hernia, heart trouble, pneumonia |
| County/city peace officers (salaried) | Hernia |

**NOT covered:** Clerical workers within these agencies (stenographers, telephone operators, officeworkers)

**Effective 2026:** Federal installation firefighters (DoD, NASA, FAA Part 139) added by amendment

---

## The Post-Termination Extension

The presumption extends beyond active service: **3 calendar months per full year of service, up to 60 months maximum**, starting from the last date actually worked. A 20-year peace officer who develops heart trouble 4 years after retirement is still covered (20 years × 3 months = 60 months = 5 years).

---

## Apportionment Bar — §3212 and §4663(e)

**Critical interaction:** Conditions covered by the §3212 presumption cannot be apportioned under §4663. LAB §4663(e) expressly exempts presumptive conditions from apportionment. If a condition is presumed industrial under §3212, 100% of the disability is industrial — no reduction for prior conditions.

---

## Application to Non-Covered Workers — The §3202 Framework

Michael is a UA Local 342 plumber — not a peace officer or firefighter. Section 3212's specific presumption does not apply directly. However, §3212's framework is relevant to Michael's cases in two ways:

1. **The liberal construction analogy:** §3212 shows that California has repeatedly expanded presumptions for workers whose occupational exposure makes proof of causation inherently difficult. Courts applying §3202 liberal construction to non-presumption cases (e.g., Michael's ankle injuries) should give weight to this legislative philosophy.

2. **The apportionment bar model:** §4663(e)'s bar against apportioning §3212 conditions establishes the principle that strong causal relationships can bar apportionment. For non-§3212 workers, substantial evidence of direct industrial causation (without significant pre-existing contribution) can defeat apportionment on the facts.
