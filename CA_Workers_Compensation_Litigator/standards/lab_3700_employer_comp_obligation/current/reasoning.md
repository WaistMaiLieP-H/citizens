# LAB §3700 — Reasoning

## Why Compulsory Coverage Is the Foundation

Without §3700's compulsory coverage mandate, the entire workers compensation system collapses. The promise — guaranteed benefits for industrial injuries — means nothing if employers can opt out. The 1913 legislature understood this: the compulsory insurance requirement was enacted alongside the compensation system, not as an afterthought.

The three-path structure (private insurance, individual self-insurance, public entity self-insurance) reflects the practical reality that different employer types have different financial structures. Large self-insured corporations and public agencies have the financial capacity to pay claims directly; small businesses do not and must use insurance markets.

## The Exclusive Remedy Trade-Off Collapses Without §3700

The fundamental workers compensation bargain is: worker gives up tort rights; employer provides guaranteed benefits. If the employer is uninsured, the employer has received the benefit of the bargain (tort immunity) without providing the consideration (guaranteed benefits). California law addresses this: an uninsured employer CANNOT assert the exclusive remedy defense. The injured worker can sue in tort AND receive UEBTF benefits.

This creates a powerful incentive structure: insure or lose tort immunity entirely.

## Enforcement Mechanisms

- §3700.5: Criminal misdemeanor
- §3702: DI certificate conditions
- §3706: Presumption that injury is compensable against uninsured employer (no affirmative defenses available)
- §3715: UEBTF jurisdiction over uninsured employer claims
- §3716: UEBTF right of recovery against uninsured employer
