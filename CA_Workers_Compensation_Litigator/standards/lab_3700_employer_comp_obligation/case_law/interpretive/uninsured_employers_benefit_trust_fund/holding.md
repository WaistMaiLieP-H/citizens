# Uninsured Employers Benefit Trust Fund (UEBTF) — Doctrine

**Type:** Statutory doctrine + regulatory framework
**Authority:** Lab. Code §§3715-3717; 8 CCR §§15600-15741
**Standard:** lab_3700_employer_comp_obligation
**Status:** VERIFIED (statutory text) — case citations PROPOSED

## The UEBTF Framework

The Uninsured Employers Benefit Trust Fund is the safety net when §3700 compliance fails. When a worker is injured by an uninsured employer, the UEBTF:

1. **Steps in** to pay the worker's WC benefits (indemnity, medical, death)
2. **Files a WCAB claim** on behalf of the worker against the uninsured employer
3. **Recovers** from the uninsured employer for everything it paid (§3717)

### The Worker's Three-Track Strategy Against Uninsured Employer

Track 1 — **WCAB/UEBTF:** File a WC claim with the UEBTF. Receive benefits. UEBTF pursues the employer.

Track 2 — **Tort suit:** Because the exclusive remedy does not protect uninsured employers, the worker can file a civil tort suit for the full damages (pain and suffering, lost future earnings beyond WC indemnity, emotional distress). Lab. Code §3706 creates a presumption that the injury was caused by the employer's negligence.

Track 3 — **Criminal referral:** Report the uninsured employer to the DIR/Labor Commissioner for §3700.5 criminal prosecution.

### §3706 — The Negligence Presumption

Labor Code §3706 provides: if the employer failed to secure compensation, there is a presumption that the injury was caused by the employer's negligence. The employer cannot raise assumption of risk, contributory negligence, or fellow servant as defenses. This is the tort-law analogue of the §3202 liberal construction rule: when the employer has violated its fundamental obligation, the law shifts the burden.

## Application to UA342 Scenario

If Michael was fraudulently classified as an independent contractor or had his employment records manipulated to remove him from the covered workforce while he continued to perform covered work:
- The fraudulent misclassification is itself a §3700 violation (failure to secure compensation for a covered employee)
- The UEBTF pathway opens
- §3706's negligence presumption applies to the underlying injury claims
- The tortious interference with WC rights is a separate civil claim outside the exclusive remedy
