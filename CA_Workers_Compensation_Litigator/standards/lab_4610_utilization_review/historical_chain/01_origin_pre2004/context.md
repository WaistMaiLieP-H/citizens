## The Pre-SB 899 Landscape

Utilization review in California workers compensation existed before 2004, but in a far less structured form. Employers could review and dispute treatment recommendations, but the process lacked:
- Mandatory physician-review requirements for denial decisions
- Binding timeliness deadlines
- An objective medical standard (the MTUS) against which treatment could be evaluated
- Mandatory criteria disclosure

The result was an unstructured conflict between treating physicians and employers over the scope of required treatment. Denials were common, appeals were slow, and the quality of the review process varied enormously by employer and insurer.
