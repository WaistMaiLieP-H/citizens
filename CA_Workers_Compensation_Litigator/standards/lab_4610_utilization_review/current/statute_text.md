## Statutory Text — Key Provisions

### Subdivision (a) — Definition
"Utilization review" means utilization review or utilization management functions that prospectively, retrospectively, or concurrently review and approve, modify, or deny, based in whole or in part on medical necessity to cure and relieve, treatment recommendations by physicians, as defined in Section 3209.3, prior to, retrospectively, or concurrent with the provision of medical treatment services pursuant to Section 4600.

### Subdivision (b) — First 30 Days (Post-2018 Injuries)
For injuries on or after January 1, 2018: emergency treatment services and medical treatment rendered for an accepted compensable body part/condition addressed by the MTUS by:
- A member of the MPN or HCO
- A physician predesignated under §4600(d)
...within the **30 days following the initial date of injury, shall be authorized without prospective utilization review** (except as provided in subdivision (c)).

*Note: Exception when employee is not subject to MPN/HCO/predesignated physician — treatment within 30 days from employer-selected physician is still exempt. Physician must submit §6409 report and complete request for authorization within five days of initial visit.*

### Subdivision (c) — Excepted Services (Subject to Prospective UR Even in First 30 Days)
The following are subject to prospective UR even within the first 30 days:
1. Pharmaceuticals not expressly exempted or authorized by formulary
2. Non-emergency inpatient/outpatient surgery (including pre/post surgical services)
3. Psychological treatment services
4. Home health care services
5. Imaging and radiology (excluding x-rays)
6. Durable medical equipment combined total exceeding $250
7. Electrodiagnostic medicine (EMG, nerve conduction studies)
8. Other services designated by administrative director rules

### Subdivision (g)(3)(A) — Physician-Only Modification/Denial Rule
**"A person other than a licensed physician who is competent to evaluate the specific clinical issues involved in the medical treatment services, if these services are within the scope of the physician's practice, requested by the physician, shall not modify or deny requests for authorization of medical treatment for reasons of medical necessity to cure and relieve or due to incomplete or insufficient information."**

*This is the critical enforcement provision: non-physician UR denials are unlawful.*

### Subdivision (g)(3)(B) — No Financial Incentive Prohibition
The employer, or any entity conducting UR on behalf of the employer, shall neither offer nor provide any financial incentive or consideration to a physician based on the number of modifications or denials made.

### Subdivision (g)(2)(A) — Medical Director Requirement
The employer, insurer, or other entity shall employ or designate a **medical director who holds an unrestricted license to practice medicine in this state** (issued under B&P Code §2050 or §2450). The medical director ensures UR process compliance.

### Subdivision (h) — Criteria/Guidelines Requirements
UR criteria must be:
1. Developed with involvement from actively practicing physicians
2. Consistent with the MTUS (including drug formulary — §5307.27)
3. Evaluated at least annually, updated if necessary
4. Disclosed to the physician and employee when used as basis for modification/denial
5. Available to the public upon request

### Subdivision (i) — Timeliness Requirements
- **Prospective UR (non-formulary):** Decision within 5 business days of receipt of request + supporting info, but no more than **14 days** from date of physician's recommendation
- **Prospective UR (formulary-covered):** Decision within **5 business days** of receipt
- **Urgent/imminent threat:** Decision within **72 hours** of receipt of necessary information
- **Communication of decision:** Within **24 hours** by telephone/fax/secure email
- **Written modification/denial:** Within 24 hours (concurrent review) or **2 business days** (prospective review)

### Subdivision (k) — 12-Month Validity
A UR decision to modify or deny remains effective for **12 months** without further action by the employer for further recommendations for the same treatment by the same physician (or practice group), unless supported by documented change in material facts.

### Subdivision (p) — Administrative Penalties
If the administrative director determines the employer/insurer/entity has failed to meet §4610 timeframes or requirements, administrative penalties may be assessed by order.
