# LAB §4553 — Stage 02: Labor Code Recodification 1937

**Stage:** 02_labor_code_1937

Recodified as Lab. Code §4553 in the 1937 Labor Code. The substantive provision was carried over unchanged from the 1917 amendment. Text: "The amount of compensation otherwise recoverable shall be increased one-half, together with costs and expenses not to exceed $250, where the employee is injured by reason of the serious and willful misconduct of any of the following: (a) The employer or his managing agent or general superintendent thereof; (b) If the employer is a partnership, on the part of one of the partners or a general superintendent thereof; (c) If the employer is a corporation, on the part of an executive, managing officer, or general superintendent thereof."

Note: The $250 costs-and-expenses cap has not been updated since 1937 — inflation adjustment is long overdue. The 50% enhancement itself, however, applies to all compensation, which scales with modern wages.
