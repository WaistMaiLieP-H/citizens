## Statute Text

The amount of compensation otherwise recoverable shall be increased one-half, together with costs and expenses not to exceed two hundred fifty dollars ($250), where the employee is injured by reason of the serious and willful misconduct of any of the following:

**(a)** The employer, or his managing representative.

**(b)** If the employer is a partnership, on the part of one of the partners or a managing representative or general superintendent thereof.

**(c)** If the employer is a corporation, on the part of an executive, managing officer, or general superintendent thereof.

## Statutory Context

LAB §4553 provides a 50% enhancement to the otherwise-recoverable workers compensation benefit when the employee's injury was caused by the employer's serious and willful misconduct. The enhancement requires:
1. **Injury** — a compensable workers compensation injury
2. **Serious and willful misconduct** — by the employer or their representative (as defined in §§(a)-(c))
3. **Causation** — the misconduct caused or substantially contributed to the injury

The enhancement is not a separate claim — it is applied to the workers comp benefit that is otherwise owed. Maximum cost/expense recovery is $250 (not adjusted for inflation in the statute).

**"Serious and willful misconduct"** is not defined in §4553 itself. California courts define it as something more than negligence: a deliberate act or omission made with knowledge of the probable consequences, OR an act that the actor knew was likely to result in injury. It is not equivalent to criminal intent — it is a heightened form of disregard for employee safety.
