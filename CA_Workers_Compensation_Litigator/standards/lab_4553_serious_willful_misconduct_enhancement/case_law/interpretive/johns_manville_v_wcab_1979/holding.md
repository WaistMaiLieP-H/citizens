## Holding

The Court of Appeal addressed what constitutes "serious and willful misconduct" under §4553 in the context of a corporate employer. Key holdings:

1. **Knowledge of probable injury is required** — "serious and willful" is not ordinary negligence and not even gross negligence. It requires that the employer (or its managing agent/officer) knew that the conduct was likely to result in injury, and acted in disregard of that knowledge.

2. **Corporate liability through the three-tier structure** — for a corporation to be liable under §4553, the misconduct must be that of an executive, managing officer, or general superintendent. Line supervisors and foremen are not sufficient unless they qualify as "managing agents."

3. **"Serious and willful" is a compound requirement** — both elements must be met: the conduct was serious (not trivial) AND it was willful (deliberate, not inadvertent). Neither element alone is sufficient.

## Application
