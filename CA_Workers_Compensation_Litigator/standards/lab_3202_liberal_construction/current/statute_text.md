## Statutory Text

> This division and Division 5 (commencing with Section 6300) shall be liberally construed by the courts with the purpose of extending their benefits for the protection of persons injured in the course of their employment.

---

## Scope of Coverage

**"This division"** — Division 4 of the Labor Code, which includes:
- LAB §3600 et seq. (workers compensation liability)
- LAB §4000 et seq. (compensation)
- LAB §4553 (serious and willful misconduct enhancement)
- LAB §4600 et seq. (medical treatment)
- LAB §4663 et seq. (apportionment)

**"Division 5 (commencing with Section 6300)"** — Cal/OSHA and workplace safety provisions

**Directed to:** Courts — the construction rule is binding on judicial interpretation of ambiguous statutory provisions within Divisions 4 and 5

**Purpose stated:** "Extending their benefits for the protection of persons injured in the course of their employment" — the beneficiary is the injured worker
