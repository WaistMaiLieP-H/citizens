# Western Growers Ins. Co. v. Workers' Comp. Appeals Bd. (1993) 16 Cal.App.4th 227

**Citation:** (1993) 16 Cal.App.4th 227
**Court:** California Court of Appeal
**Standard:** lab_3202_liberal_construction
**Status:** PROPOSED — verify against primary source before filing reliance

---

## Holding

The Court of Appeal confirmed the broad application of §3202's liberal construction mandate in the context of insurance coverage disputes. Key holdings:

1. **§3202 applies to coverage disputes, not just benefit calculations** — when a workers compensation insurer disputes whether a policy covers a particular type of claim or employer, §3202's liberal construction mandate applies to resolve coverage ambiguities in the worker's favor.

2. **The purpose clause controls interpretation** — "extending their benefits for the protection of persons injured in the course of their employment" is an active legislative direction, not merely a preamble. Courts apply it to every ambiguous term in Division 4.

3. **Exclusions are construed narrowly** — because the purpose of the statute is to extend benefits, any provision that would narrow or exclude coverage is construed strictly against the excluding party.

## Application

Western Growers is the authority for applying §3202 to insurance coverage and policy disputes — not just to the substantive benefit standards. In the UA342 identity replacement scenario, if an insurer argues that WC coverage was excluded or that Michael's employment status was terminated before the injury, §3202 requires that coverage ambiguities be resolved in Michael's favor.
