# LAB §3202 — Stage 03: Current (1937 Text — Unchanged)

**Stage:** 03_current
**Pointer:** See `standards/lab_3202_liberal_construction/current/` for full current text and provenance.

The text of §3202 has not been amended since the 1937 Labor Code recodification. Current text:

"This division and Division 5 (commencing with Section 6300) shall be liberally construed by the courts with the purpose of extending their benefits for the protection of persons injured in the course of their employment."

**Evolution chain closes here.** The rule enacted in 1913, recodified in 1937, is the operative rule today. 113 years of stability.
