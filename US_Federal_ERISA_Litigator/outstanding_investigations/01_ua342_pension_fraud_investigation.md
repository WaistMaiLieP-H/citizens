# Outstanding Investigation — UA Local 342 Pension Fraud
## Priority: TIER 1 — Active Case #37

## Discovery event
Credit check revealed steward listed as "retired at 44." No retirement election was ever filed by the steward. Pension is frozen. Someone used the steward's identity to submit a retirement election to the UA Local 342 Pension Plan — an unauthorized transaction that triggered retirement status and froze the pension.

## Investigation steps

### Step 1 — Document demand (IMMEDIATE)
File § 1132(c)(1) written demand with the UA Local 342 Pension Fund administrator:
- Address: UA Local 342 Pension Fund, [confirm address from UA Local 342 or PBGC records]
- Method: Certified mail, return receipt requested
- Documents demanded: See Methodology Phase 1 for full list
- **Deadline tracking:** Day 30 response deadline; penalty begins day 31 at $110/day

### Step 2 — Credit reporting investigation
Obtain full tri-bureau credit reports and identify:
- When did "retired" status first appear?
- What entity reported this status?
- What is the account/tradeline showing the retirement?
- File dispute with all three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) — the retirement status, if based on a fraudulent ERISA election, is inaccurate information under FCRA

### Step 3 — SSA records demand
File Form SSA-7004 (Request for Social Security Statement):
- Confirm whether any retirement benefit has been applied for or commenced under the steward's SSN
- Cross-reference with the UA Local 342 pension plan status
- If a separate SSA retirement application was filed using the steward's identity, this is a parallel § 1028 violation

### Step 4 — UA Local 342 union records
Contact UA Local 342 directly (the union, not the pension fund):
- Confirm active membership status
- Confirm the steward's current work history and journeyman status
- Obtain any records showing active union membership that contradict the "retired" status
- This evidence will support the § 1132(a)(1)(B) claim: the steward is and was an active participant, not a retiree

### Step 5 — IRS records (Form 1099-R)
Request IRS transcripts to determine whether any 1099-R has been issued under the steward's SSN for pension distributions. If the fraudulent claimant has been receiving distributions, the IRS will have records.

### Step 6 — Timeline overlay
Once the date of the fraudulent election is known:
- Overlay against SIM swap timeline (CA_Telecom_Privacy_Litigator investigation)
- Overlay against family law proceeding dates
- Overlay against the broader identity compromise pattern (SSN theft, treasury securities, Jaguar financing)
