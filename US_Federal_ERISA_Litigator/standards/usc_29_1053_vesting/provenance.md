## Legislative History

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1974 | ERISA enacted (Pub. L. 93-406); § 1053 established minimum vesting as one of ERISA's core participant protections |
| 1986 | Tax Reform Act — reduced cliff vesting from 10-year to 5-year |
| 1989 | OBRA 1989 — reduced graded vesting start from 5 years to 3 years (current 3-7 schedule) |
| 2006 | Pension Protection Act — further tightened vesting for matching contributions |
| 2019 | SECURE Act — made additional adjustments to part-time worker vesting |
