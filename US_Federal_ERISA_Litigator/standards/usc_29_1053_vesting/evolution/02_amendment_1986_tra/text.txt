29 U.S.C. § 1053 — Amendment by Tax Reform Act of 1986
Pub. L. 99-514, 100 Stat. 2085 (October 22, 1986)

The Tax Reform Act of 1986 replaced the 1974 vesting schedules with the current standard ones:

BEFORE (1974 schedules):
- 10-year cliff vesting, OR
- Rule of 45 (age + service = 45), OR
- 5-15 year graded schedule

AFTER (1986 TRA schedules):
- 5-YEAR CLIFF: 100% after 5 years (no vesting before year 5)
- 3-7 YEAR GRADED: 20% at year 3, 40% at year 4, 60% at year 5, 80% at year 6, 100% at year 7

These are the schedules that have applied since 1989 (effective date of TRA vesting changes).
