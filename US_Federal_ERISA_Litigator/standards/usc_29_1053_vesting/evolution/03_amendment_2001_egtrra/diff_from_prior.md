# Diff: 1986 TRA Schedules → 2001 EGTRRA (§ 1053)

## What changed: employer matching contributions only

EGTRRA 2001 targeted 401(k) employer matching contributions specifically. The concern: employees
were leaving jobs before their employer match vested, leaving matching contributions on the table.
By accelerating matching vesting to 3-year cliff or 2-6 year graded, Congress made employer matches
vest faster and gave workers more portability.

Standard pension benefit accruals: unchanged. 5-year cliff or 3-7 year graded still applies.

## Application to Case #37

If UA Local 342's pension is a traditional multiemployer defined-benefit plan (the most common
structure for union trades), EGTRRA's changes to employer matching don't directly apply. The standard
defined-benefit plan vesting schedules govern.

If UA 342 also offers a 401(k) component with employer matching, EGTRRA would accelerate vesting
of those matching contributions. Either way, 16 years of service = fully vested under any schedule.
