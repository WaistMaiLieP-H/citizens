29 U.S.C. § 1053 — Amendment by Economic Growth and Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2001
Pub. L. 107-16, 115 Stat. 38 (June 7, 2001)

EGTRRA 2001 added accelerated vesting schedules for EMPLOYER MATCHING CONTRIBUTIONS to
defined-contribution plans (401(k) and similar):

For employer matching contributions:
- 3-YEAR CLIFF: 100% vested after 3 years of service (0% before year 3)
- 2-6 YEAR GRADED:
    2 years = 20%
    3 years = 40%
    4 years = 60%
    5 years = 80%
    6 years = 100%

These accelerated schedules apply specifically to employer matching contributions.
Standard pension accrual benefits continue to use the 1986 TRA schedules (5-year cliff / 3-7 year graded).

Multiemployer plans were not affected by EGTRRA's matching contribution changes.
