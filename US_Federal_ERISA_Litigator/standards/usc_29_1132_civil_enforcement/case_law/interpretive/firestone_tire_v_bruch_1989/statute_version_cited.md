# Statute Version Cited — Firestone Tire & Rubber Co. v. Bruch (1989)

## ERISA version at decision: 1974 original § 1132

Firestone was decided in 1989. The § 1132(a)(1)(B) provision being interpreted was the 1974 original
text — no amendments to that subsection had been made. The 2006 PPA amendments were 17 years away.

## Evolution stage this case maps to
`evolution/01_origin_1974/` — the Court was interpreting the original 1974 civil enforcement provision.

## Significance for evolution chain
Firestone established the standard-of-review framework for § 1132(a)(1)(B) claims. Because the
statute text has not changed, this framework governs all subsequent cases under the same provision.
The Court in Firestone was filling a gap in the statute — § 1132 said participants could sue, but
said nothing about how courts should review administrator decisions. Firestone answered that
question by looking to the trust-law backdrop against which Congress enacted ERISA.
