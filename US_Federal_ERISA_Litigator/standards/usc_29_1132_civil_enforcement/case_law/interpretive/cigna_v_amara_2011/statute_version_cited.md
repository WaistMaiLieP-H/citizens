# Statute Version — CIGNA Corp. v. Amara (2011)

## Provision at Issue
29 U.S.C. § 1132(a)(3) — "appropriate equitable relief"

## Version Applied
The Court applied § 1132(a)(3) as enacted in 1974 and unchanged in the operative language through 2011. The phrase "other appropriate equitable relief" has remained constant since ERISA's enactment.

## Relevant Text Before the Court
"(a) A civil action may be brought— ... (3) by a participant, beneficiary, or fiduciary (A) to enjoin any act or practice which violates any provision of this subchapter or the terms of the plan, or (B) to obtain other appropriate equitable relief (i) to redress such violations or (ii) to enforce any provisions of this subchapter or the terms of the plan."

## Interpretive Move
The Court held that "appropriate equitable relief" means relief that was "typically available in equity" — and historically equity courts could impose surcharge on fiduciaries, order reformation of instruments obtained by fraud, and apply estoppel against parties who made material misrepresentations. All of these are therefore available under § 1132(a)(3).

## Current Applicability
The 2026 version of § 1132(a)(3) is identical to the version at issue in Amara. The holding applies without modification.
