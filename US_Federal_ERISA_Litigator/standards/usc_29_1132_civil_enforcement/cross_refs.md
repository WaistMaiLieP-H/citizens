## Federal Statutory / Regulatory Cross-References

- **29 U.S.C. § 1144** (ERISA § 514) — preemption; state law claims "relating to" ERISA plans are preempted; must be pursued under § 1132
- **29 U.S.C. § 1133** — claims and appeals procedures; plans must have internal claims procedures; exhaustion before § 1132 suit required
- **29 C.F.R. § 2560.503-1** — DOL claims procedure regulations; sets minimum timeframes for plan responses
- **28 U.S.C. § 1331** — federal question jurisdiction; ERISA claims arise under federal law, conferring jurisdiction in federal district courts
- **28 U.S.C. § 1367** — supplemental jurisdiction over related state law claims not preempted

## Key Supreme Court Cases

- **Mertens v. Hewitt Associates, 508 U.S. 248 (1993)** — "appropriate equitable relief" in § 1132(a)(3) limited to traditional equity; no compensatory damages
- **Varity Corp. v. Howe, 516 U.S. 489 (1996)** — § 1132(a)(3) individual equitable relief available for fiduciary breach where no other adequate ERISA remedy exists
- **CIGNA Corp. v. Amara, 563 U.S. 421 (2011)** — equitable remedies including surcharge, reformation, and estoppel available under § 1132(a)(3)
- **Firestone Tire & Rubber Co. v. Bruch, 489 U.S. 101 (1989)** — standard of review for benefit denial claims
