# Diff: 1974 Origin → 2006 Pension Protection Act

## What stayed the same (operative benefit-recovery provisions)

The three core causes of action were not touched:
- § 1132(a)(1)(B) — benefits due: identical
- § 1132(a)(2) — fiduciary breach relief: identical
- § 1132(a)(3) — equitable relief: identical

Federal court exclusive jurisdiction, no-amount-in-controversy, attorney fee discretion all unchanged.

## What PPA 2006 added

**New disclosure obligations → new penalties:**
PPA created new mandatory disclosures (annual funding notices, blackout notices, diversification notices)
and extended § 1132's civil penalty framework to cover those new obligations. This is the pattern:
Congress creates a disclosure duty → plugs that duty into § 1132(c)'s enforcement mechanism.

**Investment advice enforcement:**
PPA created a new class of permitted investment advisors under § 408(b)(14). It also created a new
enforcement track when those advisors violate the conditions of the exemption. The participant can sue
under § 1132 for any losses caused by the advice.

## Logical delta

1974 logic: "Give participants federal courts to enforce benefit rights + document production penalty."
2006 logic: "Add new disclosure obligations (funding transparency, blackout notices) + wire them into
the existing penalty mechanism." The architecture is unchanged; Congress keeps adding new disclosure
obligations and plugging them into the same penalty socket.

## Application to Case #37

PPA 2006 changes do not directly affect the Case #37 pension fraud theory. The core claims under
§ 1132(a)(1)(B) and (a)(3) — recovery of frozen benefits and equitable restoration — are governed
by the 1974 provisions, which PPA left intact. However:

The PPA annual funding notice requirement means the UA Local 342 pension plan is legally required
to send annual funding notices. Those notices (or failure to provide them) are evidence of the plan's
awareness of Michael's participant status — or the absence of that notice suggests his participant
status was altered without his knowledge.
