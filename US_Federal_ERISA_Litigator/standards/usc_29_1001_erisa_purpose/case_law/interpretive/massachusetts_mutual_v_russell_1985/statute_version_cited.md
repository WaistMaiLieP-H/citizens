# Statute Version — Massachusetts Mutual Life Insurance Co. v. Russell (1985)

## Provisions at Issue
- 29 U.S.C. § 1001 — Congressional findings and policy
- 29 U.S.C. § 1109 — Personal liability for fiduciary breach
- 29 U.S.C. § 1132(a)(2) — Civil enforcement via § 1109

## Version Applied
The Court applied the original 1974 ERISA text. The provisions at issue have not been amended in any way that affects this holding.

## Key Language Construed
§ 1109(a): "Any person who is a fiduciary with respect to a plan who breaches any of the responsibilities, obligations, or duties imposed upon fiduciaries by this subchapter shall be personally liable to make good to such plan any losses to the plan resulting from each such breach..."

The critical phrase is "to make good to such plan" — the plan is the beneficiary, not the individual participant.

## Current Applicability
The 2026 versions of §§ 1109 and 1132(a)(2) are identical to the versions at issue in Russell. The holding applies without modification. The subsequent Amara (2011) holding did not overrule Russell — it supplemented it by holding that § 1132(a)(3) provides individual equitable remedies that (a)(2) does not.
