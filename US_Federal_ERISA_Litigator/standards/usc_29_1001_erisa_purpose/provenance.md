## Legislative Context

ERISA was signed into law by President Ford on Labor Day, September 2, 1974. The Act was the culmination of ten years of Congressional hearings following the 1963 Studebaker pension collapse. § 1001 represents Congress's formal findings and the statutory declaration of policy that governs interpretation of all ERISA provisions.
