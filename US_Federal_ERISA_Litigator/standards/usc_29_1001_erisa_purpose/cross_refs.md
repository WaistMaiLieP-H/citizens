## Interpretive Note

§ 1001 is a "liberal construction" anchor. Courts that must choose between a narrow or broad reading of ERISA provisions cite § 1001 for the proposition that ERISA should be liberally construed to protect worker retirement benefits. The Supreme Court has cited § 1001 in cases where it expanded ERISA's coverage, broadened participant standing, or extended fiduciary accountability.
