## Federal Statutory Cross-References

- **29 U.S.C. § 1002(21)(A)** — definition of "fiduciary"; only fiduciaries are subject to § 1104
- **29 U.S.C. § 1103** — plan asset requirements; fiduciaries must hold plan assets in trust
- **29 U.S.C. § 1105** — co-fiduciary liability; extends § 1104 accountability to fiduciaries who enable or conceal breach
- **29 U.S.C. § 1106** — prohibited transactions; certain self-dealing is per se prohibited
- **29 U.S.C. § 1059** — employer recordkeeping obligations; supports § 1104 prudence duty for records
- **29 C.F.R. § 2509.75-8** — DOL interpretive bulletins on fiduciary responsibility

## Key Cases

- **Donovan v. Bierwirth, 680 F.2d 263 (2d Cir. 1982)** — leading case on loyalty duty; trustees who consider employer interests instead of participant interests breach § 1104
- **Pegram v. Herdrich, 530 U.S. 211 (2000)** — distinguishes fiduciary decisions from "settlor" (plan design) decisions; only fiduciary decisions are subject to § 1104
- **Fifth Third Bancorp v. Dudenhoeffer, 573 U.S. 409 (2014)** — ESOP fiduciaries same § 1104 prudence standard as other plan fiduciaries
