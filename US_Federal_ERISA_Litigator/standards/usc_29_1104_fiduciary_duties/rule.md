# Rule — 29 U.S.C. § 1104 (ERISA § 404)
## Fiduciary Duties

**Source:** Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, Pub. L. 93-406, § 404, 88 Stat. 874 (Sept. 2, 1974)  
**Current through:** Pub. L. 117-2 (American Rescue Plan Act, 2021)

---

### (a) Prudent man standard of care

**(1)** Subject to sections 1103(c) and (d), 1342, and 1344 of this title, a fiduciary shall discharge his duties with respect to a plan solely in the interest of the participants and beneficiaries and—

&emsp;**(A)** for the exclusive purpose of:

&emsp;&emsp;**(i)** providing benefits to participants and their beneficiaries; and

&emsp;&emsp;**(ii)** defraying reasonable expenses of administering the plan;

&emsp;**(B)** with the care, skill, prudence, and diligence under the circumstances then prevailing that a prudent man acting in a like capacity and familiar with such matters would use in the conduct of an enterprise of a similar character and with like aims;

&emsp;**(C)** by diversifying the investments of the plan so as to minimize the risk of large losses, unless under the circumstances it is clearly prudent not to do so; and

&emsp;**(D)** in accordance with the documents and instruments governing the plan insofar as such documents and instruments are consistent with the provisions of this subchapter and part 3 of this subtitle.

**(2)** In the case of an eligible individual account plan (as defined in section 1107(d)(3) of this title), the diversification requirement of paragraph (1)(C) and the prudence requirement (only to the extent that it requires diversification) of paragraph (1)(B) is not violated by acquisition or holding of qualifying employer real property or qualifying employer securities (as defined in section 1107(d)(4) and (5) of this title).

### (b) Indicia of ownership of assets

A fiduciary of a plan who is a fiduciary of the plan solely because of service on the board of directors of a corporation shall not be subject to the requirements of this section (other than paragraph (1)(B)) in his capacity as a director.

### (e) Plan to be administered in accordance with trust document

For purposes of this part, in the case of a plan that is not a trust, a fiduciary shall discharge his duties pursuant to subsection (a) in accordance with the documents and instruments governing the plan.
