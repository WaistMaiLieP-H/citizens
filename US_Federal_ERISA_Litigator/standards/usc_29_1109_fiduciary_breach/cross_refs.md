## Federal Statutory Cross-References

- **29 U.S.C. § 1002(21)(A)** — definition of "fiduciary"; the gateway requirement for § 1109 liability
- **29 U.S.C. § 1104** — the specific fiduciary duties; breach of any of these triggers § 1109
- **29 U.S.C. § 1105** — co-fiduciary liability; extends § 1109 to fiduciaries who enable or conceal breach
- **29 U.S.C. § 1108** — exemptions from § 1104 and § 1109; certain prohibited transactions are exempt; confirm no exemption applies before filing
- **29 U.S.C. § 1132(a)(2)** — the civil enforcement provision for § 1109 claims

## Key Cases

- **Massachusetts Mutual Life Insurance Co. v. Russell, 473 U.S. 134 (1985)** — § 1109 recovery goes to the plan, not directly to individual participants
- **LaRue v. DeWolff, Boberg & Associates, 552 U.S. 248 (2008)** — § 1132(a)(2) allows a participant to seek recovery for breach that affected only their individual account in a defined contribution plan, not just plan-wide breaches
