# Statute Version Cited — Fifth Third Bancorp v. Dudenhoeffer (2014)

## ERISA version at decision: Post-PPA 2006

Dudenhoeffer was decided in 2014. The Court was interpreting § 1109 as it existed after the PPA 2006
amendments. However, § 1109(a) — the core personal liability provision — was not amended by PPA, so
the operative text was the same as the 1974 original.

## Evolution stage this case maps to
`evolution/03_current/` — decided after PPA 2006; applying the current version of § 1109(a).

## Note on ESOP context
Dudenhoeffer arose in the ESOP context (§ 1108(e) plans). The UA Local 342 pension is likely a
multi-employer defined-benefit plan or a defined-contribution plan, not an ESOP. The application to
Case #37 is through the general § 1104 prudent-expert standard that Dudenhoeffer confirmed applies
to all fiduciaries, not just ESOP fiduciaries.
