# Statute Version — Tibble v. Edison International (2015)

## Provisions at Issue
- 29 U.S.C. § 1104 — Fiduciary duties (prudent expert standard)
- 29 U.S.C. § 1109 — Personal liability for breach
- 29 U.S.C. § 1113 — Statute of limitations (six-year / three-year rule)

## Version Applied
The Court applied the original 1974 ERISA text, which has not been amended in any way material to the holding. The phrase "last action which constituted a part of the breach" in § 1113 has been in the statute since 1974.

## Key Language Construed
§ 1113: "No action may be commenced under this subchapter with respect to a fiduciary's breach of any responsibility, duty, or obligation under this part, or with respect to a violation of section 1106 of this title, after the earlier of — (1) six years after (A) the date of the last action which constituted a part of the breach or violation..."

"Last action which constituted a part of the breach" — the Tibble Court held this phrase encompasses continuing monitoring failures, not just the original decision that created the imprudent condition.

## Current Applicability
The 2026 versions of §§ 1104, 1109, and 1113 are identical to the versions at issue in Tibble. The holding applies without modification. DOL has not issued regulations narrowing the Tibble monitoring duty.
