## Basis for This Request

This request is made pursuant to ERISA § 104(b)(4), 29 U.S.C. § 1024(b)(4), which requires the plan administrator to furnish copies of plan documents to any participant upon written request. The plan administrator is required to furnish the requested documents within **thirty (30) days** of receipt of this written request.

Mr. Hartmann is and has been an active participant in the Plan as a journeyman plumber and member of UA Local 342. It has come to Mr. Hartmann's attention that his pension account reflects a status change that he did not authorize, specifically indicating a retirement or benefit commencement election that Mr. Hartmann did not submit. Mr. Hartmann is not retired and has not submitted any retirement election.

The documents requested above are necessary to investigate this unauthorized change and to protect Mr. Hartmann's rights under ERISA.
