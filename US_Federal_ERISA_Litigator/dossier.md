## The Case Behind This Citizen

**UA Local 342 Pension Identity Replacement (#37):**
A credit check run on Michael Hartmann returned "retired" at age 44. He is a UA Local 342
plumber — active, not retired. The UA Local 342 pension is a multi-employer Taft-Hartley fund
governed by ERISA. If someone substituted a retirement status in the pension fund records,
the instrument of that substitution may be the same SSA record that shows "retired at 44."

The claim is §1140 interference: it is unlawful to discharge or otherwise discriminate against
a participant to prevent the attainment of any right to which the participant may become entitled
under the plan. "Otherwise discriminate" is not limited to employment action. Substituting a
participant's status in plan records to trigger a freeze on benefit accrual is interference.

The §502(a)(1)(B) claim runs in parallel: the participant may sue to recover benefits due under
the plan. If the pension was frozen on the basis of a fraudulent retirement record, the plan owes
the contributions and accrued benefits from the date of the fraudulent freeze forward.

Estimated damages: ~$2.4M total.
