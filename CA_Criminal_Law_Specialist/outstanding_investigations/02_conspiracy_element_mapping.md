## Element 2: Dual Intent (Beeman — both layers required)

**Required finding:** Each conspirator (a) intended to enter the agreement AND (b) intended that the criminal objective be achieved.

### Intent layer 1 (intent to agree)

Each actor who coordinated their conduct with another was doing so intentionally — coordination is not accidental. The fact that the same tactics (false reports, DVRO filings, synchronized court dates, CPS referrals) were deployed repeatedly over 16 years across multiple actors demonstrates that each actor knew they were part of a coordinated effort.

**Key actors and their likely Layer 1 showing:**

| Actor | Layer 1 Evidence |
|-------|-----------------|
| Mother | Orchestration role — directing, coordinating, briefing other actors across all periods |
| Christina | Consistent participation in all tactical categories (false reports, DVRO, custody filings, 6/16/2023 positioning) |
| Ryan McClaran | Technical execution of digital arm of scheme (SIM swap, proxy communications) |
| Unknown CPS referral actors | Filed at precisely coordinated times — random actors don't coordinate to the day |

### Intent layer 2 (intent that the crime be achieved)

**§ 182(a)(2) object — false arrest:**
- Intent: the 6/16/2023 positioning, APD contact, and false accusation were intended to produce an arrest. The outcome (Criminal Case 04-23-01959) is the intended result.
- Evidence: the ice cream pretext, the pre-positioned complainant, the immediate APD response — all consistent with a plan whose objective was an arrest.

**§ 182(a)(3) object — false maintenance of suit:**
- Intent: family court filings were not seeking good-faith legal relief; the volume, timing, and content show the filings were instrumentalized to maintain legal control and narrative.
- Evidence: unsigned court orders, coordinated filing dates, false factual allegations repeated across filings.

**§ 182(a)(5) object — obstruct justice:**
- Intent: blocking the steward's access to legal remedies, communications, and due process is only useful if the objective is to prevent legitimate legal outcomes.
- Evidence: SIM swap blocking legal calls; Tor connection failures; CalVCB procedural sabotage; blocked access to disability determinations.

**Evidence needed:**
- [ ] Any communications between actors showing knowledge of the criminal objective
- [ ] Prior history of false report filings that went unsubstantiated — shows pattern of intentional use of false reports as a tactic
- [ ] Any actor communications after key events acknowledging the outcome (e.g., texts or emails referencing the arrest)
