# Statute version cited in People v. Holifield (1988)

*Holifield* was decided in 1988, two years after the 1986 enactment of § 243(e). The court applied the original version of § 243(e), which is substantively similar to the current version for the cohabitation element. Subsequent amendments to § 243(e) have expanded probation conditions (§ 243(e)(2), (3)) but have not changed the underlying cohabitation element.

**Cohabitation element stability:** The specific phrase "a person with whom the defendant is cohabiting" has been in § 243(e)(1) since enactment and has not been amended. *Holifield*'s multi-factor test applies with identical force to current § 243(e)(1) prosecutions.

**Consistency check:** Modern practice applies the *Holifield* factors verbatim. No textual drift.
