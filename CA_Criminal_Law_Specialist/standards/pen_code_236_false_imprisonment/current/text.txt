California Penal Code § 236 — False imprisonment

False imprisonment is the unlawful violation of the personal liberty of another.

---

Source: leginfo.legislature.ca.gov via VernenLegal MCP, retrieved 2026-04-10.

Punishment: Pen. Code § 237 (not § 236). Misdemeanor with fine and/or county jail, or felony ("effected by violence, menace, fraud, or deceit") with state prison under § 1170(h).
