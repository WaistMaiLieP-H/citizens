# Statute version cited in People v. Haney (1977) 75 Cal.App.3d 308

**§ 236** — "The unlawful violation of the personal liberty of another."

The 1977 text of § 236 is identical to the original 1872 Penal Code enactment and unchanged through the current 2026 version. The elements framework (act of restraint, confinement, without consent, unlawfulness, general intent) elaborated in Haney applies uniformly to modern § 236 prosecutions.
