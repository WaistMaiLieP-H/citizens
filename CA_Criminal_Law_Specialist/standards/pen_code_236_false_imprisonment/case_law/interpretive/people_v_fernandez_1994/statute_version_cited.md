# Statute version cited in People v. Fernandez (1994) 26 Cal.App.4th 710

**§ 236** — "The unlawful violation of the personal liberty of another." (unchanged 1872 text)

**§ 237** — The punishment provision as it existed in 1994 established the misdemeanor/felony split based on whether the false imprisonment was effected by violence, menace, fraud, or deceit. This bifurcated structure has remained stable; current § 237 preserves the same four felony-elevating factors and substantially the same punishment ranges. The definitional framework in Fernandez (violence = force beyond what restraint requires; menace = implied or express threat; fraud/deceit = inducing submission by false representations) remains good law.
