California Penal Code § 166 — Criminal contempt of court

(a) Misdemeanor contempts of court (abbreviated structure):
  (1) Disorderly/contemptuous/insolent behavior in court
  (2) Same behavior before a referee, jury, or authorized proceeding
  (3) Breach of peace/noise/disturbance tending to interrupt court proceedings
  (4) Willful disobedience of the terms of a process or court order (including out-of-state orders and pretrial orders)
  (5) Willful resistance to lawful order or process of a court
  (6) Contumacious refusal to be sworn or answer material questions as a witness
  (7) Publication of false or grossly inaccurate court proceeding reports
  (8) Presenting unauthorized affidavits/testimony/representations in aggravation or mitigation at sentencing
  (9) Willful disobedience of gang injunction orders

(b) Enhanced penalty — contact with victim by person previously convicted of § 646.9 (stalking): up to 1 year jail, up to $5,000 fine. Each contact = separate violation.

(c) Enhanced penalty — willful and knowing violation of specific protective or stay-away orders (§ 136.2, § 1203.097(a)(2), elder/dependent adult abuse orders, § 1201.3, § 273.5(j), Family Code §§ 6320/6389 orders, exclusion orders, conduct-enjoining orders): up to 1 year jail, up to $1,000 fine. Mandatory minimum 48 hours if physical injury. Second conviction within 7 years with violence or credible threat: county jail up to 1 year OR state prison 16 months / 2 / 3 years. Primary enforcement responsibility on the prosecuting agency.

(d) Firearm possession by protected-order subject prohibited (referring to § 29825 punishment).

(e) Probation conditions and fine provisions.
