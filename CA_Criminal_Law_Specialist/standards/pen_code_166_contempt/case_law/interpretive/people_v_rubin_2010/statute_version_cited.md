# Statute version cited in People v. Rubin (2010) 188 Cal.App.4th 1279

**§ 166(a)(4)** — willful disobedience of a court order
**§ 166(c)(1)** — willful and knowing violation of specific protective orders (DV context)

The statute as it existed in 2010 is substantially identical to the current 2026 version in all elements relevant to this case. The willful-and-knowing standard and the notice/due-process requirements addressed in Rubin remain applicable to current § 166 prosecutions.
