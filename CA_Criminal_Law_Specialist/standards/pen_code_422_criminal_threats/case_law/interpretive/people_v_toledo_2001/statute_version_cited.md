# Statute version cited in People v. Toledo (2001)

*Toledo* applied § 422 as it existed in 2001. The core UUIS framework and all five elements construed in *Toledo* remain in the current statute without substantive amendment. The 2006 addition of the public official aggravating factor (subd. (b)) did not change the *Toledo* analysis.

**Consistency check:** *Toledo*'s holdings on the UUIS test, conditional threats, sustained fear, and specific intent apply directly to current § 422 claims.
