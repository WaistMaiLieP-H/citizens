# Statute version cited in Brady v. Maryland (1963)

*Brady* is a federal constitutional case — it did not construe Pen. Code § 1054.1 (which was enacted in 1990, 27 years after *Brady*). *Brady* establishes the constitutional due process floor under the Fourteenth Amendment. Section 1054.1(e)'s requirement to disclose "any exculpatory evidence" codifies *Brady* in California state law.

**Consistency check:** *Brady* applies to current § 1054.1(e) claims. The constitutional obligation is at least as protective as the statute. No drift concern.
