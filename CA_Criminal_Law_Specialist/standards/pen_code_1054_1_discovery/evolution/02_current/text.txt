§ 1054.1 has been refined by case law but not materially amended by the Legislature since 1990. The six categories remain unchanged.

Enforcement mechanism: § 1054.5 — court may make orders for continuance, sanction, or dismissal for discovery violations.

In conjunction with: § 1054.3 (defendant's reciprocal obligations), § 1054.5 (sanctions), § 1054.7 (work product privilege carve-out).

Brady/Giglio constitutional overlay: § 1054.1(e) captures the constitutional floor but California's mandatory disclosure rule is broader — exculpatory evidence must be disclosed even if the defense did not request it.
