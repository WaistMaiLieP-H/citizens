# Statute version cited in People v. Sargent (1999)

The *Sargent* court applied Pen. Code § 273a as it existed in 1999. The current alternative-acts structure of § 273a has been in place since the 1961 revision. The 1984 and 1988 amendments to subdivision (c) (probation conditions) did not affect the mens rea analysis in *Sargent*.

**Relevant statutory text (1999 = 2026):**

The court construed subdivision (a) (felony level — circumstances likely to produce GBH or death) and specifically the "willfully" scienter element and the "having the care or custody" care-or-custody theory. The text of these provisions has not changed between 1999 and 2026.

**Consistency check:** The *Sargent* opinion quotes § 273a's operative language at 19 Cal.4th 1215. The quotation matches current § 273a(a) text. The court's analysis of willfulness (general intent for direct infliction) and criminal negligence (for indirect infliction) applies with identical force to current § 273a prosecutions.
