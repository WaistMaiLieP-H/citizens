# Statute Version Cited — People v. Whitehurst (1992)

**Case:** People v. Whitehurst (1992) 9 Cal.App.4th 1045
**Statute construed:** Pen. Code § 273a

## Version at Time of Decision

**Year of decision:** 1992
**Applicable evolution stage:** `02_amendment_1976` — § 273a as amended through the 1976 reorganization that created the (a)/(b) felony/misdemeanor structure (also cited as § 273a(2) in the 1992 version, before the 1999 restructuring)

## Textual Note

In 1992, § 273a was structured differently than its current form. The misdemeanor provision (now § 273a(b)) was then cited as § 273a(2). The case addresses the misdemeanor endangerment provision. Sargent (1999) and the subsequent reorganization of the statute consolidated the numbering to the current (a)/(b) format.

The parental discipline defense analyzed in Whitehurst applies to both the felony (§ 273a(a)) and misdemeanor (§ 273a(b)) provisions — though the case arose under what is now the misdemeanor track.
