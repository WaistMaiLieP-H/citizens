# Statute version cited in People v. Valdez (2002)

The *Valdez* court applied Pen. Code § 273a as it existed in 2002. The alternative-acts structure and the probation condition framework in subdivision (c) were in their current form. The court's analysis focused on the "care or custody" element and the criminal negligence mens rea.

**Relevant statutory text (2002 = 2026):** The "having the care or custody of any child, willfully causes or permits" language in § 273a(a) has not been amended since 1961 and reads identically in both 2002 and 2026.

**Consistency check:** The *Valdez* opinion applies to current § 273a prosecutions without any textual drift concerns. The court's holdings on (a) who qualifies as having care or custody and (b) the criminal negligence standard under the care-or-custody theory control modern practice.
