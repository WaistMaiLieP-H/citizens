# Statute version cited in People v. Rathert (2000)

*Rathert* applied Pen. Code § 529 as it existed in 2000, which was substantively identical to the 1872 original. Section 529 has not been textually amended in its substantive elements since 1872 — it is one of the longest-stable California statutes. The catchall in subd. (a)(3) that *Rathert* construed is the same language in force in 2026.

**Consistency check:** The *Rathert* opinion applies to current § 529(a)(3) prosecutions with full force. No textual drift. *Rathert*'s interpretation of "might, in any event, become liable" and "benefit" is the operative framework for 2026 cases.
