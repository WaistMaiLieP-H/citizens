# Statute version cited in People v. Frahs (2020)

*Frahs* applied § 1001.36 as enacted in 2018 (Stats. 2018, ch. 1005, AB 1810). The retroactivity holding does not depend on specific statutory language — it applies to the statute in its current form. The 2022 SB 1223 amendments to subdivision (b)(2) (strengthening the pro-defendant presumption) did not disturb the Frahs retroactivity rule.

**Consistency check:** *Frahs*'s retroactivity holding applies to current § 1001.36 claims. The prima facie standard and conditional reversal remedy remain controlling.
