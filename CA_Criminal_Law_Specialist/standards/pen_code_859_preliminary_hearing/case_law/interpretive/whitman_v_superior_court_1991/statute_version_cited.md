# Statute version cited in Whitman v. Superior Court (1991) 54 Cal.3d 1063

**§§ 859, 859b, 872 as amended by Proposition 115 (June 1990)**

Proposition 115 added § 872(b) (hearsay exception for law enforcement officers at preliminary hearings) and modified the preliminary hearing framework to permit one-officer presentation of the prosecution's case. The Whitman court evaluated these provisions as enacted by the 1990 initiative.

Current 2026 versions of §§ 859, 859b, and 872 preserve the Prop 115 framework as validated by Whitman. The hearsay procedures, timing rules (10-court-day and 60-day), and one-witness structure are unchanged in all elements relevant to the Whitman holding.
