# Statute version cited in People v. Carbajal (1995) 10 Cal.4th 1114

**§ 1203 as it existed in 1995**

The 1995 version of § 1203 provided the basic probation framework. Since Carbajal, § 1203 has been amended multiple times (AB 109 Realignment 2011, subsequent DV-specific provisions, COVID-era amendments). However, the Carbajal nexus test was established as a common law / constitutional doctrine, not a statutory interpretation of specific § 1203 language. The three-part nexus test applies to the current 2026 § 1203 framework without modification — it is the standard used by every California court in evaluating probation conditions regardless of the specific version of § 1203.
