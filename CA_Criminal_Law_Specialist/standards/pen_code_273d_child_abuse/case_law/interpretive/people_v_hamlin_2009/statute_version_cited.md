# Statute version cited in People v. Hamlin (2009)

*Hamlin* applied § 273d as it existed in 2009. The traumatic condition element and the "cruel or inhuman corporal punishment" language have not been substantively amended since 2009. The holding on the traumatic condition element applies directly to the current statute.

**Consistency check:** No statutory drift. *Hamlin*'s interpretation of "traumatic condition" applies to current § 273d claims.
