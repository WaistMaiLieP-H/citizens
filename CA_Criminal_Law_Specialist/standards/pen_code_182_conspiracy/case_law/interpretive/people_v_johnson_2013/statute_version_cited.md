# Statute version cited in People v. Johnson (2013)

*Johnson* applied § 182 as it existed in 2013. The agreement element, co-conspirator liability doctrine, and venue provision construed in *Johnson* reflect the statute as it has been since 1872 — no relevant amendments since the case was decided.

**Consistency check:** *Johnson*'s holdings on agreement, single conspiracy, and co-conspirator liability apply directly to current § 182 cases without any amendment-related concern.
