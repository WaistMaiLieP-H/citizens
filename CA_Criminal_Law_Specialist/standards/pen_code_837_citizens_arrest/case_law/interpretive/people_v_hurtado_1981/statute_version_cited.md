# Statute version cited in People v. Hurtado (1981) 124 Cal.App.3d 321

**§ 837 as it existed in 1981**

The 1981 version of § 837 is identical to the 1872 original text in all substantive respects. The 2018 gender-neutral amendment did not alter the "in presence" element or any other substantive provision. The Hurtado "in presence" analysis applies without modification to current § 837 prosecutions and civil false imprisonment claims.
