# Statute version cited in People v. Snyder (1982) 136 Cal.App.3d 608

**§ 837 as it existed in 1982**

Substantively identical to the 1872 original and the current 2026 version. The "actually committed" requirement in Grounds 2 and 3, and the Snyder court's analysis of the objective character of that requirement, apply without modification to current § 837 citizen's arrest analysis.
