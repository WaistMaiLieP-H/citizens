# Statute version cited in People v. Gonzales (2012)

*Gonzales* applied § 1054.5 as it existed in 2012. Section 1054.5 has not been amended since its 1990 enactment — the sanction framework, exhaustion requirement for witness exclusion, and bar on dismissal are all unchanged.

**Consistency check:** *Gonzales*'s holdings on broad discretion, harm-tailoring, and exhaustion apply directly to current § 1054.5 enforcement motions without any amendment-related concern.
