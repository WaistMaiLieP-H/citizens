# Statute version cited in this § 1001.95 doctrinal entry (2021)

This folder documents the doctrinal framework for § 1001.95 as enacted. Section 1001.95 was enacted by AB 3234 (Stats. 2020, ch. 334), effective January 1, 2021 — this is the first and current version of the statute. There is no prior version to compare against.

**Governing statute version:** § 1001.95 as enacted January 1, 2021 (AB 3234, Stats. 2020, ch. 334). Current text is the enacted text — no amendments as of August 2025.

**Key structural notes:**
- The statute gives the court (not the prosecutor) authority to offer diversion
- Eligibility: misdemeanor offenses, excluding specific exclusions listed in § 1001.95(e) (DUI — § 23640 survives; domestic violence — § 1000.6 survives)
- Diversion period: up to 24 months
- Successful completion: charges dismissed, no conviction recorded
- The 2022 *Tan v. Superior Court* decision (see sibling folder) confirmed the DUI exclusion under § 23640 as controlling over § 1001.95

**Consistency check:** Any published case applying § 1001.95 applies the enacted 2021 version. No amendment drift concerns as of August 2025.
