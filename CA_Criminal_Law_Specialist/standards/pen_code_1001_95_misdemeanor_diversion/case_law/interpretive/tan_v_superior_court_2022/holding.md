# Holding — Tan v. Superior Court (2022)

**Cite:** Tan v. Superior Court (People) (2022), California Court of Appeal, First Appellate District, Docket No. A163715

**Key holding:** Penal Code § 1001.95 (court-initiated misdemeanor diversion, effective January 1, 2021) does not supersede Vehicle Code § 23640's categorical prohibition on diversion for DUI offenses. The two statutes can be reconciled under the specific-statute-controls-over-general rule; implied repeal of § 23640 by § 1001.95 is disfavored. DUI defendants remain ineligible for § 1001.95 diversion.

**Doctrinal rule:** A newer, general diversion statute does not impliedly repeal a specific, targeted diversion exclusion in a different code where the two can coexist.

**For what it stands:** Scope and limits of § 1001.95 — specific pre-existing exclusions survive unless expressly repealed.
