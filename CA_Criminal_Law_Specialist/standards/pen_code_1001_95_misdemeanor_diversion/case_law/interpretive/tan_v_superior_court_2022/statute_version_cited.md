# Statute version cited in Tan v. Superior Court (2022)

*Tan v. Superior Court* applied Pen. Code § 1001.95 as enacted January 1, 2021 (AB 3234, Stats. 2020, ch. 334) and Veh. Code § 23640 (the DUI diversion exclusion statute). This is the first and governing version of § 1001.95 — no amendments existed as of the 2022 decision.

**Core statutory conflict the court resolved:**
- § 1001.95 (enacted 2021): general misdemeanor diversion, court-initiated
- § 23640 (existing): no diversion, probation, or suspension of proceedings for DUI offenses

The court held that § 23640's specific DUI exclusion controls over § 1001.95's general diversion grant. Specific over general, later enactment does not override the specific prohibition in § 23640 because AB 3234 did not expressly address the DUI exclusion.

**Consistency check:** The version of § 1001.95 applied in *Tan* matches the current enacted text. The § 23640 text applied by the court is also current. No drift concerns. *Tan*'s holding that DUI defendants are ineligible for § 1001.95 judicial diversion remains governing law as of August 2025.
