# Statute version cited in People v. Uhlemann (1973)

*Uhlemann* applied § 995 as it existed in 1973. The core § 995 standard — "reasonable or probable cause" and the mechanism for setting aside an information — has not been substantively amended since 1973. The § 995a "alternative procedure" was added later (1995) but does not change the core Uhlemann standard.

**Consistency check:** *Uhlemann*'s holding applies directly to current § 995 motions. No statutory drift on the controlling standard.
