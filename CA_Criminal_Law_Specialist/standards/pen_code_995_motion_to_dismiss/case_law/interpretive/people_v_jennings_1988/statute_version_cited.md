# Statute version cited in People v. Jennings (1988)

*Jennings* applied § 995 as it existed in 1988 — before the § 995a "alternative procedure" was added (1995). The core reviewing standard is unchanged; the § 995a amendment added a prosecution option (reopen preliminary hearing) that does not affect the substantive probable cause analysis.

**Consistency check:** *Jennings*'s holding applies directly to current § 995 motions.
