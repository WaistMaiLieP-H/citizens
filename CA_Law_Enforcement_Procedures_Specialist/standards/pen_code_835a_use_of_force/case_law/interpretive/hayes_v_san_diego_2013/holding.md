# Hayes v. County of San Diego, 57 Cal.4th 622 (2013)

## The Case

A wrongful death action against San Diego County deputies who shot and killed a man during a mental health crisis call. The California Supreme Court addressed what standard governs use-of-force claims under California law, before AB 392 changed the standard.

## What the Court Held

The California Supreme Court:

1. **Pre-AB 392 reasonableness standard**: Use of force by peace officers was evaluated under an "objective reasonableness" standard — substantially the same as the federal Graham v. Connor standard. The question was whether a reasonable officer in the same circumstances would have used the same level of force.

2. **Totality of circumstances**: The court evaluated force under the totality of circumstances known to the officer at the time, including the severity of the crime at issue, whether the suspect posed an immediate threat to the safety of officers or others, and whether the suspect was actively resisting arrest or attempting to evade arrest by flight.

3. **Mental health considerations**: The court acknowledged that subjects experiencing mental health crises may not respond to commands the way other subjects would, and that this is relevant to the reasonableness analysis.

## The Rule

**Under the pre-AB 392 standard, use of force was judged by objective reasonableness under the totality of circumstances. This standard has been superseded by AB 392's necessity standard effective January 1, 2020.**

## Why This Case Matters

Hayes represents the old standard — the standard that AB 392 deliberately replaced. Understanding Hayes is necessary to understand what AB 392 changed. Under Hayes, force only had to be "reasonable." Under section 835a as rewritten, force must be "necessary." The gap between those words is the legislative intent of AB 392.

For the steward's case (June 16, 2023): Hayes is no longer the governing standard. Section 835a as rewritten by AB 392 governs. But Hayes remains relevant as the baseline from which the new standard departed — any argument that the old "reasonableness" standard should apply is foreclosed by AB 392.
