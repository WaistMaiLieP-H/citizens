# Lawful Arrest — Probable Cause and Warrantless Conditions — Cross-References

| Standard | Relationship | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| PEN_13701_DV_RESPONSE | SUPPLEMENTS | § 13701 requires agencies to adopt written DV policies; § 836(c) is the mandatory arrest provision those policies must implement — § 836 is the legal authority; § 13701 is the policy framework that operationalizes it |
| PEN_835A_USE_OF_FORCE | SUPPLEMENTS | § 836 authorizes arrest; § 835a governs the force permitted during that arrest — arrest authority and force authority operate together: a lawful arrest (§ 836) may still involve unlawful force (§ 835a violation) |
| PEN_832_7_PEACE_OFFICER_RECORDS | CROSS_REFERENCE | Sustained finding of unlawful arrest (§ 832.7(b)(1)(E)) is a mandatory disclosure category — failure to comply with § 836's probable cause requirements and mandatory DV arrest obligations generates disclosable sustained findings under § 832.7 |
| PEN_832_18_BODY_CAMERAS | CROSS_REFERENCE | BWC footage of arrests documents whether § 836 probable cause conditions and mandatory DV arrest obligations (§ 836(c)) were met — footage of arrests is evidentiary data requiring 2-year retention under § 832.18(b)(5)(B)(ii) |
| GOV_1031_POST_TRAINING_STANDARDS | SUPPLEMENTS | POST Basic Training covers arrest law — the legal standards in § 836 are among the core subjects of POST certification; failure to understand § 836 mandatory DV arrest obligations (§ 836(c)) by a certified officer is a training compliance failure |
