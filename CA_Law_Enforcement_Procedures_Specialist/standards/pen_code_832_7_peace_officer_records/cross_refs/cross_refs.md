# Peace Officer Personnel Records — Cross-References

| Standard | Relationship | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| GOV_7923_600_CPRA_LE_EXEMPTION | SUPERSEDES (partial) | § 832.7(b) explicitly overrides § 7923.600's LE exemption for the five mandatory disclosure categories — "notwithstanding subdivision (a), Section 7923.600 of the Government Code, or any other law"; § 832.7(a) incorporates § 7923.600 for records outside the mandatory categories |
| PITCHESS_V_SUPERIOR_COURT | SUPPLEMENTS | Pitchess (Evid. Code §§ 1043/1046) governs court-ordered officer record disclosure in pending civil or criminal cases; § 832.7(b) governs public access without pending case, without good cause showing, without court order — two independent disclosure pathways |
| PEN_835A_USE_OF_FORCE | CROSS_REFERENCE | § 835a defines lawful force standards; § 832.7(b)(1)(A) requires disclosure of sustained findings that force exceeded § 835a standards or constituted unreasonable/excessive force — the two sections work in tandem: 835a defines the line; 832.7 compels disclosure when the line is crossed |
| GOV_12525_2_RACIAL_PROFILING | CROSS_REFERENCE | RIPA data (§ 12525.2) documents racial disparities in stops; § 832.7(b)(1)(D) compels disclosure of sustained findings of prejudice/discrimination — aggregate data (RIPA) + individual accountability records (§ 832.7) together constitute the full accountability framework |
| PEN_1054_CRIMINAL_DISCOVERY | IMPLEMENTS | § 832.7(h) confirms § 1054 criminal discovery is unaffected by § 832.7; sustained dishonesty findings under § 832.7(b)(1)(C) are Giglio material — Brady/Giglio obligation requires prosecution disclosure under § 1054.1(e) regardless of § 832.7(a) confidentiality |
| PEN_832_18_BODY_CAMERAS | SUPPLEMENTS | § 832.18 BWC footage is a core component of the records that must be disclosed under § 832.7(b)(1)(A) for use-of-force incidents — the video is the evidentiary record that the § 832.7 disclosure obligation attaches to |
