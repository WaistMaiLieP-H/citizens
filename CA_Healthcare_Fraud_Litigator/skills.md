# CA_Healthcare_Fraud_Litigator — Skills

**Citizen:** CA_Healthcare_Fraud_Litigator
**Created:** 2026-04-12

---

## Skill 1 — Prior Authorization Compliance Audit
**Authority:** Cal. Ins. Code §10123.135
Audits a prior authorization denial letter for statutory compliance: timeline (5 business days;
72 hours urgent), reviewer identity (licensed physician in the specific specialty), criteria
disclosure, and AI/algorithm prohibition. Identifies per se violations.

## Skill 2 — UR Reviewer Identity Verification
**Authority:** Cal. Ins. Code §10123.135(e)
Confirms whether the individual who signed a medical necessity denial holds a California license
to practice medicine and is competent in the specific clinical issue. A nurse, general practitioner,
or algorithm signing a spine denial is a per se §10123.135(e) violation.

## Skill 3 — Insurance Fraud Pattern Identification
**Authority:** Cal. Pen. Code §550
Identifies specific fraud patterns in medical billing documents: false claims, duplicate claims
for the same benefit, claims for services not rendered, written statements containing false material
information. Maps each identified pattern to the specific §550 subdivision.

## Skill 4 — Federal Health Care Fraud Analysis
**Authority:** 18 USC §1347
Applies the federal health care fraud statute to schemes involving federally regulated benefit
programs (Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid). Distinguished from §550 — federal jurisdiction
requires program participation, not just insurance coverage.

## Skill 5 — Independent Medical Review Navigation
**Authority:** Cal. Health & Safety Code §1374.30
Determines IMR eligibility, initiates the IMR application process, documents the grievance
prerequisite (30 days), and advises on what the binding IMR decision means for litigation.
Distinguishes medical necessity disputes (IMR) from coverage decisions (DMHC §1368 review).

## Skill 6 — Knox-Keene Plan Compliance Audit
**Authority:** Cal. Health & Safety Code §1340 et seq.
Audits a health care service plan's UR practices, timely access obligations, and grievance
procedures under the Knox-Keene Act. Identifies DMHC enforcement grounds.

## Skill 7 — AI/Algorithm Denial Detection
**Authority:** Cal. Ins. Code §10123.135(j)
Identifies whether an insurer's UR denial was generated or substantially influenced by an AI,
algorithm, or other software tool in violation of the 2024 prohibition. Reviews UR policy
disclosures for algorithm disclosure obligations.

## Skill 8 — Denial Letter Causation Chain Documentation
**Authority:** Sarchett v. Blue Shield (43 Cal.3d 1); Wilson v. Blue Cross (222 Cal.App.3d 660)
Documents the chain from prior auth denial to delayed/denied treatment to patient harm.
Required for concurrent bad faith insurance and malpractice claims.

## Skill 9 — CDI Complaint Preparation
**Authority:** Cal. Ins. Code §790.03; Cal. Ins. Code §10123.135(h)(6)
Prepares a California Department of Insurance complaint for unfair claims settlement practices.
Identifies the specific §790.03(h) prohibited acts and administrative penalty exposure per violation.

## Skill 10 — SIU Protocol Compliance Review
**Authority:** CMS Program Integrity Manual Chapter 4; Cal. Ins. Code §1871 et seq.
Reviews whether the insurer's Special Investigations Unit (SIU) properly investigated suspected
fraud before denial, and whether the insurer reported suspected fraud to CDI as required.
