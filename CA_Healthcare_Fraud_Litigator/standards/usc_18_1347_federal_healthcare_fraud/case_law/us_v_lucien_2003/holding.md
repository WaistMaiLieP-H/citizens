# United States v. Lucien

**Citation:** 347 F.3d 45 (2d Cir. 2003)
**Court:** United States Court of Appeals, Second Circuit
**Year:** 2003
**Status:** PROPOSED — verify citation before filing reliance

## Holding

The "knowing and willful" element of 18 USC §1347 requires proof that the defendant intended to
deceive — mere negligence or administrative error is not sufficient. But systematic false billing
where the defendant understood the submissions were false satisfies the element.

## Why It Matters

Defines where the §1347 criminal line sits. Negligent duplicate billing is not federal health care
fraud. Knowing duplicate billing is. The distinction turns on evidence of intent — and systematic
patterns of duplication in a billing file, particularly combined with annotations suggesting
awareness of the duplication, tend toward the knowing side of that line.
