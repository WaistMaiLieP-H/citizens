|---|---|---|
| INS_10123_135_PRIOR_AUTH | REGULATORY_SIBLING | §10123.135 is the CDI-regulated parallel for disability insurers; §1367(g) is the Knox-Keene equivalent for DMHC-regulated health plans. | HSC_1374_30_INDEPENDENT_MEDICAL_REVIEW | ENFORCEMENT_MECHANISM | §1374.30 IMR operates within the Knox-Keene framework. DMHC administers both Knox-Keene licensing and the IMR system.
