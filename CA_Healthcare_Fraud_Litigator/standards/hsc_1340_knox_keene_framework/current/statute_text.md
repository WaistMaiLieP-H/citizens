## § 1340 — Citation

The Knox-Keene Health Care Service Plan Act of 1975.

## § 1367 — Health Plan Duties (key operative provisions)

**(a)** A health care service plan, including a specialized health care service plan, shall provide to subscribers and enrollees all health care services that the plan has contracted to provide or has arranged to provide.

**(b)** A health care service plan shall provide services in a timely fashion appropriate to the enrollee's condition.

**(g)** Medical decisions affecting covered benefits shall be rendered by qualified medical providers acting in the interest of the enrollee, unhindered by fiscal and administrative management of the plan.

**§ 1367(g) — The Core Rule:**  
"A health care service plan shall not... interfere with the practice of medicine by any person holding a valid, unrevoked certificate to practice medicine issued pursuant to Division 2 (commencing with Section 500) of the Business and Professions Code or by any other licensed health care practitioner."

This provision makes it a Knox-Keene violation for the plan to:
- Override a physician's medical necessity determination through administrative means
- Apply UR criteria that systematically override clinical judgment
- Use algorithmic or non-physician UR to deny care a licensed physician found necessary

## § 1367.01 — Timely Access

Health plans must ensure access to services within specified timeframes:
- Preventive care: within 35 business days
- Non-urgent specialist: within 15 business days
- Urgent care (non-emergency): within 48 hours
- Emergency care: immediately

Note: A prior authorization denial that delays urgent care beyond 72 hours (mirroring §10123.135(h)(2)) may independently violate §1367.01.
