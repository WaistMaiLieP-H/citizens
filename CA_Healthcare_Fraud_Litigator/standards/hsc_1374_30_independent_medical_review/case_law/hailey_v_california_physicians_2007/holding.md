# Hailey v. California Physicians' Service

**Citation:** 158 Cal.App.4th 452 (2007)
**Court:** California Court of Appeal, First District
**Year:** 2007
**Status:** PROPOSED — verify before filing reliance

## Holding

DMHC enforcement authority under the Knox-Keene Act reaches systematic patterns of denial and
discriminatory payment practices across categories of covered services, not just individual claim
disputes. IMR and the DMHC enforcement framework are tools for both individual and systemic relief.

## Why It Matters

Expands the use of IMR from individual claim remedy to systemic enforcement lever. If a pattern
of §10123.135(e) violations (non-physician UR denials for spine cases) can be documented, Hailey
supports a DMHC systemic enforcement complaint, not just individual IMR applications.
