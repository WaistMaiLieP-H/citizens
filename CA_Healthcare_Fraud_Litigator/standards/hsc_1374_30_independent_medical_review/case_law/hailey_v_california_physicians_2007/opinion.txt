Hailey v. California Physicians' Service, 158 Cal.App.4th 452 (2007)
California Court of Appeal, First District, 2007

HOLDING: A Knox-Keene health care service plan cannot systematically provide mental health benefits at a lower reimbursement rate than comparable physical health benefits; DMHC enforcement authority reaches systematic patterns of denial and discriminatory payment, not just individual claim disputes.

KEY LANGUAGE: "The [DMHC] has the authority to enforce parity in coverage and payment and to require independent medical review of denials that reflect systematic discrimination between categories of covered services."

RELEVANCE: Establishes that DMHC enforcement and the IMR framework reach systemic patterns of UR denial across a category of services — not just individual claim determinations. Supports systemic DMHC enforcement complaint where §10123.135(e) violations are documented across multiple spine/orthopedic claims, not just the single steward claim.
