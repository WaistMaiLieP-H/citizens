## Title Section (retrieved live 2026-04-12)

**§ 1340.** This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Knox-Keene Health Care Service Plan Act of 1975.

## Key Framework Sections (verify full text at leginfo before filing reliance)

**§ 1367** — Health plan duties; plans must provide or arrange for covered services ensuring
availability, accessibility, and continuity of care. Defines basic health care services.

**§ 1367.01** — Timely access to care; plans must maintain provider networks meeting access time
and distance standards established by DMHC regulation.

**§ 1368** — Grievance procedures; mandatory written grievance system; 30-day resolution standard;
enrollees may file directly with DMHC if plan fails to resolve.

**§ 1374.30** — Independent Medical Review System (full text in dedicated standard HSC_1374_30_IMR).
