# People v. Kelly

**Citation:** 40 Cal.4th 106 (2007) — VERIFY BEFORE FILING
**Court:** California Supreme Court
**Year:** 2007
**Status:** PROPOSED — citation must be verified against Westlaw/Lexis before filing reliance

## Holding

In a health care fraud prosecution under §550, actual knowledge of the false or fraudulent nature
of claims can be established through pattern evidence — the systematic and repeated nature of
duplicate billing in an operation the defendant controls is itself sufficient evidence of
knowing submission.

## Why It Matters

Defeats the most common defense to duplicate billing fraud: "it was a clerical error."
For the duplicate MRI pages in the spine surgery fraud file, this case establishes that
if billing records show multiple charges for the same study, the pattern of duplication — not
any single submission — carries the knowledge element.
