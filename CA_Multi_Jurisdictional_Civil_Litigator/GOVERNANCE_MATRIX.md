# Multi-Jurisdictional Civil Litigator — Full Governance Scope Matrix (California)

Reference companion to `SKILL.md`. This is the complete instrument-by-instrument map of
the **scope of a Multi-Jurisdictional Civil Litigator** and the governances that define and
authorize every part of that scope. Cited authorities verified against primary sources
(leginfo, courts.ca.gov, calbar.ca.gov, U.S. Code). Verify currency before relying.

> Definition. A *Multi-Jurisdictional Civil Litigator* is a civil-litigation attorney whose
> practice crosses jurisdictional boundaries: California state courts, the four California
> U.S. District Courts and other federal courts, sister-state courts, and county-to-county
> venues within California. Their lawful scope = **(authority to appear) ∩ (conduct duties
> that bind them) ∩ (the forum's own power over the case) ∩ (the procedure that governs the
> filing)**. Every layer below is one face of that intersection.

---

## LAYER A — AUTHORITY TO PRACTICE ACROSS JURISDICTIONS (the gateway)

This is the defining layer for "multi-jurisdictional": the rules that say *whether a lawyer
licensed somewhere may act here*.

### A1 — The baseline bar on practice
| Instrument | What it governs | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6125** | Active membership required | No person may practice law in California unless an active member of the State Bar (or otherwise authorized by statute/court rule). |
| **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6126** | Unauthorized practice of law (UPL) | UPL / holding out is a misdemeanor (up to 364 days county jail + $1,000). A **wobbler** (misd/felony) where the person was disbarred, suspended, resigned with charges pending, or on involuntary inactive status. |
| **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6127–6133** | Related UPL / contempt | Misuse of "attorney," contempt for UPL, penalties. |
| **Cal. R. Prof. Conduct, rule 5.5** | UPL & multijurisdictional practice | The master conduct rule. A lawyer shall not practice where not admitted, nor assist UPL; its Comment lists every authorized cross-in category and cites the CRC 9.4x rules. Mirrors but is not identical to ABA Model Rule 5.5. |

### A2 — The multijurisdictional-practice (MJP) categories — California Rules of Court, Title 9, Ch. 4
| Rule | Category | Core conditions / limits |
|---|---|---|
| **CRC 9.40** | **Counsel pro hac vice (PHV)** | Active member in good standing of a bar of another U.S. jurisdiction; **not** a California resident, **not** regularly employed/engaged in substantial business or practice in CA; must **associate active California counsel of record**; verified written application served on parties + the State Bar with the **fee** ($50 to State Bar). Court order required; per-case. |
| **CRC 9.41** | Appearances by military counsel | Limited authorization for military legal-assistance attorneys. |
| **CRC 9.42** | Certified law students | Practical-training program; supervision required. |
| **CRC 9.43** | Out-of-state attorney arbitration counsel program | Registration to represent a party in a California **arbitration** (Code Civ. Proc. § 1282.4). |
| **CRC 9.44** | Registered foreign legal consultant | Advise on the law of the foreign jurisdiction of admission only. |
| **CRC 9.45** | Registered legal-services attorneys | Practice only while employed (paid or volunteer) at a qualifying legal-services provider, ≤ 3 years, under supervision. |
| **CRC 9.46** | Registered in-house counsel | Practice **only** for the single qualifying employer; **may not** make California court appearances or do anything requiring PHV. |
| **CRC 9.47** | **Temporary practice — litigation** | Out-of-state attorney may practice temporarily in CA *as part of litigation* where the attorney reasonably expects to seek PHV or the practice arises out of/is reasonably related to the attorney's home-jurisdiction practice; subject to restrictions 9.47(d) and conditions/registration 9.47(e). |
| **CRC 9.48** | **Temporary practice — non-litigation** | Out-of-state attorney providing transactional/advisory services in CA tied to a matter centered in the attorney's home jurisdiction, federal/other-jurisdiction law issues, or as an employee of the client. |
| (CRC 9.49) | Discipline of non-members | Authorizes State Bar discipline reach over those practicing under 9.40–9.48. |

### A3 — Federal-court admission (independent of state membership)
- Admission to a **U.S. District Court** is governed by **that district's local rules**, not state membership:
  - **N.D. Cal. Civ. L.R. 11-1** (bar membership of the court), **11-3** (pro hac vice).
  - **E.D. Cal. L.R. 180–183** (admission, PHV, student practice, appearances).
  - **C.D. Cal. L.R. 83-2**, **S.D. Cal. CivLR 83.3** (parallel schemes).
- **A California-court PHV order does NOT confer federal-court admission, and vice-versa.** Each forum is a separate gate.
- **Appellate:** admission to the Ninth Circuit / other circuits and the U.S. Supreme Court are separate bars again (FRAP 46; Sup. Ct. R. 5).

> **Audit trigger:** out-of-state attorney signs/appears on a California filing with no PHV order, no associated California counsel, or no qualifying 9.4x category → potential UPL and a void/voidable filing. In federal court, check the district's L.R. PHV order instead.

## LAYER B — CONDUCT DUTIES THAT BIND THE LITIGATOR ACROSS FORUMS

Duties travel with the lawyer; **rule 8.5** chooses which jurisdiction's rules apply.

| Rule / Statute | Duty |
|---|---|
| **Cal. R. Prof. Conduct 8.5** | Disciplinary authority & **choice-of-law for conduct**: a lawyer admitted or *practicing* in CA is subject to CA discipline; for conduct in connection with a matter pending before a tribunal, the rules of the jurisdiction where the tribunal sits apply. The hinge rule for a multi-forum litigator. |
| **Rule 1.1** | Competence (no intentional/reckless/grossly-negligent/repeated incompetence; may cure by association, learning, or referral). |
| **Rule 1.3** | Diligence — no neglect or undue delay. |
| **Rule 1.2** | Scope of representation & allocation of authority. |
| **Rule 1.4** | Communication with client. |
| **Rule 1.6** + **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068(e)** | Confidentiality / duty to maintain client confidences. |
| **Rule 1.7–1.9** | Conflicts of interest (current, former, prospective clients) — acute across jurisdictions/firms. |
| **Rule 3.1** | Only meritorious claims and contentions. |
| **Rule 3.3** | Candor toward the tribunal (no false statements / must correct). |
| **Rule 3.4** | Fairness to opposing party and counsel. |
| **Rule 3.5 / 4.1 / 4.2 / 4.3** | Impartiality of tribunal; truthfulness to others; no contact with represented persons; dealing with unrepresented persons. |
| **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6068** | Statutory duties of an attorney (support constitution/laws; maintain respect for courts; never seek to mislead a judge; not encourage suits from corrupt motive; counsel only legal/just actions). |
| **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6103–6106** | Grounds for discipline; moral-turpitude / dishonesty as cause for disbarment. |

## LAYER C — SUBJECT-MATTER JURISDICTION (the forum's power over the case)

| Forum | Authority | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| California superior court | **Cal. Const., Art. VI, § 10** | General original jurisdiction over civil causes. |
| Jurisdictional classification | **CCP §§ 85–89** | **Limited** civil (≤ $35,000 — note threshold raised from $25k) vs. **unlimited** (> $35,000); reclassification CCP § 403.040. |
| Small claims | CCP §§ 116.110 et seq. | $12,500 individuals / $6,250 entities; no attorney representation at trial. |
| Federal — federal question | **28 U.S.C. § 1331** | Civil actions arising under the Constitution, laws, or treaties of the U.S. |
| Federal — diversity | **28 U.S.C. § 1332** | **Complete** diversity of citizenship + amount in controversy **> $75,000**. |
| Federal — supplemental | **28 U.S.C. § 1367** | Claims forming part of the same case/controversy. |
| Erie doctrine | *Erie R.R. v. Tompkins* (1938) | Federal court in diversity applies state **substantive** law, federal **procedural** law; *Hanna v. Plumer*; outcome-determinative test. |

> **Audit trigger:** diversity pleaded without complete diversity or the >$75k amount; a limited-jurisdiction action seeking unlimited relief; removal premised on a defect SMJ.

## LAYER D — PERSONAL JURISDICTION, VENUE & FORUM SELECTION

| Topic | Authority | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Personal jurisdiction (state long-arm) | **CCP § 410.10** | California exercises jurisdiction to the full extent permitted by the U.S. and California Constitutions. |
| PJ constitutional standard | *International Shoe*, *World-Wide Volkswagen*, *Daimler*, *Bristol-Myers Squibb* | General (at-home) vs. specific (purposeful availment + relatedness + fairness) jurisdiction. |
| State venue | **CCP §§ 392–403** | Proper county; § 395 (general — defendant's residence/where obligation incurred); § 395.5 (corporations). |
| Change of venue | **CCP § 397** | Grounds (impartiality, convenience of witnesses, no qualified judge). |
| Forum non conveniens | **CCP § 410.30** | Stay/dismiss when a suitable alternative forum elsewhere is substantially more appropriate. |
| Federal venue | **28 U.S.C. § 1391** | Residence of defendants / where substantial events occurred / fallback. |
| Federal transfer | **28 U.S.C. §§ 1404, 1406, 1407** | Convenience transfer; cure of wrong venue; MDL. |
| **Removal** | **28 U.S.C. §§ 1441, 1446** | Defendant removes to U.S. District Court; **30-day** clock from service of removable pleading; **all-defendants (unanimity)** rule; **1-year cap** on diversity removals (§ 1446(c)); forum-defendant rule (§ 1441(b)(2)). |
| **Remand** | **28 U.S.C. § 1447** | § 1447(c) — remand for lack of SMJ (any time) or procedural defect (30-day motion); § 1447(d) — limited appealability. |
| Coordination | **CCP § 404**; **CRC 3.500 et seq.** | JCCP — coordination of complex California actions in multiple counties. |
| Sister-state / foreign service & forum clauses | CCP § 410.40; Civ. Code § 1646.5 | Consent jurisdiction; enforceability of forum-selection clauses. |

## LAYER E — CHOICE OF LAW & INTER-JURISDICTIONAL JUDGMENT RECOGNITION

| Topic | Authority | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| California choice-of-law method | **Governmental-interest analysis** (*Reich v. Purcell*; *Kearney v. Salomon Smith Barney*; *McCann v. Foster Wheeler*) | Three steps: are the laws different → does each state have an interest → comparative impairment. (Not the Restatement "most significant relationship" test that some states use.) |
| Contractual choice of law | **Civ. Code § 1646.5**; *Nedlloyd Lines v. Superior Court* | Parties may select governing law; limits where it offends a fundamental CA policy. |
| Full Faith and Credit | **U.S. Const., Art. IV, § 1**; 28 U.S.C. § 1738 | Sister-state judgments entitled to recognition. |
| **Sister-State Money Judgments Act** | **CCP §§ 1710.10–1710.65** | Streamlined entry of another state's money judgment in California by application + clerk-entered judgment; debtor's motion to vacate. |
| Foreign-country judgments | **CCP §§ 1713–1724** (Uniform Foreign-Country Money Judgments Recognition Act) | Recognition standards / non-recognition grounds. |
| Out-of-state / international **service** | **CCP § 413.10**; **Hague Service Convention** | Method of service depends on where the defendant is served. |
| Out-of-state **discovery** | Interstate Depositions & Discovery Act, **CCP §§ 2029.100 et seq.** | Domesticating subpoenas across state lines. |

## LAYER F — PROCEDURAL GOVERNANCE OF THE FILING ITSELF

| Forum | Governance | Key details |
|---|---|---|
| California — procedure | **Code of Civil Procedure** | Pleadings (CCP §§ 425–475), demurrer (§ 430.10), motions & notice (**§ 1005** — 16 ct-day notice), service of papers (**§§ 1010–1014, 1013** — mailing extensions), summary judgment (§ 437c), sanctions (**§ 128.7** safe harbor; § 128.5). |
| California — format | **CRC Title 2** (2.100–2.119) | Paper, font, margins, captioning, page format, e-filing (CRC 2.250–2.261). |
| California — civil rules | **CRC Title 3** | Civil case management (3.720–3.730), law-and-motion (3.1100 et seq.), tentative rulings (3.1308). |
| California — local | County **Local Rules** | Department assignments, direct-calendar, tentative-ruling mechanics. Always verify the specific county's current local rules. |
| Federal — procedure | **Federal Rules of Civil Procedure** | **Rule 11** (signature + certification — the federal § 128.7 analog), Rules 7–16 (pleadings/motions/scheduling), Rule 26 (discovery), Rule 56 (summary judgment). |
| Federal — local | District **Local Rules** + judge **standing orders** | N.D./E.D./C.D./S.D. Cal. each differ; chambers/standing orders control format and motion practice. |
| Appellate | **CRC Title 8** (state) / **FRAP** + circuit rules (federal) | Notices of appeal, briefing, record. |

> **Audit trigger:** a filing using the wrong forum's caption/format/rules (federal-style motion filed in superior court or vice-versa); a missing **Rule 11 / CCP § 128.7** certification; service that doesn't satisfy the controlling forum's rule; a motion noticed inside the required notice period.

## LAYER G — SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION & CLIENT-FACING AUTHORITY

| Topic | Authority | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Objectives vs. means | **Rule 1.2** | Client sets objectives; lawyer controls means after consultation. |
| Limited-scope ("unbundled") | **CRC 3.35–3.37** (civil), **CRC 5.425** (family), Forms **MC-950 / MC-955** | Notice of limited-scope appearance & completion; ghost-writing disclosure. |
| Substitution | **CCP § 284**; Form **MC-050** | By consent or court order. |
| Withdrawal | **Rule 1.16**; **CRC 3.1362**; Form **MC-051/052/053** | Mandatory vs. permissive withdrawal; motion to be relieved as counsel. |
| Fee agreements | **Bus. & Prof. Code § 6147** (contingency), **§ 6148** (≥ $1,000 non-contingency hourly) | Required writing & contents; voidable at client's option if non-compliant. |
| Fees / trust | **Rule 1.5** (no unconscionable fee), **Rule 1.15** (client trust account / **IOLTA**, Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 6211–6213) | Safekeeping of client funds across matters/jurisdictions. |
| Attorney of record | CCP § 285; CRC 2.111 | Proper designation of counsel for *this* forum & case. |
