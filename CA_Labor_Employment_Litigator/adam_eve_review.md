## Certified Legal Findings

### FINDING LABOR-001: § 132a One-Year SOL Is Jurisdictional — File or Lose

Lab. Code §132a SOL is ONE YEAR from the discriminatory act or termination date. The WCAB has exclusive jurisdiction. A §132a petition filed more than one year after the discriminatory act is time-barred at the WCAB. There is no discovery rule expressly codified for §132a. SOL tolling arguments (concealment) must be raised before the WCAB and are not guaranteed. **File immediately if within the 1-year window.**

**Reliance note:** §132a text verified live 2026-04-13. Certified for immediate action.

### FINDING LABOR-002: FEHA Civil Action Requires CRD Complaint First — No Exception

Gov. Code §12965 makes the CRD administrative complaint a mandatory prerequisite to any FEHA civil action in superior court. Filing a FEHA civil complaint without a prior CRD complaint and right-to-sue notice is a fatal defect. The 3-year CRD complaint SOL (Gov. Code §12960) runs from the last discriminatory act — if it has expired, the FEHA administrative route is closed.

**Reliance note:** §12965 text verified live 2026-04-13. Certified for pleading structure.

### FINDING LABOR-003: § 1102.5 Protects Internal Disclosures — No External Report Required

Lab. Code §1102.5(b) protects disclosures to supervisors, managers, and internal investigators — not only external government agencies. An employee who reported a violation internally before being terminated is protected under §1102.5 without ever having contacted a government agency.

**Reliance note:** §1102.5 text verified live 2026-04-13. Certified for pleading.

### FINDING LABOR-004: Prevailing FEHA Defendant Cannot Routinely Recover Attorney's Fees

Gov. Code §12965(c)(6) prohibits fee awards to a prevailing defendant unless the court finds the action was "frivolous, unreasonable, or groundless when brought." This means a plaintiff bringing a meritorious FEHA claim faces no fee risk on a loss — only on a frivolous claim. The asymmetric standard encourages FEHA enforcement.

**Reliance note:** §12965 text verified live 2026-04-13. Certified for client counseling.

### FINDING LABOR-005: Concurrent EEOC/CRD Filing Tolls the State 1-Year Civil SOL

Under §12965(e) / Downs v. DWP (1997) 58 Cal.App.4th 1093 (codified in the statute itself): concurrent filing with EEOC and CRD, followed by CRD deferral to EEOC, tolls the state 1-year civil action SOL. The claimant has the longer of the federal or state right-to-sue window. File with both agencies simultaneously in any FEHA claim with a federal parallel.

**Reliance note:** §12965 text verified live 2026-04-13. Downs citation verified by statute text. Certified for pleading strategy.
