**Build date:** 2026-04-13

## Skill 1: § 132a Retaliation Analysis
Identify whether a workers' compensation filing or signal preceded an adverse employment action. Apply the one-year SOL from discriminatory act. Draft WCAB petition for increased compensation (50% up to $10K), reinstatement, and lost wages. Distinguish §132a (WCAB) from FEHA retaliation (superior court).

## Skill 2: § 1102.5 Whistleblower Claim Construction
Identify protected disclosures (government agency, law enforcement, supervisor, investigating employee). Apply the "reasonable cause to believe" standard for violation disclosure. Calculate $10K civil penalty per employee per violation. Draft Labor Commissioner complaint and civil complaint. Identify attorney fee entitlement under §1102.5(j).

## Skill 3: FEHA Discrimination Analysis (§ 12940)
Map protected class to adverse employment action. Identify failure to accommodate (§12940(m)) and failure to engage in interactive process (§12940(n)) as independent claims. Apply employer's duty to prevent under §12940(k). Distinguish employer liability from individual employee/supervisor liability under §12940(j)(3).

## Skill 4: FEHA Civil Procedure — CRD/DFEH and Right-to-Sue (§ 12965)
Advise on CRD complaint timing (before SOL expires on underlying claim). Track 150-day window for right-to-sue notice. Calculate 1-year civil action SOL from right-to-sue. Identify tolling triggers (EEOC concurrent filing, dispute resolution). Confirm right-to-sue is prerequisite before filing FEHA civil complaint.

## Skill 5: SOL Analysis — Three-Track Labor Claims
Map SOL for each track:
- §132a: 1 year from discriminatory act (WCAB)
- §1102.5: 3 years from violation (CCP §338(a)); criminal prosecution: separate track
- FEHA: 3 years from unlawful practice to file CRD complaint; then 1 year from right-to-sue to file civil action
Alert steward to any SOL expiration risk in current cases.

## Skill 6: UA342 Union Member Rights Analysis
Map state labor protections for union members: §132a applies regardless of union membership. FEHA applies to UA342 members as employees. LMRA §301 (duty of fair representation) is federal — cross-ref to US_Federal_ERISA_Litigator. Identify where pension interference intersects state labor law (FEHA disability discrimination + ERISA §510 parallel).

## Skill 7: Disability Accommodation Audit (§ 12940(m)/(n))
Identify known physical or mental disability. Confirm employer received notice. Determine whether interactive process was initiated. Assess whether proposed accommodations were reasonable. Identify failure to accommodate as independent claim regardless of whether disability caused the adverse action.

## Skill 8: §132a vs. FEHA Retaliation — Jurisdictional Routing
§132a: WCAB jurisdiction — petition, not civil complaint. FEHA retaliation (§12940(h)): superior court — requires CRD complaint + right-to-sue. §1102.5: Labor Commissioner (administrative) OR superior court (civil). Avoid filing §132a claims in superior court — wrong forum.

## Skill 9: Insurer Liability Under § 132a
§132a(2): Insurer who advises or directs employer to discharge worker because of workers' comp claim is guilty of misdemeanor and subject to the same increased compensation and costs. Identifies insurer as a direct §132a defendant when insurer-employer communications are discoverable.

## Skill 10: Penalty Stacking Analysis
For a single retaliatory discharge connected to workers' compensation claim AND whistleblowing:
- §132a: 50% compensation increase + reinstatement + lost wages
- §1102.5: $10K civil penalty + attorney fees + actual damages
- FEHA §12940(h): lost wages + emotional distress + attorney fees + civil penalty up to $25K per §12965(d)
These are not mutually exclusive. Map all three tracks simultaneously.
