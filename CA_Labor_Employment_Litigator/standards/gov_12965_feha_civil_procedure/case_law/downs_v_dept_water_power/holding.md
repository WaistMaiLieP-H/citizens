# Downs v. Department of Water and Power of City of Los Angeles (1997)
## § 12965 Case Law — Holding Summary

**Citation:** Downs v. Department of Water and Power of City of Los Angeles (1997) 58 Cal.App.4th 1093
**Court:** California Court of Appeal
**Status:** VERIFIED — expressly referenced in the text of §12965(e)(3) as codified holding
**Statute version cited:** Gov. Code § 12965 (FEHA civil procedure)

---

## Holding

The California Legislature expressly codified the holding of this case in §12965(e)(3): "This subdivision is intended to codify the holding in Downs v. Department of Water and Power of City of Los Angeles (1997) 58 Cal.App.4th 1093."

**The Downs rule:** When a FEHA complaint is filed concurrently with the EEOC and the CRD, and the CRD defers investigation to the EEOC, the one-year civil action SOL is tolled during the federal EEOC investigation period. The claimant has whichever right-to-sue period is longer — federal or state.

---

## Why This Matters

Without the Downs/§12965(e) tolling rule, concurrent filers would face a dilemma: pursuing the federal EEOC route might allow the state 1-year civil action SOL to run while the federal investigation continues. The tolling rule eliminates this trap by running the state SOL from the end of the federal process, not from the initial right-to-sue notice.

**Practice point:** Always file FEHA complaints concurrently with EEOC when a federal parallel exists. The tolling protection is in the statute, codified from Downs.
