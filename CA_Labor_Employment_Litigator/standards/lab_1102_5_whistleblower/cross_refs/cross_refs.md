# LAB § 1102.5 — Cross-References

| Standard / Citation | Relationship |
|---|---|
| lab_132a_workers_comp_retaliation (this Citizen) | Parallel retaliation claim — WC filing may also constitute protected disclosure; both §132a and §1102.5 can be pleaded |
| gov_12940_feha_prohibited_practices (this Citizen) | FEHA retaliation §12940(h) runs parallel — if the same adverse action violated §1102.5 AND constituted FEHA retaliation, both claims apply |
| Cal. Lab. Code §1102 | Companion section — prohibits employer from coercing or influencing employees in political activities; §1102.5 is the disclosure/reporting extension |
| Cal. Lab. Code §98.6 | Protected activity complaint filed with Labor Commissioner — retaliation prohibition companion to §1102.5 for Labor Commissioner complaints specifically |
| Cal. Gov. Code §8547 et seq. | California Whistleblower Protection Act — applies to government employees; §1102.5 applies to private AND public sector employees |
| 29 USC §660(c) | Federal OSHA anti-retaliation provision — runs parallel to §1102.5 for OSHA violations; federal complaint to DOL is separate track |
