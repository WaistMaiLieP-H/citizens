## Origin: Stats. 1980, Ch. 992 — FEHA Enactment

§12965 was enacted with the FEHA in 1980, providing the procedural framework for DFEH (now CRD) enforcement. The original version required a 1-year complaint filing SOL.

**Key Amendment — Stats. 2019, Ch. 709:** Extended complaint filing SOL from 1 year to 3 years. The Legislature found that 1 year was insufficient for many victims to access legal help, understand their rights, and prepare a complaint. The 2019 extension brought California closer to the EEOC's 300-day federal charge filing deadline while providing additional protection.

**Key Amendment — Stats. 2021, Ch. 357:** DFEH renamed to Civil Rights Department (CRD). Same procedures; new name. All prior DFEH procedures and administrative frameworks continue.

## Historical Gap This Filled

Before FEHA and §12965, California employment discrimination victims had two options: (1) federal EEOC/Title VII proceedings (federal agency, federal court), or (2) common law wrongful discharge tort (limited). §12965 created a state agency pathway with state court civil action option — more accessible for California workers who prefer state forum and state law protections.
