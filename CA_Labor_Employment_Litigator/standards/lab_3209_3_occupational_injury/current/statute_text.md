# Occupational Injury Definition — Statutory Text

**Source:** Cal. Lab. Code § 3208
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-12
**URL:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3208.&lawCode=LAB
**Verified:** true (VernenLegal MCP california_get_section)

## Text

§ 3208 — "Injury" includes any injury or disease arising out of the employment, including injuries to artificial members, dentures, hearing aids, eyeglasses and medical braces of all types; provided, however, that eyeglasses and hearing aids will not be replaced, repaired, or otherwise compensated for, unless injury to them is incident to an injury causing disability.

The source prep identified this standard as "Lab. Code §3209.3 — Workers comp injury definition." **This is incorrect.** Lab. The correct occupational injury definition is Lab. Code §3208 ("Injury" = any injury or disease arising out of the employment). The source prep error has been corrected here. The standard ID `lab_3209_3_occupational_injury` is preserved for tether.json consistency; content is based on §3208.

## Related Provisions (not separately fetched but documented):

**Lab. Code § 3208.1 — Cumulative Injury:**
"An injury may be either: (a) 'Specific injury' means an injury that occurred at a definite time and place; (b) 'Cumulative injury' means repetitive mentally or physically traumatic activities extending over a period of time, the combined effect of which causes any disability or need for medical treatment."

**Lab. Code § 3600 — Employer Liability Conditions:**
"Liability for the compensation provided by this division, in lieu of any other liability whatsoever to any person, shall, without regard to negligence, exist against an employer for any injury sustained by his or her employees arising out of and in the course of the employment..."

**The AOE/COE standard:** Injury must arise "out of" the employment (AOE) AND occur "in the course of" the employment (COE). Both elements required.
