# Reasoning — CAL_PROB_1826_INVESTIGATOR_PRE_APPOINTMENT

## Why §1826 Is Load-Bearing

§ 1826 is the gatekeeper statute. Conservatorship strips an adult of legal capacity. The California Legislature recognized that this deprivation requires an independent assessment — not just the petitioner's account — before the court can act. The court investigator is the Legislature's designated independent fact-finder, and the §1826 duties are the Legislature's minimum requirements for that investigation.

Every §1826 duty exists to prevent a specific abuse:
- Personal interview prevents conservatorship of a person who was never consulted
- Rights notification prevents a conservatee who never knew they could oppose from losing by default
- Family interviews prevent a petitioner from hiding opposition from family members
- Medical report review prevents appointment based on lay speculation about capacity
- Written report with 5-day notice prevents last-minute decisions without time for review

## Investigative Use for the Steward's Case

If a conservatorship was established over the steward:

**The §1826 investigation record is the first target.** The report is confidential but accessible to the conservatee or former conservatee. The following documents should exist in the court file if §1826 was complied with:
1. Written investigator's report (at least 5 days pre-hearing)
2. Separate confidential attachment for medical records and CLETS information
3. Record of personal interview with proposed conservatee
4. Record of rights notification to proposed conservatee

If these documents do not exist in the court file, §1826 was not complied with — and the appointment is procedurally void.

## The CLETS Provision

§ 1826(c)(2) places CLETS information in a separate confidential attachment accessible only to the proposed conservatee and their attorney. If CLETS information was accessed in connection with a conservatorship proceeding involving the steward, the steward has a right of access to that information through the confidential attachment.

## Comparison to §1851 (Annual Review)

§1826 is the pre-appointment gate; §1851 is the ongoing monitoring obligation. Once a conservatorship is established, §1851 requires periodic review using similar investigator obligations. The absence of §1851 compliance records across years of a conservatorship's existence is evidence of systematic non-compliance — not just a single procedural failure.
