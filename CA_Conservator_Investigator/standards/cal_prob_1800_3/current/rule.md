# Rule — CAL_PROB_1800_3_MINOR_CONSERVATEE

**Citation:** Cal. Prob. Code § 1800.3  
**Standard:** Least Restrictive Alternative Requirement — Minor Conservatee Limitation

## The Rule

**§ 1800.3(a) — Scope of conservatorship:**  
The court may appoint a conservator of the person or estate, or both, of an adult. The court may also appoint a conservator of the person of a minor who is married or whose marriage has been dissolved.

**Critical limitation:** A conservatorship of an UNMARRIED MINOR under the Probate Code general conservatorship framework is NOT authorized. An unmarried minor who has not been married cannot have a conservator appointed under Prob. Code §1801 through §1800.3.

**§ 1800.3(b) — Mandatory least restrictive alternative finding:**  
The court may not grant a petition for appointment of a conservator unless the court makes an affirmative finding that the appointment is the least restrictive alternative consistent with meeting the proposed conservatee's needs. This finding is a prerequisite to appointment — not a factor to consider.

**§ 1800.3(c) — What the court must consider:**  
In determining whether a conservatorship is the least restrictive alternative, the court shall consider all of the following:
- Supported decision-making agreements
- Powers of attorney (financial and/or health care)
- Health care surrogates
- Advance health care directives
- Any other alternatives

**§ 1800.3(d) — Developmental disability pathway:**  
If the proposed conservatee has a developmental disability and the conservator is not seeking authority under §2356.5 (authority beyond what the conservatee can do), the court shall deem the proceeding to be seeking a limited conservatorship under §1801(d).

## The Minor Conservatee Rule

**Under Prob. Code §1800.3(a):** Conservatorship of an unmarried minor is NOT available. The mechanism for an unmarried minor would be:
- Guardianship (Prob. Code Division 4, Part 2 — different statutory scheme)
- LPS conservatorship (WIC §5350+ — for psychiatric holds/gravely disabled)
- Juvenile court jurisdiction (WIC §300 — child welfare)

A purported Prob. Code conservatorship of an unmarried minor established under §1801 would be outside the court's jurisdiction under §1800.3(a).
