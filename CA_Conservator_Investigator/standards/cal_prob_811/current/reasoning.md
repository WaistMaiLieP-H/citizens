# Reasoning — CAL_PROB_811_CAPACITY_DETERMINATION

## Why Diagnosis Alone Is Insufficient

§ 811 rejects the medical model of disability as the basis for conservatorship. A diagnosis tells us what a person has; §811 requires evidence of what they cannot do as a result. This distinction matters enormously: a person with Down syndrome may be fully capable of making their own decisions about where to live, whom to befriend, and what they want for their daily life, even if they need support with financial management.

§ 811's function-specific approach requires the conservatorship petitioner to prove not just that the proposed conservatee has a condition, but that the condition causes specific functional deficits that significantly impair the specific decisions at issue. A person cannot be conserved for estate management if the only demonstrated deficit is in social cognition. A person cannot be conserved for personal care decisions if the only demonstrated deficit is in financial reasoning.

## The §811/§1801 Chain

§ 1826(a)(4)(B) requires the court investigator to determine whether §811 mental function deficits exist and significantly impair the proposed conservatee's ability to function in the §1801 grounds. This creates a chain:
1. §811 identifies what functional deficits must be proved
2. §1826 requires the investigator to assess those deficits
3. §1801 requires those deficits to rise to the level of "unable to provide for personal needs" or "substantially unable to manage finances"
4. §1801(e) requires all of this to be proved by clear and convincing evidence

## Investigative Use

If a capacity declaration (GC-348) exists in the conservatorship file, it should be audited against §811:
- Does it identify specific functional deficits or just a diagnosis?
- Does it connect those deficits to specific inability in §1801(a) or (b) functions?
- Was the physician licensed and competent in the specific area of assessment?
- Was the assessment contemporaneous with the petition?

A GC-348 that says only "proposed conservatee has [condition]" without specific §811 functional deficit assessment fails the §811 standard.
