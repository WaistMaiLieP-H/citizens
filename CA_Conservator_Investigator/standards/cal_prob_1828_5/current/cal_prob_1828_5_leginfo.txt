California Probate Code § 1828.5 — Limited Conservatorship Hearing Requirements

(a) At the hearing on the petition for appointment of a limited conservator for an allegedly developmentally disabled adult, the court shall:
  (1) Inquire into the nature and extent of general intellectual functioning
  (2) Evaluate the extent of impairment of adaptive behavior
  (3) Ascertain capacity to care for self and property
  (4) Inquire into the qualifications of the person seeking appointment
  (5) If a regional center report was not filed because the proposed conservatee withheld consent, determine the reason

(b) If the court finds the proposed conservatee POSSESSES THE CAPACITY to care for self and manage property as a reasonably prudent person, THE COURT SHALL DISMISS THE PETITION.

(c) If the court finds the proposed conservatee lacks capacity for SOME but not all tasks, the court shall appoint a LIMITED conservator.

(d) If the court finds the proposed conservatee lacks capacity for ALL tasks, the court shall appoint either a conservator or a limited conservator.

(e) The court SHALL DEFINE the powers and duties of the limited conservator SO AS TO PERMIT THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED ADULT TO CARE FOR HIMSELF OR HERSELF commensurate with ability.

(f) Prior to appointment, the court SHALL INFORM the proposed conservatee of:
  - The nature and purpose of the proceeding
  - That appointment will result in TRANSFER OF CERTAIN RIGHTS and the effect of such transfer
  - The identity of the nominated conservator
  - The RIGHT TO OPPOSE the proceeding
  - The RIGHT TO JURY TRIAL

After communicating this information, the court SHALL CONSULT THE PERSON to determine their opinion concerning the appointment.
