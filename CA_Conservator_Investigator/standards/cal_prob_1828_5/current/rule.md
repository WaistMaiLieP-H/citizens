# Rule — CAL_PROB_1828_5_HEARING_RIGHTS

**Citation:** Cal. Prob. Code § 1828.5  
**Standard:** Limited Conservatorship Hearing — Conservatee Rights and Mandatory Presence

## The Rule

§ 1828.5 governs the hearing rights of the proposed conservatee in limited conservatorship proceedings (for developmentally disabled adults — §1801(d)).

**§ 1828.5(a) — Right to be informed:**  
The proposed conservatee has the right to be present at the hearing, to be represented by counsel, and to present evidence on their behalf.

**§ 1828.5(b) — Mandatory presence (exception to court's discretion):**  
The proposed limited conservatee MUST be present at the hearing unless:
- (1) The conservatee informed the court investigator that they do not wish to attend AND the court finds attendance is not in their best interests; OR
- (2) The conservatee's medical condition prevents attendance

**§ 1828.5(c) — Consultation requirement:**  
Before making any decision about the powers to be granted to the limited conservator, the court must consult with the proposed conservatee regarding the person's preferences, values, and goals.

**§ 1828.5(d) — Mandatory appointment of counsel:**  
In limited conservatorship proceedings, the court must appoint counsel for the proposed conservatee unless the person has retained counsel of their own choosing.

## What This Means for Minors

§ 1828.5's mandatory presence and counsel requirements apply to limited conservatorship proceedings. For an unmarried minor subjected to a conservatorship-equivalent proceeding, the absence of counsel and hearing presence is evidence that the proceeding did not follow the protective framework required for limited conservatorships.
