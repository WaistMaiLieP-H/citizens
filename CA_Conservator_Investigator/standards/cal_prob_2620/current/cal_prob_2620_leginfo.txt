California Probate Code § 2620 — Conservator Accounting Requirements

(a) At the expiration of one year from appointment and thereafter not less frequently than biennially, the conservator shall present the accounting of the assets of the estate to the court for settlement and allowance. All accountings shall be on Judicial Council standard or simplified forms.

(b) The final accounting following death shall include a court accounting for the period ending on the date of death and a separate accounting for the period after death.

(c) Supporting documents required with each accounting:
  (1) Account statements from any institution where money or assets are held — original or verified electronic statements.
  (2) All account statements showing balances as of the closing date. For the first accounting, statements showing balance immediately before appointment AND as of closing date.
  (3) Original closing escrow statements for any sale of real property.
  (4) If conservatee is in a residential or long-term care facility, original bill statements.
  (6) Social security numbers and personal information shall be in a separate CONFIDENTIAL FINANCIAL STATEMENT attachment.

(d) EACH ACCOUNTING IS SUBJECT TO RANDOM OR DISCRETIONARY, FULL OR PARTIAL REVIEW BY THE COURT. If the accounting has any material error, the court shall make an express finding and may IMMEDIATELY SUSPEND the conservator without further notice and appoint a temporary conservator, or REMOVE the conservator pursuant to §2650.

(e) The conservator shall make available for inspection and copying, upon reasonable notice, ALL BOOKS AND RECORDS, including receipts for any expenditures.
