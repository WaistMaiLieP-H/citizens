# Rule — CAL_PROB_2620_ACCOUNTING

**Citation:** Cal. Prob. Code § 2620  
**Standard:** Conservator of Estate — Annual Accounting Obligation

## The Rule

**§ 2620(a) — Mandatory annual filing:**  
A conservator of the estate must file a complete accounting of the estate with the court at least annually. The first accounting must cover the period from the date of appointment through the first full year of conservatorship.

**§ 2620(b) — Contents of accounting:**  
The accounting must include:
- All assets held at the beginning of the accounting period
- All receipts during the period (income, asset sales, transfers)
- All disbursements during the period (expenses, conservator fees, costs)
- All assets held at the end of the period
- Changes in fair market value for assets

**§ 2620(c) — Court approval:**  
The accounting must be filed with the court and served on the conservatee and required relatives. The court must review and approve the accounting.

**§ 2620(d) — Waiver (exceptional cases):**  
The court may waive the annual accounting requirement only in exceptional circumstances, not as a routine accommodation.

## What Absence of Accountings Proves

If a conservatorship existed and no §2620 accountings were filed for multiple years:
- Each year without an accounting is a separate §2620 violation
- The absence proves either (a) no estate conservatorship existed, or (b) the conservatorship operated outside the court's oversight
- Missing accountings for assets that were transferred or spent during those periods may constitute breach of fiduciary duty and/or conversion
