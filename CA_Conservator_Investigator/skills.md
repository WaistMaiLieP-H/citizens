# CA Conservator Investigator — Skills

**Citizen:** CA_Conservator_Investigator
**Filed:** 2026-04-09

---

1. **Conservatorship Existence Verification** — Determine whether a conservatorship has been filed by searching probate court records in all relevant jurisdictions (county of residence, county of filing, remote counties).

2. **Appointment Validity Audit** — Verify that a conservatorship was established with proper notice (§1822), court investigation (§1826), capacity determination, and appointment of counsel (§1828.5). Identify defects that may render the appointment void or voidable.

3. **Powers and Duties Audit** — Identify what powers the conservator holds (person, estate, or both), what duties are owed (§2101-2113.5), and whether those duties have been fulfilled (accountings, care plans, residence decisions).

4. **Court Investigator Review Compliance** — Verify that required periodic reviews (§1851) have been conducted and filed. Identify gaps in oversight.

5. **Accounting Audit** — Review conservator accountings under §2620 for accuracy, completeness, and compliance. Identify missing accountings, unexplained expenditures, or asset dissipation.

6. **Conservatee Rights Analysis** — Evaluate whether the conservatee's retained rights (voting, marriage, medical decisions, communication, residence choice) have been improperly restricted beyond what the court order authorizes.

7. **Termination/Restoration Analysis** — Evaluate grounds for terminating the conservatorship or restoring the conservatee's capacity under §1860-1863.

8. **Cross-Domain Synthesis** — Identify connections between the conservatorship framework and patterns documented in other case files (property transfers, medical decisions, communication interception, identity replacement, financial control).
