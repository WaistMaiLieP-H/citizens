## GROUNDS FOR EMERGENCY/TEMPORARY CONSERVATORSHIP

Petitioner states that immediate appointment of a temporary conservator is necessary because:

### A. Immediate Risk of Financial Harm (Probate Code § 2250(a))

1. Proposed conservatee [FATHER'S NAME] has been isolated from petitioner and from petitioner's branch of the family for an extended period through the actions of [SISTER'S NAME].

2. [SISTER'S NAME] has progressively taken control over the proposed conservatee's financial affairs, property, and assets.

3. The proposed conservatee's vessel — his primary liveaboard residence and a substantial asset — was removed from its long-term slip at Jim's Holiday Harbor (formerly Lloyd's Holiday Harbor), 415 Fleming Lane, Antioch, CA 94509, in a manner timed to coincide with petitioner's discovery of that location. The marina property is currently listed for sale, creating additional urgency regarding slip records availability. This removal and concealment constitutes financial elder abuse in progress (Welf. & Inst. Code § 15610.30).

4. Substantial inheritance assets are expected to pass to petitioner as the proposed conservatee's first-born child. Petitioner has reason to believe these assets are at risk of being transferred, encumbered, dissipated, or otherwise moved beyond reach of the estate through the actions of [SISTER'S NAME] and others acting in concert with her.

5. If a temporary restraining order and temporary conservatorship are not issued immediately, irreparable financial harm will result before a full hearing can be held.

### B. No Adequate Alternative Remedy

6. The proposed conservatee's location is unknown. He cannot be consulted regarding his wishes or circumstances. He cannot consent or object to the actions being taken against his interests because he is being isolated from independent contact.

7. A Durable Power of Attorney, if one exists, was likely obtained under circumstances of undue influence (Welf. & Inst. Code § 15610.70) and would not constitute a valid alternative protective arrangement.

### C. Petitioner Is Suitable and Ready to Serve

8. Petitioner Michael Vernen Thomas Hartmann is the adult son of the proposed conservatee, has a lifelong relationship with him, and is motivated to protect his welfare and estate from exploitation. Petitioner is a California resident prepared to appear before this court and accept the duties of conservator.

## WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU FILE THIS

1. **Same day:** Clerk routes your ex parte application to the Probate Department. Judge reviews without hearing (ex parte) and may:
   - Grant the TRO immediately
   - Schedule a same-day or next-day hearing
   - Request additional information

2. **Within 5 days:** Court investigator is appointed and begins locating proposed conservatee

3. **At full hearing (usually 30-60 days out):** Both petitioner and any objecting party (sister) may appear. Court decides on permanent conservatorship.
