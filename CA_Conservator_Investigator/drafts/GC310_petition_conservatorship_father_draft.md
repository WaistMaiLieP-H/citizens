## BASIS FOR CONSERVATORSHIP
### (Probate Code §§ 1800.3, 1801)

Petitioner states the following facts in support of this petition:

**1. Inability to Provide for Personal Needs**

Proposed conservatee Michael Edwin Hartmann (DOB: 06/05/1956, age 69) is an elder adult whose ability to provide for his own personal needs — including food, clothing, shelter, and medical care — may be substantially impaired. Petitioner has been unable to contact or locate his father, indicating either incapacity or that a third party is controlling and restricting his access to communications and independent decision-making.

**2. Isolation and Undue Influence by Third Party**

Petitioner's sister, [SISTER'S FULL NAME], has over a period of years progressively assumed control over Michael Edwin Hartmann's affairs, finances, associations, and location. This pattern is consistent with financial elder abuse under Welfare & Institutions Code § 15610.07 (undue influence) and § 15610.30 (financial abuse). Specifically:

- Petitioner has been systematically excluded from contact with his father
- Petitioner has been excluded from contact with the paternal side of the family
- [SISTER'S NAME] has progressively taken control of financial matters, property, and assets that are expected to pass to petitioner as the first born
- The proposed conservatee's vessel — his primary residence for approximately ten years — was removed from its Antioch marina slip in a manner timed to coincide with petitioner's discovery of the location, suggesting active concealment
- Petitioner has reason to believe substantial inheritance assets are at risk of dissipation, transfer, or misappropriation

**3. Jurisdiction**

This court has jurisdiction under Probate Code § 1820 because Contra Costa County, California was the proposed conservatee's most recent and principal place of residence, having resided aboard a vessel docked at Jim's Holiday Harbor (formerly Lloyd's Holiday Harbor), 415 Fleming Lane, Antioch, Contra Costa County, CA 94509, from approximately 2013 to 2023. The harbormaster at (925) 757-2346 can confirm this residency through slip rental records.

**4. No Existing Conservatorship Known**

Petitioner is not aware of any existing conservatorship, guardianship, or similar protective proceeding for proposed conservatee in any jurisdiction. However, petitioner is concerned that [SISTER'S NAME] may have obtained a Power of Attorney or similar instrument through undue influence. Petitioner requests that the court investigate whether any such instrument exists and, if so, order its production and evaluation.
