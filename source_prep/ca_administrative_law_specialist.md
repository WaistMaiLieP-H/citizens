### GOV CODE § 11350 — Judicial declaration of regulation validity
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Any interested person may challenge regulation validity in superior court (declaratory relief)
- Invalid if: (1) substantial failure to comply with APA chapter; (2) necessity finding not supported by substantial evidence; (3) agency declaration of necessity conflicts with substantial evidence in record
- Emergency regulations separately challengeable if emergency facts don't constitute an actual emergency
**Standard ID:** `gov_11350_regulation_validity_challenge`

**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)

- "Party" = agency + respondent + any person allowed to appear
- "Respondent" = person against whom accusation or statement of issues filed
- "Administrative law judge" = individual qualified under §11502
- Power to act may be delegated EXCEPT "agency itself" powers
**Standard ID:** `gov_11500_apa_adjudication_definitions`

### GOV CODE § 11513 — APA hearing procedures
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Oral evidence on oath/affirmation only
- Full cross-examination rights on any relevant matter — even if not raised on direct
- Hearsay admissible to supplement/explain other evidence but NOT sufficient alone to support finding (unless admissible over objection in civil actions)
- Presiding officer may exclude evidence if probative value substantially outweighed by undue time consumption
- Privilege rules apply as required by statute
**NOTE:** §11513(c) is the "relaxed evidence" rule — administrative hearings do not follow technical evidence rules; relevant evidence admitted if responsible persons rely on it in serious affairs
**Standard ID:** `gov_11513_apa_hearing_procedures`

### CCP § 437c — Summary judgment (motion practice anchor)
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Motion after 60 days from general appearance; notice 81 days before hearing
- Granted if NO triable issue as to ANY material fact AND moving party entitled to judgment as matter of law
- Separate statements required — failure may be grounds for denial
- §437c(f): Summary adjudication available for individual causes of action, affirmative defenses, claims for damages, issues of duty
**Standard ID:** `ccp_437c_summary_judgment`

### CCP § 1094.5 — Administrative mandamus (writ review of administrative decisions)
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Primary judicial review mechanism for final administrative orders where hearing required + evidence taken + discretion vested
- Court inquires: (1) respondent acted without/in excess of jurisdiction; (2) fair trial; (3) prejudicial abuse of discretion
- Abuse of discretion = not proceeded in manner required by law, OR order not supported by findings, OR findings not supported by evidence
- **Independent judgment standard:** Where authorized, court can re-weigh evidence; if findings not supported by weight of evidence = abuse of discretion
- **Substantial evidence standard:** All other cases — findings must be supported by substantial evidence in light of whole record
- Court may stay administrative order pending judgment, UNLESS against public interest
**Standard ID:** `ccp_1094_5_administrative_mandamus`

### CCP § 425.16 — Anti-SLAPP special motion to strike
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Protects acts in furtherance of petition/free speech rights in connection with public issue
- Includes: (1) statements before legislative/executive/judicial proceedings; (2) statements re: issues under official review; (3) statements in public forum on public interest; (4) other conduct furtherance of petition/speech re: public issue
- Plaintiff must establish probability of prevailing on claim — or motion granted
- 60 days to file from service of complaint (or later with good cause)
- ALL discovery STAYS upon motion filing until order
- Prevailing defendant recovers attorney's fees and costs
- Frivolous SLAPP motion: plaintiff recovers attorney's fees under §128.5
**NOTE:** AG, CDI Commissioner, DA, city attorney acting as public prosecutor EXEMPT from anti-SLAPP
**Standard ID:** `ccp_425_16_anti_slapp`

### GOV CODE § 11503 — Accusation in APA proceedings — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Accusation initiates hearing to revoke/suspend/limit/condition a right, authority, license, or privilege
- Must be written statement of charges in ordinary and concise language — sufficient for respondent to prepare defense

**Standard ID:** `gov_11503_accusation`

### GOV CODE § 11506 — Notice of defense — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Respondent has 15 days after service to file notice of defense
- Notice may: (1) request hearing; (2) object to accusation for failure to state acts on which agency may proceed; (3) object to form if too indefinite to identify transaction; (4) admit in whole or part; (5) present new matter by way of defense; (6) object if compliance would cause material violation of another regulation
- Notice deemed specific denial of all parts not expressly admitted
- **FAILURE TO FILE = waiver of right to hearing** (but agency may in its discretion still grant hearing)
- Notice need not be verified or follow particular form; signed by or on behalf of respondent
**Standard ID:** `gov_11506_notice_of_defense`

### GOV CODE § 11517 — Proposed decision process — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- If ALJ hears case alone → must prepare proposed decision within 30 days of submission; 30 days after agency receives it, copy filed as public record and served on parties
- Agency has 100 days to act: (A) adopt in entirety; (B) reduce/mitigate penalty and adopt balance; (C) make only technical/minor clarifying changes; (D) reject and send back to same/new ALJ for more evidence; (E) reject and decide case on the record itself
- If agency fails to act within 100 days → proposed decision DEEMED ADOPTED
- If agency decides case itself (option E): must give parties opportunity for oral/written argument; no member may vote on additional oral evidence unless they heard it
- Agency decision filed immediately as public record; served on all parties
**Standard ID:** `gov_11517_proposed_decision`

## CASE LAW SEEDS

1. **Topanga Assn. for a Scenic Community v. County of Los Angeles**, 11 Cal.3d 506 (1974) — Agency must set out findings; "implied findings" doctrine limited; court cannot supply missing rationale for agency decision on mandamus review
2. **Yamaha Corp. of America v. State Bd. of Equalization**, 19 Cal.4th 1 (1998) — Agency interpretation of its own regulations: when to give deference vs. independent review; "great weight" standard for agency's own rules
3. **Bixby v. Pierno**, 4 Cal.3d 130 (1971) — Standard of review in administrative mandamus: substantial evidence vs. independent judgment; vested rights doctrine — fundamental right at stake = independent judgment
4. **Fascination, Inc. v. Hoover**, 39 Cal.2d 260 (1952) — Agency must provide fair hearing; procedural due process minimum in administrative proceedings
5. **Quintero v. City of Santa Ana**, 114 Cal.App.4th 810 (2003) — Anti-SLAPP in administrative context; public employee speech protections

## HISTORICAL CHAIN SEED

**The wound:** California's APA emerged from the same crisis the federal APA addressed — agencies making law without the accountability of the legislative process. GOV §11340's explicit legislative finding of "unprecedented growth" and "unclear and unnecessarily complex" language is the Legislature's self-indictment. The wound that runs through this Citizen's cases: when agency proceedings fail to follow the APA's procedural guarantees — proper hearing, oath-based evidence, findings tethered to evidence — the mechanism for correcting that failure is CCP §1094.5. But the CalVCB case shows the secondary wound: even when the writ mechanism exists, victims navigating administrative appeals without representation face deadline compression, inadequate notice, and single-source denials. The APA's protections are only accessible to those who know they exist.
