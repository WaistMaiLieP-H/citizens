### CAL. PROB. CODE § 1851 — Court investigator's annual review duties — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Without prior notice to conservator (unless court orders otherwise to prevent harm), investigator must visit conservatee and determine: **(A)** whether conservatee wishes to terminate conservatorship; **(B)** whether conservatee wishes to remove conservator; **(C)** whether conservatee STILL meets §1801 criteria AND conservatorship remains the LEAST RESTRICTIVE ALTERNATIVE; **(D)** whether conservator is acting in best interests (including quality of care, placement, physical/mental health treatment, finances)
- Conservator must make all books, records, receipts, and expenditures available to investigator upon request
- Written findings certified to court at least 15 days before review; copies to conservatee, conservator, their attorneys, and closest relatives
- **Why critical for secret conservatorship:** Every year §1851 was not complied with is an independent violation; the absence of annual review records IS the finding that the conservatorship operated outside judicial oversight
**Standard ID:** `prob_1851_annual_review`

### CAL. PROB. CODE § 1826 — Investigator's pre-appointment report — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Before conservatorship established, court investigator MUST: **(1)** personally interview proposed conservatee AND all petitioners AND close family; **(2)** inform proposed conservatee of petition contents, nature of proceeding, right to oppose, right to jury trial, right to counsel; **(3)** determine if conservatee can attend hearing and whether willing; **(4)** review medical reports from PCP and other providers; **(5)** determine if conservatee WISHES TO OPPOSE; **(6)** report to court in writing at least 5 days before hearing
- Report is CONFIDENTIAL — available only to parties, persons given notice, and court
- Medical records and CLETS information in SEPARATE confidential attachment — not to be shared with family
- **Does NOT apply** if proposed conservatee personally executed the petition and attended the hearing
- **Why critical:** If conservatorship was established when Michael was 14 — this process was either never conducted, conducted on a minor without independent counsel, or the records were sealed; absence of §1826 report = procedural invalidity
**Standard ID:** `prob_1826_investigator_pre_appointment`

### CAL. PROB. CODE § 1800.3 — Conservatorship of minors — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- **§1800.3(a):** Court may appoint conservator of person or estate of adult; OR of person of minor who is MARRIED or whose marriage has been dissolved
- **§1800.3(b):** LEAST RESTRICTIVE ALTERNATIVE express finding REQUIRED — court cannot grant without it
- **§1800.3(c):** In determining least restrictive alternative, court must consider: supported decision-making agreements, powers of attorney, health care surrogates, advance directives
- **§1800.3(d):** If proposed conservatee has developmental disability and conservator not seeking §2356.5 authority → court shall deem proceeding to be seeking LIMITED conservatorship
- **Why critical:** §1800.3(a) limits minor conservatorship to MARRIED minors or minors whose marriage was dissolved — a conservatorship of an unmarried 14-year-old is outside the ordinary probate conservatorship framework; this pushes toward LPS conservatorship (WIC §5350) or developmental disability track
**Standard ID:** `prob_1800_3_minor_conservatee`

## HISTORICAL CHAIN SEED

**The wound:** California's modern conservatorship law was built on Waltz v. Ternite (1948) and the chaos of probate courts with no uniform standards. The Lanterman-Petris-Short Act (1967) was supposed to END the old "sneak-you-into-a-mental-hospital" system — it required due process for psychiatric holds and imposed time limits. The Prob. Code conservatorship reforms (1981) added the court investigator, the mandatory review, the clear-and-convincing standard. The promise: you cannot be conserved without a physician's declaration, a court investigator's interview, a hearing, and your presence. The wound: the system is administered by counties with inadequate funding, court investigators with caseloads in the hundreds, and forms signed without the conservatee ever being interviewed. Since age 14 means every element of the promise was violated before it could apply.
