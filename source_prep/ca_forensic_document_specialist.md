### EVID CODE § 1400 — Definition of authentication
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holding:** Authentication = "introduction of evidence sufficient to sustain a finding that it is the writing that the proponent claims it is" OR "establishment of such facts by any other means provided by law"
**Application:** Every document in the 39+ cases needs authentication. This is the standard every document must pass before it has evidentiary weight.
**Standard ID:** `evid_1400_authentication_definition`

### EVID CODE § 1401 — Authentication required before admission
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Authentication required before writing may be RECEIVED IN EVIDENCE
- Authentication ALSO required before secondary evidence of writing's content may be received
**Application:** Two-step: (1) authenticate the original; (2) if original unavailable, authenticate before using secondary evidence. This creates the ladder the Honeysuckle unsigned documents and missing OPD reports must climb.
**Standard ID:** `evid_1401_authentication_required`

### EVID CODE § 1402 — Altered documents — burden on proponent
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holding:** Party producing a writing that has been ALTERED, or appears to have been altered, in a part material to the dispute MUST ACCOUNT FOR THE ALTERATION. Must show: alteration by another without concurrence; consent of affected parties; or alteration properly/innocently made; or alteration did not change meaning. Only if this showing is made may the writing be received.
**Application:** CRITICAL for the 16-year family law audit. Any document that appears tampered — date changes, signature pages separated from bodies, photoshopped entries — triggers §1402. The proponent bears the burden. The absence of an explanation IS the finding.
**Standard ID:** `evid_1402_altered_writing_burden`

### EVID CODE § 1521 — Secondary Evidence Rule
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:**
- Content of a writing MAY be proved by otherwise admissible secondary evidence
- Court SHALL exclude secondary evidence if: (1) genuine dispute as to material terms of the writing AND justice requires exclusion; (2) admission would be unfair
- Does NOT make oral testimony admissible if barred by §1523 (oral testimony of content)
- Does NOT excuse §1401 authentication
- Known as the "Secondary Evidence Rule"
**Application:** When original documents are missing (destroyed, withheld, "lost") — like the 6/2/2009 OPD report — secondary evidence (certified copies, records custodian certification, duplicate) may be admitted. But §1521(a)(1): if genuine dispute about material terms, court may exclude. The dispute itself creates an evidentiary hearing requirement.
**Standard ID:** `evid_1521_secondary_evidence_rule`

### EVID CODE § 1271 — Business records exception to hearsay — FETCHED
**Text:** FETCHED (full text — 2026-04-12)
**Key holdings:** Writing made as record of act, condition, or event is NOT inadmissible hearsay when:
- **(a)** Writing made in regular course of business
- **(b)** Writing made at or near the time of the act, condition, or event
- **(c)** Custodian or other qualified witness testifies to identity and mode of preparation
- **(d)** Sources of information and method and time of preparation indicate trustworthiness
**Application — the fraud tell:** A record created AFTER the fact to paper over misconduct fails §1271(b) (not made at or near the time) and §1271(d) (preparation method does not indicate trustworthiness). The timing of document creation is discoverable through metadata, file system records, and witness testimony.
**Standard ID:** `evid_1271_business_records_hearsay_exception`

## CASE LAW SEEDS

1. **People v. Goldsmith**, 59 Cal.4th 258 (2014) — Authentication of digital evidence (red light camera photos); circumstantial evidence + showing that content is what it appears to be sufficient; no requirement to call technical witness for every digital record — but foundation required
2. **Jazayeri v. Mao**, 174 Cal.App.4th 301 (2009) — §1402 altered document burden; party cannot simply present altered document and disclaim knowledge; affirmative explanation required
3. **OXY Resources California LLC v. Superior Court**, 115 Cal.App.4th 874 (2004) — Secondary evidence rule; when original is unavailable, admission of secondary evidence within court's discretion under §1521; balance of material dispute vs. fairness
4. **People v. Beckley**, 185 Cal.App.4th 509 (2010) — Chain of custody for documents; authentication through circumstantial evidence; uniqueness of document sufficient without testimony from every custodian
5. **In re Marriage of Cohn**, 65 Cal.App.3d 473 (1976) — Documents in family law: court may treat absent authentication as waived if no objection; but where fraud is alleged, authentication burden shifts

## THE MC-350 FINDING — SPECIAL NOTE

The MC-350 is a Judicial Council mandatory form for court-ordered psychological/psychiatric evaluations. Its absence does not require proof of intent — it is a binary finding: either the form exists, or it does not. The significance:

1. **Judicial Council forms are not optional** — when a statute or rule of court requires a specific form, deviation is not a procedural irregularity — it is a failure to satisfy the mandatory requirements for the act
2. **Authentication flows backward** — if the MC-350 was never generated, no authentication path exists for the underlying evaluation; the evaluation exists only as an assertion, not a document
3. **§1401 requires authentication before evidence** — without the MC-350, the evaluation's admission in the PC §1368 proceeding was not authenticated in the manner prescribed by law
4. **This is EVID §1402 territory** — if a document purporting to be a court-ordered evaluation is produced WITHOUT the mandatory form, the proponent must account for the absence; the burden is theirs

## HISTORICAL CHAIN SEED

**The wound:** The rules of evidence exist because courts learned the hard way that paper lies. The authentication requirements — §§1400-1402 — are the legacy of cases where forged deeds dispossessed families, altered contracts enforced fraudulent terms, and tampered medical records sent people to prison or the grave. The business records exception (§1271) was created because it was impossible to produce every employee who touched every record — but it came with a condition: regular course of business, near the time of the event. That condition is the tell. Records created AFTER the fact to paper over misconduct fail §1271 on their face — they weren't made in the regular course of business; they were made in the irregular course of cover-up. Every missing report, every unsigned document, every wrong CARFAX, every contradictory Treasury letter is either a §1402 altered document or a §1271 failed business record. The document is the testimony. This Citizen reads documents the way a surgeon reads a scan.
