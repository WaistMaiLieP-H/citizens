## Key Legal Principles

1. **TRUST Act (§7284.6):** California agencies SHALL NOT use agency money or personnel for federal civil immigration enforcement — no detainers, no status inquiries, no release date information, no personal address data — except where judicial warrant or §7282.5 criminal criteria met.

2. **T/U Visa exception (§7284.6(b)(4)):** Law enforcement MAY make inquiries necessary to certify crime/trafficking victims for T or U visas — this is the cooperation pathway for victims, not a loophole for enforcement.

3. **Interpreter oath (EVID §752(a)):** When a witness cannot understand or express in English, a sworn interpreter SHALL be appointed. Unsworn translations of witness testimony are not §752-compliant — they are not evidence.

4. **Civil interpreter right (GOV §68566):** In civil proceedings, upon request, the court SHALL appoint an interpreter for a party who cannot understand or speak English.

5. **Padilla doctrine (Padilla v. Kentucky, 2010):** Defense counsel MUST advise non-citizen clients of the deportation consequences of any criminal plea — failure is IAC. Applies to every California criminal proceeding involving a non-citizen.
