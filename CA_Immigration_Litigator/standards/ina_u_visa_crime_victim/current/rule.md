## The Rule

The U visa provides nonimmigrant status to aliens who:

**(I)** Have suffered substantial physical or mental abuse as a result of having been a victim of certain qualifying criminal activity;

**(II)** Possess information concerning that criminal activity;

**(III)** Have been, are being, or are likely to be helpful to a federal, state, or local law enforcement official, prosecutor, judge, or other authority investigating or prosecuting the criminal activity; AND

**(IV)** The criminal activity violated the laws of the United States or occurred in the United States.

---

## Qualifying Criminal Activities

The statute lists specific qualifying crimes, including:
- Domestic violence
- Sexual assault / sexual exploitation
- Trafficking
- Involuntary servitude
- Kidnapping / abduction
- Extortion
- False imprisonment
- Fraud in foreign labor contracting
- Stalking
- Obstruction of justice
- Perjury
- Witness tampering
- Felonious assault
- Being held hostage

**Case application — expanded theory:** If the conservatorship system constitutes involuntary servitude or false imprisonment under federal law, the qualifying criminal activity element is met through the control mechanism itself — not just through any individual assault or domestic violence incident.

---

## The Law Enforcement Certification Requirement

A U visa petition (Form I-918) must include a **law enforcement certification** (Form I-918 Supplement B) signed by the head of the certifying agency (or designee). The certification must state:

1. The petitioner is, has been, or is likely to be helpful in the investigation or prosecution of the qualifying criminal activity
2. The qualifying criminal activity described in the certification violates federal or state law

**§7284.6(b)(4) connection:** California's TRUST Act specifically preserves the right of law enforcement agencies to make inquiries and perform activities necessary for T/U visa certification. A certifying agency (including local police departments, sheriff's offices, prosecutors, judges) is affirmatively permitted to cooperate with federal immigration authorities for this purpose.

**Strategic significance:** The law enforcement certification is the bottleneck. Without it, the U visa petition cannot proceed. The TRUST Act's preservation of this pathway means that any California law enforcement agency with knowledge of qualifying criminal activity involving Butsaya (or any other potential crime victim) can — and should — provide the certification.

---

## Annual Cap and Waiting List

- **10,000 U visas per fiscal year** (principal applicants; derivatives are uncapped)
- Excess petitioners are placed on a waiting list with deferred action status (employment authorization while waiting)
- Wait times as of 2025-2026 are substantial (multiple years)

---

## Derivative Benefits

- Spouse and children (under 21) of U visa holder may also receive U nonimmigrant status
- After 3 years in U status, the holder may apply for adjustment to lawful permanent resident (LPR) status under 8 USC §1255(m)

## Application to Active Cases

### Butsaya — T/U Visa Analysis

**If Butsaya was a crime victim (not the DVRO perpetrator):**
The source prep identifies an inversion: the fabricated DVRO was presented as though Butsaya was the victim and Michael was the abuser. If the actual facts show that Butsaya was herself victimized in the broader scheme — her Thai national status exploited, her immigration vulnerability used as a control mechanism, a fabricated DVRO obtained in her name — then Butsaya may qualify for U visa status as a victim of:
- Domestic violence (if she was victimized within the household)
- Fraud in foreign labor contracting (if her immigration status was tied to work conditions)
- Perjury / obstruction of justice / witness tampering (if she was coerced into providing false statements)
- False imprisonment (if the conservatorship / control system restricted her movement)

**If Butsaya was a participant (the DVRO was genuinely her petition):**
Then the U visa analysis does not apply to Butsaya — she is not a crime victim but an actor in the scheme. The U visa analysis would instead apply to Michael as a victim of the qualifying criminal activities perpetrated through the fabricated DVRO.

**Steward decision required:** The Butsaya T/U visa theory depends on whether Butsaya is a victim or a participant. This Citizen documents both pathways; the factual determination is for the Steward.
