## § 752. Interpreter for Witness

**(a)** When a witness is incapable of understanding the English language or of expressing
himself or herself in the English language so as to be understood directly by counsel,
court, and jury, an interpreter whom the witness can understand and who can understand
the witness shall be sworn to interpret for the witness.

**(b)** In any action or proceeding in which the court appoints an interpreter to assist a
witness:

**(1)** In a criminal action or a juvenile court proceeding, the compensation of the
interpreter is a charge against the court, the county, or the state, as the case may be.

**(2)** In a civil action or proceeding, the compensation of the interpreter is to be
apportioned among the parties as the court may determine; such compensation may be taxed
as costs in the proceeding.
