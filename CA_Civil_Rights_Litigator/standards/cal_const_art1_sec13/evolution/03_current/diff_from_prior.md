# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 02_amendment_1982 — Proposition 8 added § 28(f)(2), narrowing exclusionary rule to federal standards
**This stage:** 03_current — No further textual or ballot-measure changes; all subsequent development is through case law

## Key Changes

1. **No textual changes:** Section 13 has not been amended since 1974. Section 28(f)(2) has not been amended since 1982 in any way that affects § 13 interpretation.
2. **Case law refinement:** Courts have continued to define the boundaries of where § 13 retains independent force versus where the Truth-in-Evidence provision controls. The Lance W. framework remains the governing standard.

## What Stayed the Same

Everything. The text of § 13 is identical to 1974. The Proposition 8 framework from 1982 is intact. The split between the broader right (surviving) and the narrowed exclusionary remedy (tracking federal standards) continues unchanged.
