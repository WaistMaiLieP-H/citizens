# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** People v. Disbrow, 16 Cal.3d 101 (1976)
**Provision at issue:** Cal. Const. Art. I, § 13 (search and seizure) and § 15 (self-incrimination)
**Version the court was reading:** Pre-Proposition 8 (1982) version of the California Constitution
**Evolution stage:** `../../cal_const_art1_sec13/evolution/01_origin/` (closest match)

Disbrow interpreted the pre-Prop 8 California Constitution, which the Court held provided broader protections than the federal Fourth and Fifth Amendments. This independent-state-grounds approach was partially curtailed by Proposition 8 (1982), which added the "Right to Truth-in-Evidence" provision limiting exclusionary rules to federal minimums. See In re Lance W. (1985) for the post-Prop 8 framework.
