# Munson v. Del Taco, Inc., 46 Cal. 4th 661 (2009)

## The Case

Munson, a wheelchair user, encountered accessibility barriers at Del Taco restaurants — inaccessible condiment counters and insufficient wheelchair clearance. He sued under the Unruh Act (which incorporates ADA violations via §51(f)) and sought §52 statutory damages of $4,000 per visit.

## What the Court Held

The California Supreme Court:

1. **Section 52 provides a minimum of $4,000 in statutory damages for each occasion of discrimination, regardless of actual damages.** The plaintiff does not need to prove actual harm — the statutory minimum is designed as both compensation and deterrence.

2. **Standing under §52 requires that the plaintiff was personally denied full and equal access.** The plaintiff must have actually encountered the barrier, not merely be aware that a barrier exists.

3. **Each visit to an inaccessible establishment constitutes a separate violation** supporting a separate $4,000 statutory damages award.

## The Rule

**Section 52 statutory damages ($4,000 minimum per violation) are available for each occasion a plaintiff is personally denied equal access. No actual damages need be proven. Standing requires personal encounter with the barrier.**
