# Cal. Const. Art. I, § 1 — Current Text

The current text has been unchanged since 1974.

In 1974, California voters passed Proposition 7, which changed "All men" to "All people" throughout the Constitution. This was the last amendment to Article I, Section 1.

The substance of the section — the list of inalienable rights — has not changed since the privacy amendment of 1972. The 1974 amendment was a gender-neutral language correction, not a substantive change.
