# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 02_amendment_1987 — Unruh Act categories incorporated by reference
**This stage:** 03_amendment_2004 — false police reports defined as "intimidation by threat of violence"

## Key Changes

1. **Added (b)(2):** False police reports = intimidation by threat of violence
2. **Requirements:** Report must be (a) false or made with reckless disregard for truth, AND (b) motivated by victim's protected characteristic
3. **Effect:** Weaponized law enforcement reports now civilly actionable as hate-motivated intimidation

## What This Changes

| Before 2004 | After 2004 |
|---|---|
| Ralph Act required direct violence or direct threats | False police reports are now a form of "intimidation by threat of violence" |
| Weaponized 911 calls had no specific civil rights remedy | Weaponized reports motivated by hate are Ralph Act violations |
| Indirect violence via police was a gap in the statute | Gap closed — the person who triggers the armed response is liable |
