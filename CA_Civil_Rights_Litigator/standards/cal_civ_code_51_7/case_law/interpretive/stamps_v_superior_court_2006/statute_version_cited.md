# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Stamps v. Superior Court (2006) 136 Cal.App.4th 1441
**Statute at issue:** California Civil Code § 51.7 (Ralph Civil Rights Act)
**Version the court was reading:** The Ralph Act as amended through the mid-2000s, prohibiting violence or intimidation by threat of violence against persons exercising their civil rights based on protected characteristics.
**Evolution stage:** evolution/01_origin_1976/

The court in Stamps applied Section 51.7 in its post-amendment form, but the operative language traced directly back to the 1976 original: the prohibition on violence or intimidation by threat of violence motivated by the victim's protected status. The statute had accumulated additional protected categories over the years, but the core question — did the defendant use violence or threats of violence because of who the plaintiff is — remained unchanged from the original enactment.

This case matters because the court addressed the scope of what counts as "intimidation by threat of violence" under the Ralph Act. The version being read still required a nexus between the violent conduct and a protected characteristic. Without that connection, even serious misconduct does not trigger Ralph Act liability. The court was reading a statute that had always drawn this line, from 1976 forward.
