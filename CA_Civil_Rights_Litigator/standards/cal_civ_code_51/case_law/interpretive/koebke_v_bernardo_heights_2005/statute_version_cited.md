# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Koebke v. Bernardo Heights Country Club, 36 Cal.4th 824 (2005)
**Statute at issue:** Cal. Civ. Code § 51 (Unruh Civil Rights Act)
**Version the court was reading:** Post-2000 version (as amended to add "sexual orientation" to the enumerated categories, AB 1001, 1999)
**Evolution stage:** `../../cal_civ_code_51/evolution/02_current/` (closest match)

The Court applied the Unruh Act after "sexual orientation" was added as an enumerated protected category. However, the decision also rests on the broader principle from In re Cox (1970) that the Unruh Act prohibits all arbitrary discrimination by business establishments, not just discrimination based on enumerated categories. The marriage/domestic partnership distinction was held to be a proxy for sexual orientation discrimination.
