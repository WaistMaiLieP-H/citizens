# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 01_origin_1959 — sex, race, color, religion, ancestry, national origin
**This stage:** 02_amendment_1974 — added physical disability

## Key Changes

1. **Added:** "Physical handicap" (later "disability") as enumerated protected category
2. **Effect:** Explicit statutory basis for disability discrimination claims in business establishments

## What Stayed the Same

The core structure (full and equal access to all business establishments) was unchanged. The amendment expanded the enumerated list without altering the underlying cause of action.
