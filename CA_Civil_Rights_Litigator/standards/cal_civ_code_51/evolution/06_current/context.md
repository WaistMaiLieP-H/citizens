## Pointer

## The Full Evolution Arc

| Stage | Year | Event | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 1959 | Unruh Civil Rights Act (AB 2350) | Core: full and equal access to all business establishments |
| 02 | 1974 | Disability amendment | Added physical disability to enumerated categories |
| 03 | 1992 | ADA incorporation (SB 1834) | ADA violation = automatic Unruh violation with $4,000+ damages |
| 04 | 1999 | Sexual orientation (AB 1001) | Explicit LGBTQ protections in public accommodations |
| 05 | 2005 | Genetic information (SB 559) | Predated federal GINA by 3 years |
| 06 | Current | Continuing expansion | Immigration status, primary language, CROWN Act definitions |

## What the Evolution Shows

The Unruh Act has a dual architecture:

1. **The enumerated list** — expanded by the Legislature over six decades from 6 categories (1959) to 15+ categories (2026). Each addition responds to a newly recognized form of discrimination.

2. **The catch-all doctrine** — from *In re Cox* (1970) and *Marina Point v. Wolfsen* (1982): the enumerated categories are illustrative, and the Unruh Act prohibits all arbitrary discrimination by business establishments.

These two architectures work together: the catch-all provides immediate coverage for new forms of discrimination (no legislative action needed), while enumeration provides clarity, notice, and enforcement certainty.

The intentional discrimination requirement from *Harris v. Capital Growth* (1991) is the primary limitation: the Unruh Act does not reach disparate-impact discrimination absent intentional discriminatory conduct.
