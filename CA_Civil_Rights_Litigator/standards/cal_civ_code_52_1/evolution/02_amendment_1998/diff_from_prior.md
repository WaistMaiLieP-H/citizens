# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 01_origin_1987 — Bane Act enacted with core provisions (subdivisions (a)-(i))
**This stage:** 02_amendment_1998 — speech protection added (subdivisions (j)-(l))

## Key Changes

1. **Added (j):** Speech alone insufficient for Bane Act claim unless it constitutes a true threat of violence
2. **Added (k):** Reinforcement of speech protection
3. **Added (l):** Bars content-based speech restrictions in Bane Act injunctions

## What Stayed the Same

The core liability provision (subdivision (a)) and remedies were unchanged. The elements of a Bane Act claim — interference through threat, intimidation, or coercion with constitutional or statutory rights — remained identical. The speech provisions are a defense/limitation, not a modification of the underlying cause of action.
