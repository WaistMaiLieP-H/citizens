# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 03_amendment_2000 — government accountability provisions (immunity stripping, indemnification)
**This stage:** 04_amendment_2015 — RIPA framework connects profiling data to Bane Act enforcement

## Key Changes

1. **Context shift:** Bane Act gains a data-driven evidence pipeline through RIPA stop data
2. **Profiling nexus:** Racial and identity profiling explicitly connected to constitutional rights the Bane Act protects
3. **No core text change:** §52.1(a) through (o) substantively unchanged

## Practical Impact

The 2015 amendments didn't change what the Bane Act says — they changed what it can prove. Before RIPA, a Bane Act claim alleging profiling-based rights interference required the plaintiff to establish a pattern from scratch. After RIPA, the mandated stop data creates a public evidence base that plaintiffs can use to demonstrate systematic interference with rights through discriminatory policing practices.
