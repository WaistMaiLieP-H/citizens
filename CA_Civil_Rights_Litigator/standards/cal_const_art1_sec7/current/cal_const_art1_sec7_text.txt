California Constitution, Article I, Section 7
Due Process and Equal Protection

Subdivision (a):
"A person may not be deprived of life, liberty, or property without due process of law or denied equal protection of the laws."

Subdivision (b):
"A citizen or class of citizens may not be granted privileges or immunities not granted on the same terms to all citizens. Privileges or immunities granted by the Legislature may be altered or revoked."

Citation: Cal. Const. art. I, § 7
Added: November 5, 1974 (Proposition 7)
Subdivision (a) amended: November 6, 1979 (Proposition 1 — added school-assignment language)
