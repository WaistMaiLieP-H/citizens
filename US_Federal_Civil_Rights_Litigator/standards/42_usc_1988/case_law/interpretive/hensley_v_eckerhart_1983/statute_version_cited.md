# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Hensley v. Eckerhart, 461 U.S. 424 (1983)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1988(b)
**Version the court was reading:** Original 1976 enactment (Civil Rights Attorney's Fees Awards Act, P.L. 94-559)
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1988/evolution/01_origin_1976/`

The Court established the "lodestar" method for calculating attorney's fees under § 1988: reasonable hours × reasonable rate, with adjustments for results obtained. Where a plaintiff achieves only partial success, the court must consider whether the claims on which the plaintiff failed were related to the claims on which they succeeded, and reduce fees if the unsuccessful claims were distinct and separable.
