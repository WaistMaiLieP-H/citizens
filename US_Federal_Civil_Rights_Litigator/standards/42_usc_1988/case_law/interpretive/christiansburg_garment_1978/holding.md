## Summary holding (for reference)

**Case:** Christiansburg Garment Co. v. EEOC, 434 U.S. 412 (1978)
**Standard:** 42 USC § 1988 (prevailing party fee shift)

**Core holding:** Prevailing defendants may recover § 1988 attorney fees only if the plaintiff's case was frivolous, unreasonable, or without foundation — not merely because the defendant prevailed. The asymmetric standard protects civil rights plaintiffs' access to courts: plaintiffs recover if they prevail; defendants recover only if the claim was baseless.
