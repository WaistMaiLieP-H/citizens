# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Buckhannon Board & Care Home v. West Virginia DHHR, 532 U.S. 598 (2001)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1988(b), also Fair Housing Amendments Act § 3613(c)(2)
**Version the court was reading:** Post-1991 version (as amended by Civil Rights Act of 1991, P.L. 102-166)
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1988/evolution/01_origin_1976/` (closest — intermediate amendment stages not individually built)

The Court rejected the "catalyst theory" — the idea that a plaintiff who achieves the desired result through the lawsuit's pressure (without a court judgment) qualifies as a "prevailing party" for fee-shifting purposes. To be a "prevailing party" under § 1988, the plaintiff must obtain a court-ordered change in the legal relationship between the parties — a judgment on the merits or a consent decree, not merely a voluntary policy change by the defendant.
