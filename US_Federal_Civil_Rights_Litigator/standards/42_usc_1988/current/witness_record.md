## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: § 1988 fee recovery is what makes civil rights litigation economically viable. In any case with small individual damages but significant constitutional violations — wrongful arrest, unlawful search, denial of disability accommodation — the § 1988 fee claim may be worth more than the merits damages. Carefully document hours from day one. The fee claim is as important as the damages claim and must be planned from the start of the case.
