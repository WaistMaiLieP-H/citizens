# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 05_amendment_1994 — VAWA §13981 added to §1988(b) (now dead letter)
**This stage:** 06_amendment_2000 — RLUIPA added to §1988(b) fee list

## Textual Change

**Modified:** §1988(b) — added "the Religious Land Use and Institutionalized Persons Act of 2000" to the enumerated list.

## Practical Impact

1. Prisoner religious exercise claims under RLUIPA §3 can recover fees
2. Church/religious land use claims under RLUIPA §2 can recover fees
3. RLUIPA survived constitutional challenge (*Cutter v. Wilkinson*, 2005)
4. This is the last amendment to §1988 — the statute has not been textually modified since 2000

## Cumulative State of §1988 After This Amendment

- Subsection (a): Choice of law (unchanged since R.S. §722, 1874)
- Subsection (b): Fee-shifting — now covers §§1981, 1981a, 1982, 1983, 1985, 1986, Title IX, RFRA, VAWA §13981, and RLUIPA
- Subsection (c): Expert fees in §1981/§1981a actions (unchanged since 1991)
