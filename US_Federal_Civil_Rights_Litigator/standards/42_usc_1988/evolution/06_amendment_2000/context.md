# 42 USC §1988 — 2000 Amendment (P.L. 106-274, §4(d))

## What Changed

The Religious Land Use and Institutionalized Persons Act of 2000 (RLUIPA) added a reference to itself in §1988(b). RLUIPA protects religious exercise in two specific contexts: (1) land use regulation (zoning, permits) and (2) institutionalized persons (prisons, mental health facilities). §1988(b) was amended to include RLUIPA claims in its fee-shifting coverage.

## Why It Happened

After *City of Boerne v. Flores* (1997) struck down RFRA as applied to state and local governments, Congress needed a narrower vehicle that would survive constitutional scrutiny. RLUIPA was drafted to rely on the Spending Clause (for institutionalized persons — facilities receiving federal funds) and the Commerce Clause (for land use — buildings in interstate commerce), rather than §5 enforcement power.

RLUIPA passed unanimously in both chambers — a rare achievement reflecting bipartisan consensus that religious land use and prisoner religious exercise deserved federal protection.

## Impact on §1988

Adding RLUIPA to §1988(b) followed the established pattern: Congress creates a civil rights cause of action, then ensures the fee-shift makes it economically enforceable. RLUIPA claims — particularly prisoner religious exercise claims under §3 and church zoning claims under §2 — became viable for attorneys because prevailing plaintiffs could recover fees.

## Why RLUIPA Survived Where RFRA (as to States) Did Not

RLUIPA is narrower in scope (two specific contexts, not all government action) and rests on different constitutional foundations (Spending Clause and Commerce Clause, not §5). The Supreme Court upheld RLUIPA's institutionalized-persons provisions in *Cutter v. Wilkinson* (2005).

## The Current State

This is the most recent textual amendment to §1988. As of 2026, §1988(b) covers:
- §1981 (equal rights under the law)
- §1981a (intentional employment discrimination damages)
- §1982 (property rights)
- §1983 (civil action for deprivation of rights)
- §1985 (conspiracy)
- §1986 (failure to prevent conspiracy)
- Title IX
- RFRA (federal government only, post-Boerne)
- VAWA §13981 (dead letter, post-Morrison)
- RLUIPA
