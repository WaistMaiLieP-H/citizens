# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 02_amendment_1988 — Restoration Act broadened underlying statute scope (no §1988 text change)
**This stage:** 03_amendment_1991 — Civil Rights Act of 1991 adds subsection (c)

## Textual Change

**Added:** New subsection (c) authorizing expert fees in §1981/§1981a actions.

Before 1991, §1988 had two subsections:
- (a) — choice-of-law provision (from R.S. §722, 1874)
- (b) — attorney's fees for prevailing party (from 1976 Act)

After 1991, §1988 has three subsections:
- (a) — choice-of-law (unchanged)
- (b) — attorney's fees (unchanged)
- (c) — **NEW: expert fees in §1981/§1981a actions**

## Practical Impact

1. Employment discrimination plaintiffs under §1981 can now recover expert fees
2. §1983 plaintiffs still cannot recover expert fees — the gap created by *West Virginia University Hospitals v. Casey* (1991) remains open for non-employment civil rights claims
3. This asymmetry incentivizes civil rights plaintiffs to frame claims under §1981 where possible, rather than §1983, when expert testimony is critical
