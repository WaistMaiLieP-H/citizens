# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 04_amendment_1993 — RFRA added to §1988(b) fee list
**This stage:** 05_amendment_1994 — VAWA civil remedy (§13981) added to §1988(b) fee list

## Textual Change

**Modified:** §1988(b) — added reference to 42 USC §13981 (VAWA civil rights remedy for gender-motivated violence).

## Practical Impact

1. Intended to allow fee-shifting for VAWA civil rights claims
2. **Effectively nullified by United States v. Morrison (2000)** — §13981 struck down as unconstitutional
3. The text reference to §13981 remains in §1988(b) but is a dead letter
4. Gender-motivated violence claims must now proceed under state law or §1983 (if state actors involved)
