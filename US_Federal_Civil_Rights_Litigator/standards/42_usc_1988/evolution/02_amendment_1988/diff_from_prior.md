# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 01_origin_1976 — §1988(b) fee-shifting established for civil rights actions
**This stage:** 02_amendment_1988 — Civil Rights Restoration Act (P.L. 100-259)

## What Changed in §1988 Itself

The text of §1988(b) was not directly amended. The change was to the scope of the underlying civil rights statutes that §1988(b) references. The Restoration Act amended Title VI, Title IX, §504, and the Age Discrimination Act to define "program or activity" as meaning the entire institution receiving federal funds — not just the specific program.

## Practical Impact on Fee-Shifting

Before the Restoration Act, §1988(b) fees were available only for the narrow "program-specific" claims that survived Grove City. After the Restoration Act, §1988(b) fees were available for institution-wide discrimination claims under all four statutes. This dramatically expanded the universe of cases where attorney's fees could be recovered.

## Net Effect

No textual change to §1988 itself. But the effective scope of fee-shifting expanded from program-level claims to institution-wide claims.
