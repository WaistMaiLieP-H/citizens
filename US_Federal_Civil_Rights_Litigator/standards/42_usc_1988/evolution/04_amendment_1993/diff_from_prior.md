# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 03_amendment_1991 — added subsection (c) for expert fees in §1981/§1981a
**This stage:** 04_amendment_1993 — RFRA added to §1988(b) fee list

## Textual Change

**Modified:** §1988(b) — added "the Religious Freedom Restoration Act of 1993" to the list of statutes for which a court may award attorney's fees to prevailing parties.

The structure remains three subsections (a), (b), (c). Only (b) was modified — its enumerated list of covered statutes was expanded.

## Practical Impact

1. RFRA plaintiffs can recover fees when they prevail
2. After City of Boerne v. Flores (1997), this only applies to federal-government RFRA claims
3. State/local religious freedom claims must use other vehicles (state RFRAs, or First Amendment §1983 claims, which were already covered by §1988(b))
