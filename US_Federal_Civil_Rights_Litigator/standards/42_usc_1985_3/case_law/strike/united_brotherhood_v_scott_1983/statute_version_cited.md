# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** United Brotherhood of Carpenters v. Scott, 463 U.S. 825 (1983)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1985(3)
**Version the court was reading:** Revised Statutes § 5519 / current § 1985(3) — never textually amended since 1874.
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1985_3/evolution/02_revised_statutes_1874/`

The Court held that § 1985(3) does not reach conspiracies motivated by economic or commercial animus. The "class-based, invidiously discriminatory animus" requirement from Griffin means the statute protects against racial or similarly discrete-class conspiracies, not labor disputes. The nonunion workers' claim that union members conspired to deprive them of First Amendment rights failed because opposition to nonunion labor is economic, not class-based.
