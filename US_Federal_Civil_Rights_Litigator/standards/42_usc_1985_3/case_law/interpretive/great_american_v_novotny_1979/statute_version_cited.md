# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Great American Federal Savings & Loan Ass'n v. Novotny, 442 U.S. 366 (1979)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1985(3)
**Version the court was reading:** Revised Statutes § 5519 / current § 1985(3) — never textually amended since 1874.
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1985_3/evolution/02_revised_statutes_1874/`

Novotny alleged his employer conspired to discriminate against him for advocating for women's rights, asserting § 1985(3) as a vehicle for Title VII-type claims. The Court held that § 1985(3) cannot be used as a vehicle to assert rights created by Title VII. Where Congress has enacted a comprehensive remedial scheme with its own enforcement mechanisms and procedures (Title VII), § 1985(3) does not provide a back door around those procedures.
