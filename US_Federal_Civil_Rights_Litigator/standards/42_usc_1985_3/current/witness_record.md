## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: § 1985(3) is the tool for cases where the harmful conduct involves multiple coordinated private actors motivated by discriminatory animus — not just a single government official. In cases involving coordinated family-based, institutional, or organized private discrimination against a person based on a protected class characteristic, § 1985(3) may reach actors that § 1983 cannot. The conspiracy element and animus element are both demanding; discovery is essential to proving them.
