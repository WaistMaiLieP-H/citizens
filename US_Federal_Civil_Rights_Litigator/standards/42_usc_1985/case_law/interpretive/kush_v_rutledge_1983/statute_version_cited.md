# Statute Version Cited: Kush v. Rutledge (1983)

**Evolution stage:** 02_revised_statutes_1874 (unchanged since recodification — 42 USC 1985 has never been amended)
**Text at issue:** Subsection (2) — both clauses. First clause: conspiracy to "deter, by force, intimidation, or threat, any party or witness in any court of the United States from attending such court, or from testifying to any matter pending therein." Second clause: conspiracy to "obstruct... the due course of justice in any State or Territory, with intent to deny to any citizen the equal protection of the laws."
**Original enactment:** Civil Rights Act of 1871 (Ku Klux Klan Act), Section 2
**Revised Statutes:** R.S. 1980 (1874), later recodified as R.S. 5519
**Current codification:** 42 USC 1985(2)
