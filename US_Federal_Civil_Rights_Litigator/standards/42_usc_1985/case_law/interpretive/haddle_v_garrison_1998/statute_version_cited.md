# Statute Version Cited: Haddle v. Garrison (1998)

**Evolution stage:** 02_revised_statutes_1874 (unchanged since recodification — 42 USC 1985 has never been amended)
**Text at issue:** Subsection (2), first clause — conspiracy to "deter, by force, intimidation, or threat, any party or witness in any court of the United States from attending such court, or from testifying to any matter pending therein, freely, fully, and truthfully" and the damages provision "injured in his person or property"
**Original enactment:** Civil Rights Act of 1871 (Ku Klux Klan Act), Section 2
**Revised Statutes:** R.S. 1980 (1874), later recodified as R.S. 5519
**Current codification:** 42 USC 1985(2)
