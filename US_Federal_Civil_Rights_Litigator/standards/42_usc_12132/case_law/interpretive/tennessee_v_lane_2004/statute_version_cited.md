# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Tennessee v. Lane, 541 U.S. 509 (2004)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 12132 (ADA Title II)
**Version the court was reading:** Original 1990 enactment (P.L. 101-336, § 202). Never amended.
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_12132/evolution/01_origin/`

Lane could not access a second-floor courtroom because he was a paraplegic and there was no elevator. The Court held that Title II validly abrogates state sovereign immunity when applied to cases involving the fundamental right of access to courts. Congress acted within its § 5 enforcement power under the Fourteenth Amendment because the legislative record documented a pattern of disability discrimination in access to judicial services.
