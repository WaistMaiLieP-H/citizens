# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Barnes v. Gorman, 536 U.S. 181 (2002)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 12132 (ADA Title II), also 29 U.S.C. § 794 (Rehab Act § 504)
**Version the court was reading:** Original 1990 enactment (P.L. 101-336, § 202). Never amended.
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_12132/evolution/01_origin/`

The Court held that punitive damages are not available under ADA Title II or Rehabilitation Act § 504. Because both statutes are Spending Clause legislation (recipients accept federal funds in exchange for obligations), remedies are limited to those a funding recipient could foresee — compensatory damages and injunctive relief, not punitive damages.
