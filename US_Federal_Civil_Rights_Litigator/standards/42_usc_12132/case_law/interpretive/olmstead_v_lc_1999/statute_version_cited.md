# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Olmstead v. L.C. ex rel. Zimring, 527 U.S. 581 (1999)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 12132 (ADA Title II)
**Version the court was reading:** Original 1990 enactment (P.L. 101-336, § 202). Never amended.
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_12132/evolution/01_origin/`

§ 12132 has never been textually amended since its enactment in 1990. The Court interpreted the integration mandate: "no qualified individual with a disability shall, by reason of such disability, be excluded from participation in or be denied the benefits of the services, programs, or activities of a public entity." Olmstead established the community-integration principle as inherent in this text.
