## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: ADA Title II reaches every state and local government function — courts, jails, public transit, public hospitals, and all government programs regardless of whether federal money is involved. In any case involving a government entity's treatment of a disabled individual, Title II analysis is mandatory. The sovereign immunity question matters most when suing a state agency directly in federal court — for local government agencies (cities, counties), Eleventh Amendment immunity does not apply and Title II claims proceed straightforwardly.
