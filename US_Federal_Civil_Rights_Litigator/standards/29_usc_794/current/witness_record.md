## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: § 504 and ADA Title II are almost always pled together because they overlap in coverage and have nearly identical legal standards. § 504's reach into federally funded private entities — hospitals, private social service agencies, private universities — makes it essential in cases where the defendant is not a government entity but does receive federal money. Always check whether the defendant receives Medicare, Medicaid, federal grants, or other federal assistance before limiting the analysis to ADA Title II alone.
