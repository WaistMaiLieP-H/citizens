# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Alexander v. Choate, 469 U.S. 287 (1985)
**Statute at issue:** 29 U.S.C. § 794 (Rehabilitation Act § 504)
**Version the court was reading:** Post-1978 amendment (P.L. 95-602, which broadened § 504 and added enforcement provisions)
**Evolution stage:** `../../29_usc_794/evolution/02_current/` (closest match — individual amendment stages not yet built)

The Court held that § 504 reaches disparate-impact discrimination, not just intentional discrimination. Tennessee's reduction of Medicaid inpatient days from 20 to 14 facially neutral but disproportionately burdened disabled persons. However, the Court found the reduction did not deny "meaningful access" to Medicaid services and upheld it.
