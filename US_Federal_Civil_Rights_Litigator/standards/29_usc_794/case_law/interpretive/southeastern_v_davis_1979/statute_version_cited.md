# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Southeastern Community College v. Davis, 442 U.S. 397 (1979)
**Statute at issue:** 29 U.S.C. § 794 (Rehabilitation Act § 504)
**Version the court was reading:** Original 1973 enactment (P.L. 93-112, § 504). Pre-1978 amendment.
**Evolution stage:** `../../29_usc_794/evolution/01_origin/`

First Supreme Court case interpreting § 504. The Court read the original text and held that § 504 does not require affirmative action — it prohibits discrimination but does not compel institutions to make fundamental alterations to accommodate disabilities. Davis, a hearing-impaired nursing student, was "otherwise qualified" only if she could meet the program's legitimate requirements.
