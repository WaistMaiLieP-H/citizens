# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 01_origin — single-sentence prohibition, no explicit enforcement mechanism
**This stage:** 02_amendment_1978 — regulatory authority and Title VI remedies added

## Key Changes

1. **Added:** Explicit authorization for federal agencies to issue Section 504 regulations
2. **Added:** Attorney General coordination role for enforcement across agencies
3. **Added:** Incorporation of Title VI remedies, procedures, and rights — including private right of action, administrative complaints, and fund-cutoff authority
4. **Effect:** Transformed Section 504 from a freestanding prohibition into a prohibition backed by the full Title VI enforcement apparatus

## What Stayed the Same

The core prohibition — no discrimination by federally funded programs — was unchanged. The 1978 amendment was about enforcement infrastructure, not substantive scope.
