# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 03_amendment_1988 — institution-wide coverage restored
**This stage:** 04_amendment_1991 — employment standards aligned with ADA Title I

## Key Changes

1. **Added:** Section 504 employment discrimination claims now use ADA Title I standards
2. **Effect:** Unified framework for disability employment discrimination across both statutes
3. **Scope:** Employment only — non-employment Section 504 claims (education, healthcare, government services) unaffected

## What This Means for Practitioners

- File under both §504 and ADA Title I when the employer receives federal funds
- Same substantive standards apply to both claims
- §504 still has advantages: no employee-count threshold (ADA Title I requires 15+ employees), broader coverage of federal contractors
