# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 02_amendment_1978 — regulatory authority and Title VI remedies
**This stage:** 03_amendment_1988 — institution-wide coverage restored

## Key Changes

1. **Redefined:** "program or activity" expanded from the specific program receiving federal funds to the entire institution
2. **Mechanism:** Veto override (same act that affected §1988's effective scope)

## What This Means in Practice

- Before: Hospital cardiac unit gets Medicare → only cardiac unit covered by §504
- After: Hospital cardiac unit gets Medicare → entire hospital covered by §504

The substantive prohibition was unchanged. The scope of entities covered was massively expanded back to what Congress had originally intended.
