# Diff from Prior Stage

**Prior:** 04_amendment_1991 — employment standards aligned with ADA Title I
**This stage:** 05_amendment_2014 — WIOA modernization and ADAAA alignment

## Key Changes

1. **Updated:** Terminology modernized ("individual with a disability")
2. **Aligned:** Definition of disability now matches ADAAA's broadened standard
3. **Effect:** More people qualify for Section 504 protection; mitigating measures excluded from disability determination

## What Stayed the Same

The core prohibition, the enforcement mechanism (Title VI remedies), the institution-wide coverage, and the employment standards alignment with ADA Title I were all unchanged.

## Note on 2015 Amendment

A minor technical amendment in 2015 (P.L. 114-95, Every Student Succeeds Act) made conforming changes to cross-references but did not substantively alter Section 504. The 2014 WIOA amendment is the last substantive change.
