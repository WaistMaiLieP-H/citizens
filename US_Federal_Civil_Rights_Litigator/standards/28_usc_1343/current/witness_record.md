## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: § 1343 is a technical jurisdictional provision but its inclusion in the complaint is essential pleading practice. Every federal civil rights complaint should cite § 1331 and §§ 1343(a)(3)–(4) in the jurisdictional paragraph. Courts rarely challenge jurisdiction in properly pled civil rights cases, but omitting the jurisdictional basis creates an unnecessary vulnerability. Include both § 1331 and § 1343 — belt-and-suspenders is the correct approach here.
