# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Lynch v. Household Finance Corp., 405 U.S. 538 (1972)
**Statute at issue:** 28 U.S.C. § 1343(3) (now § 1343(a)(3))
**Version the court was reading:** Pre-1979 version (before addition of "or an Act of Congress")
**Evolution stage:** `../../28_usc_1343/evolution/01_origin/`

The Court held that § 1343 jurisdiction extends to property rights, not just personal liberties. The "rights, privileges, or immunities" language in the jurisdictional statute encompasses all constitutional rights — there is no property/personal rights distinction limiting federal court access for §1983 claims.
