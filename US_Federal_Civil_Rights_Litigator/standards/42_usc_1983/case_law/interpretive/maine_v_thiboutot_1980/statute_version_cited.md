# Statute Version Cited: Maine v. Thiboutot (1980)

**Evolution stage:** 04_amendment_1979_dc
**Key language interpreted:** "rights, privileges, or immunities secured by the Constitution **and laws**"
**The "and laws" phrase was added in 1874** (R.S. §1979). The 1871 original said only "secured by the Constitution of the United States." Thiboutot gave that two-word addition its full operative meaning 106 years later.
