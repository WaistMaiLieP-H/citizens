# §1983 Evolution: R.S. §1979 → 42 USC §1983 (1952 Code)

## What Changed

Nothing. The text is identical to R.S. §1979.

The only change is the numbering: R.S. §1979 was assigned the section number 42 USC §1983 when the United States Code was organized into numbered titles and sections. This is the number the statute carries today.

## Why This Stage Matters

This is the version Monroe v. Pape (1961) revived. When Justice Douglas wrote the majority opinion bringing §1983 back from ninety years of dormancy, he was reading this exact text — the unchanged R.S. §1979 language, now bearing the number §1983.
