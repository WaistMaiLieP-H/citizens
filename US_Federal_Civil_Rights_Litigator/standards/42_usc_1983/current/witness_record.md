## HERALD Countersign

Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: § 1983 is the primary vehicle for any case involving state or local government officials violating constitutional rights. It should be evaluated in every case involving government actors — police, courts, social services, licensing agencies. The interaction with the Government Claims Act is a critical trap: § 1983 claims need no government claim; companion state law claims do. These deadlines must be tracked independently in every government entity case.
