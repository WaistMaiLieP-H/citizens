Standard witnessed. Cross-case note: Bivens is a dead end for any new constitutional claim against federal officers. Do not build a case strategy on Bivens in any context that differs from the three recognized cases. For federal officer misconduct, evaluate FTCA (negligence, not intentional torts by law enforcement), administrative remedies, and state law claims. The gap between § 1983 and Bivens is real and consequential — federal constitutional violations against individuals by federal actors are largely unremedied through civil damages under current law.
