# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Davis v. Passman, 442 U.S. 228 (1979)
**Statute at issue:** No statute — implied constitutional cause of action under the Fifth Amendment Due Process Clause
**Doctrinal stage:** Second recognized Bivens context
**Evolution stage:** `../../bivens_doctrine/evolution/01_origin/` (Bivens v. Six Unknown Named Agents, 1971)

Davis extended Bivens from the Fourth Amendment (unreasonable search) to the Fifth Amendment (gender discrimination under the equal protection component of due process). This remains one of only three surviving Bivens contexts after the Court's narrowing decisions in Ziglar v. Abbasi (2017) and Egbert v. Boule (2022). No statutory text is at issue — the Bivens doctrine is entirely judge-made.
