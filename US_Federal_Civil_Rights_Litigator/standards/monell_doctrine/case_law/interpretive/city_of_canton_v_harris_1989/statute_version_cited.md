# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** City of Canton v. Harris, 489 U.S. 378 (1989)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1983 (via Monell municipal liability doctrine)
**Version the court was reading:** Current version (post-1979 amendment)
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1983/evolution/06_current/`

Canton established the "deliberate indifference" standard for municipal failure-to-train liability under Monell. The city's failure to train officers to recognize medical emergencies was the "policy" at issue. The Court held that a municipality's failure to train employees can be the basis of § 1983 liability when that failure amounts to "deliberate indifference" to the rights of persons the employees encounter — meaning the need for training was obvious and the city consciously chose not to provide it.
