# Statute Version Cited

**Case:** Board of County Commissioners v. Brown, 520 U.S. 397 (1997)
**Statute at issue:** 42 U.S.C. § 1983
**Version the court was reading:** Post-1979 version (as amended by P.L. 96-170, Dec. 29, 1979, adding "or an Act of Congress" per Maine v. Thiboutot)
**Evolution stage:** `../../42_usc_1983/evolution/06_current/`
**Doctrinal layer:** Monell municipal liability — single-hire theory as distinct from pattern-or-practice and failure-to-train theories

The text of §1983 itself was not at issue. The question was the doctrinal standard for when a single hiring decision constitutes municipal "policy" under Monell v. Department of Social Services (1978). Brown narrows Monell by requiring a tighter causal link between the hire and the violation than Canton requires for failure-to-train claims.
