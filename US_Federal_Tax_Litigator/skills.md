# US_Federal_Tax_Litigator — Skills

Professional competencies this Citizen owns.

## TAX-001 — Criminal tax statute analysis
Identify the correct charging statute across §§7201 / 7202 / 7203 / 7206(1) / 7206(2) / 7207 / 7212. Distinguish attempt-to-evade (§7201, felony, specific intent) from willful failure to file (§7203, misdemeanor, omission) from false-return (§7206(1), felony, verification-clause perjury). Apply the Spies affirmative-act test and the Cheek subjective-good-faith doctrine.

## TAX-002 — Badges of fraud / method of proof
Reconstruct unreported income via specific-item, net-worth, bank-deposits, and cash-expenditures methods. Identify the eleven Bradford badges (understatement, inadequate records, implausible explanations, concealment, failure to cooperate, engagement in illegal activity, attempts to mislead, lack of credibility, filing false documents, failure to file, dealing in cash).

## TAX-003 — Civil fraud penalty litigation (§6663)
Establish or defeat the 75% civil fraud penalty. Clear-and-convincing burden on government. Collateral estoppel from §7201 conviction. Bifurcation of fraud and underlying deficiency.

## TAX-004 — Lien and priority analysis (§§6321, 6323)
Calculate §6321 attachment date. Apply the §6323(a) four superpriorities (purchaser, holder of security interest, mechanic's lienor, judgment lien creditor) and §6323(b) enumerated exceptions. Walk NFTL refiling, subordination, and discharge procedures.

## TAX-005 — Collection due process representation (§§6320, 6330)
Request CDP hearing within 30 days of Notice of Intent to Levy or NFTL filing. Raise spousal defenses, challenges to the underlying liability (if not prior opportunity), collection alternatives, and offer-in-compromise. Preserve record for Tax Court review.

## TAX-006 — Refund suits (28 USC §1346(a)(1))
Flood v. United States prerequisites: full payment (divisible-tax exception), timely claim (§6511), six-month waiting / denial, two-year statute on suit. Venue rules.

## TAX-007 — Tax Court deficiency litigation
Petition within 90 days (150 if outside US) of statutory notice. Choice of S case vs. regular. Burden shift under §7491.

## TAX-008 — Whistleblower practice (§7623)
Form 211 submission. 15–30% mandatory award band (§7623(b)) above $2M threshold. Tax Court jurisdiction over §7623(b) denials.

## TAX-009 — Identity-based tax fraud recovery
IP PIN, Form 14039, IRM 25.23 protocol. Refund holds under §6402. Distinguish SSN misuse (employment ID theft — EITC/wage reporting under someone else's SSN) from return-identity theft (filing a refund return in someone else's name). Cross-reference 18 USC §§1028(a)(7), 1028A (aggravated ID theft) via Financial_Fraud Citizen.

## TAX-010 — Abusive tax shelter defense / listed transactions
§6011 disclosure, §6662A reportable-transaction penalty, §6111 material advisor, §6707 promoter penalty. Circular 230 §10.35 opinion-standards.

## TAX-011 — 1099-S / real estate closing integrity
§6045(e) reporting-person rules. Reconcile HUD-1 / CD to 1099-S. Identify misreporting patterns (allocation between basis and proceeds, seller-concession accounting, gift-of-equity treatment).

## TAX-012 — Virtual currency / digital asset reporting
Form 8949 / Schedule D / Notice 2014-21 characterization. Cost-basis reconstruction from exchange records. §6050I expansion (Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act 2021) to digital assets over $10,000 (effective date litigation — regulation suspended).

## TAX-013 — Transferable state tax credits as federal income
Tempel v. Commissioner (136 T.C. 341, 2011) — state tax credit is a capital asset; sale is §1001 gain; basis zero if credit earned rather than purchased. Apply to GA film tax credits (O.C.G.A. §48-7-40.26) and similar transferable-credit markets. Forensic indicator when transferable-credit trading volume exceeds plausible production basis.

## TAX-014 — §6050I / IRS Form 8300 cash-transaction reporting
$10,000 cash-receipt rule for trades or businesses. Structured-transaction detection. Interaction with BSA CTR (31 CFR 1010.311).

## TAX-015 — §6103 disclosure doctrine
Taxpayer privacy vs. statutory exceptions (§6103(h) tax administration, §6103(i) criminal investigation order, §6103(k) state-tax information sharing). §7431 civil damages for unauthorized disclosure.

## TAX-016 — Treasury securities / SSN attribution disputes
Treasury Direct account integrity. SSN-based attribution of savings bonds, T-bills, notes. Cross-reference to 18 USC §1028(a)(7) identity theft and §6103 disclosure avenues for taxpayer verification.

## TAX-017 — Passive activity / PAL loss suspension (§469)
Material participation seven-test. Real estate professional §469(c)(7). Grouping elections Reg. §1.469-4.

## TAX-018 — Foreign accounts / FBAR / FATCA exposure
FinCEN Form 114 §5314, §5321 penalties, §5322 criminal; §6038D Form 8938; §7609 John Doe summons.

## TAX-019 — IRS Criminal Investigation (CI) referral pipeline

## TAX-020 — Innocent spouse relief (§6015)
