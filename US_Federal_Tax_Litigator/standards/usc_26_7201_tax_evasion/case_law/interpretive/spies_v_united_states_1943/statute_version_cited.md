# Statute version cited — Spies v. United States

**Case:** Spies v. United States, 317 U.S. 492 (1943)
**Decided:** January 11, 1943
**Statute construed:** Internal Revenue Code of 1939, § 145(b)
**Relationship to evolution chain:** Predecessor to 1954 § 7201. The operative phrase "wilful attempt in any manner to evade or defeat any tax imposed by this chapter" appears in § 145(b); the 1954 recodification preserved it verbatim except "this chapter" → "this title" and "wilful" → "willfully."

**Evolution-chain mapping:**
- Spies reads the 1939 version (not present as a dedicated subfolder in this evolution chain because the wound answered and the doctrine articulated both carry forward intact)
- Doctrine applied to 1954 § 7201 without modification: *Sansone v. United States*, 380 U.S. 343 (1965); *United States v. Bishop*, 412 U.S. 346 (1973)
- Doctrine applied to 1986 recodified § 7201 without modification: *Boulware v. United States*, 552 U.S. 421 (2008)

**Why this matters:** Spies' holding is the doctrinal bridge across three statutory versions. Evolution-chain integrity requires that a case construing a predecessor version be tagged with its version; downstream courts citing Spies under the 1954 or 1986 text are relying on the preservation of the operative language, and that preservation is the doctrinal warrant for the continuity.
