# CA Consumer Protection Litigator — Skills

**Citizen:** CA_Consumer_Protection_Litigator
**Filed:** 2026-04-10

---

1. **CLRA Notice-and-Cure Drafting** — Draft the Civ. Code §1782 pre-suit notice letter: identify the enumerated §1770 practice alleged, state the particular remedies demanded, calendar the 30-day cure window, preserve the injunctive-relief path (no notice required for injunctive relief only, §1782(d)).

2. **UCL Claim Construction** — Plead "unlawful" (borrowed-statute), "unfair" (balancing / Cel-Tech test in competitor cases, older FTC test in consumer cases), and "fraudulent" (likelihood to deceive a reasonable consumer) prongs separately. Track Prop 64 standing (actual injury + loss of money or property) and the 4-year SOL.

3. **Rees-Levering Single-Document Rule Analysis** — Read a retail installment sale contract for single-document compliance under Civ. Code §2981.9 and §2982. Flag spot-delivery / yo-yo sale patterns. Identify §2983.1 forfeiture consequences for non-compliant contracts (unwinding the deal).

4. **Song-Beverly Lemon Law Case Evaluation** — Determine whether a vehicle meets the §1793.22 presumption (2 repair attempts for safety-related defect / 4 total / 30 cumulative days out of service within 18 months or 18,000 miles). Calculate §1794(c) civil penalty for willful violation (up to 2x actual damages).

5. **Rosenthal Act Violation Identification** — Apply Civ. Code §1788.17 (which incorporates federal FDCPA §§1692b-1692j by reference) to a debt collection fact pattern. Distinguish original-creditor liability under Rosenthal (which the federal FDCPA does not reach) from third-party debt collector liability.

6. **Common-Law Deceit Pleading** — Plead Civ. Code §§1709-1710 fraud with the specificity California requires (who, what, when, where, how). Distinguish intentional misrepresentation, negligent misrepresentation, concealment, and promise made without intent to perform.

7. **Fee-Shift Evaluation** — Identify every fee-shifting provision applicable to a consumer case: CLRA §1780(e), Song-Beverly §1794(d), Rosenthal §1788.30(c), Rees-Levering §2983.4, UCL (no direct fee shift — must use CCP §1021.5 catalyst theory). Tracks which cause of action carries fees and under what conditions.

8. **Arbitration Clause Analysis** — Evaluate a consumer arbitration clause for unconscionability (Armendariz factors), McGill rule (no waiver of public injunctive relief), and 2019 SB 707 / §1281.97 fee-payment default consequences. Know when to move to compel, when to resist, and when to move to vacate.

9. **Class Action Preservation** — Recognize when a consumer claim must be pled as a class from the start (Prop 64 representative-action gatekeeping) and when individual claims preserve themselves. Apply Brinker and Sav-On for class cert predicate analysis.

10. **Parallel State-Federal Pleading** — Draft a complaint that pleads CLRA + UCL + Rosenthal (state) in parallel with FCRA + FDCPA + TILA (federal) so that each defendant faces the broadest viable claim matrix.
