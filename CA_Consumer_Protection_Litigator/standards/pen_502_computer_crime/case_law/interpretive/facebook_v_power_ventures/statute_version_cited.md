# Statute Version Cited — Facebook v. Power Ventures

**Case decided:** 2016
**Statute version applicable:** Cal. Penal Code §502(c)(7) (2016 version — substantively unchanged from 1988 comprehensive rewrite for "without permission" analysis)

## §502(c)(7) — The Provision Applied

§502(c)(7) prohibits anyone who "knowingly and without permission accesses or causes
to be accessed any computer, computer system, or computer network."

The court applied "without permission" as defined in §502(b)(12): access not explicitly
authorized by the owner, lessee, or authorized user.

## Current Status

§502(c)(7) has not been substantively amended since the 1988 rewrite. Power Ventures'
interpretation of "without permission" (revocation ends permission; owner controls
authorization independent of user) remains the operative standard for this provision.

## CFAA Parallel Analysis

The court analyzed both §502(c)(7) and 18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(2)'s "without authorization"
in parallel. For SIM swap claims: the same facts that establish CFAA "without
authorization" establish §502 "without permission," and vice versa.
