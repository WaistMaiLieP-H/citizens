# Statute Version — Kim v. Superior Court (2006)

**§1798.34 version:** As enacted 1977 (Stats. 1977, Ch. 709) with subsequent
amendments. The access right has not been substantively modified since enactment.
The 2006 appellate decision interpreted the statute in its then-current form.

**Current operative text of §1798.34 (as amended):**
Each agency that maintains a system of records shall permit each individual who is a
resident of California on request to inspect all personal information in any record
maintained about the individual and to receive a copy of such record or records.
