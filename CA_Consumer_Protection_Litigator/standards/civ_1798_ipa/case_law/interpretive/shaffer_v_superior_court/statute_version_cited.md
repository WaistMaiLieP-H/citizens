# Statute Version — Shaffer v. Superior Court (1995)

**§1798.53 version:** As enacted 1977 (Stats. 1977, Ch. 709). The individual liability
provision has not been substantively amended. The $2,500 punitive figure has not been
adjusted for inflation since 1977 enactment.

**Operative text of §1798.53:**
"Any employee of an agency who willfully discloses personal information, not subject
to disclosure under the provisions of this chapter, to any person or agency not
entitled to it, shall be liable to the individual to whom the information pertains in
the sum of two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) in addition to any actual
damages and attorneys' fees pursuant to Section 1798.45 of this chapter."

**Current operative text:** §1798.53 is unchanged from 1977. The $2,500 amount is
fixed in statute. Shaffer's application of the individual liability provision remains
controlling.
