# Statute Version Cited — Thompson v. 10,000 RV Sales (2005)

The Thompson court applied Rees-Levering as it read in 2005 — post the 1976 single-document-rule amendment and its subsequent technical refinements.

**Relevant provisions applied:**
- **§2981.9** — single-document rule
- **§2982** — mandatory disclosures
- **§2983** — forfeiture remedy for non-compliance
- **§2983.4** — attorney fees for prevailing party

**Evolution stage applicable:** 02_single_document_rule_1976 (operative text as of 2005)

**Version cited by the court:** Cal. Civ. Code §§2981, 2981.9, 2982, 2983, 2983.4 as in force June 2005.
