# Statute Version Cited — Flanagan v. Flanagan

**Case decided:** 2002
**Statute version applicable:** Cal. Penal Code §632 (post-1967 original; §632 text unchanged from 1967 through present)

## §632(c) — The Definition at Issue

The court interpreted §632(c):

> "Confidential communication" means any communication carried on in circumstances
> as may reasonably indicate that any party to the communication desires it to be
> confined to the parties thereto, but excludes a communication made in a public
> gathering or in any legislative, judicial, executive or administrative proceeding
> open to the public, or in any other circumstance in which the parties to the
> communication may reasonably expect that the communication may be overheard or
> recorded.

## Current Status

The §632(c) definition was not changed by any subsequent amendment. Flanagan's
interpretation of "confidential communication" remains the operative standard.

## Relationship to §632.7

For §632.7 (cellular/cordless, added 1992), the "confidential communication"
requirement was eliminated — §632.7 covers all cellular/cordless interceptions
regardless of confidentiality. Flanagan governs §632 claims; §632.7 claims do not
require this analysis.
