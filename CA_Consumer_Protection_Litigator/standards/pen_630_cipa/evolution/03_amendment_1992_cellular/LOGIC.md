# Evolution Stage 03 — 1992 Amendment: §632.7 and Elimination of Confidentiality Requirement

### THE WOUND

The 1985 amendments (§§632.5 and 632.6) covered cordless and cellular calls, but
both provisions maintained the "confidential communication" requirement from §632.
This created a practical problem: intercepting a cellular call required the plaintiff
to prove the communication was "confidential" — i.e., that the parties reasonably
expected it to be private.

Cellular call interception was particularly problematic. Radio scanner technology
was widely available and cheap. Cellular calls in the early 1990s were analog and
transmitted in radio frequency ranges accessible to consumer-grade equipment. The
expectation-of-privacy question for cellular calls was genuinely uncertain — a
defendant could argue that broadcasting calls over radio waves negated any reasonable
expectation of privacy.

More practically: requiring plaintiffs to prove confidentiality for every intercepted
cellular call created a fact-intensive inquiry that blunted CIPA enforcement for
mobile communications.

### DESIGN RESPONSE — 1992

Stats. 1992, Ch. 298 added §632.7:

**§632.7 eliminated the "confidential communication" requirement for cellular and
cordless calls.** The section prohibited intentional interception of communications
transmitted between:
- Two cellular radio telephones
- A cellular radio telephone and a landline
- A cordless telephone and a landline
- Two cordless telephones
- Any combination of the above

No showing of confidentiality was required — **any interception without consent of
all parties was prohibited, period.**

### SIGNIFICANCE FOR SIM SWAP CLAIMS

The elimination of the "confidential communication" requirement in §632.7 is critical
for SIM swap civil claims:

1. SIM swap intercepts cellular calls and SMS messages — §632.7 covers this
2. Plaintiff does not need to prove each intercepted call was "confidential"
3. The interception itself = violation; per-violation $5,000 minimum applies
4. This makes §632.7 the strongest CIPA provision for digital communications interception

### LOGICAL DELTA

| Element | §632 (1967, as amended) | §632.7 (1992) |
|---------|------------------------|----------------|
| Covered communications | Confidential communications via any device | All cellular/cordless communications |
| Confidentiality requirement | Required (§632(c) definition) | NOT required |
| Coverage trigger | Reasonable expectation of privacy | Communication type (cellular or cordless) |
| SIM swap applicability | Requires confidentiality proof | Automatically applies to cellular interception |

### PROVENANCE

Stats. 1992, Ch. 298
Added: Cal. Penal Code §632.7
