# Evolution Stage 02 — 1985 Amendment: Cordless and Cellular Telephones

### THE WOUND

By the mid-1980s, cordless phones and early cellular phones were commercially available.
The original 1967 CIPA language covered "telephone wire, line, cable, or instrument"
and "telegraph" — traditional wired communications. Wireless communications transmitted
over radio frequencies were not clearly covered.

A gap emerged: intercepting a cordless phone call (which broadcast over radio frequencies
within a short range of the base station) might not be a "wire" interception under
CIPA's original language. Early cellular calls, similarly, were transmitted by radio
frequency and potentially outside the "wire" framing of §631.

Law enforcement and intelligence agencies with radio scanners could listen to cordless
phone calls with equipment available at any electronics store. Cordless phone privacy
was nearly nonexistent.

### DESIGN RESPONSE — 1985

Stats. 1985, Ch. 1360 added two new sections:

**§632.5 — Cordless telephone interception:**
Prohibited intentional interception of communications transmitted between cordless
telephones and their base units, or between cordless telephones. Required consent of
all parties.

**§632.6 — Cellular telephone interception:**
Prohibited intentional interception of communications transmitted between cellular
radio telephones, between a cellular radio telephone and a landline telephone, or any
combination. Required consent of all parties.

**Significance:** The 1985 amendments extended CIPA's all-party consent requirement
to wireless communications technologies. However, both §632.5 and §632.6 were later
superseded by §632.7 (added 1992), which combined and clarified coverage.

### LOGICAL DELTA

| Communication Type | Pre-1985 Coverage | Post-1985 Coverage |
|-------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Wired telephone | §631 (prohibited) | §631 (unchanged) |
| Cordless telephone | Not covered (gap) | §632.5 (new coverage) |
| Cellular telephone | Not covered (gap) | §632.6 (new coverage) |
| All-party consent requirement | Only wired | Extended to wireless |

### PROVENANCE

Stats. 1985, Ch. 1360
Added: Cal. Penal Code §§632.5, 632.6
