# Evolution Stage 04 — 2016 Amendment: Electronic Communications Modernization

### THE WOUND

By 2016, the CIPA framework — enacted in 1967 and last substantively amended for
cellular coverage in 1992 — was again showing its age. Internet-based voice communications
(VoIP), SMS over data networks, and communications transmitted via hybrid cellular/data
paths were not clearly covered by the language of §632.7, which referred to "cellular
radio telephones" and "cordless telephones" — the dominant wireless technology of 1992.

Modern smartphones communicate through cellular data (4G/LTE) for voice calls, SMS,
and internet-based messaging. The "cellular radio telephone" framing was increasingly
inadequate for describing how communications actually traveled. Class action plaintiffs
had sued major technology companies under §632 for allegedly recording or monitoring
online chat and communication sessions, raising the question of how CIPA applied to
internet-based communications.

### DESIGN RESPONSE — 2016

Stats. 2016, Ch. 541 amended §632.7 to clarify and expand its scope:

**Modernized language:** The amendment updated §632.7 to ensure coverage of
communications "transmitted to or from a cellular radio telephone or a cordless
telephone" — clarifying that the interception can be at either end of the
communication (not just between two cellular devices).

**Coverage of hybrid communications:** The amendment addressed communications paths
that cross between cellular and other networks, ensuring coverage is not limited to
purely cellular-to-cellular calls.

**Context:** The 2016 amendment occurred in the midst of significant CIPA litigation
against technology companies regarding chat monitoring, website analytics, and session
recording. The amendment focused on the cellular provisions rather than resolving all
online communication questions.

### CURRENT OPERATIVE PROVISION — §632.7

As amended through 2016, §632.7 is the operative provision covering:
- All cellular phone calls (voice and data)
- SMS/text messages transmitted via cellular network
- Cordless phone calls
- Any combination of cellular/cordless/landline

**For SIM swap claims:** The 2016 amendment does not materially change the SIM swap
analysis — §632.7 has covered cellular interception since 1992. The amendment
strengthens coverage of hybrid communication paths.

### PROVENANCE

Stats. 2016, Ch. 541
Amended: Cal. Penal Code §632.7
Effective: January 1, 2017
