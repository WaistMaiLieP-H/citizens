# Statute Version at Time of Cel-Tech (1999)

**UCL version:** B&P §17200 as of 1999 (pre-Prop 64; "any person" standing still operative).

The Court interpreted the "unfair" prong of §17200 as it existed before Prop 64's 2004
standing amendments. The substantive three-prong structure that Cel-Tech interpreted was
not changed by Prop 64 — only the private plaintiff standing requirement changed.

**Current operative law:** Cel-Tech's "unfair" standard for the competitor context remains
the controlling California Supreme Court authority. For consumer cases, many courts apply a
different (FTC-based) test, but Cel-Tech is the baseline from which consumer-case analysis
begins. No subsequent California Supreme Court case has overruled or significantly limited
Cel-Tech's substantive holdings.
