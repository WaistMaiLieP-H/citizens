# Statute Version at Time of Korea Supply (2003)

**UCL version:** B&P §17203 as of 2003 (pre-Prop 64; standing was "any person").

**Text of §17203:** "Any person who engages, has engaged, or proposes to engage in unfair
competition may be enjoined in any court of competent jurisdiction. The court may make such
orders or judgments, including the appointment of a receiver, as may be necessary to prevent
the use or employment by any person of any practice which constitutes unfair competition, as
defined in this chapter, or as may be necessary to restore to any person in interest any money
or property, real or personal, which may have been acquired by means of such unfair competition."

**"Restore... any money or property... acquired by means of such unfair competition":**
The Court interpreted "restore" as limited to money/property taken from the plaintiff — return
of unjust enrichment at the plaintiff's expense. "Acquired" does not include all profits from
all third parties.

**Current operative law:** Korea Supply remains controlling. The 2004 Prop 64 amendments did
not change §17203's remedy language. Korea Supply is consistently applied by California courts
to limit UCL monetary relief to true restitution from this plaintiff, not industrywide disgorgement.
