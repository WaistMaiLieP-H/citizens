# Statute Version at Time of Kwikset v. Superior Court (2011)

**UCL version:** B&P §§17200, 17204 as amended by Proposition 64 (November 2, 2004).

**Text of §17204 post-Prop 64:** "Actions for relief pursuant to this chapter shall be
prosecuted exclusively in a court of competent jurisdiction by the Attorney General or any
district attorney or by any county counsel... or by a city attorney... or by a city prosecutor
... or by a person who has suffered injury in fact and has lost money or property as a result
of the unfair competition."

**The Court's interpretation of "lost money or property":** The Court gave this phrase broad
scope — "injury in fact" and "lost money or property" are related but distinct; a plaintiff
need only show one concrete financial loss flowing from the defendant's conduct. The standard
does not require quantification of the loss at the pleading stage; it requires only a concrete
economic injury, not a specific dollar amount.

**Current operative law:** Kwikset is the controlling California Supreme Court interpretation
of Prop 64 standing. It remains good law. No subsequent California Supreme Court case has
narrowed or overruled it.
