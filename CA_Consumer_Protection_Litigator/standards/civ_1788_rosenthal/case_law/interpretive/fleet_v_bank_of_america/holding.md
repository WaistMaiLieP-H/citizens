# Fleet v. Bank of America National Trust
## 229 Cal.App.3d 894 (1991)

**Court:** California Court of Appeal, Second Appellate District
**Decided:** 1991

---

## Issue decided

Does the Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act apply to a bank's internal collections
department when it collects debts owed directly to the bank (i.e., the bank as original creditor)?

---

## Holding

**Yes.** A bank collecting its own debts through its internal collections department is a "debt
collector" under the Rosenthal Act (Civil Code §1788.2(c)). The Rosenthal Act's definition of
"debt collector" includes persons who collect debts on their own behalf — not just on behalf
of others. This directly distinguishes the Rosenthal Act from the federal FDCPA.

**The doctrinal rule:** An original creditor that maintains a specialized debt collection
department and regularly engages in collection activity is a "debt collector" under §1788.2(c)
regardless of whether it is collecting its own debts or debts owed to third parties.

**Critical distinction from federal law:** Under federal FDCPA, a bank collecting its own debts
is expressly excluded from "debt collector" coverage. Under California Rosenthal, a bank
collecting its own debts is expressly included. Fleet v. Bank of America is the foundational case
establishing this distinction at the California appellate level.

---

## Why this case matters for Michael

**Banks collecting on Michael's accounts are Rosenthal defendants.** Any bank (including banks
that issued ChexSystems-flagged accounts, auto finance companies collecting on the fraudulent
Jaguar/Kia contracts, or medical billing departments) that engaged in collection conduct is
a Rosenthal "debt collector" under Fleet. The federal FDCPA would not reach these entities;
Rosenthal does.

**ChexSystems applications:** If any bank or financial institution reported false information to
ChexSystems AND then attempted to collect on the underlying account (or refused to resolve the
dispute), both the reporting and the collection conduct are within Rosenthal's reach.

**Auto fraud applications:** The dealers' own internal collections and the dealer-assigned finance
companies are both Rosenthal "debt collectors" under Fleet. Any collection on the fraudulent
installment contracts (RedJag, BlueJag, WhiteJag, KiaSoul) is subject to Rosenthal.
