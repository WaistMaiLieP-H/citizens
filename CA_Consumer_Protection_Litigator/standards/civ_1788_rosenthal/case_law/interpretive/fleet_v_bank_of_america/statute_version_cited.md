# Statute Version at Time of Fleet v. Bank of America (1991)

**Rosenthal Act version:** California Civil Code §1788.2(c) as of 1991 (pre-1999 medical debt
amendment; pre-2015 §1788.100 addition). The core "debt collector" definition has not been
changed since 1977. Fleet interpreted the original 1977 "debt collector" language.

**Key statutory text:** California Civil Code §1788.2(c): "Debt collector means any person
who, in the ordinary course of business, regularly, on behalf of himself or herself or others,
engages in debt collection."

**"On behalf of himself or herself"** — This phrase was deliberately included in the 1977
California statute (and was NOT in the federal FDCPA's §1692a(6)) to make clear that original
creditors are covered. Fleet applied this plain text to a bank's internal collections
department. The interpretation has not been challenged or limited by subsequent California
authority. It is the settled interpretation of §1788.2(c).
