# Statute Version at Time of Gutierrez v. Barclays Group (2011)

**Rosenthal Act version:** California Civil Code §1788.30(b) as of 2011 (unchanged since
1977 enactment — the damages provision has not been amended).

**Text of §1788.30(b):** "In the case of any action by an individual, such additional damages
as the court may allow."

**Interpretation history:** California courts and federal courts applying California law have
consistently interpreted "such additional damages" to include punitive damages when the conduct
meets the §3294 standard. There is no contrary California Supreme Court authority limiting
§1788.30(b) to non-punitive damages. Gutierrez reflects this consensus.

**Civil Code §3294 (punitive damages):** Requires by clear and convincing evidence that
defendant is guilty of oppression, fraud, or malice. §3294(b) limits employer liability for
punitive damages to acts of officers, directors, or managing agents. For corporations (dealers,
banks), must show a managing agent authorized or ratified the abusive collection conduct.

**Current operative law:** The §1788.30(b) + §3294 punitive damages framework remains
settled. The 2022 AB 35 amendment to §3294 increased the standard to clear and convincing
evidence (already required). No amendment has narrowed §1788.30(b)'s scope.
