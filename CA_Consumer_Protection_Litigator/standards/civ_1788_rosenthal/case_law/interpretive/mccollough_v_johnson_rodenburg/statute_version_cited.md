# Statute Version at Time of McCollough v. Johnson, Rodenburg (2011)

**FDCPA version:** 15 USC §§1692e(2)(A) and 1692e(10) as of 2011 (post-Dodd-Frank 2010,
but Dodd-Frank did not alter these sections).

**Rosenthal Act version:** California Civil Code §1788.17 as of 2011. §1788.17's incorporation
of federal §§1692b–1692j means the §1692e prohibitions also apply in state court.

**Core statutory text at issue:**
- §1692e(2)(A): "A debt collector may not use any false, deceptive, or misleading representation
  or means in connection with the collection of any debt. Without limiting the general application
  of the foregoing, the following conduct is a violation of this section: (2) The false
  representation of — (A) the character, amount, or legal status of any debt..."
- §1692e(10): "The use of any false representation or deceptive means to collect or attempt to
  collect any debt or to obtain information concerning a consumer."

**Current operative law:** McCollough remains authoritative in the Ninth Circuit. Collection
litigation misconduct (filing without documentation, false affidavits, false debt character
representations) is established FDCPA / Rosenthal violation territory.
