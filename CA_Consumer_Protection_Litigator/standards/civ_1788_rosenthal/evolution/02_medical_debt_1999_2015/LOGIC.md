# Rosenthal Act — Evolution Stage 02: Medical Debt Extension (1999 + 2015)

**Key amendments:**
- Stats. 1999, Ch. 319 — Medical debt collectors expressly covered
- Stats. 2015, Ch. 418 (AB 1521) — §1788.100 added: medical debt collector specific requirements

---

## The wound this stage answered

By the late 1990s, medical debt had become a dominant source of consumer financial distress.
Medical debt collectors (hospital billing departments, medical collection agencies) were
engaging in abusive practices targeting patients who had recently received medical care —
calling repeatedly, threatening credit reporting, failing to disclose that charity care or
Medi-Cal might cover the debt. The Rosenthal Act's coverage was technically broad enough to
cover medical debt collection, but application was inconsistent.

The 1999 amendment clarified that medical debt collectors are covered. The 2015 amendment
(§1788.100) added specific affirmative requirements for medical debt collectors, including:
- Required disclosure of charity care and financial assistance programs before dunning
- Prohibition on credit reporting for 150 days following the first bill
- Required reasonable payment plan offers
- Specific consumer rights disclosures in medical collection communications

## Why this stage matters for Michael

**Medical debt in Michael's portfolio:** Multiple medical cases involve billing disputes and
potential collection activity — Dr. Wiita, Muir Ortho, Blue Shield denials, spine surgery
billing, SIRVA treatment billing. Any medical debt collection on these accounts:
- Is covered by Rosenthal Act (original creditor: hospital or medical group directly)
- If assigned to collection agency: FDCPA also applies
- 150-day credit reporting prohibition (§1788.100) if applicable
- Charity care disclosure requirement (§1788.100) — Blue Shield bad faith denials may implicate

**Compound with Medical Malpractice Citizen:** The collection of fraudulent medical billings
(e.g., billing for the failed spine surgery that Michael did not meaningfully consent to, or
billing for SIRVA treatment following negligent vaccine administration) is a compound claim:
CA_Medical_Malpractice_Litigator handles the malpractice/SIRVA claim; CA_Consumer_Protection_
Litigator's Rosenthal standard handles the abusive collection of the resulting bills.
