# Statute Version Cited — Daugherty v. American Honda (2006)

The Daugherty court applied the CLRA as it read in 2006 (post-Prop 64, 2004). Relevant provisions:

- **§1770(a)(5)** — misrepresentation of characteristics
- **§1770(a)(7)** — misrepresentation of standard or quality
- **§1770(a)(9)** — advertising without intent to sell as advertised

The court held these subsections do not reach silent non-disclosure absent a duty to speak.

**Evolution stage applicable:** post-Prop 64 CLRA text, pre-2009 housing-lending amendments.

**Version cited by the court:** Cal. Civ. Code §1770 as in force November 2006.
