# Statute Version Cited — Broughton v. CIGNA (1999)

The Broughton court applied the CLRA as it read in 1999. The relevant provisions were:

- **§1780(a)(2)** — injunctive relief authorized as a CLRA remedy
- **§1782** — notice-and-cure provision (inapplicable to injunctive relief under §1782(c))
- **§1770** — the enumerated list of deceptive practices alleged

The 1999 version of §1770 contained subdivisions (a)(1) through (a)(18), substantially similar to the current text but without the post-2000 additions for senior citizen protection, telecom regulation, and home-solicitation lending.

**Evolution stage applicable:** 01_origin_1970 + intervening amendments through 1998 (no material change to §1780(a)(2) or §1782(c) between 1970 and 1999).

**Version cited by the court:** Cal. Civ. Code §§1770, 1780, 1782 as in force December 1999 (pre-Prop 64 / UCL amendments).
