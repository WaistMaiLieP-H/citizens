CLRA STANDING UNDER KWIKSET:
Kwikset was decided under both the UCL and the CLRA. The CLRA's standing requirement (Civil Code § 1780(a) — "Any consumer who suffers any damage as a result of the use or employment by any person of a method, act, or practice declared to be unlawful") is interpreted in parallel with the UCL § 17204 "lost money or property" test.

The economic injury standard is essentially identical: a consumer who paid money based on a fraudulent misrepresentation, and would not have paid that amount (or any amount) absent the misrepresentation, has suffered "damage as a result of" the CLRA violation.

CLRA REMEDIES BEYOND UCL:
Unlike the UCL (which provides only restitution and injunctive relief per Korea Supply), the CLRA provides:
- Actual damages (§ 1780(a)(1));
- Restitution of property (§ 1780(a)(2));
- Punitive damages (§ 1780(a)(4)) — requires intentional violation;
- Injunctive relief (§ 1780(a)(3));
- Attorneys' fees (§ 1780(e) — mandatory for prevailing plaintiff in individual actions; discretionary for class actions).
