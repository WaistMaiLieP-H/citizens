# Statute Version Cited — Kwikset v. Superior Court (2011)

The Kwikset court applied the CLRA and UCL as they read in 2011, post-Prop 64 (2004). The relevant CLRA provisions:

- **§1780(a)** — "any consumer who suffers any damage as a result of … may bring an action" — the standing gateway
- **§1770(a)(4)** — deceptive representation of geographic origin (the "Made in U.S.A." subsection)
- **§1770(a)(5)** — misrepresentation of characteristics

**Evolution stage applicable:** post-Prop 64 (November 2004) CLRA text. The Prop 64 amendment affected UCL §17204 directly; CLRA §1780 was amended by later legislation to mirror the UCL standing language for consistency.

**Version cited by the court:** Cal. Civ. Code §§1770, 1780 as in force January 2011.
