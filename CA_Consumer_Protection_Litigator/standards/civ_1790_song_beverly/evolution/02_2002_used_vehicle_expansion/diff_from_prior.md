# Song-Beverly — Diff: Pre-2002 vs. Post-2002 (Used Vehicle Expansion)

| Dimension | Original (1971-2001) | After AB 2331 (2002+) |
|---|---|---|
| New consumer goods | Full coverage | Full coverage (unchanged) |
| Used vehicles with express warranty | NOT covered | COVERED — replacement/refund right during warranty period |
| Used vehicles without warranty | NOT covered | NOT covered (implied warranty 30-60 days, or "as is" with disclosure) |
| Tanner presumption | New vehicles only | New + used with express warranty |
| Express warranty duration | N/A for used | Coverage equals express warranty term |
| Civil penalty (§1794(c)) | New vehicles | New + used with express warranty |
| Attorney fees (§1794(d)) | New vehicles | New + used with express warranty |

## What did NOT change

1. Prohibition on implied warranty disclaimer (§1790.1) — still void for consumer goods
2. Manufacturer service/repair obligation (§1793.2(a)) — still applies
3. Mileage offset calculation — still applies on refund (reduced by pre-defect use)
4. Willfulness standard for civil penalty — still from pre-2002 case law
5. CLRA/UCL/Rees-Levering interaction — unchanged
