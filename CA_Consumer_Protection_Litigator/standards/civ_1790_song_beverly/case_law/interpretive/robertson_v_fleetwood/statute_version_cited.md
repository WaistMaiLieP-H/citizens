# Statute Version at Time of Robertson (2006)

**§1793.2(d) version:** Post-Tanner Act (1994). Text of operative provision:
"(1) Except as provided in paragraph (2), if the manufacturer or its representative in this state
is unable to service or repair a new motor vehicle to conform to the applicable express warranty
after a reasonable number of attempts, the manufacturer shall either promptly replace the new motor
vehicle in accordance with subparagraph (A) or promptly make restitution to the buyer in accordance
with subparagraph (B)."

**§1793.22 (Tanner Act) version:** As enacted 1994. The rebuttable presumption (4 attempts or 30
days) was in effect at time of Robertson.

**Current operative text:** §1793.2(d) text is substantively unchanged. Robertson's cumulative
defects rule and substantial impairment standard are currently operative.
