# Statute Version Cited — Ford Motor Credit Co. v. Milhollin

**Case decided:** 1980 (before the 1980 TILA Simplification Act took effect)
**Statute version applicable:** 15 U.S.C. §1638 (original 1968 version as amended through 1980)

## The Provision at Issue

The court addressed disclosures required under TILA §1638 (codifying the original Truth
in Lending Act §128). The specific disclosures at issue were what information had to be
included in an installment sales contract for an auto loan.

## Post-1980 Updates

The Truth in Lending Simplification Act (Pub. L. 96-221, 1980) amended §1638 shortly
after this decision. The current §1638 reflects the simplified disclosure requirements
from the 1980 Act and subsequent amendments.

## Current Rule (for Jaguar XE Analysis)

The current required disclosures are in 15 U.S.C. §1638(a)(1)-(19) as amended through
Dodd-Frank (2010). The implementing regulation is 12 C.F.R. §1026.18 (CFPB Regulation Z).

Milhollin's core holding — that the agency's implementing regulations and commentary
are the authoritative source for specific disclosure requirements — remains applicable
under the current CFPB framework (subject to Loper Bright's limits on mandatory deference).
