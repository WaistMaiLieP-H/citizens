# Statute Version at Time of Lazar (1996)

**§1710(4) version:** As enacted 1872. Text:
"A promise, made without any intention of performing it."

**§1709 version:** As enacted 1872. Text:
"One who willfully deceives another with intent to induce him to alter his position to his injury
or risk, is liable for any damage which he thereby suffers."

**Current operative text:** Both sections are unchanged from 1872. Lazar's promissory fraud
framework — promise + no intent to perform at time of making — is the currently operative
standard for §1710(4) claims in California. No legislative amendment has modified this standard.
