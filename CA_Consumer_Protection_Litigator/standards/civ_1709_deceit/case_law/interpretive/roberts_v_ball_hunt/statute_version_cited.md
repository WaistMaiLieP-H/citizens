# Statute Version at Time of Roberts (1976)

**§1710 version:** As enacted 1872. Text of §1710(2):
"The assertion, as a fact, of that which is not true, by one who has no reasonable ground for
believing it to be true."

**Current operative text:** §1710(2) text is unchanged from 1872. Roberts' interpretation —
that §1710(2) requires no knowledge of falsity, only absence of reasonable grounds — is
currently operative and is the standard definition of negligent misrepresentation in California.
