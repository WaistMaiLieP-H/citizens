# WIC § 5325 — Case Law Anchors

**Layer:** 4 — Case Law
**Date:** 2026-04-15

---

## *Riese v. St. Mary's Hospital & Medical Center* (1987) 209 Cal.App.3d 1303

Leading California case on patient medication-refusal rights within the LPS scheme. Held that LPS patients retain the right to refuse antipsychotic medication absent an emergency or a judicial finding of incapacity. Read together with § 5325, *Riese* clarifies that:
- Medication refusal operates alongside § 5325's enumerated rights, not as a substitute;
- § 5325(f) and (g) rights (convulsive treatment, psychosurgery) are absolute and not subject to *Riese*-style incapacity override.

## *Conservatorship of Wendland* (2001) 26 Cal.4th 519

California Supreme Court on conservator authority over medical decisions. Confirms that conservator authority is bounded by statute and does not extend beyond what the Legislature has explicitly granted. Supports § 5325's non-waiver clause — a conservator cannot reach rights the Legislature has placed outside conservator authority.

## *Thor v. Superior Court* (1993) 5 Cal.4th 725

Confirms the fundamental right to refuse medical treatment under the California Constitution, even for incarcerated persons. By analogy, reinforces the absolute nature of § 5325(f), (g).

## *Welfare Rights Organization v. Crisan* (1983) 33 Cal.3d 766

California Supreme Court case on LPS patient rights generally; supports strict construction of procedural protections for involuntary commitment.
