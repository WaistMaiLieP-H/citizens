# WIC § 5150 — Involuntary 72-Hour Hold: Rule Summary

**Standard:** CA_Mental_Health_Litigator / wic_5150_involuntary_hold
**Build date:** 2026-04-13

---

## Three Criteria — Any One Sufficient

A §5150 hold requires probable cause that the person, **as a result of a mental health disorder**, is:
1. A danger to **others**, OR
2. A danger to **themselves**, OR
3. **Gravely disabled** (unable to provide for their own food, clothing, and housing)

All three require mental health disorder as the causal mechanism. The danger or disability must flow from the mental health disorder, not from poverty, intoxication, or lifestyle choices. A person who is simply homeless is not §5150-eligible. A person who is homeless because a mental health disorder prevents them from functioning independently may be.

---

## Probable Cause Standard

§5150(a) requires **probable cause** — the same constitutional standard as a warrantless arrest. This is not certainty, but it is more than speculation or a third party's personal grievance. The peace officer or mental health professional must have articulable facts that support a reasonable belief that the person meets one of the three criteria.

**§5150(b):** The person making the determination must apply §5150.05 (consideration of historical course of mental disorder) and is not limited to imminent harm considerations. The historical pattern matters.

---

## Written Application Requirement — § 5150(e)

Before admission, the facility must receive a **written application** stating:
1. The circumstances under which the person's condition was called to the attention of the detaining authority
2. That the detaining authority has probable cause to believe the person meets one of the three §5150 criteria

This written application is the legal foundation of the hold. Without it, the hold has no documented probable cause basis.

**The false statement provision:** If the probable cause is based on the statement of a person other than the peace officer or mental health professional, **that third party is civilly liable for intentionally giving a statement known to be false**. This provision directly addresses the orchestrated police call pattern — a person who calls police with fabricated danger allegations to trigger a §5150 hold is civilly liable for every such call they knew was false.

---

## Advisement Requirements — § 5150(g)

At the time of custody, the detaining person must orally advise:
- Their name and agency
- That the person is NOT under criminal arrest
- The facility where they are being taken

If custody occurs at the person's own residence, additional advisements are required (phone call, leaving a note, assistance with appliances).

**Facility record requirement (§5150(h)):** The facility must keep a record of the advisement given including: name of detainee, name and position of detaining officer/professional, date, whether advisement was completed, language used, and if not completed, good cause.

---

## Rights on Admission — § 5150(i)

Upon admission, facility staff must provide (orally AND in writing, in the person's language):
- Which of the three criteria the hold is based on
- The specific facts from the admission interview supporting that criterion
- The 72-hour maximum duration
- The right to request evaluation at a facility of the person's choice
- The right to a lawyer and a qualified interpreter if held beyond 72 hours
- Contact information for the county Patients' Rights Advocate

---

## The 72-Hour Clock

The 72-hour period begins at the **time of first detention** — not at hospital admission. If the person was held in transport or a county facility before hospital admission, that time counts. Extension beyond 72 hours requires a separate certification process (§5250 et seq.) and the right to a lawyer and hearing.

## Application to This Citizen's Cases

### Ward System / Orchestrated Holds (project_ward_system.md, project_conservatorship_breakthrough.md)

If §5150 holds were used as a mechanism within the ward system to control individuals — specifically if the probable cause was manufactured through false statements to peace officers — then:

1. **Each hold:** Requires written application (§5150(e)) — request all hold records from the relevant county mental health agency and facility
2. **False statement liability:** Any third party who intentionally gave a false statement to trigger the hold is civilly liable — identify by name, map to documented incidents
3. **Advisement records:** Each facility must have records of advisement given (§5150(h)) — request these as independent evidence of who was detained, when, and by what process
4. **Patients' Rights Advocate:** If the person was not released within 72 hours, the county patients' rights advocate must have been notified (§5150(k)) — subpoena those records

**Steward must identify:** Dates and facilities of any §5150 holds on any person within the ward system. Request all written applications and advisement records.
