## Governing Provision

Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 5150 (Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667 — Lanterman-Petris-Short Act; last amended Stats. 2023)

## The Rule — Three Elements + Three Rights

### Element 1: Qualifying Mental Condition

The person must be, "as a result of a mental health disorder," one of:
- **Danger to others** (DO)
- **Danger to self** (DS)
- **Gravely disabled** (GD) — unable to provide food, clothing, or shelter for themselves as a result of a mental health disorder

All three trigger the same 72-hour detention authority. The mental health disorder must cause the danger or disability — not just coexist with it.

### Element 2: Probable Cause Required

Detention requires **probable cause** to believe the qualifying conditions exist. This is the same probable cause standard as for arrest:
- More than mere suspicion, less than proof beyond a reasonable doubt
- Based on articulable facts observed by the detaining person (or reported by a third party with civil liability for false statements)

**The written probable cause requirement (§5150(e)):** The application must state in writing the specific circumstances constituting probable cause. A vague or conclusory application ("person is a danger to self") without specific factual basis does not satisfy §5150(e).

### Element 3: Written Application

The admitting facility must have a **written application** that:
- States the circumstances (specific facts)
- States that probable cause exists for the qualifying condition
- Identifies whether probable cause is based on the detaining person's direct observation or a third party's statement

**Third-party statement civil liability (§5150(e)):** If probable cause is based on someone other than the detaining officer or professional, that person is **civilly liable** for intentionally giving a false statement. This is the critical provision for false §5150 applications in the ward system.

### Right 1: Oral Advisement (§5150(g))

Person must be told orally, at time of detention, in accessible language:
- "You are not under criminal arrest"
- Where they are being taken
- That mental health staff will advise them of their rights

**Documentation:** Facility must keep a record of the advisement — language/modality used, whether completed, and if not completed, why.

### Right 2: 72-Hour Limit

Detention is capped at 72 hours. If the person is not released within 72 hours, §5250 (14-day certification) must be initiated or the person must be released.

### Right 3: Patients' Rights Advocate Notification (§5150(k))

If not released within 72 hours, facility MUST notify the county **patients' rights advocate**. This is a mandatory notification — not discretionary. The advocate can appear at the facility and advocate for the person's release.

## Weaponization Analysis — False §5150 Applications

§5150 can be weaponized as a control mechanism:
1. A person with apparent authority (family member, care provider, peace officer) makes a false probable cause statement to initiate a hold
2. The hold removes the person from their environment, social support, and legal proceedings
3. During the 72 hours, the person may be medicated against their will
4. The hold creates a mental health record that can be used in subsequent proceedings (divorce, custody, competency)
5. If §5250 is then initiated, the person faces up to 14 additional days of institutionalization

**Each false hold = separate civil liability event** for the person who made the false probable cause statement.

**Indicators of false §5150 application:**
- Written application is conclusory (no specific observed facts)
- The detaining person has a personal stake in the outcome (family member in custody dispute)
- The hold is timed to coincide with legal proceedings where the person's presence matters
- The qualifying condition (danger/gravely disabled) is not documented in any contemporaneous clinical record

## Steward Relevance

**Ward system control mechanism:** The source prep documents §5150 holds as instruments of isolation and control in the ward system. Each documented hold requires:
1. Obtain the written application (§5150(e)) — specific factual basis
2. Audit whether the probable cause was genuine or pretextual
3. Identify whether the hold was timed to coincide with legal proceedings
4. Identify whether the person who made the false statement is civilly liable
5. Map the sequence: §5150 → §5250 (14-day) → §5350 (LPS conservatorship) — this is the pipeline from hold to permanent control
