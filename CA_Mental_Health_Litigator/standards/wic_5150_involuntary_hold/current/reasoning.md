# WIC § 5150 — Involuntary Hold — Reasoning

**Standard ID:** `wic_5150_involuntary_hold`
**Layer:** 2 — Reasoning
**Date:** 2026-04-12

## The LPS Act and Its Original Purpose

§5150 was enacted as part of the Lanterman-Petris-Short (LPS) Act in 1967. The LPS Act was a landmark reform that ended the era of indefinite civil commitment — prior to LPS, California could commit a person to a state mental hospital indefinitely based on a vague "gravely disabled" standard with no judicial review and no time limits.

The LPS Act replaced this with a structured, time-limited system: 72-hour hold (§5150), then 14-day certification (§5250), then 30-day or 180-day additional commitment (§§5270, 5300), then LPS conservatorship (§5350) — each step requiring additional findings and judicial review. The structure was designed to protect civil liberties while providing care for genuinely dangerous or disabled persons.

## The False Statement Civil Liability as the Accountability Mechanism

§5150(e)'s civil liability provision for intentionally false probable cause statements is the only direct civil accountability mechanism within §5150 itself. Unlike the criminal system where false police reports are crimes (Pen. Code §148.5), the LPS system's false-statement accountability is civil — the person who made the false statement is liable in a civil action.

This is the provision that makes each false hold an independently actionable event. If the source prep documents multiple holds in the ward system, each one where probable cause was false and intentionally so creates a separate civil liability claim against the person who made the false statement. Multiply: five false holds × civil damages per hold = significant aggregate exposure.

## The §5150 → §5250 → §5350 Pipeline as a Control Architecture

The critical insight for the ward system analysis: §5150 is not just a standalone hold — it is the entry point to an escalating commitment architecture. Once a §5150 hold is initiated:
- Person is institutionalized and isolated from their support network
- Clinical staff have 72 hours to document "continued danger" — which is easier to document if the person is agitated by the wrongful detention
- §5250 certification then continues the hold for 14 more days
- If the person cannot demonstrate they can survive safely with available support, §5350 LPS conservatorship proceedings begin

This pipeline, once entered falsely, can keep a person institutionalized for months or years. Each stage requires its own statutory compliance audit — but the audit starts at §5150(e)'s probable cause statement, because that is where the false premise entered the system.

## The Patients' Rights Advocate as the Immediate Remedy

§5150(k)'s mandatory notification to the county patients' rights advocate is the built-in emergency exit. Advocates can:
- Appear at the facility
- Review the application and clinical record
- Advocate for release
- Initiate a certification review hearing (§5256 et seq.)
- Refer to legal counsel

For false holds, the patients' rights advocate notification is the first line of defense. Failure to notify the advocate (a mandatory obligation) is itself a separate statutory violation.
