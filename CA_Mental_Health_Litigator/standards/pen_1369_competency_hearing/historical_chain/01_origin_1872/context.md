## Origin: Stats. 1872; Post-Dusky Reform

§1369 was part of the 1872 Penal Code. The post-Dusky (1960) and post-Drope (1975) amendments modernized the process to include the specific Dusky two-prong language in §1369(b)(1)(B).

**The DSH Guidelines Requirement — Stats. 2016:** The §1369(d) training guidelines provision was added in 2016, following documented cases of unqualified evaluators conducting incompetent competency evaluations. The Legislature acted after reviewing cases where general clinicians without forensic training produced reports that were legally insufficient and clinically unreliable. The 2016 amendment created the framework for minimum standards — which DSH adopted by July 2017.

**Significance for Dr. Wiita case:** The DSH guidelines were in effect at the time of any post-2017 evaluation. Any appointment that did not verify compliance with those guidelines — or document equivalent experience — violated §1369(d).
