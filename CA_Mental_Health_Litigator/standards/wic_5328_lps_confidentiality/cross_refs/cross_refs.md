# WIC § 5328 — Cross-References

**Layer:** 5 — Cross-References
**Date:** 2026-04-15

---

## Within CA_Mental_Health_Litigator

- `wic_5150_involuntary_hold`, `wic_5250_14_day_certification`, `wic_5270_15_additional_30_day` — all records generated during these confinements are § 5328-confidential.
- `wic_5325_patients_rights` — correspondence (e), telephone (d), and advocate access (h) rights are privacy companions to § 5328.
- `evid_1016_psychotherapist_privilege` — overlapping but distinct protection; § 5328 governs records, Evid. § 1014 governs testimonial privilege.

## Federal Parallel

- **42 U.S.C. § 290dd-2** (`usc_42_290dd_2_substance_abuse_confidentiality`) — federal substance-abuse confidentiality. Stricter than § 5328 for federally-assisted SUD programs. Dual-regime analysis required when records include SUD treatment.
- **42 C.F.R. Part 2** — implements § 290dd-2.

## External Citizens

- **CA_Medical_Privacy_Officer** — CMIA (Civ. §§ 56.05 et seq.) governs medical records generally; § 5328 governs LPS/DD records specifically. Both regimes apply to overlapping records; the stricter standard controls.
- **CA_Family_Law_Litigator** — § 5328 is the admissibility gate for LPS records in custody, DVRO, dissolution proceedings. Every LPS reference in the family-law audit must trace to a § 5328 exception.
- **CA_Forensic_Document_Specialist** — Dr. Wiita record chain-of-custody analysis is incomplete without the § 5328 disclosure-chain audit.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — state-actor unauthorized disclosure of LPS records = § 1983 privacy claim, often paired with *Whalen v. Roe* federal constitutional privacy analysis.
- **HERALD** — witness authentication of disclosure logs, release-of-information forms, subpoena compliance records.

## HIPAA Harmonization Note

§ 5328(a)(25) and (a)(27) expressly reference the HIPAA framework. When § 5328 and HIPAA diverge, the stricter rule applies. California's rule is generally stricter; exceptions exist for some federal-program disclosures.
