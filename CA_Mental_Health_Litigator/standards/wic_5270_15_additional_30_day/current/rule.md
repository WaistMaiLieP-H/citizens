# WIC § 5270.15 — Additional 30-Day Intensive Treatment (Gravely Disabled) — Rule

**Standard ID:** `wic_5270_15_additional_30_day`
**Citizen:** CA_Mental_Health_Litigator
**Layer:** 1 — Current Operative Rule
**Date:** 2026-04-15

---

## Governing Provision

Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code § 5270.15 (Article 4.5 — Additional Intensive Treatment of Gravely Disabled Persons; county-opt-in)

## The Rule — Two Cumulative Conditions Plus Procedural Protections

Upon completion of a § 5250 14-day IT window, a gravely-disabled person may be certified for **up to 30 additional days** of intensive treatment **only if both**:

**(a)(1) Continued grave-disability finding.** The facility's professional staff has found the person *remains* gravely disabled as a result of a mental disorder or impairment by chronic alcoholism.

**(a)(2) Continued refusal/incapacity.** The person remains unwilling or unable to accept treatment voluntarily.

## Mandatory Procedural Protections

- **(b) Certification review hearing** under § 5256 — unless judicial review has been requested under § 5275. The hearing is mandatory, not discretionary, whenever the facility wants an additional 30 days.
- **(b)(1) 36-hour family notice.** The facility must make reasonable attempts to notify family members or any other person designated by the patient at least 36 hours before the certification review hearing — unless the patient requests otherwise. The patient must be *advised* of the right to suppress that notification. This is a patient-choice provision; suppression must be the patient's choice, not the facility's default.
- **(a)(2)** (subdivision (b)(2)): the professional staff must analyze the person's condition **at intervals not to exceed 10 days** during the 30-day period, and must **daily** monitor the treatment plan and progress. Early termination is under § 5270.35.

## Scope Limit — Gravely-Disabled Only

§ 5270.15 is only available for the **gravely-disabled** ground. It is **not** an extension pathway for "danger-to-self" or "danger-to-others" findings — those must proceed under § 5300 (180-day post-certification for dangerousness). A facility that uses § 5270.15 to continue a dangerousness-only hold is exceeding its statutory authority.

## County Opt-In Status

Article 4.5 (§§ 5270.10–5270.65) is only operative in counties that have adopted it. In non-opt-in counties, the pathway from § 5250 for gravely-disabled persons goes directly to § 5350 LPS conservatorship proceedings, not through § 5270.15. Before citing § 5270.15 for a case, confirm the relevant county has adopted Article 4.5.

## What Must Exist on the Record

1. A valid § 5250 predicate (see `wic_5250_14_day_certification`).
2. An **independent** continued-grave-disability finding — not a re-statement of the § 5250 finding.
3. Documentation of continued refusal or incapacity.
4. A § 5256 certification review hearing record (with documented 36-hour notice attempts).
5. Interval-review notes (every 10 days) and daily treatment-plan monitoring.
6. County-adoption proof (if challenged, the facility must be able to show Article 4.5 applies).

## Steward Relevance — Why § 5270.15 Matters to the IT Record

Every "IT" event in the ward record that exceeds 14 days is either a § 5270.15 event (gravely-disabled extension), a § 5300 event (dangerousness extension), or a step toward § 5350 conservatorship. § 5270.15 is the stage where the gravely-disabled label solidifies. Each 30-day certification requires the full procedural stack above; defects (missing interval reviews, missing daily monitoring, suppressed family notice without patient request) are preservable challenges at the § 5256 hearing and collaterally attackable thereafter.
