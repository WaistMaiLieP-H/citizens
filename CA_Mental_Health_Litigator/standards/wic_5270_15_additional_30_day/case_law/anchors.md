**Layer:** 4 — Case Law
**Date:** 2026-04-15

## *Conservatorship of Roulet* (1979) 23 Cal.3d 219

Same liberty-interest reasoning controls — each LPS gate implicates criminal-equivalent deprivation and requires heightened standards. Cite for procedural strictness at § 5270.15 certification review.

## *Conservatorship of Walker* (1987) 196 Cal.App.3d 1082

Court of Appeal addressed the application of LPS procedural protections during the intensive-treatment period. Supports strict reading of procedural steps required before any extension of involuntary treatment.

## *Riese v. St. Mary's Hospital* (1987) 209 Cal.App.3d 1303

Medication-refusal rights continue during any LPS certification including § 5270.15. A 30-day extension is not a medication-consent event.
