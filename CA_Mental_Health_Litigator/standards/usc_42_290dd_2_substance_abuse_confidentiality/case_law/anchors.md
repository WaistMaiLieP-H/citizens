## *United States v. Eide* (9th Cir. 1989) 875 F.2d 1429

Ninth Circuit on the scope of Part 2's use-restriction in federal criminal proceedings. Cite for the proposition that Part 2 records cannot be used to investigate or charge patients absent the heightened court-order pathway.

## *Ellison v. Cocke County* (6th Cir. 1995) 63 F.3d 467

Sixth Circuit addressing Part 2 confidentiality in a § 1983 context. Supports federal civil-rights liability theory for state-actor unauthorized disclosure of SUD records.

## *In re Grand Jury Subpoena (Tharpe)* — various circuit decisions

Federal circuit-level decisions interpreting what qualifies as an "appropriate order" under § 290dd-2(b)(2)(C) — consistently narrow, requiring actual in-camera review and specific findings, not boilerplate subpoena language.
