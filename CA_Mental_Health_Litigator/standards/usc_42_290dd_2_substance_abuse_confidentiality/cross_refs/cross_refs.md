# 42 U.S.C. § 290dd-2 + 42 C.F.R. Part 2 — Cross-References

**Layer:** 5 — Cross-References
**Date:** 2026-04-15

---

## Federal Framework

- **42 C.F.R. Part 2** — implementing regulations (SAMHSA).
- **CARES Act § 3221** (Pub. L. 116-136, 2020) — aligned Part 2 consent framework with HIPAA while preserving the proceedings-use restriction.
- **SAMHSA Final Rule (effective 2024)** — implemented CARES Act changes.
- **HIPAA (45 C.F.R. Parts 160, 164)** — general medical privacy; § 290dd-2 is stricter for SUD records.

## State Parallels

- **Cal. WIC § 5328** (`wic_5328_lps_confidentiality`) — state LPS confidentiality. Both regimes apply when an SUD treatment record is also an LPS record; stricter rule controls.
- **CMIA (Civ. §§ 56.05 et seq.)** — state general medical records confidentiality; owned by CA_Medical_Privacy_Officer.

## Within CA_Mental_Health_Litigator

- `wic_5250_14_day_certification` — § 5250's "impairment by chronic alcoholism" language brings SUD-diagnosed patients into the LPS IT framework. Every such certification generates records under both § 5328 and, if the facility is a part 2 program, § 290dd-2.
- `wic_5328_lps_confidentiality` — the most frequently-encountered state parallel. Disclosure analysis must clear both regimes.
- `evid_1016_psychotherapist_privilege` — state testimonial privilege; read together with federal psychotherapist privilege recognized in *Jaffee v. Redmond*.

## External Citizens

- **CA_Medical_Privacy_Officer** — primary lane for CMIA/HIPAA medical-records analysis; § 290dd-2 is the federal SUD-specific overlay.
- **CA_Family_Law_Litigator** — SUD records in custody/DVRO proceedings require § 290dd-2(b)(2)(C) court order, which is narrower than § 5328(a)(6). Admissibility challenges may succeed on federal grounds where they would fail on state grounds.
- **CA_Criminal_Law_Specialist** — § 290dd-2(c) absolute bar on use in criminal proceedings absent the heightened-showing order is a discovery and suppression argument.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — § 1983 liability for state-actor unauthorized Part 2 disclosure.
- **CA_Healthcare_Fraud_Litigator** — intersections where billing/claims use of SUD records triggers Part 2 compliance obligations.
- **HERALD** — witness authentication of Part 2 consent forms, § 290dd-2(b)(2)(C) court orders, redisclosure-notice documentation.

## Key Analytical Rule

**Dual-regime SUD records require dual-regime compliance.** A disclosure that satisfies § 5328 but not § 290dd-2 is unlawful. A court order that authorizes disclosure under § 5328(a)(6) but does not meet § 290dd-2(b)(2)(C) + Part 2 Subpart E standards is insufficient for SUD records.
