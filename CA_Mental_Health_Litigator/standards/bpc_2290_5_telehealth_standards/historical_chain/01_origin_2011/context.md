# BPC § 2290.5 — Historical Chain: Origin

**Standard ID:** `bpc_2290_5_telehealth_standards`
**Layer:** 3 — Historical Chain
**Date:** 2026-04-12

---

## Origin: Stats. 2011, Ch. 547

§2290.5 was enacted in 2011 as California's comprehensive telehealth framework for licensed health care providers. The 2011 enactment was driven by the rapid expansion of telemedicine platforms and the need to clarify that existing professional standards applied to the new delivery modality.

**Key design decision:** The Legislature rejected an "innovation exception" approach — which would have relaxed standards to encourage telehealth adoption. Instead, it adopted a "technology-neutral" approach: same standards apply regardless of modality. §2290.5(g) is the statutory expression of this policy.

**2019-2021 Amendments:** The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated telehealth adoption and triggered temporary relaxations of some telehealth requirements under emergency orders. §2290.5 was amended in 2021 to clarify that licensure requirements still apply notwithstanding the emergency expansions.
