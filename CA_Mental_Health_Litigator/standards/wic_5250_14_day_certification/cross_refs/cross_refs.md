# WIC § 5250 — Cross-References

**Layer:** 5 — Cross-References
**Date:** 2026-04-15

---

## Within CA_Mental_Health_Litigator

- **wic_5150_involuntary_hold** — predicate. § 5250 cannot certify what § 5150 could not lawfully hold. A successful § 5150(e) probable-cause attack voids every § 5250 certification that followed it.
- **wic_5270_15_additional_30_day** — escalation. § 5250's expiration without release triggers a § 5270.15 gravely-disabled extension.
- **wic_5325_patients_rights** — rights attachment. Patients' Bill of Rights applies throughout IT. Refusal of convulsive treatment and psychosurgery is absolute.
- **wic_5328_lps_confidentiality** — records. All evaluation and treatment records during the IT period are § 5328-confidential.
- **pen_1368_competency_doubt** / **pen_1369_competency_determination** — intersection. A § 5250 certification that occurs during pending criminal proceedings intersects with § 1368 competency doubt duties; the two pathways are separate but both may attach to the same person.

## External Citizens

- **CA_Probate_Conservatorship_Litigator (wic_5350_lps)** — § 5350 LPS conservatorship petition requires a § 5250 grave-disability predicate. Undermining § 5250 undermines the downstream § 5350 order.
- **US_Federal_Civil_Rights_Litigator** — § 1983 due process liability attaches when state actors direct or knowingly accept false § 5250 certifications.
- **CA_Family_Law_Litigator** — § 5250 hold records are the primary factual exhibits through which the LPS pathway enters family-court proceedings (custody, competency, DVRO).
- **HERALD** — witness authentication of § 5250 certification documents, facility designation letters, and § 5256 hearing transcripts.

## Cross-Standard Dependency Chain (LPS pipeline)

§ 5150 → § 5250 → § 5270.15 → § 5300 (dangerousness branch) → § 5350 (conservatorship).

A defect at any earlier gate is a defense at every later gate. Build the record working backward from the latest order.
