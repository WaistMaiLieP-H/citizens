## Origin: Stats. 1965, Ch. 299 — Evidence Code Enacted

The California Evidence Code was enacted in 1965, replacing the common law evidence framework. The psychotherapist-patient privilege (§§1010-1027) was a new creation — the common law did not recognize a psychotherapist-patient privilege separate from the physician-patient privilege.

The 1965 Legislature created the privilege to encourage treatment. §1016 was part of the original enactment — the patient-tender exception was recognized from the beginning as necessary to prevent the privilege from being used as a sword in litigation.

**The Jaffee v. Redmond parallel:** In 1996, the U.S. Supreme Court recognized a federal psychotherapist-patient privilege in Jaffee v. Redmond, 518 U.S. 1 (1996). California had recognized this privilege 31 years earlier. Jaffee's reasoning tracked California's §§1012/1016 framework closely.
