# Dusky v. United States — Cross-Reference for § 1369

**Citation:** Dusky v. United States, 362 U.S. 402 (1960) — VERIFIED
**Relationship to §1369:** §1369(b)(1)(B) implements Dusky's two prongs.

The Dusky two-prong standard IS the §1369(b)(1)(B) inquiry:
- Prong 1: Sufficient present ability to consult with lawyer with a reasonable degree of rational understanding = "assist counsel in the conduct of a defense in a rational manner"
- Prong 2: Rational as well as factual understanding of proceedings = "understand the nature of the criminal proceedings"

An evaluation report that addresses §1369(b)(1)(B) has satisfied the Dusky standard. An evaluation that does not address both prongs of §1369(b)(1)(B) has not assessed Dusky at all — regardless of what diagnosis is given.
