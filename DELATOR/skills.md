## SKILL-DEL-001: False Claims Act Qui Tam Filing

**Skill slug:** `fca-qui-tam-filing`
**Type:** LITIGATION
**Governing standards:** 31 USC §§ 3729–3733; Escobar (2016)

What this skill does: Takes a documented fraud record and converts it into a properly structured FCA qui tam complaint. Analyzes: (1) whether the fraud is against a federal program, (2) Escobar materiality, (3) first-to-file bar, (4) public disclosure bar / original-source analysis, (5) relator standing under Vermont Agency (2000), (6) SOL under § 3731. Produces a sealed complaint structure with factual allegations mapped to statutory elements.

**Audit triggers:** any document showing billing to Medicare, Medi-Cal, federal grants, federal contracts, DOL/pension programs, SSA

## SKILL-DEL-002: FCA Anti-Retaliation (§ 3730(h))

**Skill slug:** `fca-anti-retaliation`
**Type:** LITIGATION
**Governing standards:** 31 USC § 3730(h)

What this skill does: Analyzes whether adverse actions taken against the steward — employment termination, professional license suspension, financial isolation, communication interception — were taken "because of" lawful acts in furtherance of a qui tam action or investigation. Elements: (1) protected activity, (2) employer/contractor knew of activity, (3) adverse action, (4) causal connection. Remedies: reinstatement, double back pay, special damages, attorneys' fees and costs.

**Audit triggers:** termination notices, license actions, financial account closures, pension freezes following any report of federal fraud

## SKILL-DEL-003: IRS Whistleblower (§ 7623(b))

**Skill slug:** `irs-whistleblower-claim`
**Type:** LITIGATION
**Governing standards:** 26 USC § 7623(b)

What this skill does: Analyzes whether tax fraud information meets the § 7623(b) threshold (tax, penalties, interest exceed $2M; taxpayer income > $200K in at least one year). Evaluates whether information substantially contributed to collection. Prepares IRS Form 211 (Application for Award for Original Information). Coordinates with FCA filing where fraud involves both federal program and tax obligations.

**Audit triggers:** false W-2s, false pension reporting, unreported income, false payroll tax filings, false contractor payments

## SKILL-DEL-004: California False Claims Act (Gov. Code § 12652)

**Skill slug:** `ca-fca-qui-tam-filing`
**Type:** LITIGATION
**Governing standards:** Cal. Government Code §§ 12650–12656

What this skill does: Parallel state qui tam for Medi-Cal fraud and fraud against California state programs. Procedure mirrors federal FCA: sealed complaint filed in superior court, 60-day seal, CFCA relator share of 15–33%. Coordinates with federal FCA where fraud touches both federal Medicare and state Medi-Cal — dual filing maximizes relator recovery and government intervention probability.

**Audit triggers:** Medi-Cal claims, DHCS billing records, California state grant reports, CDSS records

## SKILL-DEL-005: Fraud Pattern Synthesis (Pre-Filing)

**Skill slug:** `fraud-pattern-synthesis`
**Type:** ANALYSIS
**Governing standards:** 31 USC § 3729(a)(1)(A)-(G); Escobar materiality standard

What this skill does: Takes the full document record from CA_Forensic_Document_Specialist, HERALD, and US_Federal_Financial_Fraud_Litigator and maps it to FCA statutory predicates. Identifies which billing records, pension reports, SSA contacts, and federal agency submissions constitute "false claims" under § 3729. Produces a predicate map: document → statutory element → materiality analysis → recovery estimate.

**Audit triggers:** any cross-case fraud synthesis request from council
