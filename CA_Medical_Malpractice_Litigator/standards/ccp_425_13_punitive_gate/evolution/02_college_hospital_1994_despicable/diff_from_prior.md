# §425.13 — Diff: Post-Enactment vs. Post-College Hospital

| Dimension | 1988–1993 | Post-College Hospital (1994+) |
|---|---|---|
| "Substantial probability" threshold | Unsettled — courts varied | Anchored to College Hospital "despicable" standard |
| Negligence alone | Some courts: sufficient | Not sufficient — must reach moral-culpability level |
| Gross negligence | Some courts: sufficient | Not alone sufficient — must show conscious disregard |
| Financial motive for clinical decisions | Persuasive | Strong — conscious disregard of patient for financial gain |
| Intentional tort framing | Unclear whether §425.13 applied | Increasingly recognized as outside §425.13 scope |
| Expert declaration content | Variable | Must address conscious disregard, not just standard-of-care deviation |

## What did NOT change

1. The gate itself — §425.13 still requires pre-filing court permission for punitive prayer
2. The §3294 substantive standard — malice, oppression, or fraud (clear and convincing)
3. The timing rules — motion before close of discovery
4. The health care provider scope definition (from CCP §340.5)
5. The absence of a punitive damages cap in medical malpractice cases
