# Statute Version at Time of Deyo v. Kilbourne (1978)

**§3333.1 version:** As originally enacted in 1975 MICRA package; only 3 years old when
Deyo was decided. The Court was applying the statute in its first years of operation.

**The original text's structure:** §3333.1(a) — defendant introduces collateral source
evidence; §3333.1(b) — plaintiff introduces premium evidence in response; §3333.1(c) —
anti-subrogation. This three-part structure has not been amended since 1975.

**Current operative law:** Deyo's practical-utility analysis of §3333.1(b) remains the
framework courts use to understand how the premium-evidence counter works in trial. The case
is regularly cited in MICRA damages jury instruction discussions. No subsequent California
Supreme Court case has overruled Deyo's interpretation of the premium-evidence right.
