# Statute Version Cited — Fein v. Permanente Medical Group (1985)

The Fein court applied the original 1975 version of §3333.2 — the flat $250,000 cap with no distinction between death and non-death cases and no inflation adjustment.

**Relevant text (1975 original):**
> In no action shall the amount of damages for noneconomic losses exceed two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000).

**Evolution stage applicable:** 01_origin_1975

**Version cited by the court:** Cal. Civ. Code §3333.2 as in force February 1985 (identical to original 1975 text; no amendments had been made in the interim).

**Relationship to current (AB 35) text:** Fein upheld the constitutional validity of the cap concept. Its holding applies equally to the AB 35 version — indeed, the AB 35 cap is less constitutionally vulnerable than the 1975 version because it is higher and indexed, removing the "erosion" critique that appeared in the Fein dissent.
