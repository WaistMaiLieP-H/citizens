# Statute Version Cited — Woods v. Young (1991)

The Woods court applied CCP §364 as it read in 1991 — essentially the original 1975 text with minor technical amendments.

**Relevant language applied:**
- §364(a) — 90-day pre-suit notice requirement
- §364(d) — tolling provision for late-discovered claims

**Evolution stage applicable:** 01_origin_1975 (operative text as of 1991)

**Version cited by the court:** CCP §364 as in force May 1991.

**Stability note:** §364 has not been materially amended between 1991 and 2026. The Woods holding applies to the current text verbatim.
