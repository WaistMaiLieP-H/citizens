# Statute Version at Time of Roa v. Lodi Medical Group (1985)

**§6146 version:** As originally enacted in 1975 MICRA package, with original lower thresholds
(40%/$50K, 33.33%/$100K, 25%/$200K, 10% over $200K). The AB 35 threshold update (2023)
did not change the constitutional structure upheld by Roa — it only changed the dollar amounts.

**The constitutional holding is independent of the specific thresholds.** Roa upheld the
mandatory sliding scale concept. Whether the thresholds are at 1975 levels or 2023 levels,
the constitutional analysis is the same: the Legislature may regulate medical malpractice
attorney fees as a rational response to a perceived medical liability crisis.

**Current operative law:** Roa's constitutional holding remains controlling. The 2023 AB 35
update to the thresholds did not disturb the constitutional foundation Roa established. The
voidability principle (fee agreements exceeding §6146 are voidable by client) remains unchanged.
