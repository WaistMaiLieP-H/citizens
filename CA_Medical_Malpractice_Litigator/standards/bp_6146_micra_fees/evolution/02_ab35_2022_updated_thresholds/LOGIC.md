# §6146 — Evolution Stage 02: AB 35 (2022) — Updated Fee Thresholds

**Enacted:** Stats. 2022, Ch. 395 (AB 35, Assembly Member Chiu)
**Effective:** January 1, 2023

---

## The wound this stage answered

The original 1975 §6146 fee thresholds had not been adjusted in 47 years. $200,000 in 1975
had the purchasing power of approximately $1.1 million in 2022. The 10% tier on all amounts
over $200K made large medical malpractice cases economically marginal for plaintiff's attorneys
even when the underlying harm was severe and the case was strong.

Simultaneously, AB 35 raised §3333.2's noneconomic damages cap from the flat $250,000 figure
(unchanged since 1975) to escalating caps ($350K in 2023, rising to $750K by 2033 for death
cases). Raising the cap without adjusting the fee schedule would have created a compound problem:
higher potential verdicts subject to the same economically limiting old fee scale.

---

## What AB 35 changed in §6146

**New fee thresholds (effective January 1, 2023):**

| Recovery Range | Maximum Attorney Fee Percentage |
|---|---|
| First $250,000 | 40% |
| Next $250,000 ($250K–$500K) | 33.33% |
| Next $500,000 ($500K–$1M) | 25% |
| Any amount over $1,000,000 | 15% |

**Increase from original thresholds:**
- Tier 1 ceiling: $50K → $250K (5× increase)
- Tier 2 ceiling: $100K → $500K (5× increase)
- Tier 3 ceiling: $200K → $1M (5× increase)
- Tier 4: 10% → 15% (rate increase) on amounts over $1M

**What AB 35 did NOT change:**
- The mandatory disclosure requirement (§6146(a))
- The application to all forms of recovery (verdict, settlement, arbitration)
- The scope (medical malpractice cases only)
- The client's inability to waive the cap

---

## Why AB 35's fee update matters for Michael

**The new thresholds make SIRVA and spine surgery cases more economically viable for attorneys.**
Under the original 1975 thresholds, a $670K expected recovery (§3333.2 cap + economic damages)
would have been subject to a much more limiting fee:
- 40% × $50K = $20K
- 33.33% × $50K = $16.7K
- 25% × $100K = $25K
- 10% × $470K = $47K
- Total old fee: ~$108.7K (approximately 16.2% of $670K)

Under the current AB 35 thresholds:
- 40% × $250K = $100K
- 33.33% × $250K = $83.3K
- 25% × $170K = $42.5K
- Total new fee: ~$225.8K (approximately 33.7% of $670K)

**The AB 35 fee update means more attorneys will take legitimate medical malpractice cases.
The SIRVA case ($670K+ expected recovery) is now in a fee range that makes it viable for
plaintiff's malpractice firms.**
