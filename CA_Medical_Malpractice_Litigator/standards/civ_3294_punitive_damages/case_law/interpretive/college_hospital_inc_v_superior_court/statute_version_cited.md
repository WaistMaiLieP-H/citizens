# Statute Version Cited — College Hospital Inc. v. Superior Court

**Case:** College Hospital Inc. v. Superior Court, 8 Cal.4th 704 (1994)
**Decided:** 1994
**Statute interpreted:** Cal. Civil Code §3294

---

## Version of §3294 in Effect at Decision Date

**Stage in evolution chain:** `03_1987_amendment_employer_liability`

The 1987 amendment (Ch. 1498, Stats. 1987) is the operative version in College Hospital.
The 1987 amendment introduced the term "despicable" into both the malice definition
(§3294(c)(1): "despicable conduct which is carried on by the defendant with a willful and
conscious disregard of the rights or safety of others") and the oppression definition
(§3294(c)(2): "despicable conduct that subjects a person to cruel and unjust hardship in
conscious disregard of that person's rights").

The College Hospital court was explicitly tasked with defining what the Legislature meant
when it added "despicable" in 1987 — the case is the Supreme Court's authoritative
interpretation of that amendment. This places the case squarely in interpretation of the
`03_1987_amendment_employer_liability` stage.

## Why This Matters for Corpus Integrity

A later case citing College Hospital for the "despicable" definition is always invoking
the 1987 amendment text, even if the citation chain goes through intermediate courts.
When cross-referencing College Hospital, note: "interpreting §3294 as amended 1987
(Stats. 1987, Ch. 1498); definition of 'despicable' in §3294(c)(1) and (c)(2)."
