# Statute Version at Time of Foxborough v. Van Atta (1994)

**§340.5 version:** MICRA enactment (Stats. 1975, Ch. 2, 2d Ex. Sess., effective 1976-01-01),
unchanged. The 1994 Court of Appeal applied §340.5 in its current form.

**Current operative text:** §340.5 is unchanged from MICRA enactment. Foxborough's
application of the Jolly "reason to suspect" standard to physician-reassurance situations
remains good law and is regularly applied in §340.5 summary judgment proceedings.
