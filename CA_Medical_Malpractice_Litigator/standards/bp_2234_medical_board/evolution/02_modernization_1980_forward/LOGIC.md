# §2234 Modernization — 1980s Forward: Subcategory Enumeration

## The wound this evolution answered

The pre-1980s catch-all "unprofessional conduct" standard, while flexible, created uncertainty
about what the Medical Board needed to prove in complex cases and what standard physicians should
expect. Due process concerns arose: physicians facing discipline argued the catch-all standard
was too vague.

The Legislature responded through the 1970s-1980s by codifying specific subcategories within
the unprofessional conduct definition — making the most common and serious grounds for discipline
explicit while preserving the broader catch-all authority.

## The key subcategory additions

**§2234(b) — gross negligence:** Explicit gross negligence standard, distinguishable from civil
negligence. The extreme-departure standard provided due process clarity: a single egregious
episode can satisfy this without requiring a pattern.

**§2234(c) — repeated negligent acts:** The "two or more" standard provided a lower threshold
than gross negligence while still requiring more than a single bad outcome. This addressed the
recurring-substandard-practice physician who never had a single episode bad enough for gross
negligence but consistently practiced below standard.

**§2234(d) — incompetence:** Addressed physicians whose knowledge or technical ability had
deteriorated below the expected standard, independent of any specific negligent act.

**§2234(f) — dishonesty/corruption:** Addressed fraud in billing, false diagnosis, and other
financially motivated misconduct. Became increasingly important with the growth of Medicare/Medi-Cal
fraud enforcement.

## The due process holding that governs

**Holden v. Medical Examining Board** type cases (see case_law/) — California courts confirmed
that §2234's "unprofessional conduct" standard is sufficiently definite to satisfy due process
when applied against physicians. The standard is defined by reference to the conduct expected
of licensed physicians, which is a known professional standard.

## Diff from prior stage

| Dimension | Pre-1980 | Post-1980 Modernization |
|---|---|---|
| Basis for discipline | Generic "unprofessional conduct" catch-all | Specific subcategories (b)-(n) + general catch-all |
| Gross negligence | Implicit in catch-all | §2234(b) explicit |
| Repeated negligent acts | Implicit | §2234(c) explicit — two or more acts |
| Incompetence | Implicit | §2234(d) explicit |
| Fraud/billing dishonesty | Implicit | §2234(f) explicit |
| Due process clarity | Subject to challenge | Subcategories provide fair notice |
