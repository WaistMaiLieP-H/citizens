# §2234(b) Gross Negligence Standard — Statutory Anchor

**Primary authority:** Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §2234(b)

**Operative text (§2234(b)):**
"Gross negligence. As used in this section, 'gross negligence' means an extreme departure from
the applicable standard of care, whether applied in the same or similar community."

## The rule comes from the statute

The gross negligence standard is defined by the statute itself — not by judicial gloss. The
Legislature enacted the definition in the statute's text. The standard is:

**Extreme departure from the applicable standard of care** (same or similar community reference).

This is a higher threshold than civil malpractice negligence (which requires only a deviation
from the standard of care). "Extreme departure" means the conduct must be egregious — not merely
below standard but so far below standard as to represent reckless or conscious disregard for
patient safety.

**Operational application (from statute + Board practice):**
- One episode can qualify if the departure is extreme enough
- The "same or similar community" language imports the civil malpractice standard of care baseline
  but applies the extreme-departure threshold on top of it
- Board expert consultants evaluate cases against this standard

## Case law status

Case law applying §2234(b) exists in:
- Medical Board of California Office of Administrative Hearings (OAH) decisions
- California courts of appeal decisions reviewing Board discipline under CCP §1094.5
