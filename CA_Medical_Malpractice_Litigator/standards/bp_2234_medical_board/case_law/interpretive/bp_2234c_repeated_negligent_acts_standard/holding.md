# §2234(c) Repeated Negligent Acts — Statutory Definition (Anchor Entry)

**Entry type:** STATUTORY ANCHOR — not a case holding
**Primary authority:** Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §2234(c)
**Why this entry exists:** §2234(c) defines "repeated negligent acts" in the statute's own
text. The rule comes directly from the Legislature. This entry holds that definition.

---

## The Rule

**"Repeated negligent acts"** means **two or more negligent acts or omissions.**
An initial negligent act or omission followed by a separate and distinct negligent act
or omission.

*(Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §2234(c), verbatim)*

---

## What This Means in Practice

**Two-act minimum:** A single act of negligence, no matter how serious, does not satisfy
§2234(c). There must be at least two separate acts.

**"Separate and distinct":** The two acts must be independent — the second is not merely
a continuation of the first. Treating the same patient twice with substandard care on
separate visits satisfies §2234(c). One continuous course of negligent treatment during
a single procedure is analyzed under §2234(b) (gross negligence) instead.

**Lower threshold than §2234(b):** Each individual act under §2234(c) need only be
negligent — not "extremely departing." Two ordinary-negligence-level acts, if separate
and distinct, satisfy the statute.

**Pattern inference:** In Board proceedings, a pattern of two or more negligent acts
across multiple patients can support §2234(c) even without a single "extreme" event.
This makes §2234(c) the broader net in Medical Board cases involving recurring substandard
practice patterns.

---

## Relevance to Corpus Cases

**SSA/DDS medical evidence fraud (systematic):** If multiple providers across multiple
visits made separate and distinct negligent omissions in documenting the steward's disability
(each visit a separate act), the pattern may support §2234(c) Medical Board referrals
against individual providers, independent of the fraud claims.

**Systematic denial of care:** Each discrete denial of medically necessary treatment by
the same provider across separate encounters is a separate negligent act for §2234(c)
purposes.
