# Statute Version Cited — §2234(c) Repeated Negligent Acts Anchor

**Entry type:** STATUTORY ANCHOR
**Primary authority:** Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §2234(c)

---

## Version in Effect

This entry reflects §2234(c) as currently enacted. The "two or more negligent acts"
and "separate and distinct" language is the operative statutory definition.

**Evolution stage cross-reference:** Refer to `bp_2234_medical_board/evolution/` for the
full amendment chain. The definition in §2234(c) is the current stage.

---

## Relationship to §2234(b)

§2234(b) and §2234(c) are alternative grounds for Medical Board discipline — not
cumulative requirements. A Board accusation may allege either or both:
- §2234(b): one act, but extreme departure
- §2234(c): two or more acts, each negligent (not necessarily extreme)

When reviewing a pattern of care, pleading both in the alternative is standard practice.
