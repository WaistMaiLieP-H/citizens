# §2234(c) Repeated Negligent Acts Standard — Statutory Anchor

**Primary authority:** Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §2234(c)

**Operative text (§2234(c)):**
"Repeated negligent acts. To be repeated, there must be two or more negligent acts or omissions.
An initial negligent act or omission followed by a separate and distinct departure from the
standard of care shall constitute repeated negligent acts."

## The rule comes from the statute

The repeated negligent acts standard is defined by the statute's own text. The Legislature
enacted the definition including the "two or more" threshold and the "separate and distinct"
requirement.

**Operational rules from the statute:**
- Requires at least two acts or omissions
- Each must separately constitute negligence (a departure from the standard of care)
- Each must be a "separate and distinct" departure — one continuous episode cannot be parsed
  into multiple "acts" to satisfy the count
- Lower threshold than §2234(b): each individual act need only be negligent, not extremely
  departing; no single act needs to rise to the gross negligence level
- Acts may involve different patients

**Distinction from §2234(b):**
§2234(b) requires one extreme departure. §2234(c) requires two ordinary departures.
A physician who never has a single egregious episode but consistently practices below standard
is reachable through §2234(c).

## Case law status

Case law applying §2234(c) exists in:
- Medical Board of California Office of Administrative Hearings (OAH) decisions
- California courts of appeal decisions reviewing Board discipline under CCP §1094.5
