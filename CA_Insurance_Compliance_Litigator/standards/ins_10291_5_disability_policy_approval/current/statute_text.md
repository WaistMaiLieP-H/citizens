# Disability Policy Approval Standards — Statutory Text

## Key Operative Provisions

(a) [Commissioner SHALL NOT approve disability policies meeting the following conditions:]

(b) The commissioner shall not approve any form under Section 10290 if the commissioner finds that:
(1) The benefits are unreasonable in relation to the premium charged, or if any policy form or contract contains any provisions which are unjust, unfair, inequitable, misleading, deceptive, which encourage misrepresentation, or which are untrue, misleading, or deceptive as contemplated by Section 780, or which contains provisions which are unintelligible, uncertain, ambiguous, or abstruse, or likely to mislead a person to whom the policy is offered, delivered, or issued.
(2) Any policy provides benefits... where the benefit in any one event is more than three times the benefit in any other event, for the same loss...
(7) Benefits are not sufficient to be of real economic value to the insured.
(13) The form does not conform with any provision of this code or any other applicable provision of law.

(c) The application form for any disability policy... shall not contain any question that is misleading or that would tend to produce an incorrect medical history... No question or questionnaire shall discriminate on account of race, color, religion, or national origin... Prior to performing any test for human immunodeficiency virus... the insurer shall inform the applicant of the test...

(i) The commissioner shall not fix the rate of premium to be charged for disability insurance.

**⚠ IMPORTANT CORRECTION (from source prep):** Prior characterizations of §10291.5 as creating a "bad faith private right of action" or authorizing "punitive damages" were INCORRECT. Section 10291.5 is a CDI policy-form approval standard — it governs what policy forms the Commissioner will approve, not private litigation rights. The bad faith private action is common law Gruenberg/Brandt. This correction is fully documented.
