# Rule — Ins. Code § 1861.02 Proposition 103 Auto Rate Regulation

**Standard ID:** ins_1861_02_prop103_auto_rates
**Authority:** Cal. Ins. Code § 1861.02 (Proposition 103, approved by voters November 8, 1988)

## Core Rule — Mandatory Rating Factor Hierarchy

Auto insurance rates MUST be determined using the following factors in DECREASING ORDER OF IMPORTANCE:
1. Driving safety record (primary)
2. Annual miles driven (secondary)
3. Years of driving experience (tertiary)
4. CDI-approved additional factors with substantial relationship to risk (quaternary)

**Use of any criterion NOT on this list and NOT approved by CDI = UNFAIR DISCRIMINATION per §1861.02(a).**

## Good Driver Discount (§1861.02(b))

Any person meeting §1861.025 criteria (no more than 1 at-fault accident in prior 3 years; no DUI/reckless in prior 7 years; minimum driving experience) MUST be offered a Good Driver Discount policy by any insurer from whom they seek coverage. The insurer SHALL NOT REFUSE.

The Good Driver Discount rate must be AT LEAST 20% below the otherwise applicable rate for the same coverage.

## Critical Anti-Discrimination Provision (§1861.02(c))

**Absence of prior auto insurance coverage ALONE cannot:**
- Disqualify a person from Good Driver Discount eligibility
- Affect rates, premiums, or insurability

This directly prevents the "you have no insurance history, so you get no discount" circular penalty that previously trapped low-income drivers (often those who could not afford insurance) in a permanent high-risk classification regardless of their actual driving record.

## Unapproved Criteria = Unfair Discrimination

If an insurer uses any factor not on the mandatory list and not CDI-approved:
- That use constitutes "unfair discrimination" per § 1861.02(a)
- It is per se a §790.03 unfair practice
- CDI can revoke the rate filing, order restitution, and take enforcement action
- Private bad faith claim available if the insurer used an unapproved criterion to inflate premiums charged to the insured

## Application — RedJag Forced GAP Insurance

If the RedJag dealer or its financing/insurance affiliate forced GAP insurance as a condition of the sale at rates that were:
- Not CDI-approved for the mandatory rating factor hierarchy
- Based on factors not on the §1861.02(a) list (e.g., lack of prior coverage per §1861.02(c))
- Priced above the CDI-approved rate for that coverage type

...then the forced insurance scheme constitutes unfair discrimination under §1861.02(a) and an unfair claims practice under §790.03.

**Note:** GAP insurance may be regulated separately as Mechanical Breakdown Insurance (MBI) or Debt Cancellation Contract (DCC) under Ins. Code §779.23 et seq. If the RedJag product was not properly licensed/filed as a GAP/DCC product, additional violations apply. Flag for Steward investigation.
