# Rule — INS §790.09: CDI Enforcement Does Not Extinguish Civil or Criminal Liability

**Citation:** Cal. Ins. Code §790.09
**Standard ID:** ins_790_09_enforcement_nonabsolution
**Status:** PUBLISHED

---

## The Rule

A CDI cease-and-desist order issued against an insurer, agent, or adjuster — and any administrative proceeding to enforce it — does NOT relieve or absolve the person from:

1. **Administrative action against their license or certificate** (CDI can still revoke, suspend, or restrict)
2. **Civil liability** under California law arising from the same unfair or deceptive practices
3. **Criminal penalty** under California law for the same conduct

## Why This Matters: The Nonabsolution Principle

§790.09 closes a structural loophole. Without it, an insurer caught in a CDI investigation could argue: "We cooperated with CDI, we received a cease-and-desist, the matter is resolved — no civil lawsuit can proceed." §790.09 expressly forecloses that argument.

The CDI enforcement track and the civil tort track run on parallel rails. Resolution on one does not affect the other.

## Practical Operation

### Track 1: CDI Administrative Enforcement
- Cease-and-desist order under INS §790.05
- License suspension/revocation under CDI authority
- Administrative penalties to Insurance Fund under §10123.135(h)(6)

### Track 2: Civil Liability (Preserved by §790.09)
- First-party bad faith tort (Gruenberg v. Aetna Ins. Co. (1973) 9 Cal.3d 566)
- Breach of implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing
- CCP §1021.5 private attorney general fee shift if case vindicates public interest

### Track 3: Criminal Penalty (Preserved by §790.09)
- PEN §548 et seq. (insurance fraud)
- INS §1871.4 (workers' compensation fraud)
- Federal 18 USC §1347 (health care fraud) — not preempted by §790.09

## Application to Steward Cases

### Blue Shield Prior Authorization Case
Blue Shield's compliance with any CDI order or investigation does NOT resolve Michael Hartmann's civil bad faith claim. The civil track proceeds independently regardless of CDI enforcement history.

If Blue Shield received a CDI inquiry about its AI utilization review practices and subsequently changed its policies, that change does not:
- Retroactively remedy the specific denials issued to Michael
- Constitute admission or denial of wrongdoing for civil purposes
- Bar Michael from proceeding on his individual bad faith claim

### State Farm / Hillberg UIT Case
Any CDI administrative action against the State Farm agent or Northern Trust for the Hillberg UIT policy does not extinguish:
- Ann Hillberg's or her estate's civil claim for insurance bad faith
- Criminal referral liability if fraud elements are established
- Mandated reporter liability under WIC §15630 (separate statutory track)
