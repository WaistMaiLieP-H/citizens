## Skill 1: § 790.03(h) Unfair Claims Settlement — 16-Practice Audit
Apply each of the 16 enumerated unfair claims settlement practices to any insurer's claims-handling conduct. Identify which specific practice was violated. Distinguish between individual violations (actionable as bad faith) and general business practices (CDI enforcement threshold). Document each violation with claim file evidence.

## Skill 2: § 10123.135 Prior Authorization Compliance Audit
For any denied or delayed prior authorization:
- Who made the denial decision? (Licensed physician/health care professional required)
- Was an AI/algorithm tool used? (If yes: per se violation of §10123.135(j)(2) for medical necessity denials)
- Did the decision meet the timeline? (5 business days standard; 72 hours for urgent/imminent threat)
- Was the denial communicated in writing with clinical reasons, criteria used, and reviewer's name? (§10123.135(h)(4))
- Was an explanation of appeal rights included? (§10123.135(h)(4))

## Skill 3: AI-Denial Identification and Documentation
Identify insurers using AI/algorithm tools for UR. Document whether any denial, delay, or modification of health care services was made "based, in whole or in part, on medical necessity" by AI rather than a licensed clinician. Each AI-based medical necessity denial since §10123.135(j)(2)'s effective date is an independent violation.

## Skill 4: CDI Complaint Drafting
Structure CDI administrative complaints under INS §790.03 unfair practices and §10123.135 UR violations. Document the "frequency as to indicate a general business practice" element for §790.03(h) claims. Identify CDI penalties available under §10123.135(h)(6): administrative penalties per failure.

## Skill 5: Bad Faith Civil Complaint Structuring
First-party insurance bad faith claims (insured vs. insurer):
- Identify breach of the covenant of good faith and fair dealing
- Map to §790.03(h) violations as evidence of bad faith (Moradi-Shalal: §790.03 violations are not independent private right of action for third parties, but are evidence of bad faith in first-party claims)
- Calculate damages: contract damages + tort damages (emotional distress, consequential) + punitive if malice/oppression/fraud (Civ. Code §3294)

## Skill 6: ERISA Preemption Analysis
Before filing state law insurance claims: determine whether the plan is ERISA-governed. ERISA §514 preempts state law claims that "relate to" employee benefit plans. Individual market policies: state law applies. Group employer plans: ERISA governs. Cross-ref to US_Federal_ERISA_Litigator for ERISA §502(a) claims.

## Skill 7: § 10123.135 UR Timeline Violation Calculation
For each UR decision: calculate time from receipt of necessary information to decision. Compare against statutory timelines (5 business days standard; 72 hours urgent). Identify each day of delay beyond the statutory window as a potential violation. §10123.135(h)(6): CDI may assess administrative penalties per failure.

## Skill 8: Duplicate Document / Altered Application Analysis
§790.03(h)(8): settling claims on the basis of an application altered without notice to insured = unfair practice. For Blue Shield duplicate MRI pages in prior auth file: analyze whether the record presented to the UR reviewer was altered, incomplete, or misrepresented. Document chain of custody of the original records versus the UR submission.

## Skill 9: Vehicle Insurance Fraud Pattern Analysis
For RedJag and similar cases:
- Was the vehicle properly disclosed as clean title when it was not?
- Was GAP insurance properly offered and explained?
- Was any claim denied on the basis of misrepresented vehicle history?
- §790.03(h)(1): misrepresenting pertinent policy provisions to claimants = unfair practice

## Skill 10: Penalty Stack for UR Violations
For Blue Shield prior authorization violations:
- CDI administrative penalty per §10123.135(h)(6): per failure
- Bad faith tort damages: contract + emotional distress
- Punitive damages if malice/oppression/fraud (Civil Code §3294)
- §10123.135 audit of all claims in the same timeframe for pattern evidence
