## Cases Covered

### Blue Shield Prior Authorization Violations (Cases #11-14)

Blue Shield denied prior authorization for spine surgery and related treatments. The issues:

1. **§10123.135(e):** Only a licensed physician or licensed health care professional competent in the specific clinical issues may deny or modify requests for authorization for medical necessity reasons. A non-physician denial is a per se violation.
2. **§10123.135(j)(2):** AI/algorithm tools CANNOT deny, delay, or modify health care services for medical necessity reasons. If Blue Shield used algorithmic UR tools for any denial, the AI prohibition applies.
3. **§10123.135(h)(1):** Standard UR decisions must be made within 5 business days; urgent/imminent-threat decisions within 72 hours. Delays beyond these windows are §10123.135 violations.
4. **§790.03(h)(4):** Failing to affirm or deny coverage of claims within a reasonable time after proof of loss = unfair claims settlement practice.
5. **Duplicate MRI pages in the prior auth file:** Evidence of record manipulation in the claims-handling process — potential §790.03(h)(8) violation (settling claims on the basis of an application altered without notice to insured).

The yo-yo financing and stolen/stripped vehicle created an insurance question: what insurance was on the vehicle at time of delivery, what coverage applied when it was found stripped, and whether GAP insurance was properly disclosed. §790.03(h)(1) (misrepresenting policy provisions) and §790.03(h)(13) (failing to explain basis for denial) are operative provisions.

## ERISA Preemption Alert

ERISA §514 preempts state law causes of action that "relate to" employee benefit plans. If Blue Shield was providing coverage under an employer-sponsored ERISA health plan, §790.03 claims may be preempted and must be brought under ERISA §502(a) instead. **Steward must determine whether Blue Shield coverage was through an ERISA-governed employer plan or an individual market policy.** If individual market: state law applies. If ERISA: federal track.
